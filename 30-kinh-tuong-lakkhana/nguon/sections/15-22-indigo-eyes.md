## dn30:2.10.0

- **EN**: 21–22. Indigo Eyes

## dn30:2.10.1  `bj9.270, dr11.186, ms8D_514, msdiv228, pts-cs2.10, sya11.182`
- **PLI**: “Yampi, bhikkhave, tathāgato purimaṁ jātiṁ purimaṁ bhavaṁ purimaṁ niketaṁ pubbe manussabhūto samāno
- **EN**: “Mendicants, in some past lives the Realized One was reborn as a human being.

## dn30:2.10.2
- **PLI**: na ca visaṭaṁ, na ca visāci, na ca pana viceyya pekkhitā, ujuṁ tathā pasaṭamujumano, piyacakkhunā bahujanaṁ udikkhitā ahosi.
- **EN**: When looking at others he didn’t glare, look askance, or avert his eyes. Being straightforward, he reached out to others with straightforward intentions, looking at people with kindly eyes.

## dn30:2.10.3
- **PLI**: So tassa kammassa kaṭattā …pe…
- **EN**: Due to performing those deeds he was reborn in a heavenly realm.

## dn30:2.10.4
- **PLI**: so tato cuto itthattaṁ āgato samāno imāni dve mahāpurisalakkhaṇāni paṭilabhati.
- **EN**: When he came back to this place he obtained these two marks:

## dn30:2.10.5
- **PLI**: Abhinīlanetto ca hoti gopakhumo ca.
- **EN**: his eyes are indigo, and he has eyelashes like a cow’s.
- **NOTE**: Dark colored eyes give the impression of wide pupils and an open gaze, while the eyelashes of cows lend them a tenderness.
