## dn30:2.28.2

- **EN**: He gave up wrong livelihood and earned a living by right livelihood. He refrained from falsifying weights, metals, or measures; bribery, fraud, cheating, and duplicity; mutilation, murder, abduction, banditry, plunder, and violence.

## dn30:2.28.3
- **PLI**: So tassa kammassa kaṭattā upacitattā ussannattā vipulattā kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā sugatiṁ saggaṁ lokaṁ upapajjati.
- **EN**: Due to performing, accumulating, heaping up, and amassing those deeds, when his body broke up, after death, he was reborn in a good place, a heavenly realm.

## dn30:2.28.4

- **EN**: There he surpassed the other gods in ten respects: heavenly lifespan, beauty, happiness, glory, sovereignty, sights, sounds, smells, tastes, and touches.

## dn30:2.28.5
- **PLI**: So tato cuto itthattaṁ āgato samāno imāni dve mahāpurisalakkhaṇāni paṭilabhati, samadanto ca hoti susukkadāṭho ca.
- **EN**: When he came back to this place he obtained these two marks: his teeth are even and perfectly white.
- **NOTE**: His teeth are not “crooked” or “stained” by corruption.

- **EN**: Possessing these marks, if he stays at home he becomes a king, a wheel-turning monarch, a just and principled king. His dominion extends to all four sides, he achieves stability in the country, and he possesses the seven treasures.

- **EN**: He has over a thousand sons who are valiant and heroic, crushing the armies of his enemies.

## dn30:2.29.5

- **EN**: After conquering this land girt by sea—free of harassment by bandits, successful and prosperous, safe, blessed, free of pests—he reigns by principle, without rod or sword.
