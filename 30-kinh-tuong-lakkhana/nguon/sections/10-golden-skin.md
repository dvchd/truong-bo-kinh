# Muc 1.28 — Golden Skin  (33 đoạn)

## dn30:1.28.0
- **PLI**: 13. Suvaṇṇavaṇṇalakkhaṇaṁ
- **EN**: 13. Golden Skin

## dn30:1.28.1  `cck11.172, csp1ed8.130, csp2ed8.130, ms8D_474, msdiv218, ndp8.123, pts-cs1.28, sya11.174`
- **PLI**: “Yampi, bhikkhave, tathāgato purimaṁ jātiṁ purimaṁ bhavaṁ purimaṁ niketaṁ pubbe manussabhūto samāno
- **EN**: “Mendicants, in some past lives the Realized One was reborn as a human being.

## dn30:1.28.2
- **PLI**: akkodhano ahosi anupāyāsabahulo,
- **EN**: He wasn’t irritable or bad-tempered.

## dn30:1.28.3
- **PLI**: bahumpi vutto samāno nābhisajji na kuppi na byāpajji na patitthīyi, na kopañca dosañca appaccayañca pātvākāsi.
- **EN**: Even when heavily criticized he didn’t lose his temper, become annoyed, hostile, and hard-hearted, or display annoyance, hate, and bitterness.

## dn30:1.28.4
- **PLI**: Dātā ca ahosi sukhumānaṁ mudukānaṁ attharaṇānaṁ pāvuraṇānaṁ khomasukhumānaṁ kappāsikasukhumānaṁ koseyyasukhumānaṁ kambalasukhumānaṁ.
- **EN**: He donated soft and delicate mats and blankets, and cloaks of delicate linen, cotton, silk, and wool.

## dn30:1.28.5
- **PLI**: So tassa kammassa kaṭattā upacitattā …pe…
- **EN**: Due to performing those deeds he was reborn in a heavenly realm.

## dn30:1.28.6
- **PLI**: so tato cuto itthattaṁ āgato samāno imaṁ mahāpurisalakkhaṇaṁ paṭilabhati.
- **EN**: When he came back to this place he obtained this mark:

## dn30:1.28.7
- **PLI**: Suvaṇṇavaṇṇo hoti kañcanasannibhattaco.
- **EN**: he is golden colored; his skin shines like lustrous gold.
- **NOTE**: Giving fine coverings leads to fine skin. Less obviously, anger is said to lead to discoloring and ugliness.

## dn30:1.29.1  `dr11.178, ms8D_475, pts-cs1.29`
- **PLI**: So tena lakkhaṇena samannāgato sace agāraṁ ajjhāvasati, rājā hoti cakkavattī …pe…
- **EN**: Possessing this mark, if he stays at home he becomes a wheel-turning monarch.

## dn30:1.29.2
- **PLI**: rājā samāno kiṁ labhati?
- **EN**: And what does he obtain as king?

## dn30:1.29.3
- **PLI**: Lābhī hoti sukhumānaṁ mudukānaṁ attharaṇānaṁ pāvuraṇānaṁ khomasukhumānaṁ kappāsikasukhumānaṁ koseyyasukhumānaṁ kambalasukhumānaṁ.
- **EN**: He obtains soft and delicate mats and blankets, and delicate cloaks of linen, cotton, silk, and wool.

## dn30:1.29.4
- **PLI**: Rājā samāno idaṁ labhati …pe…
- **EN**: That’s what he obtains as king.

## dn30:1.29.5
- **PLI**: buddho samāno kiṁ labhati?
- **EN**: And what does he obtain as Buddha?

## dn30:1.29.6
- **PLI**: Lābhī hoti sukhumānaṁ mudukānaṁ attharaṇānaṁ pāvuraṇānaṁ khomasukhumānaṁ kappāsikasukhumānaṁ koseyyasukhumānaṁ kambalasukhumānaṁ.
- **EN**: He obtains soft and delicate mats and blankets, and cloaks of delicate linen, cotton, silk, and wool.

## dn30:1.29.7
- **PLI**: Buddho samāno idaṁ labhati”.
- **EN**: That’s what he obtains as Buddha.”

## dn30:1.29.8
- **PLI**: Etamatthaṁ bhagavā avoca.
- **EN**: The Buddha spoke this matter.

## dn30:1.30.1  `ms8D_476, msdiv219, pts-cs1.30`
- **PLI**: Tatthetaṁ vuccati:
- **EN**: On this it is said:

## dn30:1.30.2  `ms8D_477, pts-vp-pli3.160`
- **PLI**: “Akkodhañca adhiṭṭhahi adāsi,
- **EN**: “Fixated on good will, he gave gifts.
- **NOTE**: For this positive sense of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>adhiṭṭhahi</i> compare <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an6.52/en/sujato#8.2'>AN 6.52:8.2</a> and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn140/en/sujato#11.1'>MN 140:11.1</a>.

## dn30:1.30.3
- **PLI**: Dānañca vatthāni sukhumāni succhavīni;
- **EN**: In an earlier life he poured forth cloth

## dn30:1.30.4
- **PLI**: Purimatarabhave ṭhito abhivissaji,
- **EN**: fine and soft to touch,

## dn30:1.30.5
- **PLI**: Mahimiva suro abhivassaṁ.
- **EN**: like a god pouring rain on this broad earth.
- **NOTE**: There’s a play on <i lang='pi' translate='no'>abhivissaji</i> (“gave away”) and <i lang='pi' translate='no'>abhivassa</i> (“pouring rain”).

## dn30:1.30.6  `bj9.260, ms8D_478, vri3.120`
- **PLI**: Taṁ katvāna ito cuto dibbaṁ,
- **EN**: So doing he passed from here to heaven,

## dn30:1.30.7
- **PLI**: Upapajji sukataphalavipākamanubhutvā;
- **EN**: where he enjoyed the fruits of deeds well done.

## dn30:1.30.8
- **PLI**: Kanakatanusannibho idhābhibhavati,
- **EN**: Here he wins a figure <j>shining like honey-yellow gold,
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Kanaka</i>, one of the many Indic words for gold, is from the same Proto-Indo European root as the English word “honey”.

## dn30:1.30.9
- **PLI**: Suravarataroriva indo.
- **EN**: like Indra, the finest of gods.

## dn30:1.30.10  `csp1ed8.131, csp2ed8.131, ms8D_479, ndp8.124, sya11.175`
- **PLI**: Gehañcāvasati naro apabbajjam’,
- **EN**: If that man stays in the house, not wishing to go forth,

## dn30:1.30.11
- **PLI**: icchaṁ mahatimahiṁ anusāsati;
- **EN**: he conquers and rules this vast, broad earth,

## dn30:1.30.12
- **PLI**: Pasayha sahidha sattaratanaṁ,
- **EN**: together with the seven treasures here.

## dn30:1.30.13
- **PLI**: Paṭilabhati vimalasukhumacchaviṁ suciñca.
- **EN**: He gets immaculate, delicate, pure skin,

## dn30:1.30.14  `cck11.173, dr11.179, ms8D_480`
- **PLI**: Lābhī acchādanavatthamokkhapāvuraṇānaṁ,
- **EN**: receiving clothing, cloth, and the finest cloaks
- **NOTE**: Here <i lang='pi' translate='no'>mokkha</i> = <i lang='pi' translate='no'>pāmokkha</i> (“chief, best”).

## dn30:1.30.15
- **PLI**: Bhavati yadi anāgāriyataṁ upeti;
- **EN**: if he chooses the life gone forth.

## dn30:1.30.16
- **PLI**: Sahito purimakataphalaṁ anubhavati,
- **EN**: For he still partakes of past deed’s fruit;

## dn30:1.30.17
- **PLI**: Na bhavati katassa panāso”ti.
- **EN**: what’s been done is never lost.”
