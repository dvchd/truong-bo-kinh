# Muc 1.7 — Wheels on the Feet  (41 đoạn)

## dn30:1.7.0
- **PLI**: 2. Pādatalacakkalakkhaṇaṁ
- **EN**: 2. Wheels on the Feet

## dn30:1.7.1  `ms8D_416, msdiv204, pts-cs1.7, pts-vp-pli3.148, vri3.110`
- **PLI**: “Yampi, bhikkhave, tathāgato purimaṁ jātiṁ purimaṁ bhavaṁ purimaṁ niketaṁ pubbe manussabhūto samāno
- **EN**: “Mendicants, in some past lives the Realized One was reborn as a human being.

## dn30:1.7.2
- **PLI**: bahujanassa sukhāvaho ahosi, ubbegauttāsabhayaṁ apanuditā, dhammikañca rakkhāvaraṇaguttiṁ saṁvidhātā, saparivārañca dānaṁ adāsi.
- **EN**: He conveyed happiness to many people, casting out threats, terror, and danger, providing just protection and security, and giving gifts with all the trimmings.
- **NOTE**: He “conveyed happiness” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>sukhāvaho</i>), like a wheeled cart would convey goods. | <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Parivāra</i> means “surroundings, accompaniments”, and I follow the commentary, which takes it in the sense of the “trimmings” accompanying the food. Given, however, that the kammic result is a large <i lang='pi' translate='no'>parivāra</i> (“following”), it might mean that he gave not just physical things, but was generous with his followers also, engaging them in such beneficial tasks.

## dn30:1.7.3
- **PLI**: So tassa kammassa kaṭattā upacitattā ussannattā vipulattā kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā sugatiṁ saggaṁ lokaṁ upapajjati …pe…
- **EN**: Due to performing those deeds he was reborn in a heavenly realm.

## dn30:1.7.4
- **PLI**: so tato cuto itthattaṁ āgato samāno imaṁ mahāpurisalakkhaṇaṁ paṭilabhati.
- **EN**: When he came back to this place he obtained this mark:

## dn30:1.7.5
- **PLI**: Heṭṭhāpādatalesu cakkāni jātāni honti sahassārāni sanemikāni sanābhikāni sabbākāraparipūrāni suvibhattantarāni.
- **EN**: on the soles of his feet there are thousand-spoked wheels, with rims and hubs, complete in every detail and well divided inside.

## dn30:1.8.1  `cck11.164, dr11.166, ms8D_417, pts-cs1.8, sya11.163`
