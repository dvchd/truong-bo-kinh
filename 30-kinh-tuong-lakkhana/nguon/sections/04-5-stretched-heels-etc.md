## dn30:1.10.1  `cck11.165, ms8D_426, msdiv206, pts-cs1.10, pts-vp-pli3.150`
- **PLI**: “Yampi, bhikkhave, tathāgato purimaṁ jātiṁ purimaṁ bhavaṁ purimaṁ niketaṁ pubbe manussabhūto samāno
- **EN**: “Mendicants, in some past lives the Realized One was reborn as a human being.

## dn30:1.10.2
- **PLI**: pāṇātipātaṁ pahāya pāṇātipātā paṭivirato ahosi nihitadaṇḍo nihitasattho lajjī dayāpanno, sabbapāṇabhūtahitānukampī vihāsi.
- **EN**: He gave up killing living creatures, renouncing the rod and the sword. He was scrupulous and kind, living full of sympathy for all living beings.

## dn30:1.10.3
- **PLI**: So tassa kammassa kaṭattā upacitattā ussannattā vipulattā …pe…
- **EN**: Due to performing those deeds he was reborn in a heavenly realm.

## dn30:1.10.4
- **PLI**: so tato cuto itthattaṁ āgato samāno imāni tīṇi mahāpurisalakkhaṇāni paṭilabhati.
- **EN**: When he came back to this place he obtained these three marks:

## dn30:1.10.5
- **PLI**: Āyatapaṇhi ca hoti, dīghaṅguli ca brahmujugatto ca.
- **EN**: he has stretched heels, long fingers, and his body is tall and straight-limbed.
- **NOTE**: Giving up killing results in long life. This seems to be applied by metaphor to having these long and elegant body parts.
