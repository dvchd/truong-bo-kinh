## dn30:1.31.0
- **PLI**: 14. Kosohitavatthaguyhalakkhaṇaṁ
- **EN**: 14. Privates in Foreskin

## dn30:1.31.1  `ms8D_482, msdiv220, pts-cs1.31, pts-vp-pli3.161`
- **PLI**: “Yampi, bhikkhave, tathāgato purimaṁ jātiṁ purimaṁ bhavaṁ purimaṁ niketaṁ pubbe manussabhūto samāno
- **EN**: “Mendicants, in some past lives the Realized One was reborn as a human being.

## dn30:1.31.2
- **PLI**: cirappanaṭṭhe sucirappavāsino ñātimitte suhajje sakhino samānetā ahosi.
- **EN**: He reunited long-lost and long-separated relatives, friends, loved ones, and comrades.

## dn30:1.31.3
- **PLI**: Mātarampi puttena samānetā ahosi, puttampi mātarā samānetā ahosi, pitarampi puttena samānetā ahosi, puttampi pitarā samānetā ahosi, bhātarampi bhātarā samānetā ahosi, bhātarampi bhaginiyā samānetā ahosi, bhaginimpi bhātarā samānetā ahosi, bhaginimpi bhaginiyā samānetā ahosi, samaṅgīkatvā ca abbhanumoditā ahosi.
- **EN**: He reunited mother with child and child with mother; father with child and child with father; brother with brother, brother with sister, sister with brother, and sister with sister, bringing them together with rejoicing.

## dn30:1.31.4
- **PLI**: So tassa kammassa kaṭattā …pe…
- **EN**: Due to performing those deeds he was reborn in a heavenly realm.

## dn30:1.31.5
- **PLI**: so tato cuto itthattaṁ āgato samāno imaṁ mahāpurisalakkhaṇaṁ paṭilabhati—
- **EN**: When he came back to this place he obtained this mark:

## dn30:1.31.6
- **PLI**: kosohitavatthaguyho hoti.
- **EN**: his private parts are sheathed in a foreskin.
- **NOTE**: The penis is the generative organ and hence part-creator of family. Being covered suggests that his sexuality was moderated and contained. His role was more than having children, it was building family.
