# Muc 2.16 — –25. One Hair Per Pore, and a Tuft  (31 đoạn)

## dn30:2.16.0
- **PLI**: 24–25. Ekekalomatāuṇṇālakkhaṇāni
- **EN**: 24–25. One Hair Per Pore, and a Tuft

## dn30:2.16.1  `ms8D_530, msdiv232, pts-cs2.16, sya11.185`
- **PLI**: “Yampi, bhikkhave, tathāgato purimaṁ jātiṁ purimaṁ bhavaṁ purimaṁ niketaṁ pubbe manussabhūto samāno
- **EN**: “Mendicants, in some past lives the Realized One was reborn as a human being.

## dn30:2.16.2
- **PLI**: musāvādaṁ pahāya musāvādā paṭivirato ahosi, saccavādī saccasandho theto paccayiko avisaṁvādako lokassa.
- **EN**: He refrained from lying. He spoke the truth and stuck to the truth. He was honest and dependable, and didn’t trick the world with his words.

## dn30:2.16.3
- **PLI**: So tassa kammassa kaṭattā upacitattā …pe…
- **EN**: Due to performing those deeds he was reborn in a heavenly realm.

## dn30:2.16.4
- **PLI**: so tato cuto itthattaṁ āgato samāno imāni dve mahāpurisalakkhaṇāni paṭilabhati.
- **EN**: When he came back to this place he obtained these two marks:

## dn30:2.16.5
- **PLI**: Ekekalomo ca hoti, uṇṇā ca bhamukantare jātā hoti odātā mudutūlasannibhā.
- **EN**: his hairs grow one per pore, and between his eyebrows there grows a tuft, soft and white like cotton-wool.
- **NOTE**: One hair per pore, because his words of truth have only one meaning. Likewise, the tuft conveys purity and integrity.

## dn30:2.17.1  `cck11.181, csp1ed8.139, csp2ed8.139, ms8D_531, pts-cs2.17, pts-vp-pli3.171`

## dn30:2.17.2

- **EN**: And what does he obtain as king?

## dn30:2.17.3

- **EN**: He has many close adherents among the brahmins and householders, people of town and country, treasurers and chief ministers, military officers, guardsmen, ministers, councillors, rulers, tax beneficiaries, and princes.

## dn30:2.17.4
- **PLI**: Rājā samāno idaṁ labhati …pe…
- **EN**: That’s what he obtains as king.

## dn30:2.17.5
- **PLI**: buddho samāno kiṁ labhati?
- **EN**: And what does he obtain as Buddha?

## dn30:2.17.6

- **EN**: He has many close adherents among the monks, nuns, laymen, laywomen, gods, humans, titans, dragons, and centaurs.

## dn30:2.17.7
- **PLI**: Buddho samāno idaṁ labhati”.
