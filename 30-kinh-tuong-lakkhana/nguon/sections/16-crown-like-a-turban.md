# Muc 2.13 — Crown Like a Turban  (31 đoạn)

## dn30:2.13.0
- **PLI**: 23. Uṇhīsasīsalakkhaṇaṁ
- **EN**: 23. Crown Like a Turban

## dn30:2.13.1  `dr11.188, ms8D_522, msdiv230, pts-cs2.13, vri3.127`
- **PLI**: “Yampi, bhikkhave, tathāgato purimaṁ jātiṁ purimaṁ bhavaṁ purimaṁ niketaṁ pubbe manussabhūto samāno
- **EN**: “Mendicants, in some past lives the Realized One was reborn as a human being.

## dn30:2.13.2
- **PLI**: bahujanapubbaṅgamo ahosi kusalesu dhammesu bahujanapāmokkho kāyasucarite vacīsucarite manosucarite dānasaṁvibhāge sīlasamādāne uposathupavāse matteyyatāya petteyyatāya sāmaññatāya brahmaññatāya kule jeṭṭhāpacāyitāya aññataraññataresu ca adhikusalesu dhammesu.
- **EN**: He was the leader and forerunner of people in skillful behaviors such as good conduct by way of body, speech, and mind, giving and sharing, taking precepts, observing the sabbath, paying due respect to mother and father, ascetics and brahmins, honoring the elders in the family, and various other things pertaining to skillful behaviors.
- **NOTE**: The commentary says he took the lead in good deeds “unembarrassed, with head held high, his head filled with rapture and joy”.

## dn30:2.13.3
- **PLI**: So tassa kammassa kaṭattā …pe…
- **EN**: Due to performing those deeds he was reborn in a heavenly realm.

## dn30:2.13.4
- **PLI**: so tato cuto itthattaṁ āgato samāno imaṁ mahāpurisalakkhaṇaṁ paṭilabhati—
- **EN**: When he came back to this place he obtained this mark:

## dn30:2.13.5
- **PLI**: uṇhīsasīso hoti.
- **EN**: the crown of his head is like a turban.

## dn30:2.14.1  `ms8D_523, pts-cs2.14, sya11.184`
- **PLI**: So tena lakkhaṇena samannāgato sace agāraṁ ajjhāvasati, rājā hoti cakkavattī …pe…
- **EN**: Possessing this mark, if he stays at home he becomes a wheel-turning monarch.

## dn30:2.14.2
- **PLI**: rājā samāno kiṁ labhati?
- **EN**: And what does he obtain as king?

## dn30:2.14.3
- **PLI**: Mahāssa jano anvāyiko hoti, brāhmaṇagahapatikā negamajānapadā gaṇakamahāmattā anīkaṭṭhā dovārikā amaccā pārisajjā rājāno bhogiyā kumārā.
- **EN**: He has a large following of brahmins and householders, people of town and country, treasurers and chief ministers, military officers, guardsmen, ministers, councillors, rulers, tax beneficiaries, and princes.

## dn30:2.14.4
- **PLI**: Rājā samāno idaṁ labhati …pe…
- **EN**: That’s what he obtains as king.

## dn30:2.14.5
- **PLI**: buddho samāno kiṁ labhati?
- **EN**: And what does he obtain as Buddha?

## dn30:2.14.6
- **PLI**: Mahāssa jano anvāyiko hoti, bhikkhū bhikkhuniyo upāsakā upāsikāyo devā manussā asurā nāgā gandhabbā.
- **EN**: He has a large following of monks, nuns, laymen, laywomen, gods, humans, titans, dragons, and centaurs.

## dn30:2.14.7
- **PLI**: Buddho samāno idaṁ labhati”.
- **EN**: That’s what he obtains as Buddha.”
