# Muc 1.4 — Well-Planted Feet  (41 đoạn)

## dn30:1.4.0
- **PLI**: 1. Suppatiṭṭhitapādatālakkhaṇaṁ
- **EN**: 1. Well-Planted Feet

## dn30:1.4.1  `cck11.161, dr11.163, ms8D_407, msdiv201, pts-cs1.4, pts-vp-pli3.146, sya11.160`
- **PLI**: Yampi, bhikkhave, tathāgato purimaṁ jātiṁ purimaṁ bhavaṁ purimaṁ niketaṁ pubbe manussabhūto samāno daḷhasamādāno ahosi kusalesu dhammesu, avatthitasamādāno kāyasucarite vacīsucarite manosucarite dānasaṁvibhāge sīlasamādāne uposathupavāse matteyyatāya petteyyatāya sāmaññatāya brahmaññatāya kule jeṭṭhāpacāyitāya aññataraññataresu ca adhikusalesu dhammesu.
- **EN**: In some past lives, past existences, past abodes the Realized One was reborn as a human being. He firmly and persistently undertook skillful behaviors such as good conduct by way of body, speech, and mind, giving and sharing, taking precepts, observing the sabbath, paying due respect to mother and father, ascetics and brahmins, honoring the elders in the family, and various other things pertaining to skillful behaviors.
- **NOTE**: The key terms here are <i lang='pi' translate='no'>daḷha</i> (“firm”) and <i lang='pi' translate='no'>avatthita</i> (“persistently”), which result in his firm stance.

## dn30:1.4.2
- **PLI**: So tassa kammassa kaṭattā upacitattā ussannattā vipulattā kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā sugatiṁ saggaṁ lokaṁ upapajjati.
- **EN**: Due to performing, accumulating, heaping up, and amassing those deeds, when his body broke up, after death, he was reborn in a good place, a heavenly realm.

## dn30:1.4.3
- **PLI**: So tattha aññe deve dasahi ṭhānehi adhiggaṇhāti dibbena āyunā dibbena vaṇṇena dibbena sukhena dibbena yasena dibbena ādhipateyyena dibbehi rūpehi dibbehi saddehi dibbehi gandhehi dibbehi rasehi dibbehi phoṭṭhabbehi.
- **EN**: There he surpassed the other gods in ten respects: heavenly lifespan, beauty, happiness, glory, sovereignty, sights, sounds, smells, tastes, and touches.

## dn30:1.4.4
- **PLI**: So tato cuto itthattaṁ āgato samāno imaṁ mahāpurisalakkhaṇaṁ paṭilabhati.
- **EN**: When he passed away from there and came back to this place he obtained this mark of a great man:

## dn30:1.4.5
- **PLI**: Suppatiṭṭhitapādo hoti.
- **EN**: he has well-planted feet.

## dn30:1.4.6
- **PLI**: Samaṁ pādaṁ bhūmiyaṁ nikkhipati, samaṁ uddharati, samaṁ sabbāvantehi pādatalehi bhūmiṁ phusati.
- **EN**: He places his foot on the ground evenly, raises it evenly, and touches the ground evenly with the whole sole of his foot.

## dn30:1.5.1  `bj9.242, cck11.162, csp1ed8.120, csp2ed8.120, dr11.164, ms8D_408, msdiv202, ndp8.113, pts-cs1.5, pts-vp-pli3.147, sya11.161`
- **PLI**: So tena lakkhaṇena samannāgato sace agāraṁ ajjhāvasati, rājā hoti cakkavattī dhammiko dhammarājā cāturanto vijitāvī janapadatthāvariyappatto sattaratanasamannāgato.
- **EN**: Possessing this mark, if he stays at home he becomes a wheel-turning monarch.

## dn30:1.5.2
- **PLI**: Tassimāni satta ratanāni bhavanti;
- **EN**: He has the following seven treasures:

## dn30:1.5.3
- **PLI**: Seyyathidaṁ—cakkaratanaṁ hatthiratanaṁ assaratanaṁ maṇiratanaṁ itthiratanaṁ gahapatiratanaṁ pariṇāyakaratanameva sattamaṁ.
- **EN**: the wheel, the elephant, the horse, the jewel, the woman, the householder, and the commander as the seventh treasure.

## dn30:1.5.4
- **PLI**: Parosahassaṁ kho panassa puttā bhavanti sūrā vīraṅgarūpā parasenappamaddanā.
- **EN**: He has over a thousand sons who are valiant and heroic, crushing the armies of his enemies.

## dn30:1.5.5
- **PLI**: So imaṁ pathaviṁ sāgarapariyantaṁ akhilamanimittamakaṇṭakaṁ iddhaṁ phītaṁ khemaṁ sivaṁ nirabbudaṁ adaṇḍena asatthena dhammena abhivijiya ajjhāvasati.
- **EN**: After conquering this land girt by sea—free of harassment by bandits, successful and prosperous, safe, blessed, free of pests—he reigns by principle, without rod or sword.
- **NOTE**: The commentary explain <i lang='pi' translate='no'>akhilamanimittamakaṇṭakaṁ</i> as “free of bandits” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>niccoraṁ</i>). Bandits are also regarded as “pests” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>abbuda</i>, literally “tumor” or “cancer”) at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn1.77/en/sujato#3.4'>SN 1.77:3.4</a>.

## dn30:1.5.6
- **PLI**: Rājā samāno kiṁ labhati?
- **EN**: And what does he obtain as king?

## dn30:1.5.7
- **PLI**: Akkhambhiyo hoti kenaci manussabhūtena paccatthikena paccāmittena.
- **EN**: He can’t be stopped by any human foe or enemy.

## dn30:1.5.8
- **PLI**: Rājā samāno idaṁ labhati.
- **EN**: That’s what he obtains as king.

## dn30:1.5.9

- **EN**: But if he goes forth from the lay life to homelessness, he becomes a perfected one, a fully awakened Buddha, who has cast off the world’s veil.

## dn30:1.5.10
- **PLI**: Buddho samāno kiṁ labhati?
- **EN**: And what does he obtain as Buddha?

## dn30:1.5.11
- **PLI**: Akkhambhiyo hoti abbhantarehi vā bāhirehi vā paccatthikehi paccāmittehi rāgena vā dosena vā mohena vā samaṇena vā brāhmaṇena vā devena vā mārena vā brahmunā vā kenaci vā lokasmiṁ.
- **EN**: He can’t be stopped by any foe or enemy whether internal or external; nor by greed, hate, or delusion; nor by any ascetic or brahmin or god or Māra or divinity or by anyone in the world.

## dn30:1.5.12
