- **EN**: “Mendicants, in some past lives the Realized One was reborn as a human being.

## dn30:1.16.2
- **PLI**: catūhi saṅgahavatthūhi janaṁ saṅgāhako ahosi—
- **EN**: He brought people together using the four ways of being inclusive:

## dn30:1.16.3

- **EN**: giving, kindly words, taking care, and equality.

## dn30:1.16.4

- **EN**: Due to performing those deeds he was reborn in a heavenly realm.

- **PLI**: so tato cuto itthattaṁ āgato samāno imāni dve mahāpurisalakkhaṇāni paṭilabhati.
- **EN**: When he came back to this place he obtained these two marks:

## dn30:1.16.6
- **PLI**: Mudutalunahatthapādo ca hoti jālahatthapādo ca.
- **EN**: his hands and feet are soft and tender, and they are serried.
- **NOTE**: “Tender” because he treated people tenderly; “serried” because his fingers and toes are kept gracefully together, not splayed and separated.

## dn30:1.17.1  `ms8D_442, pts-cs1.17, sya11.168`

## dn30:1.17.3

- **EN**: His retinue is inclusive, cohesive, and well-managed. This includes brahmins and householders, people of town and country, treasurers and chief ministers, military officers, guardsmen, ministers, councillors, rulers, tax beneficiaries, and princes.
- **NOTE**: “Inclusive and well-managed” renders <i lang='pi' translate='no'>saṅgahita</i>. It means “included”, as in those people who, due to the kindly actions, feel that they belong. For the sense “well-managed” see <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an8.49/en/sujato#2.3'>AN 8.49:2.3</a>. Because of acting kindly and inclusively, people are well-disposed and work well together.
