# Muc 2.1 — –16. Equal Proportions and Touching the Knees  (34 đoạn)

- **EN**: 15–16. Equal Proportions and Touching the Knees

## dn30:2.1.1  `csp1ed8.133, csp2ed8.133, dr11.181, ms8D_491, msdiv222, pts-cs2.1, sya11.177, vri3.122`
- **PLI**: “Yampi, bhikkhave, tathāgato purimaṁ jātiṁ purimaṁ bhavaṁ purimaṁ niketaṁ pubbe manussabhūto samāno
- **EN**: “Mendicants, in some past lives the Realized One was reborn as a human being.

## dn30:2.1.2
- **PLI**: mahājanasaṅgahaṁ samekkhamāno samaṁ jānāti sāmaṁ jānāti, purisaṁ jānāti purisavisesaṁ jānāti:
- **EN**: Examining the gathered population, he knew what they had in common and what was their own; he knew each person, and he knew the distinctions between people.

## dn30:2.1.3
- **PLI**: ‘ayamidamarahati ayamidamarahatī’ti tattha tattha purisavisesakaro ahosi.
- **EN**: In each case, he made appropriate distinctions between people: ‘This one deserves that; that one deserves this.’

## dn30:2.1.4
- **PLI**: So tassa kammassa kaṭattā …pe…
- **EN**: Due to performing those deeds he was reborn in a heavenly realm.

## dn30:2.1.5
- **PLI**: so tato cuto itthattaṁ āgato samāno imāni dve mahāpurisalakkhaṇāni paṭilabhati.
- **EN**: When he came back to this place he obtained these two marks:

## dn30:2.1.6
- **PLI**: Nigrodhaparimaṇḍalo ca hoti, ṭhitakoyeva ca anonamanto ubhohi pāṇitalehi jaṇṇukāni parimasati parimajjati.
- **EN**: he has the proportional circumference of a banyan tree; and when standing upright and not bending over, the palms of both hands touch the knees.
- **NOTE**: The banyan tree is not just proportioned, it offers shelter to many creatures. Touching the knees indicates connecting with the manyfolk, who at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an5.196/en/sujato#3.1'>AN 5.196:3.1</a> are likened to caterpillars reaching the knees.
