- **EN**: ‘How might they flourish in faith, ethics, learning, generosity, teachings, and wisdom; in wealth and grain, fields and land, birds and beasts, children and partners; in bondservants, workers, and staff; in family, friends, and kin?’

- **EN**: Due to performing those deeds he was reborn in a heavenly realm.

## dn30:2.4.6
- **PLI**: Sīhapubbaddhakāyo ca hoti citantaraṁso ca samavaṭṭakkhandho ca.
- **EN**: his chest is like that of a lion; he is filled out between the shoulders.; and his torso is cylindrical.
- **NOTE**: A <i lang='pi' translate='no'>khema</i> is a sanctuary where animals flourish in peace, without fear of the hunter. Likewise, the Buddha’s body flourishes and has a fullness to it. He is slim but not skinny.

- **EN**: He’s not liable to decline. He doesn’t decline in wealth and grain, fields and land, birds and beasts, children and partners; in bondservants, workers, and staff; in family, friends, and kin. He doesn’t decline in any of his accomplishments.
