# Muc 2.7 — Ridged Taste Buds  (26 đoạn)

## dn30:2.7.0
- **PLI**: 20. Rasaggasaggitālakkhaṇaṁ
- **EN**: 20. Ridged Taste Buds

## dn30:2.7.1  `bj9.268, cck11.177, ms8D_507, msdiv226, pts-cs2.7, pts-vp-pli3.166`
- **PLI**: “Yampi, bhikkhave, tathāgato purimaṁ jātiṁ purimaṁ bhavaṁ purimaṁ niketaṁ pubbe manussabhūto samāno
- **EN**: “Mendicants, in some past lives the Realized One was reborn as a human being.

## dn30:2.7.2
- **PLI**: sattānaṁ aviheṭhakajātiko ahosi pāṇinā vā leḍḍunā vā daṇḍena vā satthena vā.
- **EN**: He would never hurt any sentient being with fists, stones, rods, or swords.

## dn30:2.7.3
- **PLI**: So tassa kammassa kaṭattā upacitattā …pe…
- **EN**: Due to performing those deeds he was reborn in a heavenly realm.

## dn30:2.7.4
- **PLI**: so tato cuto itthattaṁ āgato samāno imaṁ mahāpurisalakkhaṇaṁ paṭilabhati, rasaggasaggī hoti, uddhaggāssa rasaharaṇīyo gīvāya jātā honti samābhivāhiniyo.
- **EN**: When he came back to this place he obtained this mark: he has ridged taste buds. His raised taste receptors, originating in the throat, disperse evenly.

## dn30:2.8.1  `dr11.185, ms8D_508, pts-cs2.8, vri3.125`
- **PLI**: So tena lakkhaṇena samannāgato sace agāraṁ ajjhāvasati, rājā hoti cakkavattī …pe…
- **EN**: Possessing this mark, if he stays at home he becomes a wheel-turning monarch.

## dn30:2.8.2
- **PLI**: rājā samāno kiṁ labhati?
- **EN**: And what does he obtain as king?

## dn30:2.8.3
- **PLI**: Appābādho hoti appātaṅko, samavepākiniyā gahaṇiyā samannāgato nātisītāya nāccuṇhāya.
- **EN**: He is rarely ill or unwell. His stomach digests well, being neither too hot nor too cold.
- **NOTE**: The “ridged taste buds” are not merely for tasting, but support excellent digestion. Kammically, the rationale is that since he did not kill others, now nothing hurts him when he eats.

## dn30:2.8.4
- **PLI**: Rājā samāno idaṁ labhati …pe…
- **EN**: That’s what he obtains as king.

## dn30:2.8.5
- **PLI**: buddho samāno kiṁ labhati?
- **EN**: And what does he obtain as Buddha?

## dn30:2.8.6
- **PLI**: Appābādho hoti appātaṅko samavepākiniyā gahaṇiyā samannāgato nātisītāya nāccuṇhāya majjhimāya padhānakkhamāya.
- **EN**: He is rarely ill or unwell. His stomach digests well, being neither too hot nor too cold, but just right, and fit for meditation.

## dn30:2.8.7
- **PLI**: Buddho samāno idaṁ labhati”.
- **EN**: That’s what he obtains as Buddha.”
