# Muc 5.9 — The Wheel-Treasure Appears  (31 đoạn)

## dn26:5.9.0
- **PLI**: 2.2. Cakkaratanapātubhāva
- **EN**: 2.2. The Wheel-Treasure Appears

## dn26:5.10  `bj9.104, csp1ed8.51, csp2ed8.51, dr11.66, ms8D_191, msdiv85, pts-vp-pli3.62, sya11.66, vri3.45`
- **PLI**: ‘Evaṁ, devā’ti kho, bhikkhave, rājā khattiyo muddhābhisitto rājisissa paṭissutvā ariye cakkavattivatte vatti.
- **EN**: ‘Yes, Your Majesty,’ replied the new king to the royal seer. And he proceeded in the noble duty of a wheel-turning monarch.

## dn26:5.11
- **PLI**: Tassa ariye cakkavattivatte vattamānassa tadahuposathe pannarase sīsaṁnhātassa uposathikassa uparipāsādavaragatassa
- **EN**: While he was proceeding in that duty, on a fifteenth day sabbath, he had bathed his head and gone upstairs in the royal longhouse to observe the sabbath.

## dn26:5.12
- **PLI**: dibbaṁ cakkaratanaṁ pāturahosi sahassāraṁ sanemikaṁ sanābhikaṁ sabbākāraparipūraṁ.
- **EN**: And the heavenly wheel-treasure appeared to him, with a thousand spokes, with rim and hub, complete in every detail.

## dn26:5.13
- **PLI**: Disvāna rañño khattiyassa muddhābhisittassa etadahosi:
- **EN**: Seeing this, the king thought,

## dn26:5.14
- **PLI**: ‘sutaṁ kho pana metaṁ—yassa rañño khattiyassa muddhābhisittassa tadahuposathe pannarase sīsaṁnhātassa uposathikassa uparipāsādavaragatassa dibbaṁ cakkaratanaṁ pātubhavati sahassāraṁ sanemikaṁ sanābhikaṁ sabbākāraparipūraṁ, so hoti rājā cakkavattīti.
- **EN**: ‘I have heard that when the heavenly wheel-treasure appears to a king in this way, he becomes a wheel-turning monarch.

## dn26:5.15
- **PLI**: Assaṁ nu kho ahaṁ rājā cakkavattī’ti.
- **EN**: Am I then a wheel-turning monarch?’

## dn26:6.6
- **PLI**: Rājā cakkavattī evamāha:

## dn26:6.7
- **PLI**: ‘pāṇo na hantabbo, adinnaṁ nādātabbaṁ, kāmesumicchā na caritabbā, musā na bhāsitabbā, majjaṁ na pātabbaṁ, yathābhuttañca bhuñjathā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Do not kill living creatures. Do not steal. Do not commit sexual misconduct. Do not lie. Do not drink liquor. Maintain the current level of taxation.’
