# Muc 19 — When People Live for Ten Years  (33 đoạn)

## dn26:19.0
- **PLI**: 5. Dasavassāyukasamaya
- **EN**: 5. When People Live for Ten Years

## dn26:19.1  `dr11.78, ms8D_219, msdiv103, ndp8.57, pts-cs19, sya11.78`
- **PLI**: Bhavissati, bhikkhave, so samayo, yaṁ imesaṁ manussānaṁ dasavassāyukā puttā bhavissanti.
- **EN**: There will come a time, mendicants, when these people will have children who live for ten years.
- **NOTE**: Here the Buddha paints a devastating picture of societal collapse. Life expectancy as low as thirty years has been recorded in some countries in the 20th century.

## dn26:19.2
- **PLI**: Dasavassāyukesu, bhikkhave, manussesu pañcavassikā kumārikā alaṁpateyyā bhavissanti.
- **EN**: Among the humans who live for ten years, girls will be marriageable at five.
- **NOTE**: The age of onset of puberty has been dropping globally over the past century, with isolated cases as young as five.

## dn26:19.3
- **PLI**: Dasavassāyukesu, bhikkhave, manussesu imāni rasāni antaradhāyissanti, seyyathidaṁ—
- **EN**: The following flavors will disappear:

## dn26:19.4
- **PLI**: sappi navanītaṁ telaṁ madhu phāṇitaṁ loṇaṁ.
- **EN**: ghee, butter, oil, honey, molasses, and salt.

## dn26:19.5
- **PLI**: Dasavassāyukesu, bhikkhave, manussesu kudrūsako aggaṁ bhojanānaṁ bhavissati.
- **EN**: The best kind of food will be finger millet,
- **NOTE**: “Finger millet” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>kudrūsaka</i>; Pahari <i lang='pi' translate='no'>kodra</i>, Garhwal <i lang='pi' translate='no'>koda</i>) is a robust secondary grain in Asia and Africa, growing in places rice cannot. Due to its resilience in the face of global heating, 2023 was declared the International Year of Millets by the UN.

## dn26:19.6
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, bhikkhave, etarahi sālimaṁsodano aggaṁ bhojanānaṁ;
- **EN**: just as fine rice with meat is the best kind of food today.
- **NOTE**: The Buddha’s father gave this luxury food to servants and staff (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/an3.39/en/sujato#2.3'>AN 3.39:2.3</a>).

## dn26:19.7
- **PLI**: evameva kho, bhikkhave, dasavassāyukesu manussesu kudrūsako aggaṁ bhojanānaṁ bhavissati.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn26:19.8  `cck11.80, csp1ed8.60, csp2ed8.60, ms8D_220, pts-vp-pli3.72`
- **PLI**: Dasavassāyukesu, bhikkhave, manussesu dasa kusalakammapathā sabbena sabbaṁ antaradhāyissanti, dasa akusalakammapathā atibyādippissanti.
- **EN**: The ten ways of doing skillful deeds will totally disappear, and the ten ways of doing unskillful deeds will explode in popularity.
- **NOTE**: The narrative above traced the gradual disappearance of these ten factors. | <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Atibyādippissanti</i> is a unique term. At <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an7.66/en/sujato#9.2'>AN 7.66:9.2</a> a sign of the end of days is the “erupting” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>ādippanti</i>) of volcanoes, suggesting “explode” for the intensive form here.

## dn26:19.9
- **PLI**: Dasavassāyukesu, bhikkhave, manussesu kusalantipi na bhavissati, kuto pana kusalassa kārako.
- **EN**: Those humans will not even have the word ‘skillful’, still less anyone who does what is skillful.
- **NOTE**: “Skillful” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>kusala</i>, otherwise translated as “good” or “wholesome”.

## dn26:19.10
- **PLI**: Dasavassāyukesu, bhikkhave, manussesu ye te bhavissanti amatteyyā apetteyyā asāmaññā abrahmaññā na kule jeṭṭhāpacāyino, te pujjā ca bhavissanti pāsaṁsā ca.
- **EN**: And anyone who disrespects mother and father, ascetics and brahmins, and fails to honor the elders in the family will be venerated and praised,

## dn26:19.11
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, bhikkhave, etarahi matteyyā petteyyā sāmaññā brahmaññā kule jeṭṭhāpacāyino pujjā ca pāsaṁsā ca;
- **EN**: just as the opposite is venerated and praised today.

## dn26:19.12
- **PLI**: evameva kho, bhikkhave, dasavassāyukesu manussesu ye te bhavissanti amatteyyā apetteyyā asāmaññā abrahmaññā na kule jeṭṭhāpacāyino, te pujjā ca bhavissanti pāsaṁsā ca.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn26:20.1  `dr11.79, ms8D_221, pts-cs20, sya11.79, vri3.53`
- **PLI**: Dasavassāyukesu, bhikkhave, manussesu na bhavissati mātāti vā mātucchāti vā mātulānīti vā ācariyabhariyāti vā garūnaṁ dārāti vā.
- **EN**: There’ll be no recognition of the status of mother, aunts, or wives and partners of tutors and respected people.
- **NOTE**: At <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an2.9/en/sujato'>AN 2.9</a> = <a href='https://suttacentral.net/iti42/en/sujato'>Iti 42</a> the world is protected from this by conscience and prudence.

## dn26:20.2
- **PLI**: Sambhedaṁ loko gamissati yathā ajeḷakā kukkuṭasūkarā soṇasiṅgālā.
- **EN**: The world will become dissolute, like goats and sheep, chickens and pigs, and hounds and jackals.
- **NOTE**: For <i lang='pi' translate='no'>sambheda</i> in the sense of “dissolving, leaking”, see <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn3/en/sujato#1.15.11'>DN 3:1.15.11</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an5.103/en/sujato#6.4'>AN 5.103:6.4</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an10.45/en/sujato#4.1'>AN 10.45:4.1</a>. The commentary here says “mixedness, breaking of boundaries”.

- **EN**: They’ll be full of hostility toward each other, with acute ill will, malevolence, and thoughts of murder.

## dn26:20.4

## dn26:20.6

- **EN**: brother for sister, and sister for brother.

## dn26:20.7
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, bhikkhave, māgavikassa migaṁ disvā tibbo āghāto paccupaṭṭhito hoti tibbo byāpādo tibbo manopadoso tibbaṁ vadhakacittaṁ;
- **EN**: They’ll be just like a deer hunter when he sees a deer—full of hostility, ill will, malevolence, and thoughts of killing.

## dn26:21.1  `csp1ed8.61, csp2ed8.61, ms8D_223, msdiv104, pts-cs21, sya11.80`
- **PLI**: Dasavassāyukesu, bhikkhave, manussesu sattāhaṁ satthantarakappo bhavissati.
- **EN**: Among the humans who live for ten years, there will be an interregnum of swords lasting seven days.
- **NOTE**: “Interregnum of swords” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>satthantarakappo</i>. Seven days seems optimistic.

## dn26:21.2
- **PLI**: Te aññamaññamhi migasaññaṁ paṭilabhissanti.
- **EN**: During that time they will see each other as beasts.
- **NOTE**: Dehumanization of the other is an essential precursor to genocide.

## dn26:21.3
- **PLI**: Tesaṁ tiṇhāni satthāni hatthesu pātubhavissanti.
- **EN**: Sharp swords will appear in their hands,

## dn26:21.4
- **PLI**: Te tiṇhena satthena ‘esa migo esa migo’ti aññamaññaṁ jīvitā voropessanti.
- **EN**: with which they’ll take each other’s life, crying, ‘It’s a beast! It’s a beast!’

## dn26:21.5  `cck11.82, dr11.80, ms8D_224`
- **PLI**: Atha kho tesaṁ, bhikkhave, sattānaṁ ekaccānaṁ evaṁ bhavissati:
- **EN**: But then some of those beings will think,

## dn26:21.6
- **PLI**: ‘mā ca mayaṁ kañci, mā ca amhe koci, yannūna mayaṁ tiṇagahanaṁ vā vanagahanaṁ vā rukkhagahanaṁ vā nadīviduggaṁ vā pabbatavisamaṁ vā pavisitvā vanamūlaphalāhārā yāpeyyāmā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Let us neither kill nor be killed! Why don’t we hide in thick grass, thick jungle, thick trees, inaccessible riverlands, or rugged mountains and survive on forest roots and fruits?’
- **NOTE**: “Let us neither kill nor be killed” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>mā ca mayaṁ kañci, mā ca amhe koci</i>) is an idiomatic phrase, the Golden Rule framed as a negative: “Let us not do unto others nor let others do unto us.” It is explained by the commentary as, “Let us not take even a single person’s life or let them take ours”.

## dn26:21.7
- **PLI**: Te tiṇagahanaṁ vā vanagahanaṁ vā rukkhagahanaṁ vā nadīviduggaṁ vā pabbatavisamaṁ vā pavisitvā sattāhaṁ vanamūlaphalāhārā yāpessanti.
- **EN**: So that’s what they do.

## dn26:21.8
- **PLI**: Te tassa sattāhassa accayena tiṇagahanā vanagahanā rukkhagahanā nadīviduggā pabbatavisamā nikkhamitvā aññamaññaṁ āliṅgitvā sabhāgāyissanti samassāsissanti:
- **EN**: When those seven days have passed, having emerged from their hiding places and embraced each other, they will come together and cry in one voice,
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Sabhāgāyissanti</i> = <i lang='pi' translate='no'>sabhāga</i> + <i lang='pi' translate='no'>āyissanti</i> = “will come together”. | <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Samassāsissanti</i> = <i lang='pi' translate='no'>sama</i> + <i lang='pi' translate='no'>sāsati</i> = “cry in one voice”. The latter term is absent from some manuscripts.

## dn26:21.9
- **PLI**: ‘diṭṭhā, bho, sattā jīvasi, diṭṭhā, bho, sattā jīvasī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Fantastic, dear foe, you live! Fantastic, dear foe, you live!’
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Diṭṭhā</i> is Sanskrit <i lang='pi' translate='no'>diṣṭyā</i>, “fantastic, how fortunate”, per <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#2.24.5'>DN 16:2.24.5</a> and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/ja81/en/sujato#1.4'>Ja 81:1.4</a>. | <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Sattā</i> is nominative singular for <i lang='pi' translate='no'>sattar</i> (“enemy”), not plural of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>satta</i> (“being”), which disagrees with both verb and vocative in number.
