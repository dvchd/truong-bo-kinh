# Muc 1 — Taking Refuge in Oneself  (17 đoạn)

## dn26:1.0
- **PLI**: 1. Attadīpasaraṇatā
- **EN**: 1. Taking Refuge in Oneself

## dn26:1.1  `bj9.96, cck11.63, csp1ed8.48, csp2ed8.48, dr11.62, ms8D_183, msdiv80, ndp8.46, pts-cs1, pts-vp-pli3.58, sya11.62, vri3.42`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—
- **EN**: So I have heard.

## dn26:1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā magadhesu viharati mātulāyaṁ.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying in the land of the Magadhans at Mātulā.
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Mātulā</i> means “maternal uncle”. This Magadhan village is mentioned only here and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/cp9/en/sujato#39.2'>Cp 9:39.2</a>.

## dn26:1.3
- **PLI**: Tatra kho bhagavā bhikkhū āmantesi:
- **EN**: There the Buddha addressed the mendicants,

## dn26:1.4
- **PLI**: “bhikkhavo”ti.
- **EN**: “Mendicants!”

## dn26:1.5
- **PLI**: “Bhaddante”ti te bhikkhū bhagavato paccassosuṁ.
- **EN**: “Venerable sir,” they replied.

## dn26:1.6
- **PLI**: Bhagavā etadavoca:
- **EN**: The Buddha said this:

## dn26:1.7  `ms8D_184`
- **PLI**: “Attadīpā, bhikkhave, viharatha attasaraṇā anaññasaraṇā, dhammadīpā dhammasaraṇā anaññasaraṇā.
- **EN**: “Mendicants, live as your own island, your own refuge, with no other refuge. Let the teaching be your island and your refuge, with no other refuge.
- **NOTE**: As at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#2.26.1'>DN 16:2.26.1</a>.

## dn26:1.8
- **PLI**: Kathañca pana, bhikkhave, bhikkhu attadīpo viharati attasaraṇo anaññasaraṇo, dhammadīpo dhammasaraṇo anaññasaraṇo?
- **EN**: And how does a mendicant do this?

## dn26:1.9
- **PLI**: Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu kāye kāyānupassī viharati ātāpī sampajāno satimā vineyya loke abhijjhādomanassaṁ.
- **EN**: They meditate observing an aspect of the body—keen, aware, and mindful, rid of covetousness and displeasure for the world.

## dn26:1.10
- **PLI**: Vedanāsu vedanānupassī …pe…
- **EN**: They meditate observing an aspect of feelings …

## dn26:1.11
- **PLI**: citte cittānupassī …pe…
- **EN**: mind …

## dn26:1.12
- **PLI**: dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati ātāpī sampajāno satimā vineyya loke abhijjhādomanassaṁ.
- **EN**: principles—keen, aware, and mindful, rid of covetousness and displeasure for the world.

## dn26:1.13
- **PLI**: Evaṁ kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhu attadīpo viharati attasaraṇo anaññasaraṇo, dhammadīpo dhammasaraṇo anaññasaraṇo.
- **EN**: That’s how a mendicant lives with themselves as island and refuge, without other refuge; with the teachings as island and refuge, without other refuge.

## dn26:1.14  `ms8D_185`
- **PLI**: Gocare, bhikkhave, caratha sake pettike visaye.
- **EN**: You should roam inside your own territory, the domain of your fathers.
- **NOTE**: Two parables on mindfulness expand this idea (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn47.6/en/sujato'>SN 47.6</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn47.7/en/sujato'>SN 47.7</a>).

## dn26:1.15
- **PLI**: Gocare, bhikkhave, carataṁ sake pettike visaye na lacchati māro otāraṁ, na lacchati māro ārammaṇaṁ.
- **EN**: If you roam inside your own territory, the domain of your fathers, Māra won’t find a vulnerability or get an opportunity.

## dn26:1.16
- **PLI**: Kusalānaṁ, bhikkhave, dhammānaṁ samādānahetu evamidaṁ puññaṁ pavaḍḍhati.
- **EN**: It is due to undertaking skillful qualities that merit grows.
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Evamidaṁ</i> indicates entailment, as at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn56.21/en/sujato#1.3'>SN 56.21:1.3</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn73/en/sujato#13.2'>MN 73:13.2</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#4.2.4'>DN 16:4.2.4</a>.
