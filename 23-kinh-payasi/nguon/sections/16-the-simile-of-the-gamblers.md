## dn23:27.2
- **PLI**: upamāya m’idhekacce viññū purisā bhāsitassa atthaṁ ājānanti.
- **EN**: For by means of a simile some sensible people understand the meaning of what is said.

## dn23:27.3
- **PLI**: Bhūtapubbaṁ, rājañña, dve akkhadhuttā akkhehi dibbiṁsu.
- **EN**: Once upon a time, two gamblers were playing with seed dice.

## dn23:27.4
- **PLI**: Eko akkhadhutto āgatāgataṁ kaliṁ gilati.
- **EN**: One gambler, every time they took a bad hand, swallowed the losing seed.
- **NOTE**: The ancient Indian game of dice involved casting <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vibhītaka</i> seeds (also known as “bedda nuts”, from Terminalia bellirica) in a hollow, from which the players would take a handful. A number divisible by four was “perfect” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>kaṭa</i>), so the fifth seed meant a “losing” hand (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>kali</i>; cf. <i lang='pi' translate='no'>apaṇṇaka</i> in <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn60/en/sujato'>MN 60</a> and notes).

## dn23:27.5
- **PLI**: Addasā kho dutiyo akkhadhutto taṁ akkhadhuttaṁ āgatāgataṁ kaliṁ gilantaṁ, disvā taṁ akkhadhuttaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: The second gambler saw him, and said,

## dn23:27.6
- **PLI**: ‘tvaṁ kho, samma, ekantikena jināsi, dehi me, samma, akkhe pajohissāmī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Well, dear, you’ve won it all! Give me the seed dice, I will roll them.’
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Pajohissāmi</i> is related to Sanskrit <i lang='pi' translate='no'>juhoti</i>, usually used in the sense “to offer as libation”, a meaning accepted by the commentary here. More likely it simply means to “roll out” (like pouring a sacrifice) by analogy.

## dn23:27.7
- **PLI**: ‘Evaṁ, sammā’ti kho so akkhadhutto tassa akkhadhuttassa akkhe pādāsi.
- **EN**: ‘Yes, dear,’ the gambler replied, and gave them.

## dn23:27.8
- **PLI**: Atha kho so akkhadhutto akkhe visena paribhāvetvā taṁ akkhadhuttaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: Then the gambler soaked the seed dice in poison and said to the other,

## dn23:27.9

- **EN**: ‘Come, dear, let’s play seed dice.’

## dn23:27.10

## dn23:27.11

## dn23:27.12
