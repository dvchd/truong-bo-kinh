# Muc 17 — The Simile of the Hot Iron Ball  (47 đoạn)

- **EN**: 2.8. The Simile of the Hot Iron Ball

## dn23:17.1  `cck10.390, csp1ed7.267, csp2ed7.267, dr10.369, mc10.285, ms7D_1112, msdiv424, ndp7.250, pts-cs17, pts-vp-pli2.335, sya10.373, vri2.249`
- **PLI**: “Tena hi, rājañña, upamaṁ te karissāmi.
- **EN**: “Well then, chieftain, I shall give you a simile.

## dn23:17.2
- **PLI**: Upamāya m’idhekacce viññū purisā bhāsitassa atthaṁ ājānanti.
- **EN**: For by means of a simile some sensible people understand the meaning of what is said.

## dn23:17.3
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, rājañña, puriso divasaṁ santattaṁ ayoguḷaṁ ādittaṁ sampajjalitaṁ sajotibhūtaṁ tulāya tuleyya.
- **EN**: Suppose a person was to heat an iron ball all day until it was burning, blazing, and glowing, and then they weigh it with scales.

## dn23:17.4
- **PLI**: Tamenaṁ aparena samayena sītaṁ nibbutaṁ tulāya tuleyya.
- **EN**: After some time, when it had cooled and become quenched, they’d weigh it again.

## dn23:17.5
- **PLI**: Kadā nu kho so ayoguḷo lahutaro vā hoti mudutaro vā kammaññataro vā, yadā vā āditto sampajjalito sajotibhūto, yadā vā sīto nibbuto”ti?
- **EN**: When would that iron ball be lighter, softer, and more workable—when it’s burning or when it’s cool?”
- **NOTE**: The misconception that iron is lighter when heated is repeated elsewhere (eg. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn51.22/en/sujato#4.1'>SN 51.22:4.1</a>). In fact, assuming there are no chemical reactions, it will be very slightly heavier due to relativistic effects, yet less dense and hence more buoyant. These changes are too small to be detected by Pāyāsi’s methods, though.

## dn23:17.6
- **PLI**: “Yadā so, bho kassapa, ayoguḷo tejosahagato ca hoti vāyosahagato ca āditto sampajjalito sajotibhūto, tadā lahutaro ca hoti mudutaro ca kammaññataro ca.
- **EN**: “So long as the iron ball is full of heat and air—burning, blazing, and glowing—it’s lighter, softer, and more workable.

## dn23:17.7
- **PLI**: Yadā pana so ayoguḷo neva tejosahagato hoti na vāyosahagato sīto nibbuto, tadā garutaro ca hoti patthinnataro ca akammaññataro cā”ti.
- **EN**: But when it lacks heat and air—cooled and quenched—it’s heavier, stiffer, and less workable.”

## dn23:17.8
- **PLI**: “Evameva kho, rājañña, yadāyaṁ kāyo āyusahagato ca hoti usmāsahagato ca viññāṇasahagato ca, tadā lahutaro ca hoti mudutaro ca kammaññataro ca.
- **EN**: “In the same way, so long as this body is full of life and warmth and consciousness it’s lighter, softer, and more flexible.

## dn23:17.9
- **PLI**: Yadā panāyaṁ kāyo neva āyusahagato hoti na usmāsahagato na viññāṇasahagato tadā garutaro ca hoti patthinnataro ca akammaññataro ca.
- **EN**: But when it lacks life and warmth and consciousness it’s heavier, stiffer, and less flexible.
