# Muc 25 — The Simile of the Dung-Carrier  (24 đoạn)

## dn23:25.0
- **PLI**: 2.12. Gūthabhārikaupamā
- **EN**: 2.12. The Simile of the Dung-Carrier

## dn23:25.1  `cck10.403, dr10.382, mc10.294, ms7D_1127, msdiv432, pts-cs25, pts-vp-pli2.348`
- **PLI**: “Tena hi, rājañña, upamaṁ te karissāmi.
- **EN**: “Well then, chieftain, I shall give you a simile.

## dn23:25.2
- **PLI**: Upamāya m’idhekacce viññū purisā bhāsitassa atthaṁ ājānanti.
- **EN**: For by means of a simile some sensible people understand the meaning of what is said.

## dn23:25.3
- **PLI**: Bhūtapubbaṁ, rājañña, aññataro sūkaraposako puriso sakamhā gāmā aññaṁ gāmaṁ agamāsi.
- **EN**: Once upon a time, a certain swineherd went from his own village to another village.

## dn23:25.4
- **PLI**: Tattha addasa pahūtaṁ sukkhagūthaṁ chaḍḍitaṁ.
- **EN**: There he saw a large pile of dry dung abandoned.

## dn23:25.5
- **PLI**: Disvānassa etadahosi:
- **EN**: He thought,

## dn23:25.6
- **PLI**: ‘ayaṁ kho pahūto sukkhagūtho chaḍḍito, mama ca sūkarabhattaṁ;
- **EN**: ‘This pile of dry dung can serve as food for my pigs.

## dn23:25.7
- **PLI**: yannūnāhaṁ ito sukkhagūthaṁ hareyyan’ti.
- **EN**: Why don’t I carry it off?’

## dn23:25.8
- **PLI**: So uttarāsaṅgaṁ pattharitvā pahūtaṁ sukkhagūthaṁ ākiritvā bhaṇḍikaṁ bandhitvā sīse ubbāhetvā agamāsi.
- **EN**: So he spread out his upper robe, shoveled the dry dung onto it, tied it up into a parcel, lifted it on to his head, and went on his way.

## dn23:25.9
- **PLI**: Tassa antarāmagge mahāakālamegho pāvassi.
- **EN**: While on his way a large storm out of season poured down.

## dn23:25.10
- **PLI**: So uggharantaṁ paggharantaṁ yāva agganakhā gūthena makkhito gūthabhāraṁ ādāya agamāsi.
- **EN**: Smeared with leaking, oozing dung down to his fingernails, he kept on carrying the load of dung.

## dn23:25.11
- **PLI**: Tamenaṁ manussā disvā evamāhaṁsu:
- **EN**: When people saw him they said,

## dn23:25.12
- **PLI**: ‘kacci no tvaṁ, bhaṇe, ummatto, kacci viceto, kathañhi nāma uggharantaṁ paggharantaṁ yāva agganakhā gūthena makkhito gūthabhāraṁ harissasī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Have you gone mad, lad? Have you lost your mind? For how on earth can you, smeared with leaking, oozing dung down to your fingernails, keep on carrying that load of dung?’

## dn23:25.13
- **PLI**: ‘Tumhe khvettha, bhaṇe, ummattā, tumhe vicetā, tathā hi pana me sūkarabhattan’ti.
- **EN**: ‘You’re the mad ones, lads! You’re the ones who’ve lost your minds! For this will serve as food for my pigs.’

## dn23:25.14
- **PLI**: Evameva kho tvaṁ, rājañña, gūthabhārikūpamo maññe paṭibhāsi.
- **EN**: In the same way, chieftain, you seem like the dung carrier in the simile.

## dn23:25.15
- **PLI**: Paṭinissajjetaṁ, rājañña, pāpakaṁ diṭṭhigataṁ; paṭinissajjetaṁ, rājañña, pāpakaṁ diṭṭhigataṁ.
- **EN**: Let go of this harmful misconception, chieftain, let go of it!

## dn23:25.16
- **PLI**: Mā te ahosi dīgharattaṁ ahitāya dukkhāyā”ti.
- **EN**: Don’t create lasting harm and suffering for yourself!”

## dn23:26.1  `bj8.550, cck10.404, csp1ed7.276, csp2ed7.276, ms7D_1128, msdiv433, pts-cs26, sya10.386`
- **PLI**: “Kiñcāpi bhavaṁ kassapo evamāha, atha kho nevāhaṁ sakkomi idaṁ pāpakaṁ diṭṭhigataṁ paṭinissajjituṁ.
- **EN**: “Even though the worthy Kassapa says this, still I’m not able to let go of that harmful misconception.

## dn23:26.2
- **PLI**: Rājāpi maṁ pasenadi kosalo jānāti tirorājānopi:
- **EN**: King Pasenadi of Kosala knows my views, and so do foreign kings.

## dn23:26.3
- **PLI**: ‘pāyāsi rājañño evaṁvādī evaṁdiṭṭhī:
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn23:26.4
- **PLI**: “itipi natthi paro loko …pe… vipāko”’ti.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn23:26.5
- **PLI**: Sacāhaṁ, bho kassapa, idaṁ pāpakaṁ diṭṭhigataṁ paṭinissajjissāmi, bhavissanti me vattāro:
- **EN**: If I let go of this harmful misconception, there will be some who say to me,  (= dn23:22.5)

## dn23:26.6
- **PLI**: ‘yāva bālo pāyāsi rājañño abyatto duggahitagāhī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘How foolish is the chieftain Pāyāsi, how incompetent, that he should hold on to a mistake!’  (= dn23:22.6)

## dn23:26.7
- **PLI**: Kopenapi naṁ harissāmi, makkhenapi naṁ harissāmi, palāsenapi naṁ harissāmī”ti.
- **EN**: I shall carry on with this view out of anger, contempt, and spite!”
