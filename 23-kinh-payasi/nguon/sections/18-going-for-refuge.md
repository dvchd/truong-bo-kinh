# Muc 30 — Going for Refuge  (8 đoạn)

## dn23:30.0
- **PLI**: 3. Saraṇagamana
- **EN**: 3. Going for Refuge

## dn23:30.1  `bj8.556, csp1ed7.279, csp2ed7.279, dr10.387, mc10.298, ms7D_1137, msdiv437, pts-cs30, sya10.391`
- **PLI**: “Purimeneva ahaṁ opammena bhoto kassapassa attamano abhiraddho.
- **EN**: “I was delighted and satisfied with your very first simile, worthy Kassapa!
- **NOTE**: Say what you will about Pāyāsi, he had character.

## dn23:30.2
- **PLI**: Api cāhaṁ imāni vicitrāni pañhāpaṭibhānāni sotukāmo evāhaṁ bhavantaṁ kassapaṁ paccanīkaṁ kātabbaṁ amaññissaṁ.
- **EN**: Nevertheless, I wanted to hear your various solutions to the problem, so I thought I’d oppose you in this way.

## dn23:30.3
- **PLI**: Abhikkantaṁ, bho kassapa, abhikkantaṁ, bho kassapa.
- **EN**: Excellent, worthy Kassapa! Excellent!

## dn23:30.4
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, bho kassapa, nikkujjitaṁ vā ukkujjeyya, paṭicchannaṁ vā vivareyya, mūḷhassa vā maggaṁ ācikkheyya, andhakāre vā telapajjotaṁ dhāreyya: ‘cakkhumanto rūpāni dakkhantī’ti; evamevaṁ bhotā kassapena anekapariyāyena dhammo pakāsito.
- **EN**: As if he were righting the overturned, or revealing the hidden, or pointing out the path to the lost, or lighting a lamp in the dark so people with clear eyes can see what’s there, the worthy Kassapa has made the teaching clear in many ways.

## dn23:30.5
- **PLI**: Esāhaṁ, bho kassapa, taṁ bhavantaṁ gotamaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi, dhammañca, bhikkhusaṅghañca.
- **EN**: I go for refuge to the worthy Gotama, to the teaching, and to the mendicant Saṅgha.

## dn23:30.6
- **PLI**: Upāsakaṁ maṁ bhavaṁ kassapo dhāretu ajjatagge pāṇupetaṁ saraṇaṁ gataṁ.
- **EN**: From this day forth, may the worthy Kassapa remember me as a lay follower who has gone for refuge for life.

## dn23:30.7  `ms7D_1138`
- **PLI**: Icchāmi cāhaṁ, bho kassapa, mahāyaññaṁ yajituṁ, anusāsatu maṁ bhavaṁ kassapo, yaṁ mamassa dīgharattaṁ hitāya sukhāyā”ti.
- **EN**: Worthy Kassapa, I wish to perform a great sacrifice. Please instruct me so it will be for my lasting welfare and happiness.”
