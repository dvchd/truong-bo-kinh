# Muc 33 — The Godling Pāyāsi  (23 đoạn)

## dn23:33.0
- **PLI**: 6. Pāyāsidevaputta
- **EN**: 6. The Godling Pāyāsi

## dn23:33.1  `cck10.413, csp1ed7.282, csp2ed7.282, dr10.392, mc10.301, ms7D_1144, msdiv441, ndp7.265, pts-cs33, sya10.395, sya10.396, vri2.262`
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena āyasmā gavampati abhikkhaṇaṁ suññaṁ serīsakaṁ vimānaṁ divāvihāraṁ gacchati.
- **EN**: Now at that time Venerable Gavampati would often go to that empty sirisa palace for the day’s meditation.
- **NOTE**: Gavampati also appears in <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn56.30/en/sujato'>SN 56.30</a>; see note there for a detailed discussion. | <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Divāvihāra</i> is the “day’s meditation”, while <i lang='pi' translate='no'>divāseyya</i> is “siesta”.

## dn23:33.2
- **PLI**: Atha kho pāyāsi devaputto yenāyasmā gavampati tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā āyasmantaṁ gavampatiṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ aṭṭhāsi. Ekamantaṁ ṭhitaṁ kho pāyāsiṁ devaputtaṁ āyasmā gavampati etadavoca:
- **EN**: Then the godling Pāyāsi went up to him, bowed, and stood to one side. Gavampati said to him,

## dn23:33.3
- **PLI**: “kosi tvaṁ, āvuso”ti?
- **EN**: “Who are you, reverend?”

## dn23:33.4
- **PLI**: “Ahaṁ, bhante, pāyāsi rājañño”ti.
- **EN**: “Sir, I am the chieftain Pāyāsi.”

## dn23:33.5
- **PLI**: “Nanu tvaṁ, āvuso, evaṁdiṭṭhiko ahosi:
- **EN**: “Didn’t you have the view that

## dn23:33.6
- **PLI**: ‘itipi natthi paro loko, natthi sattā opapātikā, natthi sukatadukkaṭānaṁ kammānaṁ phalaṁ vipāko’”ti?
- **EN**: there is no afterlife, no beings are reborn spontaneously, and there’s no fruit or result of good and bad deeds?”

## dn23:33.7
- **PLI**: “Saccāhaṁ, bhante, evaṁdiṭṭhiko ahosiṁ:
- **EN**: “It’s true, sir, I did have such a view.

## dn23:33.8
- **PLI**: ‘itipi natthi paro loko, natthi sattā opapātikā, natthi sukatadukkaṭānaṁ kammānaṁ phalaṁ vipāko’ti.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn23:33.9  `pts-vp-pli2.357`
- **PLI**: Api cāhaṁ ayyena kumārakassapena etasmā pāpakā diṭṭhigatā vivecito”ti.
- **EN**: But Master Kassapa the Prince dissuaded me from that harmful misconception.”

## dn23:33.10
- **PLI**: “Yo pana te, āvuso, dāne vāvaṭo ahosi uttaro nāma māṇavo, so kuhiṁ upapanno”ti?
- **EN**: “But the student named Uttara who organized that offering for you—where has he been reborn?”

## dn23:33.11
- **PLI**: “Yo me, bhante, dāne vāvaṭo ahosi uttaro nāma māṇavo, so sakkaccaṁ dānaṁ datvā sahatthā dānaṁ datvā cittīkataṁ dānaṁ datvā anapaviddhaṁ dānaṁ datvā kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā sugatiṁ saggaṁ lokaṁ upapanno devānaṁ tāvatiṁsānaṁ sahabyataṁ.
- **EN**: “Sir, Uttara gave gifts carefully, thoughtfully, with his own hands, not giving the dregs. When his body broke up, after death, he was reborn in company with the gods of the thirty-three.

## dn23:33.12
- **PLI**: Ahaṁ pana, bhante, asakkaccaṁ dānaṁ datvā asahatthā dānaṁ datvā acittīkataṁ dānaṁ datvā apaviddhaṁ dānaṁ datvā kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā cātumahārājikānaṁ devānaṁ sahabyataṁ upapanno suññaṁ serīsakaṁ vimānaṁ.
- **EN**: But I gave gifts carelessly, thoughtlessly, not with my own hands, giving the dregs. When my body broke up, after death, I was reborn in company with the gods of the four great kings, in an empty sirisa palace.

## dn23:33.13
- **PLI**: Tena hi, bhante gavampati, manussalokaṁ gantvā evamārocehi:
- **EN**: So, sir, when you’ve returned to the human realm, please announce this:

## dn23:33.14
- **PLI**: ‘sakkaccaṁ dānaṁ detha, sahatthā dānaṁ detha, cittīkataṁ dānaṁ detha, anapaviddhaṁ dānaṁ detha.
- **EN**: ‘Give gifts carefully, thoughtfully, with your own hands, not giving the dregs.

## dn23:33.15
- **PLI**: Pāyāsi rājañño asakkaccaṁ dānaṁ datvā asahatthā dānaṁ datvā acittīkataṁ dānaṁ datvā apaviddhaṁ dānaṁ datvā kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā cātumahārājikānaṁ devānaṁ sahabyataṁ upapanno suññaṁ serīsakaṁ vimānaṁ.
- **EN**: The chieftain Pāyāsi gave gifts carelessly, thoughtlessly, not with his own hands, giving the dregs. When his body broke up, after death, he was reborn in company with the gods of the four great kings, in an empty palace of sirisa.

## dn23:33.16
- **PLI**: Yo pana tassa dāne vāvaṭo ahosi uttaro nāma māṇavo, so sakkaccaṁ dānaṁ datvā sahatthā dānaṁ datvā cittīkataṁ dānaṁ datvā anapaviddhaṁ dānaṁ datvā kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā sugatiṁ saggaṁ lokaṁ upapanno devānaṁ tāvatiṁsānaṁ sahabyatan’”ti.
- **EN**: But the student Uttara who organized the offering gave gifts carefully, thoughtfully, with his own hands, not giving the dregs. When his body broke up, after death, he was reborn in company with the gods of the thirty-three.’”
