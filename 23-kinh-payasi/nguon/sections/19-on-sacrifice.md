## dn23:31.1  `bj8.558, cck10.409, dr10.388, ms7D_1139, msdiv438, ndp7.262, pts-cs31, pts-vp-pli2.353, vri2.260`
- **PLI**: “Yathārūpe kho, rājañña, yaññe gāvo vā haññanti ajeḷakā vā haññanti, kukkuṭasūkarā vā haññanti, vividhā vā pāṇā saṅghātaṁ āpajjanti, paṭiggāhakā ca honti micchādiṭṭhī micchāsaṅkappā micchāvācā micchākammantā micchāājīvā micchāvāyāmā micchāsatī micchāsamādhī, evarūpo kho, rājañña, yañño na mahapphalo hoti na mahānisaṁso na mahājutiko na mahāvipphāro.
- **EN**: “Chieftain, take the kind of sacrifice where cattle, goats and sheep, chickens and pigs, and various kinds of creatures are slaughtered. And the recipients have wrong view, wrong thought, wrong speech, wrong action, wrong livelihood, wrong effort, wrong mindfulness, and wrong immersion. That kind of sacrifice is not very fruitful or beneficial or brilliant or bountiful.

## dn23:31.2
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, rājañña, kassako bījanaṅgalaṁ ādāya vanaṁ paviseyya.
- **EN**: Suppose a farmer was to enter a wood taking seed and plough.

## dn23:31.3
- **PLI**: So tattha dukkhette dubbhūme avihatakhāṇukaṇṭake bījāni patiṭṭhāpeyya khaṇḍāni pūtīni vātātapahatāni asāradāni asukhasayitāni.
- **EN**: And on that barren field, that barren ground, with uncleared stumps he sowed seeds that were broken, spoiled, weather-damaged, infertile, and ill kept.

## dn23:31.4
- **PLI**: Devo ca na kālena kālaṁ sammādhāraṁ anuppaveccheyya.
- **EN**: And the heavens don’t provide enough rain when needed.

## dn23:31.5

- **EN**: Would those seeds grow, increase, and mature, and would the farmer get abundant fruit?”

## dn23:31.6
- **PLI**: “No hidaṁ, bho kassapa”.
- **EN**: “No, worthy Kassapa.”
