# Muc 5 — Nihilism  (7 đoạn)

## dn23:5.0
- **PLI**: 2. Natthikavāda
- **EN**: 2. Nihilism

## dn23:5.1  `dr10.352, ms7D_1095, msdiv410, ndp7.238, pts-cs5, vri2.239`
- **PLI**: Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho pāyāsi rājañño āyasmantaṁ kumārakassapaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: Seated to one side, the chieftain Pāyāsi said to Venerable Kassapa the Prince,

## dn23:5.2
- **PLI**: “ahañhi, bho kassapa, evaṁvādī evaṁdiṭṭhī:
- **EN**: “Worthy Kassapa, this is my doctrine and view:

## dn23:5.3
- **PLI**: ‘itipi natthi paro loko, natthi sattā opapātikā, natthi sukatadukkaṭānaṁ kammānaṁ phalaṁ vipāko’”ti.
- **EN**: ‘There is no afterlife. No beings are reborn spontaneously. There’s no fruit or result of good and bad deeds.’”

## dn23:5.4
- **PLI**: “Nāhaṁ, rājañña, evaṁvādiṁ evaṁdiṭṭhiṁ addasaṁ vā assosiṁ vā.
- **EN**: “Chieftain, may I never see or hear of anyone holding such a doctrine or view!
- **NOTE**: This idiom recurs at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an6.38/en/sujato#1.5'>AN 6.38:1.5</a> where, as here, it refers to a well-known view and cannot mean that they have never heard of it. There the text and commentary read <i lang='pi' translate='no'>māhaṁ</i>, which with the aorist is prohibitive not negatory. It is an idiom expressing dislike.

## dn23:5.5
- **PLI**: Kathañhi nāma evaṁ vadeyya:
- **EN**: For how on earth can anyone say such a thing?

## dn23:5.6
- **PLI**: ‘itipi natthi paro loko, natthi sattā opapātikā, natthi sukatadukkaṭānaṁ kammānaṁ phalaṁ vipāko’ti?
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
