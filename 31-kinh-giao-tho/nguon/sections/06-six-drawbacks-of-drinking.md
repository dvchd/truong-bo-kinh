# Muc 8 — Six Drawbacks of Drinking  (4 đoạn)

## dn31:8.0
- **PLI**: 5. Surāmerayassa cha ādīnavā
- **EN**: 5. Six Drawbacks of Drinking

## dn31:8.1  `ms8D_586, msdiv248, pts-cs8, pts-vp-pli3.183`
- **PLI**: Cha khome, gahapatiputta, ādīnavā surāmerayamajjappamādaṭṭhānānuyoge.
- **EN**: There are these six drawbacks of habitually consuming intoxicants of beer, wine, and liquor.
- **NOTE**: Now each of the six drains on wealth are expanded and explained.

## dn31:8.2
- **PLI**: Sandiṭṭhikā dhanajāni, kalahappavaḍḍhanī, rogānaṁ āyatanaṁ, akittisañjananī, kopīnanidaṁsanī, paññāya dubbalikaraṇītveva chaṭṭhaṁ padaṁ bhavati.
- **EN**: Immediate loss of wealth, promotion of quarrels, susceptibility to illness, disrepute, indecent exposure; and weakened wisdom is the sixth thing.

## dn31:8.3
- **PLI**: Ime kho, gahapatiputta, cha ādīnavā surāmerayamajjappamādaṭṭhānānuyoge.
- **EN**: These are the six drawbacks of habitually consuming intoxicants of beer, wine, and liquor.
