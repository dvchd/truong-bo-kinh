# Mo dau  (14 đoạn)

## dn31:1.1  `bj9.288, cck11.188, csp1ed8.146, csp2ed8.146, dr11.199, ms8D_575, msdiv242, ndp8.139, pts-cs1, pts-vp-pli3.180, sya11.194, vri3.136`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—
- **EN**: So I have heard.

## dn31:1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā rājagahe viharati veḷuvane kalandakanivāpe.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying near Rājagaha in the Bamboo Grove, the squirrels’ feeding ground.

## dn31:1.3
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena siṅgālako gahapatiputto kālasseva uṭṭhāya rājagahā nikkhamitvā allavattho allakeso pañjaliko puthudisā namassati—
- **EN**: Now at that time the householder’s son Sigālaka rose early and left Rājagaha. With his clothes and hair all wet, he raised his cupped palms to revere the various quarters—
- **NOTE**: Sigālaka only appears in this sutta. His name means “little jackal”.

## dn31:1.4
- **PLI**: puratthimaṁ disaṁ dakkhiṇaṁ disaṁ pacchimaṁ disaṁ uttaraṁ disaṁ heṭṭhimaṁ disaṁ uparimaṁ disaṁ.
- **EN**: east, south, west, north, below, and above.
- **NOTE**: Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa 5.5.1 recommends offerings to Agni in the east, Indra or Soma in the south, the All-gods in the west, Mitra and Varuṇa in the north, and Bṛhaspati in the middle. The quarters themselves are divine, since one can travel them and never reach the end (Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad 4.1.5). Atharva Veda 12.3.7–10 gives a domestic prayer for the quarters; here Yama dwells in the south with the Fathers, while Soma is in the west, and the other directions are not associated with specific deities. Vājasaneyi Saṁhitā 22.24a worships the six directions. Maitrī Upaniṣad 7.1–6 details the spiritual meanings of the six quarters. In Buddhism, the quarters are said to be inhabited by the respective Four Great Kings as per <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn32/en/sujato'>DN 32</a>.

## dn31:2.1  `ms8D_576, msdiv243, pts-cs2, pts-vp-pli3.181, sya11.195`
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā pubbaṇhasamayaṁ nivāsetvā pattacīvaramādāya rājagahaṁ piṇḍāya pāvisi.
- **EN**: Then the Buddha robed up in the morning and, taking his bowl and robe, entered Rājagaha for alms.

## dn31:2.2
- **PLI**: Addasā kho bhagavā siṅgālakaṁ gahapatiputtaṁ kālasseva vuṭṭhāya rājagahā nikkhamitvā allavatthaṁ allakesaṁ pañjalikaṁ puthudisā namassantaṁ—
- **EN**: He saw Sigālaka revering the various quarters

## dn31:2.3
- **PLI**: puratthimaṁ disaṁ dakkhiṇaṁ disaṁ pacchimaṁ disaṁ uttaraṁ disaṁ heṭṭhimaṁ disaṁ uparimaṁ disaṁ.
- **EN**: east, south, west, north, below, and above.  (= dn31:1.4)

## dn31:2.4
- **PLI**: Disvā siṅgālakaṁ gahapatiputtaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: and said to him,

## dn31:2.5
- **PLI**: “kiṁ nu kho tvaṁ, gahapatiputta, kālasseva vuṭṭhāya rājagahā nikkhamitvā allavattho allakeso pañjaliko puthudisā namassasi—
- **EN**: “Householder’s son, why are you revering the various quarters in this way?”

## dn31:2.6
- **PLI**: puratthimaṁ disaṁ dakkhiṇaṁ disaṁ pacchimaṁ disaṁ uttaraṁ disaṁ heṭṭhimaṁ disaṁ uparimaṁ disan”ti?
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn31:2.7
- **PLI**: “Pitā maṁ, bhante, kālaṁ karonto evaṁ avaca:
- **EN**: “Sir, on his deathbed my father said to me:

## dn31:2.8
- **PLI**: ‘disā, tāta, namasseyyāsī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘My dear, please revere the quarters.’
- **NOTE**: Sigālaka followed blindly without considering the meaning as urged by Chāndogya Upaniṣad 1.3.11.

## dn31:2.9
- **PLI**: So kho ahaṁ, bhante, pituvacanaṁ sakkaronto garuṁ karonto mānento pūjento kālasseva uṭṭhāya rājagahā nikkhamitvā allavattho allakeso pañjaliko puthudisā namassāmi—
- **EN**: Honoring, respecting, and venerating my father’s words, I rose early and left Rājagaha and, with my clothes and hair all wet, raised my cupped palms to revere the various quarters—

## dn31:2.10
- **PLI**: puratthimaṁ disaṁ dakkhiṇaṁ disaṁ pacchimaṁ disaṁ uttaraṁ disaṁ heṭṭhimaṁ disaṁ uparimaṁ disan”ti.
- **EN**: east, south, west, north, below, and above.”
