# Muc 9 — Six Drawbacks of Roaming the Street Markets at Night  (4 đoạn)

## dn31:9.0
- **PLI**: 6. Vikālacariyāya cha ādīnavā
- **EN**: 6. Six Drawbacks of Roaming the Street Markets at Night

## dn31:9.1  `cck11.191, ms8D_587, msdiv249, pts-cs9, sya11.197`
- **PLI**: Cha khome, gahapatiputta, ādīnavā vikālavisikhācariyānuyoge.
- **EN**: There are these six drawbacks of roaming the street markets at night.

## dn31:9.2
- **PLI**: Attāpissa agutto arakkhito hoti, puttadāropissa agutto arakkhito hoti, sāpateyyampissa aguttaṁ arakkhitaṁ hoti, saṅkiyo ca hoti pāpakesu ṭhānesu, abhūtavacanañca tasmiṁ rūhati, bahūnañca dukkhadhammānaṁ purakkhato hoti.
- **EN**: Yourself, your partners and children, and your property are all left unguarded. You’re suspected of bad deeds. Untrue rumors spread about you. You’re at the forefront of many things that entail suffering.

## dn31:9.3
- **PLI**: Ime kho, gahapatiputta, cha ādīnavā vikālavisikhācariyānuyoge.
- **EN**: These are the six drawbacks of roaming the street markets at night.
