# Muc 2.11 — The Six Directions  (10 đoạn)

## dn31:2.11.0
- **PLI**: 1. Cha disā
- **EN**: 1. The Six Directions

## dn31:2.12  `cck11.189, dr11.200, ms8D_577, msdiv244`
- **PLI**: “Na kho, gahapatiputta, ariyassa vinaye evaṁ cha disā namassitabbā”ti.
- **EN**: “Householder’s son, that’s not how the six directions should be revered in the training of the Noble One.”
- **NOTE**: It is unusual to find “six” directions. Normally it is four, then eight (with intermediate directions), then ten (above and below). Praśna Upaniṣad 1.6 lists the six, then the intermediates.

## dn31:2.13
- **PLI**: “Yathā kathaṁ pana, bhante, ariyassa vinaye cha disā namassitabbā?
- **EN**: “But sir, how should the six directions be revered in the training of the Noble One?

## dn31:2.14
- **PLI**: Sādhu me, bhante, bhagavā tathā dhammaṁ desetu, yathā ariyassa vinaye cha disā namassitabbā”ti.
- **EN**: Sir, please teach me this.”

## dn31:2.15  `bj9.290, ms8D_578, ndp8.140, vri3.137`
- **PLI**: “Tena hi, gahapatiputta, suṇohi sādhukaṁ manasikarohi bhāsissāmī”ti.
- **EN**: “Well then, householder’s son, listen and apply your mind well, I will speak.”

## dn31:2.16
- **PLI**: “Evaṁ, bhante”ti kho siṅgālako gahapatiputto bhagavato paccassosi.
- **EN**: “Yes, sir,” replied Sigālaka.

## dn31:2.17
- **PLI**: Bhagavā etadavoca:
- **EN**: The Buddha said this:

## dn31:3.1  `csp1ed8.147, csp2ed8.147, ms8D_579, pts-cs3`
- **PLI**: “Yato kho, gahapatiputta, ariyasāvakassa cattāro kammakilesā pahīnā honti, catūhi ca ṭhānehi pāpakammaṁ na karoti, cha ca bhogānaṁ apāyamukhāni na sevati, so evaṁ cuddasa pāpakāpagato chaddisāpaṭicchādī
- **EN**: “Householder’s son, a noble disciple gives up four corrupt deeds, doesn’t do bad deeds on four grounds, and avoids six drains on wealth. When they’ve left these fourteen bad things behind they have the six directions covered.
- **NOTE**: The Buddha outlines the contents of the discourse to follow.

## dn31:3.2
- **PLI**: ubholokavijayāya paṭipanno hoti. Tassa ayañceva loko āraddho hoti paro ca loko.
- **EN**: They’re practicing to win in both worlds, and they succeed in this world and the next.

## dn31:3.3
- **PLI**: So kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā sugatiṁ saggaṁ lokaṁ upapajjati.
- **EN**: When their body breaks up, after death, they’re reborn in a good place, a heavenly realm.
