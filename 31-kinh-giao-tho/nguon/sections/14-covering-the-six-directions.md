- **PLI**: “Kathañca, gahapatiputta, ariyasāvako chaddisāpaṭicchādī hoti?
- **EN**: “And how, householder’s son, does a noble disciple cover the six directions?
- **NOTE**: Having explained the six drains on wealth, the Buddha moves on to the next item in the table of contents.

## dn31:27.2
- **PLI**: Cha imā, gahapatiputta, disā veditabbā.
- **EN**: These six directions should be recognized:

## dn31:27.3
- **PLI**: Puratthimā disā mātāpitaro veditabbā, dakkhiṇā disā ācariyā veditabbā, pacchimā disā puttadārā veditabbā, uttarā disā mittāmaccā veditabbā, heṭṭhimā disā dāsakammakarā veditabbā, uparimā disā samaṇabrāhmaṇā veditabbā.
- **EN**: parents as the east, tutors as the south, partner and children as the west, friends and colleagues as the north, bondservants and workers as beneath, and ascetics and brahmins as above.
- **NOTE**: Parents are in the east because that is where the sun is born. Tutors are to the south (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>dakkhiṇā</i>) as they are owed an offering (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>dakkhiṇā</i>). Family is one’s legacy, so they lie with the setting sun in the west. Good friends keep you on the upright way to the north. Servants support you and make your life possible, like the earth below. And spiritual teachers lead you to heaven or transcendence above.
