# Muc 13 — Six Drawbacks of Laziness  (43 đoạn)

## dn31:13.0
- **PLI**: 10. Ālasyassa cha ādīnavā
- **EN**: 10. Six Drawbacks of Laziness

## dn31:13.1  `dr11.204, ms8D_591, msdiv253, pts-cs13, sya11.198`
- **PLI**: Cha khome, gahapatiputta, ādīnavā ālasyānuyoge.
- **EN**: There are these six drawbacks of habitual laziness.

## dn31:13.2
- **PLI**: Atisītanti kammaṁ na karoti, atiuṇhanti kammaṁ na karoti, atisāyanti kammaṁ na karoti, atipātoti kammaṁ na karoti, atichātosmīti kammaṁ na karoti, atidhātosmīti kammaṁ na karoti.
- **EN**: You don’t get your work done because you think: ‘It’s too cold! It’s too hot. It’s too late! It’s too early! I’m too hungry! I’m too full!’

## dn31:13.3
- **PLI**: Tassa evaṁ kiccāpadesabahulassa viharato anuppannā ceva bhogā nuppajjanti, uppannā ca bhogā parikkhayaṁ gacchanti.
- **EN**: By dwelling on so many excuses for not working, you don’t make any more money, and the money you already have runs out.

## dn31:13.4
- **PLI**: Ime kho, gahapatiputta, cha ādīnavā ālasyānuyoge”ti.
- **EN**: These are the six drawbacks of habitual laziness.”
