# Muc 5 — Four Grounds  (15 đoạn)

## dn31:5.0
- **PLI**: 3. Catuṭhānaṁ
- **EN**: 3. Four Grounds

## dn31:5.1  `cck11.190, ms8D_582, msdiv246, pts-cs5, sya11.196`
- **PLI**: “Katamehi catūhi ṭhānehi pāpakammaṁ na karoti?
- **EN**: “On what four grounds do they not do bad deeds?
- **NOTE**: First we learned what the bad deeds were, now we learn why people do them.

## dn31:5.2
- **PLI**: Chandāgatiṁ gacchanto pāpakammaṁ karoti, dosāgatiṁ gacchanto pāpakammaṁ karoti, mohāgatiṁ gacchanto pāpakammaṁ karoti, bhayāgatiṁ gacchanto pāpakammaṁ karoti.
- **EN**: One does bad deeds prejudiced by favoritism, hostility, stupidity, and cowardice.

## dn31:5.3
- **PLI**: Yato kho, gahapatiputta, ariyasāvako neva chandāgatiṁ gacchati, na dosāgatiṁ gacchati, na mohāgatiṁ gacchati, na bhayāgatiṁ gacchati;
- **EN**: When a noble disciple is not prejudiced by favoritism, hostility, stupidity, and cowardice,

## dn31:5.4
- **PLI**: imehi catūhi ṭhānehi pāpakammaṁ na karotī”ti.
- **EN**: they don’t do bad deeds on these four grounds.”

## dn31:5.5
- **PLI**: Idamavoca bhagavā.
- **EN**: That is what the Buddha said.

## dn31:5.6
- **PLI**: Idaṁ vatvāna sugato athāparaṁ etadavoca satthā:
- **EN**: Then the Holy One, the Teacher, went on to say:

## dn31:6.1  `ms8D_583`
- **PLI**: “Chandā dosā bhayā mohā,
- **EN**: “If you act against the teaching

## dn31:6.2
- **PLI**: yo dhammaṁ ativattati;
- **EN**: out of favoritism, hostility, cowardice, or stupidity,

## dn31:6.3
- **PLI**: Nihīyati yaso tassa,
- **EN**: your fame shrinks,

## dn31:6.4
- **PLI**: kāḷapakkheva candimā.
- **EN**: like the moon in the waning fortnight.

## dn31:6.5  `bj9.292, ms8D_584, ndp8.141, vri3.138`
- **PLI**: Chandā dosā bhayā mohā,
- **EN**: If you don’t act against the teaching

## dn31:6.6
- **PLI**: yo dhammaṁ nātivattati;
- **EN**: out of favoritism, hostility, cowardice, and stupidity,

## dn31:6.7
- **PLI**: Āpūrati yaso tassa,
- **EN**: your fame swells,

## dn31:6.8
- **PLI**: sukkapakkheva candimā”ti.
- **EN**: like the moon in the waxing fortnight.”
