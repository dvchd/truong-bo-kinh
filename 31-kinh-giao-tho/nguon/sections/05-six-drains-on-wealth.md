# Muc 7 — Six Drains on Wealth  (3 đoạn)

## dn31:7.0
- **PLI**: 4. Cha apāyamukhāni
- **EN**: 4. Six Drains on Wealth

## dn31:7.1  `csp1ed8.148, csp2ed8.148, dr11.202, ms8D_585, msdiv247, pts-cs7`
- **PLI**: “Katamāni cha bhogānaṁ apāyamukhāni na sevati?
- **EN**: “What six drains on wealth do they avoid?
- **NOTE**: “Drains on wealth” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>apāyamukha</i>, literally “openings for departure”. This is emphasized here since Sigālaka wants to be a good son and not squander the family fortune.

## dn31:7.2
- **PLI**: Surāmerayamajjappamādaṭṭhānānuyogo kho, gahapatiputta, bhogānaṁ apāyamukhaṁ, vikālavisikhācariyānuyogo bhogānaṁ apāyamukhaṁ, samajjābhicaraṇaṁ bhogānaṁ apāyamukhaṁ, jūtappamādaṭṭhānānuyogo bhogānaṁ apāyamukhaṁ, pāpamittānuyogo bhogānaṁ apāyamukhaṁ, ālasyānuyogo bhogānaṁ apāyamukhaṁ.
- **EN**: Habitually engaging in the following things is a drain on wealth: beer, wine, and liquor intoxicants; roaming the street markets at night; frequenting festivals; gambling; bad friends; laziness.
- **NOTE**: The most common phrase in Pali for alcoholic beverages lists three items. <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Surā</i> is brewed from grains with yeast (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-bu-vb-pc51/en/sujato#2.1.2'>Bu Pc 51:2.1.2</a>) <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Meraya</i> is made from flowers, fruits, or sugars (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-bu-vb-pc51/en/sujato#2.1.4'>Bu Pc 51:2.1.4</a>). <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Madya</i> is apparently a catchall. Together they correspond fairly well with the modern classification of alcoholic drinks as “beer, wine, and liquor”. See also Manu 11.94, Arthaśāstra 2.25, Suśrutasaṁhitā 1.45, Amarakośodghāṭana 3.6.
