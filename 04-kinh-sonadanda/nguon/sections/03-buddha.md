## dn4:6.46
- **PLI**: “yathā kho bhavaṁ soṇadaṇḍo samaṇassa gotamassa vaṇṇe bhāsati ito cepi so bhavaṁ gotamo yojanasate viharati, alameva saddhena kulaputtena dassanāya upasaṅkamituṁ api puṭosenā”ti.
- **EN**: “According to Soṇadaṇḍa’s praises, if the worthy Gotama were staying within a hundred leagues, it’d be worthwhile for a faithful gentleman to go to see him, even if he had to carry his own provisions in a knapsack.”
- **NOTE**: “Knapsack” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>puṭosa</i>, where <i lang='pi' translate='no'>puṭa</i> is “bag” and <i lang='pi' translate='no'>osa</i> is “food” (Sanskrit <i lang='pi' translate='no'>avasa</i>). The compound is a dative-dependent <i lang='pi' translate='no'>tappurisa</i> with reversal of the usual order, yielding the sense “bag for food”.
