# 1 — Các bà-la-môn và gia chủ xứ Campā  (27 đoạn)

## dn4:1.0
- **PLI**: 1. Campeyyakabrāhmaṇagahapatikā
- **EN**: 1. The Brahmins and Householders of Campā

## dn4:1.1  `bj7.222, cck9.141, csp1ed6.104, csp2ed6.104, dr9.147, km14.264, lv1.301, mc9.110, ms6D_426, msdiv300, ndp6.97, pts-cs1, pts-vp-pli1.111, sya9.142, vri1.97`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—
- **EN**: So I have heard.
- **NOTE**: This sutta shows how the conversion of Pokkharasāti in <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn3/en/sujato'>DN 3</a> affected the brahmins as far away as Campā.

## dn4:1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā aṅgesu cārikaṁ caramāno mahatā bhikkhusaṅghena saddhiṁ pañcamattehi bhikkhusatehi yena campā tadavasari.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was wandering in the land of the Aṅgas together with a large Saṅgha of five hundred mendicants when he arrived at Campā,
- **NOTE**: Campā is modern Champapuri near Bhagalpur in Bihar state, not far from West Bengal. It is near the eastern-most reach of the Buddha’s journeys. Campā was the capital of Aṅga, one of the sixteen “great nations” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>mahājanapadā</i>). It was a flourishing trade center at which Northern Black Polished Ware has been found, and became a sacred city for the Jains.

## dn4:1.3
- **PLI**: Tatra sudaṁ bhagavā campāyaṁ viharati gaggarāya pokkharaṇiyā tīre.
- **EN**: where he stayed by the banks of the Gaggarā Lotus Pond.
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Gaggarā</i>, an onomatopoeic reduplication (“gargle”), is the name of a number of rivers and whirlpools in Sanskrit (cp. the modern Ghaggar River in north-west India).

## dn4:1.4
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena soṇadaṇḍo brāhmaṇo campaṁ ajjhāvasati sattussadaṁ satiṇakaṭṭhodakaṁ sadhaññaṁ rājabhoggaṁ raññā māgadhena seniyena bimbisārena dinnaṁ rājadāyaṁ brahmadeyyaṁ.
- **EN**: Now at that time the brahmin Soṇadaṇḍa was living in Campā. It was a crown property given by King Seniya Bimbisāra of Magadha, teeming with living creatures, full of hay, wood, water, and grain, a royal park endowed to a brahmin.
- **NOTE**: Here we see how the endowment of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>brahmadeyya</i> helped the king of Magadha establish his influence over the Aṅgas.

## dn4:2.1  `cck9.142, dr9.148, km14.265, km14.266, lv1.302, lv1.303, ms6D_427, msdiv301, pts-cs2, pts-vp-pli1.112, sya9.143`
- **PLI**: Assosuṁ kho campeyyakā brāhmaṇagahapatikā:
- **EN**: The brahmins and householders of Campā heard:
- **NOTE**: “Householders” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>gahapati</i>) is literal; it means land owners. Thus the “brahmins and householders” (not “brahmin householders”) were the wealthy class.

## dn4:2.2
- **PLI**: “samaṇo khalu bho gotamo sakyaputto sakyakulā pabbajito aṅgesu cārikaṁ caramāno mahatā bhikkhusaṅghena saddhiṁ pañcamattehi bhikkhusatehi campaṁ anuppatto campāyaṁ viharati gaggarāya pokkharaṇiyā tīre.
- **EN**: “It seems the ascetic Gotama—a Sakyan, gone forth from a Sakyan family—has arrived at Campā and is staying on the banks of the Gaggarā Lotus Pond.

## dn4:2.3
- **PLI**: Taṁ kho pana bhavantaṁ gotamaṁ evaṁ kalyāṇo kittisaddo abbhuggato:
- **EN**: He has this good reputation:

## dn4:2.4
- **PLI**: ‘itipi so bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho vijjācaraṇasampanno sugato lokavidū anuttaro purisadammasārathi satthā devamanussānaṁ buddho bhagavā’.
- **EN**: ‘That Blessed One is perfected, a fully awakened Buddha, accomplished in knowledge and conduct, holy, knower of the world, supreme guide for those fit for training, teacher of gods and humans, awakened, blessed.’

## dn4:2.5
- **PLI**: So imaṁ lokaṁ sadevakaṁ samārakaṁ sabrahmakaṁ sassamaṇabrāhmaṇiṁ pajaṁ sadevamanussaṁ sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā pavedeti.
- **EN**: He has realized with his own insight this world—with its gods, Māras, and divinities, this population with its ascetics and brahmins, gods and humans—and he makes it known to others.

## dn4:2.6
- **PLI**: So dhammaṁ deseti ādikalyāṇaṁ majjhekalyāṇaṁ pariyosānakalyāṇaṁ sātthaṁ sabyañjanaṁ kevalaparipuṇṇaṁ parisuddhaṁ brahmacariyaṁ pakāseti.
- **EN**: He teaches Dhamma that’s good in the beginning, good in the middle, and good in the end, meaningful and well-phrased. And he reveals a spiritual practice that’s entirely full and pure.

## dn4:2.7
- **PLI**: Sādhu kho pana tathārūpānaṁ arahataṁ dassanaṁ hotī”ti.
- **EN**: It’s good to see such perfected ones.”

## dn4:2.8
- **PLI**: Atha kho campeyyakā brāhmaṇagahapatikā campāya nikkhamitvā saṅghasaṅghī gaṇībhūtā yena gaggarā pokkharaṇī tenupasaṅkamanti.
- **EN**: Then, exiting Campā, they formed into companies and headed to the Gaggarā Lotus Pond.

## dn4:3.1  `bj7.224, cck9.143, csp1ed6.105, csp2ed6.105, dr9.149, km14.267, km14.268, lv1.304, lv1.305, mc9.111, ms6D_428, msdiv302, ndp6.98, pts-cs3, pts-vp-pli1.113, sya9.144, vri1.98`
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena soṇadaṇḍo brāhmaṇo uparipāsāde divāseyyaṁ upagato hoti.
- **EN**: Now at that time the brahmin Soṇadaṇḍa had retired to the upper floor of his stilt longhouse for his midday nap.

## dn4:3.2
- **PLI**: Addasā kho soṇadaṇḍo brāhmaṇo campeyyake brāhmaṇagahapatike campāya nikkhamitvā saṅghasaṅghī gaṇībhūte yena gaggarā pokkharaṇī tenupasaṅkamante.
- **EN**: He saw the brahmins and householders heading for the lotus pond,

## dn4:3.3
- **PLI**: Disvā khattaṁ āmantesi:
- **EN**: and addressed his butler,

## dn4:3.4
- **PLI**: “kiṁ nu kho, bho khatte, campeyyakā brāhmaṇagahapatikā campāya nikkhamitvā saṅghasaṅghī gaṇībhūtā yena gaggarā pokkharaṇī tenupasaṅkamantī”ti?
- **EN**: “My butler, why are the brahmins and householders headed for the Gaggarā Lotus Pond?”

## dn4:3.5
- **PLI**: “Atthi kho, bho, samaṇo gotamo sakyaputto sakyakulā pabbajito aṅgesu cārikaṁ caramāno mahatā bhikkhusaṅghena saddhiṁ pañcamattehi bhikkhusatehi campaṁ anuppatto campāyaṁ viharati gaggarāya pokkharaṇiyā tīre.
- **EN**: “The ascetic Gotama has arrived at Campā and is staying on the banks of the Gaggarā Lotus Pond.

## dn4:3.6
- **PLI**: Taṁ kho pana bhavantaṁ gotamaṁ evaṁ kalyāṇo kittisaddo abbhuggato:
- **EN**: He has this good reputation:

## dn4:3.7
- **PLI**: ‘itipi so bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho vijjācaraṇasampanno sugato lokavidū anuttaro purisadammasārathi satthā devamanussānaṁ buddho bhagavā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘That Blessed One is perfected, a fully awakened Buddha, accomplished in knowledge and conduct, holy, knower of the world, supreme guide for those fit for training, teacher of gods and humans, awakened, blessed.’

## dn4:3.8
- **PLI**: Tamete bhavantaṁ gotamaṁ dassanāya upasaṅkamantī”ti.
- **EN**: They’re going to see that worthy Gotama.”

## dn4:3.9
- **PLI**: “Tena hi, bho khatte, yena campeyyakā brāhmaṇagahapatikā tenupasaṅkama, upasaṅkamitvā campeyyake brāhmaṇagahapatike evaṁ vadehi:
- **EN**: “Well then, go to the brahmins and householders and say to them:

## dn4:3.10
- **PLI**: ‘soṇadaṇḍo, bho, brāhmaṇo evamāha—
- **EN**: ‘Sirs, the brahmin Soṇadaṇḍa asks

## dn4:3.11
- **PLI**: āgamentu kira bhavanto, soṇadaṇḍopi brāhmaṇo samaṇaṁ gotamaṁ dassanāya upasaṅkamissatī’”ti.
- **EN**: you to wait, as he will also go to see the ascetic Gotama.’”

## dn4:3.12
- **PLI**: “Evaṁ, bho”ti kho so khattā soṇadaṇḍassa brāhmaṇassa paṭissutvā yena campeyyakā brāhmaṇagahapatikā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā campeyyake brāhmaṇagahapatike etadavoca:
- **EN**: “Yes, worthy sir,” replied the butler, and did as he was asked.

## dn4:3.13
- **PLI**: “soṇadaṇḍo bho brāhmaṇo evamāha:
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn4:3.14
- **PLI**: ‘āgamentu kira bhavanto, soṇadaṇḍopi brāhmaṇo samaṇaṁ gotamaṁ dassanāya upasaṅkamissatī’”ti.
