- **EN**: But if Aṅgaka were to kill living creatures, steal, commit adultery, lie, and drink liquor, then what’s the use of his appearance, his hymns, or his birth?

## dn4:20.11
- **PLI**: Yato kho, bho, brāhmaṇo sīlavā ca hoti vuddhasīlī vuddhasīlena samannāgato,
- **EN**: It’s when a brahmin is ethical, mature in ethical conduct;

## dn4:20.12
- **PLI**: paṇḍito ca hoti medhāvī paṭhamo vā dutiyo vā sujaṁ paggaṇhantānaṁ.
- **EN**: and he’s astute and intelligent, being the first or second to hold the sacrificial ladle.

## dn4:20.13
- **PLI**: Imehi kho, bho, dvīhaṅgehi samannāgataṁ brāhmaṇā brāhmaṇaṁ paññapenti;
- **EN**: A brahmin must possess these two factors for the brahmins to rightly describe him as a brahmin.”

## dn4:20.14
- **PLI**: ‘brāhmaṇosmī’ti ca vadamāno sammā vadeyya, na ca pana musāvādaṁ āpajjeyyā”ti.
- **EN**: and so that when he says ‘I am a brahmin’ he speaks rightly, without falling into falsehood.”  (= dn4:13.11)
