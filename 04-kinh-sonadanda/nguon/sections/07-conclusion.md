# 7 — Soṇadaṇḍa xin làm người cư sĩ  (28 đoạn)

## dn4:24.0
- **PLI**: 7. Soṇadaṇḍaupāsakattapaṭivedanā
- **EN**: 7. Soṇadaṇḍa Declares Himself a Lay Follower

## dn4:24.1  `cck9.160, csp1ed6.118, csp2ed6.118, lv1.342, mc9.125, ms6D_488, msdiv319, pts-cs24, pts-vp-pli1.125, sya9.160`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ vutte, soṇadaṇḍo brāhmaṇo bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: When he had spoken, Soṇadaṇḍa said to the Buddha,

## dn4:24.2
- **PLI**: “abhikkantaṁ, bho gotama, abhikkantaṁ, bho gotama.
- **EN**: “Excellent, worthy Gotama! Excellent!

## dn4:24.3
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, bho gotama, nikkujjitaṁ vā ukkujjeyya, paṭicchannaṁ vā vivareyya, mūḷhassa vā maggaṁ ācikkheyya, andhakāre vā telapajjotaṁ dhāreyya: ‘cakkhumanto rūpāni dakkhantī’ti; evamevaṁ bhotā gotamena anekapariyāyena dhammo pakāsito.
- **EN**: As if he were righting the overturned, or revealing the hidden, or pointing out the path to the lost, or lighting a lamp in the dark so people with clear eyes can see what’s there, worthy Gotama has made the Teaching clear in many ways.

## dn4:24.4
- **PLI**: Esāhaṁ bhavantaṁ gotamaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi, dhammañca, bhikkhusaṅghañca.
- **EN**: I go for refuge to the worthy Gotama, to the teaching, and to the mendicant Saṅgha.

## dn4:24.5
- **PLI**: Upāsakaṁ maṁ bhavaṁ gotamo dhāretu ajjatagge pāṇupetaṁ saraṇaṁ gataṁ.
- **EN**: From this day forth, may the worthy Gotama remember me as a lay follower who has gone for refuge for life.

## dn4:24.6
- **PLI**: Adhivāsetu ca me bhavaṁ gotamo svātanāya bhattaṁ saddhiṁ bhikkhusaṅghenā”ti.
- **EN**: Would you and the mendicant Saṅgha please accept tomorrow’s meal from me?”

## dn4:24.7
- **PLI**: Adhivāsesi bhagavā tuṇhībhāvena.
- **EN**: The Buddha consented with silence.

## dn4:24.8  `bj7.246, dr9.168, km14.298, lv1.343, ms6D_489, msdiv320, ndp6.108, vri1.111`
- **PLI**: Atha kho soṇadaṇḍo brāhmaṇo bhagavato adhivāsanaṁ viditvā uṭṭhāyāsanā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā padakkhiṇaṁ katvā pakkāmi.
- **EN**: Then, knowing that the Buddha had consented, Soṇadaṇḍa rose from his seat, bowed, and respectfully circled the Buddha, keeping him on his right, before leaving.

## dn4:24.9
- **PLI**: Atha kho soṇadaṇḍo brāhmaṇo tassā rattiyā accayena sake nivesane paṇītaṁ khādanīyaṁ bhojanīyaṁ paṭiyādāpetvā bhagavato kālaṁ ārocāpesi:
- **EN**: And when the night had passed Soṇadaṇḍa had delicious fresh and cooked foods prepared in his own home. Then he had the Buddha informed of the time, saying,

## dn4:24.10
- **PLI**: “kālo, bho gotama, niṭṭhitaṁ bhattan”ti.
- **EN**: “It’s time, worthy Gotama, the meal is ready.”

## dn4:25.1  `pts-cs25`
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā pubbaṇhasamayaṁ nivāsetvā pattacīvaramādāya saddhiṁ bhikkhusaṅghena yena soṇadaṇḍassa brāhmaṇassa nivesanaṁ tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā paññatte āsane nisīdi.
- **EN**: Then the Buddha robed up in the morning and, taking his bowl and robe, went to the home of Soṇadaṇḍa together with the mendicant Saṅgha, where he sat down on the seat spread out.

## dn4:25.2
- **PLI**: Atha kho soṇadaṇḍo brāhmaṇo buddhappamukhaṁ bhikkhusaṅghaṁ paṇītena khādanīyena bhojanīyena sahatthā santappesi sampavāresi.
- **EN**: Then Soṇadaṇḍa served and satisfied the mendicant Saṅgha headed by the Buddha with his own hands with delicious fresh and cooked foods.

## dn4:26.1  `cck9.161, csp1ed6.119, csp2ed6.119, dr9.169, km14.299, km14.300, lv1.344, lv1.345, mc9.126, ms6D_490, msdiv321, pts-cs26, pts-vp-pli1.126, sya9.161`
- **PLI**: Atha kho soṇadaṇḍo brāhmaṇo bhagavantaṁ bhuttāviṁ onītapattapāṇiṁ aññataraṁ nīcaṁ āsanaṁ gahetvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi.
- **EN**: When the Buddha had eaten and washed his hand and bowl, Soṇadaṇḍa took a low seat and sat to one side.

## dn4:26.2
- **PLI**: Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho soṇadaṇḍo brāhmaṇo bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: Seated to one side he said to the Buddha:

## dn4:26.3
- **PLI**: “ahañceva kho pana, bho gotama, parisagato samāno āsanā vuṭṭhahitvā bhavantaṁ gotamaṁ abhivādeyyaṁ, tena maṁ sā parisā paribhaveyya.
- **EN**: “Worthy Gotama, if, when I have gone to an assembly, I rise from my seat and bow to the worthy Gotama, that assembly might disparage me for that.

## dn4:26.4
- **PLI**: Yaṁ kho pana sā parisā paribhaveyya, yasopi tassa hāyetha.
- **EN**: And when you’re disparaged by the assembly, your reputation diminishes.

## dn4:26.5
- **PLI**: Yassa kho pana yaso hāyetha, bhogāpi tassa hāyeyyuṁ.
- **EN**: When your reputation diminishes, your wealth also diminishes.

## dn4:26.6
- **PLI**: Yasoladdhā kho panamhākaṁ bhogā.
- **EN**: But my wealth relies on my reputation.

## dn4:26.7
- **PLI**: Ahañceva kho pana, bho gotama, parisagato samāno añjaliṁ paggaṇheyyaṁ, āsanā me taṁ bhavaṁ gotamo paccuṭṭhānaṁ dhāretu.
- **EN**: If, when I have gone to an assembly, I raise my cupped palms, please take it that I have risen from my seat.

## dn4:26.8
- **PLI**: Ahañceva kho pana, bho gotama, parisagato samāno veṭhanaṁ omuñceyyaṁ, sirasā me taṁ bhavaṁ gotamo abhivādanaṁ dhāretu.
- **EN**: And if I undo my turban, please take it that I have bowed.

## dn4:26.9
- **PLI**: Ahañceva kho pana, bho gotama, yānagato samāno yānā paccorohitvā bhavantaṁ gotamaṁ abhivādeyyaṁ, tena maṁ sā parisā paribhaveyya.
- **EN**: And the worthy Gotama, if, when I am in a carriage, I get down from my carriage and bow to the worthy Gotama, that assembly might disparage me for that.

## dn4:26.10
- **PLI**: Yaṁ kho pana sā parisā paribhaveyya, yasopi tassa hāyetha, yassa kho pana yaso hāyetha, bhogāpi tassa hāyeyyuṁ.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn4:26.11
- **PLI**: Yasoladdhā kho panamhākaṁ bhogā.
- **EN**: But my wealth relies on my reputation.  (= dn4:8.8)

## dn4:26.12
- **PLI**: Ahañceva kho pana, bho gotama, yānagato samāno patodalaṭṭhiṁ abbhunnāmeyyaṁ, yānā me taṁ bhavaṁ gotamo paccorohanaṁ dhāretu.
- **EN**: If, when I am in a carriage, I hold up my goad, please take it that I have got down from my carriage.

## dn4:26.13
- **PLI**: Ahañceva kho pana, bho gotama, yānagato samāno chattaṁ apanāmeyyaṁ, sirasā me taṁ bhavaṁ gotamo abhivādanaṁ dhāretū”ti.
- **EN**: And if I lower my parasol, please take it that I have bowed.”
- **NOTE**: Soṇadaṇḍa’s attachment to his reputation betrays his lack of inner confidence and stands in contrast with Pokkharasāti. Perhaps it may also be read as a regional characteristic, since Buddhism was less well established in the Aṅga region than in central Magadha.
