# Muc 9.3 — Questioning Lohicca  (66 đoạn)

## dn12:9.3.0
- **PLI**: 1. Lohiccabrāhmaṇānuyoga
- **EN**: 1. Questioning Lohicca

## dn12:9.4  `cck9.291, csp1ed6.217, csp2ed6.217, dr9.309, km15.228, ms6D_874, msdiv509, sya9.288`
- **PLI**: Atha kho lohicco brāhmaṇo bhagavantaṁ bhuttāviṁ onītapattapāṇiṁ aññataraṁ nīcaṁ āsanaṁ gahetvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi.
- **EN**: When the Buddha had eaten and washed his hand and bowl, Lohicca took a low seat and sat to one side.

## dn12:9.5
- **PLI**: Ekamantaṁ nisinnaṁ kho lohiccaṁ brāhmaṇaṁ bhagavā etadavoca:
- **EN**: The Buddha said to him,

## dn12:9.6
- **PLI**: “saccaṁ kira te, lohicca, evarūpaṁ pāpakaṁ diṭṭhigataṁ uppannaṁ:
- **EN**: “Is it really true, Lohicca, that you have such a harmful misconception:
- **NOTE**: Here the Buddha takes the initiative. “Harmful misconception” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>pāpakaṁ diṭṭhigataṁ</i>.

## dn12:9.7
- **PLI**: ‘idha samaṇo vā brāhmaṇo vā kusalaṁ dhammaṁ adhigaccheyya, kusalaṁ dhammaṁ adhigantvā na parassa āroceyya, kiñhi paro parassa karissati.
- **EN**: ‘Should an ascetic or brahmin achieve some skillful quality, they ought not inform anyone else. For what can one person do for another?

## dn12:9.8
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi nāma purāṇaṁ bandhanaṁ chinditvā aññaṁ navaṁ bandhanaṁ kareyya;
- **EN**: Suppose someone cut off an old bond, only to create another new bond.

## dn12:9.9
- **PLI**: evaṁ sampadamidaṁ pāpakaṁ lobhadhammaṁ vadāmi, kiñhi paro parassa karissatī’”ti?
- **EN**: That’s the consequence of such a wicked, greedy deed, I say. For what can one person do for another?’”

## dn12:9.10
- **PLI**: “Evaṁ, bho gotama”.
- **EN**: “Yes, worthy Gotama.”

## dn12:10.1  `pts-cs10`
- **PLI**: “Taṁ kiṁ maññasi, lohicca,
- **EN**: “What do you think, Lohicca?

## dn12:10.2
- **PLI**: nanu tvaṁ sālavatikaṁ ajjhāvasasī”ti?
- **EN**: Do you reside in Sālavatikā?”

## dn12:10.3
- **PLI**: “Evaṁ, bho gotama”.
- **EN**: “Yes, worthy Gotama.”

## dn12:10.4
- **PLI**: “Yo nu kho, lohicca, evaṁ vadeyya:
- **EN**: “Lohicca, suppose someone were to say:

## dn12:10.5
- **PLI**: ‘lohicco brāhmaṇo sālavatikaṁ ajjhāvasati.
- **EN**: ‘The brahmin Lohicca resides in Sālavatikā.

## dn12:10.6
- **PLI**: Yā sālavatikāya samudayasañjāti lohiccova taṁ brāhmaṇo ekako paribhuñjeyya, na aññesaṁ dadeyyā’ti.
- **EN**: He alone should enjoy the revenues produced in Sālavatikā and not share them with anyone else.’

## dn12:10.7
- **PLI**: Evaṁ vādī so ye taṁ upajīvanti, tesaṁ antarāyakaro vā hoti, no vā”ti?
- **EN**: Would the one who spoke like that make it difficult for those whose living depends on you or not?”

## dn12:10.8  `dr9.310, km15.229, mc9.228, ms6D_875, ndp6.194, vri1.208`
- **PLI**: “Antarāyakaro, bho gotama”.
- **EN**: “They would, worthy Gotama.”

## dn12:10.9
- **PLI**: “Antarāyakaro samāno hitānukampī vā tesaṁ hoti ahitānukampī vā”ti?
- **EN**: “But is someone who creates difficulties for others acting kindly or unkindly?”

## dn12:10.10
- **PLI**: “Ahitānukampī, bho gotama”.
- **EN**: “Unkindly, sir.”

## dn12:10.11
- **PLI**: “Ahitānukampissa mettaṁ vā tesu cittaṁ paccupaṭṭhitaṁ hoti sapattakaṁ vā”ti?
- **EN**: “But does an unkind person have love in their heart or hostility?”

## dn12:10.12
- **PLI**: “Sapattakaṁ, bho gotama”.
- **EN**: “Hostility, sir.”

## dn12:10.13
- **PLI**: “Sapattake citte paccupaṭṭhite micchādiṭṭhi vā hoti sammādiṭṭhi vā”ti?
- **EN**: “And when the heart is full of hostility, is there right view or wrong view?”

## dn12:10.14
- **PLI**: “Micchādiṭṭhi, bho gotama”.
- **EN**: “Wrong view, worthy Gotama.”

## dn12:10.15
- **PLI**: “Micchādiṭṭhissa kho ahaṁ, lohicca, dvinnaṁ gatīnaṁ aññataraṁ gatiṁ vadāmi—
- **EN**: “An individual with wrong view is reborn in one of two places, I say:

## dn12:10.16
- **PLI**: nirayaṁ vā tiracchānayoniṁ vā.
- **EN**: hell or the animal realm.
