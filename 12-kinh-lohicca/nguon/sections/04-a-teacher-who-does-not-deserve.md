## dn12:78.1  `dr9.317, km15.242, ms6D_888, msdiv517, pts-cs78, vri1.213`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ vutte, lohicco brāhmaṇo bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: When he had spoken, Lohicca said to the Buddha:

## dn12:78.2
- **PLI**: “seyyathāpi, bho gotama, puriso purisaṁ narakapapātaṁ patantaṁ kesesu gahetvā uddharitvā thale patiṭṭhapeyya;
- **EN**: “Suppose, Worthy Gotama, a person was falling over a cliff, and another person were to grab them by the hair, pull them up, and place them on solid ground.
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Naraka</i> means “cliff” or “abyss” in early Pali (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn49/en/sujato#5.9'>MN 49:5.9</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn86/en/sujato#6.15'>MN 86:6.15</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/snp3.11/en/sujato#28.4'>Snp 3.11:28.4</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/thag16.8/en/sujato#4.2'>Thag 16.8:4.2</a>). It does not have the sense “hell” until the late canonical period; the early Pali term for hell is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>niraya</i>. Notably, <i lang='pi' translate='no'>naraka</i> is not strongly attested in the sense of “hell” for pre-Buddhist Sanskrit either, although we do find <i lang='pi' translate='no'>nāraka</i>, apparently in the sense of “hell being”, at Atharva Veda 12.4.36c and Śukla Yajur Veda 30.5.

## dn12:78.3
- **PLI**: evamevāhaṁ bhotā gotamena narakapapātaṁ papatanto uddharitvā thale patiṭṭhāpito.
- **EN**: In the same way, when I was falling off a cliff Worthy Gotama pulled me up and placed me on solid ground.

## dn12:78.4
- **PLI**: Abhikkantaṁ, bho gotama, abhikkantaṁ, bho gotama.
- **EN**: Excellent, worthy Gotama! Excellent!

## dn12:78.5
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, bho gotama, nikkujjitaṁ vā ukkujjeyya, paṭicchannaṁ vā vivareyya, mūḷhassa vā maggaṁ ācikkheyya, andhakāre vā telapajjotaṁ dhāreyya: ‘cakkhumanto rūpāni dakkhantī’ti; evamevaṁ bhotā gotamena anekapariyāyena dhammo pakāsito.
- **EN**: As if he were righting the overturned, or revealing the hidden, or pointing out the path to the lost, or lighting a lamp in the dark so people with clear eyes can see what’s there, worthy Gotama has made the Teaching clear in many ways.

## dn12:78.6
