## dn9:55.2

- **EN**: “Excellent, sir! Excellent!

## dn9:55.3
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, bhante, nikkujjitaṁ vā ukkujjeyya, paṭicchannaṁ vā vivareyya, mūḷhassa vā maggaṁ ācikkheyya, andhakāre vā telapajjotaṁ dhāreyya: ‘cakkhumanto rūpāni dakkhantī’ti; evamevaṁ bhagavatā anekapariyāyena dhammo pakāsito.
- **EN**: As if he were righting the overturned, or revealing the hidden, or pointing out the path to the lost, or lighting a lamp in the dark so people with clear eyes can see what’s there, so too the Buddha has made the teaching clear in many ways.

## dn9:55.4

- **EN**: I go for refuge to the Buddha, to the teaching, and to the mendicant Saṅgha.

## dn9:55.5
- **PLI**: Labheyyāhaṁ, bhante, bhagavato santike pabbajjaṁ, labheyyaṁ upasampadan”ti.
- **EN**: Sir, may I receive the going forth, the ordination in the Buddha’s presence?”

## dn9:56.1  `dr9.268, km15.159, mc9.196, ms6D_767, msdiv443, pts-cs56, pts-vp-pli1.203`
- **PLI**: Alattha kho citto hatthisāriputto bhagavato santike pabbajjaṁ, alattha upasampadaṁ.
- **EN**: And Citta Hatthisāriputta received the going forth, the ordination in the Buddha’s presence.

## dn9:56.2
- **PLI**: Acirūpasampanno kho panāyasmā citto hatthisāriputto eko vūpakaṭṭho appamatto ātāpī pahitatto viharanto na cirasseva—yassatthāya kulaputtā sammadeva agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajanti, tadanuttaraṁ—brahmacariyapariyosānaṁ diṭṭheva dhamme sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja vihāsi.
- **EN**: Not long after his ordination, Venerable Citta Hatthisāriputta, living alone, withdrawn, diligent, keen, and resolute, soon realized the supreme culmination of the spiritual path in this very life. He lived having achieved with his own insight the goal for which gentlemen rightly go forth from the lay life to homelessness.
- **NOTE**: In <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an6.60/en/sujato'>AN 6.60</a> we find Citta Hatthisāriputta, still a somewhat junior monk, rudely interrupting his seniors. After admonition he disrobed, but he ordained again and later achieved arahantship. We can reconcile these two accounts by recognizing that the phrase <i lang='pi' translate='no'>acira</i> “not long after” is a conventional term, which might be several years. Thus the events of AN 6.60 occurred some time between his ordination and awakening.

## dn9:56.3

- **EN**: He understood: “Rebirth is ended; the spiritual journey has been completed; what had to be done has been done; there is nothing further for this place.”

## dn9:56.4

- **EN**: And Venerable Citta Hatthisāriputta became one of the perfected.
