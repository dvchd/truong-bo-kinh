# Muc 39 — Three Kinds of Incarnation  (121 đoạn)

## dn9:39.0
- **PLI**: 2.2. Tayoattapaṭilābha
- **EN**: 2.2. Three Kinds of Incarnation

## dn9:39.1  `mc9.190, ms6D_745, msdiv428, pts-cs39`
- **PLI**: “Tayo kho me, poṭṭhapāda, attapaṭilābhā—
- **EN**: “Poṭṭhapāda, there are these three kinds of incarnation:
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Attapaṭilābha</i> is literally “re-acquisition of self”, where <i lang='pi' translate='no'>attā</i> is explained by the commentary as <i lang='pi' translate='no'>attabhāva</i>, the “state of the self” or “life-form” that is acquired or “incarnated” at rebirth, i.e. the body (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>sarīra</i>), whether material or immaterial. Here we see an echo of the old use of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>ātman</i> as “body”, although it is applied even to the formless realms.

## dn9:39.2
- **PLI**: oḷāriko attapaṭilābho, manomayo attapaṭilābho, arūpo attapaṭilābho.
- **EN**: a solid incarnation, a mind-made incarnation, and a formless incarnation.
- **NOTE**: These recap the three theses of perception and the self posted by Poṭṭhapāda from <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn9/en/sujato#22.1'>DN 9:22.1</a>.

## dn9:39.3
- **PLI**: Katamo ca, poṭṭhapāda, oḷāriko attapaṭilābho?
- **EN**: And what is a solid incarnation?

## dn9:39.4
- **PLI**: Rūpī cātumahābhūtiko kabaḷīkārāhārabhakkho, ayaṁ oḷāriko attapaṭilābho.
- **EN**: It is formed, made up of the four principal states, and consumes edible food.

## dn9:39.5
- **PLI**: Katamo manomayo attapaṭilābho?
- **EN**: What is a mind-made incarnation?

## dn9:39.6
- **PLI**: Rūpī manomayo sabbaṅgapaccaṅgī ahīnindriyo, ayaṁ manomayo attapaṭilābho.
- **EN**: It is formed, mind-made, whole in its major and minor limbs, not deficient in any faculty.

## dn9:39.7
- **PLI**: Katamo arūpo attapaṭilābho?
- **EN**: What is a formless incarnation?

## dn9:39.8
- **PLI**: Arūpī saññāmayo, ayaṁ arūpo attapaṭilābho.
- **EN**: It is formless, made of perception.
