## dn9:32.1  `cck9.236, csp1ed6.176, csp2ed6.176, dr9.252, km15.131, km15.132, mc9.185, ms6D_720, msdiv422, pts-cs32, pts-vp-pli1.191, sya9.235, vri1.169`
- **PLI**: Atha kho dvīhatīhassa accayena citto ca hatthisāriputto poṭṭhapādo ca paribbājako yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkamiṁsu; upasaṅkamitvā citto hatthisāriputto bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi.
- **EN**: Then after two or three days had passed, Citta Hatthisāriputta and Poṭṭhapāda went to the Buddha. Citta Hatthisāriputta bowed and sat down to one side.
- **NOTE**: The commentary says Citta was the son of an elephant trainer. Here he shows greater respect to the Buddha than does Poṭṭhapāda.

## dn9:32.2

- **EN**: But the wanderer Poṭṭhapāda exchanged greetings with the Buddha, and when the greetings and polite conversation were over, he sat down to one side.

## dn9:33.1  `bj7.418, km15.133, ms6D_721, msdiv423, ndp6.159, pts-cs33, sya9.236`
- **PLI**: “Sabbeva kho ete, poṭṭhapāda, paribbājakā andhā acakkhukā;
- **EN**: “All those wanderers, Poṭṭhapāda, are blind and sightless.

## dn9:33.2
- **PLI**: tvaṁyeva nesaṁ eko cakkhumā.
- **EN**: You are the only one whose eyes are clear.

## dn9:33.3
- **PLI**: Ekaṁsikāpi hi kho, poṭṭhapāda, mayā dhammā desitā paññattā;
- **EN**: For I have taught and pointed out teachings that are categorical
- **NOTE**: This point seems to be lost on a number of modern commentators, who infer from passages such as the ten undeclared points that the Buddha refused to make any definitive declarations at all. The Buddha, rather, was a <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vibhajjavādin</i> (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn99/en/sujato#4.4'>MN 99:4.4</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an10.94/en/sujato#4.7'>AN 10.94:4.7</a>), “one who speaks after analysis”.

## dn9:33.4
- **PLI**: anekaṁsikāpi hi kho, poṭṭhapāda, mayā dhammā desitā paññattā.
- **EN**: and also teachings that are not categorical.

## dn9:33.16  `km15.135, mc9.186, ms6D_723`
- **PLI**: Kasmā ca te, poṭṭhapāda, mayā anekaṁsikā dhammā desitā paññattā?
- **EN**: And why have I taught and pointed out such teachings as not categorical?

## dn9:33.17
- **PLI**: Na hete, poṭṭhapāda, atthasaṁhitā na dhammasaṁhitā na ādibrahmacariyakā na nibbidāya na virāgāya na nirodhāya na upasamāya na abhiññāya na sambodhāya na nibbānāya saṁvattanti.
- **EN**: Because those things aren’t beneficial or relevant to the fundamentals of the spiritual life. They don’t lead to disillusionment, dispassion, cessation, peace, insight, awakening, and extinguishment.

## dn9:33.18
- **PLI**: Tasmā te mayā anekaṁsikā dhammā desitā paññattā.
- **EN**: That’s why I have taught and pointed out such teachings as not categorical.
