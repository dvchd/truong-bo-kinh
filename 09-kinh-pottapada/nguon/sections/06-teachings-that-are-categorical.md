- **NOTE**: “No demonstrable basis” renders <i lang='pi' translate='no'>appāṭihīrakataṁ</i>. The Vedic <i lang='pi' translate='no'>pratiharate</i> means “to yearn for, to welcome, to delight in”, from which <i lang='pi' translate='no'>pāṭihāriya</i> means “that which should be delighted in, an amusement, an entertainment, an attraction”. In early Pali, the primary sense is something demonstrated or displayed that is convincing or inspiring. In later Pali this narrows to “miracle”.
