# Mục 5 — Ajātasattu xin quy y  (22 đoạn)

## dn2:99.0
- **PLI**: 5. Ajātasattuupāsakattapaṭivedanā
- **EN**: 5. Ajātasattu Declares Himself a Lay Follower

## dn2:99.1  `km14.212, lv1.232, lv1.233, ms6D_341, msdiv250, ndp6.75, pts-cs99, vri1.75`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ vutte, rājā māgadho ajātasattu vedehiputto bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: When the Buddha had spoken, King Ajātasattu said to him,

## dn2:99.2
- **PLI**: “abhikkantaṁ, bhante, abhikkantaṁ, bhante.
- **EN**: “Excellent, sir! Excellent!
- **NOTE**: The king’s distress has been alleviated by the Buddha’s uplifting words.

## dn2:99.3
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, bhante, nikkujjitaṁ vā ukkujjeyya, paṭicchannaṁ vā vivareyya, mūḷhassa vā maggaṁ ācikkheyya, andhakāre vā telapajjotaṁ dhāreyya: ‘cakkhumanto rūpāni dakkhantī’ti; evamevaṁ, bhante, bhagavatā anekapariyāyena dhammo pakāsito.
- **EN**: As if he were righting the overturned, or revealing the hidden, or pointing out the path to the lost, or lighting a lamp in the dark so people with clear eyes can see what’s there, the Buddha has made the teaching clear in many ways.

## dn2:99.4
- **PLI**: Esāhaṁ, bhante, bhagavantaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi dhammañca bhikkhusaṅghañca.
- **EN**: I go for refuge to the Buddha, to the teaching, and to the mendicant Saṅgha.
- **NOTE**: This is the standard form in which lay people went for refuge. It is not something that the Buddha required, but a spontaneous act of inspiration. Conventionally, it indicates that someone is a “Buddhist”. Today Theravadins recite the going for refuge thrice, but in the early texts this is found only as the ordination for novices (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#12.4.1'>Kd 1:12.4.1</a>).

## dn2:99.5
- **PLI**: Upāsakaṁ maṁ bhagavā dhāretu ajjatagge pāṇupetaṁ saraṇaṁ gataṁ.
- **EN**: From this day forth, may the Buddha remember me as a lay follower who has gone for refuge for life.

## dn2:99.6
- **PLI**: Accayo maṁ, bhante, accagamā yathābālaṁ yathāmūḷhaṁ yathāakusalaṁ, yohaṁ pitaraṁ dhammikaṁ dhammarājānaṁ issariyakāraṇā jīvitā voropesiṁ.
- **EN**: I made a mistake, sir. It was foolish, stupid, and unskillful of me to take the life of my father, a just and principled king, for the sake of authority.
- **NOTE**: The king, unprompted, makes an astonishing confession. To say it in such a public forum, before a spiritual community and his own retinue, displays courage and integrity.

## dn2:99.7
- **PLI**: Tassa me, bhante bhagavā, accayaṁ accayato paṭiggaṇhātu āyatiṁ saṁvarāyā”ti.
- **EN**: Please, sir, accept my mistake for what it is, so I will restrain myself in future.”
- **NOTE**: The Buddha hears his confession, but it is Ajātasattu’s responsibility to do better.

## dn2:100.1  `bj7.148, cck9.111, dr9.113, km14.213, mc9.86, ms6D_342, msdiv251, pts-cs100, sya9.113`
- **PLI**: “Taggha tvaṁ, mahārāja, accayo accagamā yathābālaṁ yathāmūḷhaṁ yathāakusalaṁ, yaṁ tvaṁ pitaraṁ dhammikaṁ dhammarājānaṁ jīvitā voropesi.
- **EN**: “Indeed, Great King, you made a mistake. It was foolish, stupid, and unskillful of you to take the life of your father, a just and principled king, for the sake of sovereignty.
- **NOTE**: Even before such a dangerous and emotionally volatile king, the Buddha does not mince words. The Buddha makes a point to acknowledge what the king had done, without dismissing it and thereby discounting the inner work he had achieved to get to this point.

## dn2:100.2
- **PLI**: Yato ca kho tvaṁ, mahārāja, accayaṁ accayato disvā yathādhammaṁ paṭikarosi, taṁ te mayaṁ paṭiggaṇhāma.
- **EN**: But since you have recognized your mistake for what it is, and have dealt with it properly, I accept it.

## dn2:100.3
- **PLI**: Vuddhihesā, mahārāja, ariyassa vinaye, yo accayaṁ accayato disvā yathādhammaṁ paṭikaroti, āyatiṁ saṁvaraṁ āpajjatī”ti.
- **EN**: For it is growth in the training of the Noble One to recognize a mistake for what it is, deal with it properly, and commit to restraint in the future.”
- **NOTE**: Confession does not erase the past, but it does set a better course for the future. This confession is similar to that done by monastics when they have broken Vinaya rules.

## dn2:101.1  `csp1ed6.81, csp2ed6.81, lv1.234, ms6D_343, msdiv252, pts-cs101`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ vutte, rājā māgadho ajātasattu vedehiputto bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: When the Buddha had spoken, King Ajātasattu said to him,

## dn2:101.2
- **PLI**: “handa ca dāni mayaṁ, bhante, gacchāma bahukiccā mayaṁ bahukaraṇīyā”ti.
- **EN**: “Well, now, sir, I must go. I have many duties, and much to do.”

## dn2:101.3
- **PLI**: “Yassadāni tvaṁ, mahārāja, kālaṁ maññasī”ti.
- **EN**: “Please, Great King, go at your convenience.”

## dn2:101.4
- **PLI**: Atha kho rājā māgadho ajātasattu vedehiputto bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinanditvā anumoditvā uṭṭhāyāsanā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā padakkhiṇaṁ katvā pakkāmi.
- **EN**: Then the king, having approved and agreed with what the Buddha said, rose from his seat, bowed, and respectfully circled him, keeping him on his right, before leaving.

## dn2:102.1  `km14.214, lv1.235, ms6D_344, msdiv253, pts-cs102, pts-vp-pli1.86`
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā acirapakkantassa rañño māgadhassa ajātasattussa vedehiputtassa bhikkhū āmantesi:
- **EN**: Soon after the king had left, the Buddha addressed the mendicants:

## dn2:102.2
- **PLI**: “khatāyaṁ, bhikkhave, rājā.
- **EN**: “The king is broken, mendicants,
- **NOTE**: These terms are commonly used in the context of keeping precepts (eg. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an3.50/en/sujato#4.1'>AN 3.50:4.1</a>). The Buddha is supportive when he speaks with Ajātasattu, but does not hide the severity of his crime.

## dn2:102.3
- **PLI**: Upahatāyaṁ, bhikkhave, rājā.
- **EN**: he is ruined.

## dn2:102.4
- **PLI**: Sacāyaṁ, bhikkhave, rājā pitaraṁ dhammikaṁ dhammarājānaṁ jīvitā na voropessatha, imasmiññeva āsane virajaṁ vītamalaṁ dhammacakkhuṁ uppajjissathā”ti.
- **EN**: If he had not taken the life of his father, a just and principled king, the stainless, immaculate vision of the Dhamma would have arisen in him in that very seat.”
- **NOTE**: The killing of one’s father is one of five “incurable” acts that doom a person to hell in the next life (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/an5.129/en/sujato#1.3'>AN 5.129:1.3</a>). If he had not done so, he would have become a stream-enterer.

## dn2:102.6
- **PLI**: Attamanā te bhikkhū bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti.
- **EN**: Satisfied, the mendicants approved what the Buddha said.
