## dn2:32.1  `bj7.102, cck9.75, csp1ed6.55, csp2ed6.55, dr9.76, km14.144, km14.145, lv1.152, lv1.153, mc9.59, ms6D_259, msdiv180, pts-cs32, pts-vp-pli1.59, sya9.77, vri1.52`

## dn2:32.2

- **EN**: ‘Suppose you were to ask me whether there is another world. If I believed that to be the case, I would say so.

## dn2:32.3
- **PLI**: Evantipi me no, tathātipi me no, aññathātipi me no, notipi me no, no notipi me no.
- **EN**: But I don’t say it’s like this. I don’t say it’s like that. I don’t say it’s otherwise. I don’t say it’s not so. And I don’t deny it’s not so.
- **NOTE**: This places him among the “endless flip-floppers” of <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn1/en/sujato#2.23.1'>DN 1:2.23.1</a>. However, we do not know on which of the four grounds he justified his evasiveness.
