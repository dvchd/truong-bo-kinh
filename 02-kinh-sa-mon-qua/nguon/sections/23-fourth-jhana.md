# 4.3.2.8 — Tứ thiền  (6 đoạn)

## dn2:81.0
- **PLI**: 4.3.2.8. Catutthajhāna
- **EN**: 4.3.2.8. Fourth Absorption

## dn2:81.1  `lv1.205, ms6D_323, msdiv232, ndp6.67, pts-cs81, pts-vp-pli1.76`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, mahārāja, bhikkhu sukhassa ca pahānā dukkhassa ca pahānā, pubbeva somanassadomanassānaṁ atthaṅgamā adukkhamasukhaṁ upekkhāsatipārisuddhiṁ catutthaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja viharati.
- **EN**: Furthermore, with the giving up of pleasure and pain and the disappearance of former happiness and sadness, a mendicant enters and remains in the fourth absorption, without pleasure or pain, with pure equanimity and mindfulness.
- **NOTE**: The emotional poise of equanimity leads to the feeling of pleasure settling into the more subtle neutral feeling. Pain and sadness have been abandoned long before, but are emphasized here as they are subtle counterpart of pleasure.

## dn2:81.2
- **PLI**: So imameva kāyaṁ parisuddhena cetasā pariyodātena pharitvā nisinno hoti, nāssa kiñci sabbāvato kāyassa parisuddhena cetasā pariyodātena apphuṭaṁ hoti.
- **EN**: They sit spreading their body through with pure bright mind. There’s no part of the body that’s not spread with pure bright mind.
- **NOTE**: The equanimity of the fourth <i lang='pi' translate='no'>jhāna</i> is not dullness and indifference, but a brilliant and radiant awareness.

## dn2:82.1  `cck9.98, csp1ed6.72, csp2ed6.72, km14.190, lv1.206, mc9.77, ms6D_324, msdiv233, pts-cs82, sya9.101`
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, mahārāja, puriso odātena vatthena sasīsaṁ pārupitvā nisinno assa, nāssa kiñci sabbāvato kāyassa odātena vatthena apphuṭaṁ assa;
- **EN**: It’s like someone sitting wrapped from head to foot with white cloth. There’s no part of the body that’s not spread over with white cloth.
- **NOTE**: The white cloth is the purity and brightness of equanimity. The commentary explains this as a person who has just got out of a bath and sits perfectly dry and content.

## dn2:82.2
- **PLI**: evameva kho, mahārāja, bhikkhu imameva kāyaṁ parisuddhena cetasā pariyodātena pharitvā nisinno hoti, nāssa kiñci sabbāvato kāyassa parisuddhena cetasā pariyodātena apphuṭaṁ hoti.
- **EN**: In the same way, they sit spreading their body through with pure bright mind. There’s no part of the body that’s not spread with pure bright mind.

## dn2:82.3
- **PLI**: Idampi kho, mahārāja, sandiṭṭhikaṁ sāmaññaphalaṁ purimehi sandiṭṭhikehi sāmaññaphalehi abhikkantatarañca paṇītatarañca.
- **EN**: This too, Great King, is a fruit of the ascetic life that’s apparent in the present life which is better and finer than the former ones.
