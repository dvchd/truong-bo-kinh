# 4.1 — Quả thứ nhất của đời sống sa-môn  (33 đoạn)

## dn2:34.0.1
- **PLI**: 4. Sāmaññaphala
- **EN**: 4. The Fruits of the Ascetic Life

## dn2:34.0.2
- **PLI**: 4.1. Paṭhamasandiṭṭhikasāmaññaphala
- **EN**: 4.1. The First Fruit of the Ascetic Life

## dn2:34.1  `bj7.104, cck9.76, csp1ed6.56, csp2ed6.56, km14.147, km14.148, lv1.156, mc9.60, ms6D_261, msdiv182, pts-cs34, pts-vp-pli1.60`
- **PLI**: Sohaṁ, bhante, bhagavantampi pucchāmi:
- **EN**: And so I ask the Buddha:

## dn2:34.2
- **PLI**: ‘yathā nu kho imāni, bhante, puthusippāyatanāni
- **EN**: Sir, there are many different professional fields.

## dn2:34.3
- **PLI**: seyyathidaṁ—hatthārohā assārohā rathikā dhanuggahā celakā calakā piṇḍadāyakā uggā rājaputtā pakkhandino mahānāgā sūrā cammayodhino dāsikaputtā
- **EN**: These include elephant marshals, cavalry, charioteers, archers, bannermen, adjutants, food servers, warrior-chiefs, princes, chargers, great warriors, heroes, leather-clad soldiers, and sons of bondservants.

## dn2:34.4
- **PLI**: āḷārikā kappakā nhāpakā sūdā mālakārā rajakā pesakārā naḷakārā kumbhakārā gaṇakā muddikā, yāni vā panaññānipi evaṅgatāni puthusippāyatanāni, te diṭṭheva dhamme sandiṭṭhikaṁ sippaphalaṁ upajīvanti,
- **EN**: They also include bakers, barbers, bathroom attendants, cooks, garland-makers, dyers, embroiderers, basket-makers, potters, accountants, finger-talliers, or those following any similar professions. All these live off the fruits of their profession which are apparent in this very life.

## dn2:34.5
- **PLI**: te tena attānaṁ sukhenti pīṇenti, mātāpitaro sukhenti pīṇenti, puttadāraṁ sukhenti pīṇenti, mittāmacce sukhenti pīṇenti, samaṇabrāhmaṇesu uddhaggikaṁ dakkhiṇaṁ patiṭṭhapenti sovaggikaṁ sukhavipākaṁ saggasaṁvattanikaṁ.
- **EN**: With that they make themselves happy and pleased. They make their parents, their children and partners, and their friends and colleagues happy and pleased. And they establish an uplifting religious donation for ascetics and brahmins that’s conducive to heaven, ripens in happiness, and leads to heaven.

## dn2:34.6
- **PLI**: Sakkā nu kho, bhante, evameva diṭṭheva dhamme sandiṭṭhikaṁ sāmaññaphalaṁ paññapetun’”ti?
- **EN**: Sir, can you point out a fruit of the ascetic life that’s likewise apparent in this very life?”

## dn2:34.7  `cck9.77, dr9.78, km14.149, lv1.157, lv1.158, ms6D_262, msdiv183, sya9.79, vri1.53`
- **PLI**: “Sakkā, mahārāja.
- **EN**: “I can, Great King.
- **NOTE**: The Buddha answers directly, with confidence. This whole passage is a masterclass in effective dialogue.

## dn2:34.8
- **PLI**: Tena hi, mahārāja, taññevettha paṭipucchissāmi. Yathā te khameyya, tathā naṁ byākareyyāsi.
- **EN**: Well then, I’ll ask you about this in return, and you can answer as you like.
- **NOTE**: He engages Ajātasattu rather than lecturing him.

## dn2:35.1  `pts-cs35`
- **PLI**: Taṁ kiṁ maññasi, mahārāja,
- **EN**: What do you think, Great King?

## dn2:35.2
- **PLI**: idha te assa puriso dāso kammakāro pubbuṭṭhāyī pacchānipātī kiṅkārapaṭissāvī manāpacārī piyavādī mukhullokako.
- **EN**: Suppose you had a person who was a bondservant, a worker. They get up before you and go to bed after you, and are obliging, behaving nicely and speaking politely, and gazing up at your face.
- **NOTE**: See <i lang='pi' translate='no'>mukhaṁ ullokentī</i> at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn79/en/sujato'>MN 79</a> and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn56.39/en/sujato'>SN 56.39</a>.

## dn2:35.3
- **PLI**: Tassa evamassa:
- **EN**: They’d think:

## dn2:35.4
- **PLI**: ‘acchariyaṁ, vata bho, abbhutaṁ, vata bho, puññānaṁ gati, puññānaṁ vipāko.
- **EN**: ‘The outcome and result of good deeds is just so incredible, so amazing!
- **NOTE**: Even a servant believed in the doctrine of kamma.

## dn2:35.5
- **PLI**: Ayañhi rājā māgadho ajātasattu vedehiputto manusso; ahampi manusso.
- **EN**: For this King Ajātasattu is a human being, and so am I.
- **NOTE**: There is no question of the divinity of kings.

## dn2:35.6
- **PLI**: Ayañhi rājā māgadho ajātasattu vedehiputto pañcahi kāmaguṇehi samappito samaṅgībhūto paricāreti, devo maññe.
- **EN**: Yet he amuses himself, supplied and provided with the five kinds of sensual stimulation as if he were a god.

## dn2:35.7
- **PLI**: Ahaṁ panamhissa dāso kammakāro pubbuṭṭhāyī pacchānipātī kiṅkārapaṭissāvī manāpacārī piyavādī mukhullokako.
- **EN**: Whereas I’m his bondservant, his worker. I get up before him and go to bed after him, and am obliging, behaving nicely and speaking politely, and gazing up at his face.

## dn2:35.8
- **PLI**: So vatassāhaṁ puññāni kareyyaṁ.
- **EN**: I really should do good deeds.
- **NOTE**: The doctrine of kamma leads to living a better life, not stewing in resentment.

## dn2:35.9
- **PLI**: Yannūnāhaṁ kesamassuṁ ohāretvā kāsāyāni vatthāni acchādetvā agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajeyyan’ti.
- **EN**: Why don’t I shave off my hair and beard, dress in ocher robes, and go forth from the lay life to homelessness?’
- **NOTE**: <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#47.1.1'>Kd 1:47.1.1</a> penalizes the ordination of bondservants or slaves, despite the fact that Ajātasattu’s father, Bimbisāra, had ordered that no action was to be taken against any bondservant who ordained under the Buddha.

## dn2:35.10  `csp1ed6.57, csp2ed6.57, km14.150, lv1.159, mc9.61, ms6D_263, ndp6.53`
- **PLI**: So aparena samayena kesamassuṁ ohāretvā kāsāyāni vatthāni acchādetvā agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajeyya.
- **EN**: After some time, that is what they do.

## dn2:35.11
- **PLI**: So evaṁ pabbajito samāno kāyena saṁvuto vihareyya, vācāya saṁvuto vihareyya, manasā saṁvuto vihareyya, ghāsacchādanaparamatāya santuṭṭho, abhirato paviveke.
- **EN**: Having gone forth they’d live restrained in body, speech, and mind, living content with nothing more than food and clothes, delighting in seclusion.
- **NOTE**: Here the Buddha foreshadows the larger themes detailed later.

## dn2:35.12
- **PLI**: Tañce te purisā evamāroceyyuṁ:
- **EN**: And suppose your men were to report all this to you.

## dn2:35.13
- **PLI**: ‘yagghe, deva, jāneyyāsi, yo te so puriso dāso kammakāro pubbuṭṭhāyī pacchānipātī kiṅkārapaṭissāvī manāpacārī piyavādī mukhullokako;
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

- **PLI**: so, deva, kesamassuṁ ohāretvā kāsāyāni vatthāni acchādetvā agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajito.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn2:35.15
- **PLI**: So evaṁ pabbajito samāno kāyena saṁvuto viharati, vācāya saṁvuto viharati, manasā saṁvuto viharati, ghāsacchādanaparamatāya santuṭṭho, abhirato paviveke’ti.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn2:35.16
- **PLI**: Api nu tvaṁ evaṁ vadeyyāsi:
- **EN**: Would you say to them:

## dn2:35.17
- **PLI**: ‘etu me, bho, so puriso, punadeva hotu dāso kammakāro pubbuṭṭhāyī pacchānipātī kiṅkārapaṭissāvī manāpacārī piyavādī mukhullokako’”ti?
- **EN**: ‘Bring that person to me! Let them once more be my bondservant, my worker’?”

## dn2:36.1  `bj7.106, cck9.78, dr9.79, km14.151, lv1.160, ms6D_264, msdiv184, pts-cs36, pts-vp-pli1.61, sya9.80`
- **PLI**: “No hetaṁ, bhante.
- **EN**: “No, sir.

## dn2:36.2
- **PLI**: Atha kho naṁ mayameva abhivādeyyāmapi, paccuṭṭheyyāmapi, āsanenapi nimanteyyāma, abhinimanteyyāmapi naṁ cīvarapiṇḍapātasenāsanagilānappaccayabhesajjaparikkhārehi, dhammikampissa rakkhāvaraṇaguttiṁ saṁvidaheyyāmā”ti.
- **EN**: Rather, I would bow to them, rise in their presence, and offer them a seat. I’d invite them to accept robes, almsfood, lodgings, and medicines and supplies for the sick. And I’d organize their lawful guarding and protection.”
- **NOTE**: Even under a king so compromised as Ajātasattu, a runaway slave who has ordained is rewarded not punished.

## dn2:36.3  `ms6D_265, msdiv185`
- **PLI**: “Taṁ kiṁ maññasi, mahārāja,
- **EN**: “What do you think, Great King?

## dn2:36.4
- **PLI**: yadi evaṁ sante hoti vā sandiṭṭhikaṁ sāmaññaphalaṁ no vā”ti?
- **EN**: If this is so, is there a fruit of the ascetic life apparent in the present life or not?”
- **NOTE**: In contrast with the former teachers, the Buddha gives a clear answer in terms that Ajātasattu would understand.

## dn2:36.5
- **PLI**: “Addhā kho, bhante, evaṁ sante hoti sandiṭṭhikaṁ sāmaññaphalan”ti.
- **EN**: “Clearly, sir, there is.”
- **NOTE**: The Buddha establishes common ground with the king before venturing into deeper waters.

## dn2:36.6
- **PLI**: “Idaṁ kho te, mahārāja, mayā paṭhamaṁ diṭṭheva dhamme sandiṭṭhikaṁ sāmaññaphalaṁ paññattan”ti.
- **EN**: “This is the first fruit of the ascetic life that’s apparent in this very life, which I point out to you.”
