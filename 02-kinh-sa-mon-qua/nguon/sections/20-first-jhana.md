# 4.3.2.5 — Sơ thiền  (6 đoạn)

## dn2:75.0
- **PLI**: 4.3.2.5. Paṭhamajhāna
- **EN**: 4.3.2.5. First Absorption

## dn2:75.1  `csp1ed6.70, csp2ed6.70, ms6D_317, msdiv226`
- **PLI**: So vivicceva kāmehi, vivicca akusalehi dhammehi savitakkaṁ savicāraṁ vivekajaṁ pītisukhaṁ paṭhamaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja viharati.
- **EN**: Quite secluded from sensual pleasures, secluded from unskillful qualities, they enter and remain in the first absorption, which has the rapture and bliss born of seclusion, while placing the mind and keeping it connected.
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Jhāna</i> is a state of “elevated consciousness” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>adhicitta</i>), so all the terms have an elevated sense. | The plural form indicates that “sensual pleasures” includes sense experience, which the meditator can turn away from since they no longer have any desire for it. | The “unskillful qualities” are the five hindrances. | The “rapture and bliss born of seclusion” is the happiness of abandoning the hindrances and freedom from sense impingement. | “Placing the mind and keeping it connected” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>vitakka</i>, <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vicāra</i>) uses terms that mean “thought” in coarse consciousness, but which in “elevated consciousness” refer to the subtle function of applying the mind to the meditation.

## dn2:75.2
- **PLI**: So imameva kāyaṁ vivekajena pītisukhena abhisandeti parisandeti paripūreti parippharati, nāssa kiñci sabbāvato kāyassa vivekajena pītisukhena apphuṭaṁ hoti.
- **EN**: They drench, steep, fill, and spread themselves with rapture and bliss born of seclusion. There’s no part of the body that’s not spread with rapture and bliss born of seclusion.
- **NOTE**: As a meditator proceeds, their subjective experience of the “body” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>kāya</i>) evolves from tactile sense impressions in the “coarse body” of matter (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>olarikakāya</i>), to the settling of the breath and physical energies in the body and mind (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>kāyapassaddhi</i>), to the interior mental experience of bliss and light of the “subtle body” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>sukhumakāya</i>) or “mind-made body” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>manomayakāya</i>), to being a “body witness” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>kāyasakkhī</i>) of the formless attainments (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn70/en/sujato#17.1'>MN 70:17.1</a>), to the direct realization of the highest truth of Nibbana “with the body” (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn70/en/sujato#23.2'>MN 70:23.2</a>: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>kāyena ceva paramasaccaṁ sacchikaroti</i>). Along the way, the sense of “physical body” recedes and <i lang='pi' translate='no'>kāya</i> refers to direct personal experience.

## dn2:76.1  `dr9.98, km14.186, lv1.200, mc9.75, ms6D_318, msdiv227, pts-cs76, pts-vp-pli1.74`
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, mahārāja, dakkho nhāpako vā nhāpakantevāsī vā kaṁsathāle nhānīyacuṇṇāni ākiritvā udakena paripphosakaṁ paripphosakaṁ sanneyya, sāyaṁ nhānīyapiṇḍi snehānugatā snehaparetā santarabāhirā phuṭā snehena, na ca paggharaṇī;
- **EN**: It’s like when a deft bathroom attendant or their apprentice pours bath powder into a bronze dish, sprinkling it little by little with water. They knead it until the ball of bath powder is soaked and saturated with moisture, spread through inside and out; yet no moisture oozes out.
- **NOTE**: The kneading is the “placing the mind and keeping it connected”, the water is bliss, while the lack of leaking speaks to the contained interiority of the experience. | Here as elsewhere, water is used as a metaphor for the mind in absorption. Compare Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad 4.3.32: “He becomes like water, one, the seer without duality; this is the world of Brahmā.”

## dn2:76.2
- **PLI**: evameva kho, mahārāja, bhikkhu imameva kāyaṁ vivekajena pītisukhena abhisandeti parisandeti paripūreti parippharati, nāssa kiñci sabbāvato kāyassa vivekajena pītisukhena apphuṭaṁ hoti.
- **EN**: In the same way, a mendicant drenches, steeps, fills, and spreads themselves with rapture and bliss born of seclusion. There’s no part of the body that’s not spread with rapture and bliss born of seclusion.

## dn2:76.3
- **PLI**: Idampi kho, mahārāja, sandiṭṭhikaṁ sāmaññaphalaṁ purimehi sandiṭṭhikehi sāmaññaphalehi abhikkantatarañca paṇītatarañca.
