## dn2:17.1  `cck9.66, dr9.67, km14.128, lv1.134, mc9.53, ms6D_244, msdiv166, ndp6.46, pts-cs17, pts-vp-pli1.53, vri1.47`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ vutte, bhante, pūraṇo kassapo maṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: He said to me:

## dn2:17.2
- **PLI**: ‘karoto kho, mahārāja, kārayato, chindato chedāpayato, pacato pācāpayato socayato, socāpayato, kilamato kilamāpayato, phandato phandāpayato, pāṇamatipātāpayato, adinnaṁ ādiyato, sandhiṁ chindato, nillopaṁ harato, ekāgārikaṁ karoto, paripanthe tiṭṭhato, paradāraṁ gacchato, musā bhaṇato, karoto na karīyati pāpaṁ. Khurapariyantena cepi cakkena yo imissā pathaviyā pāṇe ekaṁ maṁsakhalaṁ ekaṁ maṁsapuñjaṁ kareyya, natthi tatonidānaṁ pāpaṁ, natthi pāpassa āgamo.
- **EN**: ‘Great king, the one who acts does nothing wrong when they punish, mutilate, torture, aggrieve, oppress, intimidate, or when they encourage others to do the same. They do nothing wrong when they kill, steal, break into houses, plunder wealth, steal from isolated buildings, commit highway robbery, commit adultery, and lie. If you were to reduce all the living creatures of this earth to one heap and mass of flesh with a razor-edged chakram, no evil comes of that, and no outcome of evil.
- **NOTE**: This is a denial of the doctrine of kamma. While his doctrine appears to be morally nihilistic, it seems unlikely this was Pūraṇa Kassapa’s full teaching. He may have subscribed to hard determinism, so that we have no choice in what we do. He may also have believed that we should keep moral rules as a social contract, but that this had no effect on the afterlife. | In such contexts, <i lang='pi' translate='no'>kar-</i> means “punish, inflict” (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn129/en/sujato#29.2'>MN 129:29.2</a>).

## dn2:17.3
- **PLI**: Dakkhiṇañcepi gaṅgāya tīraṁ gaccheyya hananto ghātento chindanto chedāpento pacanto pācāpento, natthi tatonidānaṁ pāpaṁ, natthi pāpassa āgamo.
- **EN**: If you were to go along the south bank of the Ganges killing, mutilating, and torturing, and encouraging others to do the same, no evil comes of that, and no outcome of evil.

## dn2:17.4
- **PLI**: Uttarañcepi gaṅgāya tīraṁ gaccheyya dadanto dāpento yajanto yajāpento, natthi tatonidānaṁ puññaṁ, natthi puññassa āgamo.
- **EN**: If you were to go along the north bank of the Ganges giving and sacrificing and encouraging others to do the same, no merit comes of that, and no outcome of merit.

## dn2:17.5
- **PLI**: Dānena damena saṁyamena saccavajjena natthi puññaṁ, natthi puññassa āgamo’ti.
- **EN**: In giving, self-control, restraint, and truthfulness there is no merit or outcome of merit.’

## dn2:18.1  `bj7.92, csp1ed6.50, csp2ed6.50, dr9.68, km14.129, lv1.135, ms6D_245, pts-cs18, sya9.69`
- **PLI**: Itthaṁ kho me, bhante, pūraṇo kassapo sandiṭṭhikaṁ sāmaññaphalaṁ puṭṭho samāno akiriyaṁ byākāsi.
- **EN**: And so, when I asked Pūraṇa Kassapa about the fruits of the ascetic life apparent in the present life, he answered with the doctrine of inaction.
- **NOTE**: The unsatisfying nature of the answers given by these teachers is also emphasized at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn36/en/sujato#48.4'>MN 36:48.4</a>.

## dn2:18.2
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, bhante, ambaṁ vā puṭṭho labujaṁ byākareyya, labujaṁ vā puṭṭho ambaṁ byākareyya;
- **EN**: It was like someone who, when asked about a mango, answered with a breadfruit, or when asked about a breadfruit, answered with a mango.
- **NOTE**: Breadfruit is a starchy, fibrous fruit that is, needless to say, very different from a mango.

## dn2:18.4
- **PLI**: Tassa mayhaṁ, bhante, etadahosi:
- **EN**: I thought:

## dn2:18.5
- **PLI**: ‘kathañhi nāma mādiso samaṇaṁ vā brāhmaṇaṁ vā vijite vasantaṁ apasādetabbaṁ maññeyyā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘How on earth could one such as I presume to rebuke an ascetic or brahmin living in my realm?’
- **NOTE**: Kings had a duty to protect all religions in their realm, even those with such extreme views.

## dn2:18.6
- **PLI**: So kho ahaṁ, bhante, pūraṇassa kassapassa bhāsitaṁ neva abhinandiṁ nappaṭikkosiṁ.
- **EN**: So I neither approved nor rejected that statement of Pūraṇa Kassapa.

## dn2:18.7
- **PLI**: Anabhinanditvā appaṭikkositvā anattamano, anattamanavācaṁ anicchāretvā, tameva vācaṁ anuggaṇhanto anikkujjanto uṭṭhāyāsanā pakkamiṁ.
- **EN**: I was displeased, but did not express my displeasure. Neither accepting what he said nor contradicting it, I rose from my seat and left.
- **NOTE**: The commentary takes <i lang='pi' translate='no'>uggahita</i> and <i lang='pi' translate='no'>nikkujjita</i> as synonyms. Elsewhere, however, <i lang='pi' translate='no'>nikkujjati</i> always means “overturns”.
