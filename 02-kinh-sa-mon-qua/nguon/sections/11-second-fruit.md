# 4.2 — Quả thứ hai của đời sống sa-môn  (27 đoạn)

## dn2:37.0
- **PLI**: 4.2. Dutiyasandiṭṭhikasāmaññaphala
- **EN**: 4.2. The Second Fruit of the Ascetic Life

## dn2:37.1  `dr9.80, km14.152, lv1.161, ms6D_266, msdiv186, ndp6.54, pts-cs37, vri1.54`
- **PLI**: “Sakkā pana, bhante, aññampi evameva diṭṭheva dhamme sandiṭṭhikaṁ sāmaññaphalaṁ paññapetun”ti?
- **EN**: “But sir, can you point out another fruit of the ascetic life that’s likewise apparent in this very life?”
- **NOTE**: By starting with a very basic and obvious fruit, the Buddha stimulates Ajātasattu to seek a deeper answer.

## dn2:37.2
- **PLI**: “Sakkā, mahārāja.
- **EN**: “I can, Great King.

## dn2:37.3
- **PLI**: Tena hi, mahārāja, taññevettha paṭipucchissāmi. Yathā te khameyya, tathā naṁ byākareyyāsi.
- **EN**: Well then, I’ll ask you about this in return, and you can answer as you like.

## dn2:37.4
- **PLI**: Taṁ kiṁ maññasi, mahārāja,
- **EN**: What do you think, Great King?

## dn2:37.5
- **PLI**: idha te assa puriso kassako gahapatiko karakārako rāsivaḍḍhako.
- **EN**: Suppose you had a person who was a farmer, a householder, a hard worker, someone who builds up their capital.
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Karakārako rāsivaḍḍhako</i> is a unique phrase. For <i lang='pi' translate='no'>karakāraka</i>, compare <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn57/en/sujato#2.3'>MN 57:2.3</a>, where a naked ascetic “does a hard thing”. <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Rāsi</i> means “heap” (of grain or wealth according to the commentary).

## dn2:37.6
- **PLI**: Tassa evamassa:
- **EN**: They’d think:

## dn2:37.7
- **PLI**: ‘acchariyaṁ vata bho, abbhutaṁ vata bho, puññānaṁ gati, puññānaṁ vipāko.
- **EN**: ‘The outcome and result of good deeds is just so incredible, so amazing!

## dn2:37.8
- **PLI**: Ayañhi rājā māgadho ajātasattu vedehiputto manusso, ahampi manusso.
- **EN**: For this King Ajātasattu is a human being, and so am I.

## dn2:37.9
- **PLI**: Ayañhi rājā māgadho ajātasattu vedehiputto pañcahi kāmaguṇehi samappito samaṅgībhūto paricāreti, devo maññe.
- **EN**: Yet he amuses himself, supplied and provided with the five kinds of sensual stimulation as if he were a god.

## dn2:37.10
- **PLI**: Ahaṁ panamhissa kassako gahapatiko karakārako rāsivaḍḍhako.
- **EN**: Whereas I’m a farmer, a householder, a hard worker, someone who builds up their capital.

## dn2:37.11
- **PLI**: So vatassāhaṁ puññāni kareyyaṁ.
- **EN**: I really should do good deeds.

## dn2:37.12
- **PLI**: Yannūnāhaṁ kesamassuṁ ohāretvā kāsāyāni vatthāni acchādetvā agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajeyyan’ti.
- **EN**: Why don’t I shave off my hair and beard, dress in ocher robes, and go forth from the lay life to homelessness?’

## dn2:37.13  `cck9.79, csp1ed6.58, csp2ed6.58, km14.153, lv1.162, lv1.163, mc9.62, ms6D_267, pts-vp-pli1.62, sya9.81`
- **PLI**: So aparena samayena appaṁ vā bhogakkhandhaṁ pahāya mahantaṁ vā bhogakkhandhaṁ pahāya, appaṁ vā ñātiparivaṭṭaṁ pahāya mahantaṁ vā ñātiparivaṭṭaṁ pahāya kesamassuṁ ohāretvā kāsāyāni vatthāni acchādetvā agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajeyya.
- **EN**: After some time they give up a large or small fortune, and a large or small family circle. They’d shave off hair and beard, dress in ocher robes, and go forth from the lay life to homelessness.
- **NOTE**: The bonded servant had no wealth or family to renounce, but the worker does.

## dn2:37.14
- **PLI**: So evaṁ pabbajito samāno kāyena saṁvuto vihareyya, vācāya saṁvuto vihareyya, manasā saṁvuto vihareyya, ghāsacchādanaparamatāya santuṭṭho, abhirato paviveke.
- **EN**: Having gone forth they’d live restrained in body, speech, and mind, living content with nothing more than food and clothes, delighting in seclusion.

## dn2:37.15
- **PLI**: Tañce te purisā evamāroceyyuṁ:
- **EN**: And suppose your men were to report all this to you.

## dn2:37.16
- **PLI**: ‘yagghe, deva, jāneyyāsi, yo te so puriso kassako gahapatiko karakārako rāsivaḍḍhako;
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn2:37.17
- **PLI**: so deva kesamassuṁ ohāretvā kāsāyāni vatthāni acchādetvā agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajito.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn2:37.18
- **PLI**: So evaṁ pabbajito samāno kāyena saṁvuto viharati, vācāya saṁvuto viharati, manasā saṁvuto viharati, ghāsacchādanaparamatāya santuṭṭho, abhirato paviveke’ti.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn2:37.19
- **PLI**: Api nu tvaṁ evaṁ vadeyyāsi:
- **EN**: Would you say to them:

## dn2:37.20
- **PLI**: ‘etu me, bho, so puriso, punadeva hotu kassako gahapatiko karakārako rāsivaḍḍhako’”ti?
- **EN**: ‘Bring that person to me! Let them once more be a farmer, a householder, a hard worker, someone who builds up their capital’?”

## dn2:38.1  `bj7.108, km14.154, ms6D_268, msdiv187, pts-cs38`
- **PLI**: “No hetaṁ, bhante.
- **EN**: “No, sir.

## dn2:38.2
- **PLI**: Atha kho naṁ mayameva abhivādeyyāmapi, paccuṭṭheyyāmapi, āsanenapi nimanteyyāma, abhinimanteyyāmapi naṁ cīvarapiṇḍapātasenāsanagilānappaccayabhesajjaparikkhārehi, dhammikampissa rakkhāvaraṇaguttiṁ saṁvidaheyyāmā”ti.
- **EN**: Rather, I would bow to them, rise in their presence, and offer them a seat. I’d invite them to accept robes, almsfood, lodgings, and medicines and supplies for the sick. And I’d organize their lawful guarding and protection.”

## dn2:38.3  `dr9.81, lv1.164, ms6D_269, msdiv188, vri1.55`
- **PLI**: “Taṁ kiṁ maññasi, mahārāja?
- **EN**: “What do you think, Great King?

## dn2:38.4
- **PLI**: Yadi evaṁ sante hoti vā sandiṭṭhikaṁ sāmaññaphalaṁ no vā”ti?
- **EN**: If this is so, is there a fruit of the ascetic life apparent in the present life or not?”

## dn2:38.5
- **PLI**: “Addhā kho, bhante, evaṁ sante hoti sandiṭṭhikaṁ sāmaññaphalan”ti.
- **EN**: “Clearly, sir, there is.”

## dn2:38.6
- **PLI**: “Idaṁ kho te, mahārāja, mayā dutiyaṁ diṭṭheva dhamme sandiṭṭhikaṁ sāmaññaphalaṁ paññattan”ti.
- **EN**: “This is the second fruit of the ascetic life that’s apparent in this very life, which I point out to you.”
