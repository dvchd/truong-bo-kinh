## dn2:26.1  `cck9.72, csp1ed6.53, csp2ed6.53, dr9.73, km14.139, lv1.146, lv1.147, mc9.57, ms6D_253, msdiv174, pts-cs26, sya9.74, vri1.50`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ vutte, bhante, pakudho kaccāyano maṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: He said:

## dn2:26.2
- **PLI**: ‘sattime, mahārāja, kāyā akaṭā akaṭavidhā animmitā animmātā vañjhā kūṭaṭṭhā esikaṭṭhāyiṭṭhitā.
- **EN**: ‘Great king, these seven substances are not made, not derived, not created, without a creator, barren, steady as a mountain peak, standing firm like a pillar.
- **NOTE**: This is a reductive atomism. It argues that since all things are made of the seven fundamental substances (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>kāya</i>), higher-order entities have no significance.

## dn2:26.3
- **PLI**: Te na iñjanti, na vipariṇamanti, na aññamaññaṁ byābādhenti, nālaṁ aññamaññassa sukhāya vā dukkhāya vā sukhadukkhāya vā.
- **EN**: They don’t move or deteriorate or obstruct each other. They’re unable to cause pleasure, pain, or both pleasure and pain to each other.

## dn2:26.4
- **PLI**: Katame satta?
- **EN**: What seven?

## dn2:26.5
- **PLI**: Pathavikāyo, āpokāyo, tejokāyo, vāyokāyo, sukhe, dukkhe, jīve sattame—
- **EN**: The substances of earth, water, fire, air; pleasure, pain, and the soul is the seventh.
- **NOTE**: Unlike the materialism of Ajita Kesakambala, one of the basic substances is the soul. He uses <i lang='pi' translate='no'>jīva</i>, the same term used by the Jains, rather than <i lang='pi' translate='no'>attā</i> as preferred by the brahmins. Likewise, the Jains held a similar doctrine of six uncreated and eternal “substances” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>kāya</i> or <i lang='pi' translate='no'>dravya</i>): soul, the media for motion and rest, matter, space, and time.

## dn2:26.6
- **PLI**: ime satta kāyā akaṭā akaṭavidhā animmitā animmātā vañjhā kūṭaṭṭhā esikaṭṭhāyiṭṭhitā.
- **EN**: These seven substances are not made, not derived, not created, without a creator, barren, steady as a mountain peak, standing firm like a pillar.

## dn2:26.7
- **PLI**: Te na iñjanti, na vipariṇamanti, na aññamaññaṁ byābādhenti, nālaṁ aññamaññassa sukhāya vā dukkhāya vā sukhadukkhāya vā.
- **EN**: They don’t move or deteriorate or obstruct each other. They’re unable to cause pleasure, pain, or both pleasure and pain to each other.

## dn2:26.8
- **PLI**: Tattha natthi hantā vā ghātetā vā, sotā vā sāvetā vā, viññātā vā viññāpetā vā.
- **EN**: And here there is no one who kills or who makes others kill; no one who learns or who educates others; no one who understands or who helps others understand.
- **NOTE**: Compare <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an8.16/en/sujato#1.3'>AN 8.16:1.3</a>.

## dn2:26.9
- **PLI**: Yopi tiṇhena satthena sīsaṁ chindati, na koci kiñci jīvitā voropeti;
- **EN**: If you chop off someone’s head with a sharp sword, you don’t take anyone’s life.

## dn2:26.10
- **PLI**: sattannaṁ tveva kāyānamantarena satthaṁ vivaramanupatatī’ti.
- **EN**: The sword simply passes through the gap between the seven substances.’

## dn2:27.1  `km14.140, lv1.148, ms6D_254, msdiv175, ndp6.50, pts-cs27, pts-vp-pli1.57`
- **PLI**: Itthaṁ kho me, bhante, pakudho kaccāyano sandiṭṭhikaṁ sāmaññaphalaṁ puṭṭho samāno aññena aññaṁ byākāsi.
- **EN**: And so, when I asked Pakudha Kaccāyana about the fruits of the ascetic life apparent in the present life, he answered with something else entirely.

## dn2:27.2
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, bhante, ambaṁ vā puṭṭho labujaṁ byākareyya, labujaṁ vā puṭṭho ambaṁ byākareyya;
- **EN**: It was like someone who, when asked about a mango, answered with a breadfruit, or when asked about a breadfruit, answered with a mango.

## dn2:27.3
- **PLI**: evameva kho me, bhante, pakudho kaccāyano sandiṭṭhikaṁ sāmaññaphalaṁ puṭṭho samāno aññena aññaṁ byākāsi.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn2:27.4

## dn2:27.5

- **EN**: ‘How on earth could one such as I presume to rebuke an ascetic or brahmin living in my realm?’

## dn2:27.6
- **PLI**: So kho ahaṁ, bhante, pakudhassa kaccāyanassa bhāsitaṁ neva abhinandiṁ nappaṭikkosiṁ,
- **EN**: So I neither approved nor rejected that statement of Pakudha Kaccāyana.

## dn2:27.7
- **PLI**: anabhinanditvā appaṭikkositvā anattamano, anattamanavācaṁ anicchāretvā tameva vācaṁ anuggaṇhanto anikkujjanto uṭṭhāyāsanā pakkamiṁ.
- **EN**: I was displeased, but did not express my displeasure. Neither accepting what he said nor contradicting it, I rose from my seat and left.
