# Muc 12 — The Four Accessories  (10 đoạn)

## dn5:12.0
- **PLI**: 4.1. Catuparikkhāra
- **EN**: 4.1. The Four Accessories

## dn5:12.1  `cck9.175, csp1ed6.130, csp2ed6.130, dr9.184, km15.23, mc9.137, ms6D_550, msdiv339, ndp6.117, pts-vp-pli1.137, sya9.174`
- **PLI**: Tena hi bhavaṁ rājā ye bhoto rañño janapade khattiyā ānuyantā negamā ceva jānapadā ca te bhavaṁ rājā āmantayataṁ: ‘icchāmahaṁ, bho, mahāyaññaṁ yajituṁ, anujānantu me bhavanto yaṁ mama assa dīgharattaṁ hitāya sukhāyā’ti. Ye bhoto rañño janapade amaccā pārisajjā negamā ceva jānapadā ca …pe… brāhmaṇamahāsālā negamā ceva jānapadā ca …pe… gahapatinecayikā negamā ceva jānapadā ca, te bhavaṁ rājā āmantayataṁ:
- **EN**: ‘In that case, let the king announce this throughout the realm to the aristocrat vassals of both town and country; the ministers and councillors of both town and country; the well-to-do brahmins of both town and country; and the well-off householders of both town and country.
- **NOTE**: Here the phrase “of both town and country” qualifies each group. At <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an4.70/en/sujato#1.3'>AN 4.70:1.3</a>, however, “brahmins and householders” and “people of town and country” are separate groups of people. Elsewhere the context does not always decide between these two possibilities. Generally the idiom aims at inclusivity, as opposed to here where the king is consulting the rich and powerful, so I treat them as two separate groups, thus including the common folk.

## dn5:12.2
- **PLI**: ‘icchāmahaṁ, bho, mahāyaññaṁ yajituṁ, anujānantu me bhavanto yaṁ mama assa dīgharattaṁ hitāya sukhāyā’ti.
- **EN**: “I wish to perform a great sacrifice. Please grant your approval, worthy sirs; it will be for my lasting welfare and happiness.”’

## dn5:12.3
- **PLI**: ‘Evaṁ, bho’ti kho, brāhmaṇa, rājā mahāvijito purohitassa brāhmaṇassa paṭissutvā ye rañño janapade khattiyā ānuyantā negamā ceva jānapadā ca, te rājā mahāvijito āmantesi:
- **EN**: The king agreed with the high priest’s advice and followed his recommendation.

## dn5:12.9
- **PLI**: Itime cattāro anumatipakkhā tasseva yaññassa parikkhārā bhavanti.
- **EN**: And so these four consenting factions became accessories to the sacrifice.
- **NOTE**: “Consenting factions” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>anumatipakkhā</i>. The king governs with the consent of his people, although only the landowning classes are considered.
