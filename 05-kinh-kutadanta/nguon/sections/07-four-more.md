# Muc 14 — Four More Accessories  (8 đoạn)

## dn5:14.0
- **PLI**: 4.3. Catuparikkhāra
- **EN**: 4.3. Four More Accessories

## dn5:14.1  `cck9.176, csp1ed6.131, csp2ed6.131, km15.26, ms6D_552, msdiv341, ndp6.118, pts-cs14, pts-vp-pli1.138`
- **PLI**: Purohito brāhmaṇo catūhaṅgehi samannāgato,
- **EN**: And the brahmin high priest had four factors.
- **NOTE**: These are four of the five qualities that Soṇadaṇḍa identifies as the qualities of a brahmin at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn4/en/sujato#13.2'>DN 4:13.2</a>. Missing is appearance, which is the first factor that Soṇadaṇḍa admits is unnecessary.

## dn5:14.2
- **PLI**: ubhato sujāto mātito ca pitito ca saṁsuddhagahaṇiko yāva sattamā pitāmahayugā akkhitto anupakkuṭṭho jātivādena;
- **EN**: He was well born on both his mother’s and father’s side, stemming from a clean vessel, with irrefutable and impeccable genealogy back to the seventh paternal generation.

## dn5:14.3
- **PLI**: ajjhāyako mantadharo tiṇṇaṁ vedānaṁ pāragū sanighaṇḍukeṭubhānaṁ sākkharappabhedānaṁ itihāsapañcamānaṁ padako veyyākaraṇo lokāyatamahāpurisalakkhaṇesu anavayo;
- **EN**: He recited and remembered the hymns, and had mastered the three Vedas, together with their vocabularies and ritual performance, their phonology and word classification, and the testaments as fifth. He knew them word-by-word, and their grammar. He was well versed in cosmology and the marks of a great man.

## dn5:14.4
- **PLI**: sīlavā vuddhasīlī vuddhasīlena samannāgato;
- **EN**: He was ethical, mature in ethical conduct.

## dn5:14.5
- **PLI**: paṇḍito viyatto medhāvī paṭhamo vā dutiyo vā sujaṁ paggaṇhantānaṁ.
- **EN**: He was astute, competent, and intelligent, being the first or second to hold the sacrificial ladle.

## dn5:14.6
- **PLI**: Purohito brāhmaṇo imehi catūhaṅgehi samannāgato.
- **EN**: These are the four factors that the brahmin high priest possessed.

## dn5:14.7
- **PLI**: Iti imānipi cattāri aṅgāni tasseva yaññassa parikkhārā bhavanti.
- **EN**: And so these four factors also became accessories to the sacrifice.
