# Muc 1 — The Brahmins and Householders of Khāṇumata  (36 đoạn)

## dn5:1.0
- **PLI**: 1. Khāṇumatakabrāhmaṇagahapatikā
- **EN**: 1. The Brahmins and Householders of Khāṇumata

## dn5:1.1  `bj7.248, cck9.162, csp1ed6.120, csp2ed6.120, dr9.170, km15.1, km15.2, mc9.127, ms6D_493, msdiv323, ndp6.109, pts-cs1, pts-vp-pli1.127, sya9.162, vri1.112`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—
- **EN**: So I have heard.
- **NOTE**: When asked about sacrifice, the Buddha tells a story of how a king was persuaded to forgo cruelty and institute a constructive social policy of welfare instead.

## dn5:1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā magadhesu cārikaṁ caramāno mahatā bhikkhusaṅghena saddhiṁ pañcamattehi bhikkhusatehi yena khāṇumataṁ nāma magadhānaṁ brāhmaṇagāmo tadavasari.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was wandering in the land of the Magadhans together with a large Saṅgha of five hundred mendicants when he arrived at a village of the Magadhan brahmins named Khāṇumata.
- **NOTE**: Khāṇumata is only mentioned here. It means “stumpy”, perhaps indicating that it was, or had recently been, a rough out-of-the-way place.

## dn5:1.3
- **PLI**: Tatra sudaṁ bhagavā khāṇumate viharati ambalaṭṭhikāyaṁ.
- **EN**: There he stayed nearby at Ambalaṭṭhikā.
- **NOTE**: The commentary says this was similar to the Ambalaṭṭhikā of <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn1/en/sujato#1.2.1'>DN 1:1.2.1</a>.

## dn5:1.4
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena kūṭadanto brāhmaṇo khāṇumataṁ ajjhāvasati sattussadaṁ satiṇakaṭṭhodakaṁ sadhaññaṁ rājabhoggaṁ raññā māgadhena seniyena bimbisārena dinnaṁ rājadāyaṁ brahmadeyyaṁ.
- **EN**: Now at that time the brahmin Kūṭadanta was living in Khāṇumata. It was a crown property given by King Seniya Bimbisāra of Magadha, teeming with living creatures, full of hay, wood, water, and grain, a royal park endowed to a brahmin.
- **NOTE**: Like the town Khāṇumata, the brahmin Kūṭadanta appears only here. His name means “pointy teeth”, but there is no evidence that he was a vampire.

## dn5:1.5
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena kūṭadantassa brāhmaṇassa mahāyañño upakkhaṭo hoti.
- **EN**: Now at that time Kūṭadanta had prepared a great sacrifice.

## dn5:1.6
- **PLI**: Satta ca usabhasatāni satta ca vacchatarasatāni satta ca vacchatarīsatāni satta ca ajasatāni satta ca urabbhasatāni thūṇūpanītāni honti yaññatthāya.
- **EN**: Bulls, bullocks, heifers, goats and rams—seven hundred of each—had been led to the post for the sacrifice.
- **NOTE**: At <a href='https://suttacentral.net/snp2.7/en/sujato#26.1'>Snp 2.7:26.1</a>, the sacrifice of cows in particular was said to be the nadir of decline for brahmins. When similar sacrifices are described elsewhere in the canon, the number is five hundred rather than seven hundred (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn3.9/en/sujato#1.2'>SN 3.9:1.2</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an7.47/en/sujato#1.2'>AN 7.47:1.2</a>).

## dn5:2.1  `cck9.163, dr9.171, km15.3, ms6D_494, msdiv324, pts-cs2, pts-vp-pli1.128, sya9.163`
- **PLI**: Assosuṁ kho khāṇumatakā brāhmaṇagahapatikā:
- **EN**: The brahmins and householders of Khāṇumata heard:

## dn5:2.2
- **PLI**: “samaṇo khalu, bho, gotamo sakyaputto sakyakulā pabbajito magadhesu cārikaṁ caramāno mahatā bhikkhusaṅghena saddhiṁ pañcamattehi bhikkhusatehi khāṇumataṁ anuppatto khāṇumate viharati ambalaṭṭhikāyaṁ.
- **EN**: “It seems the ascetic Gotama—a Sakyan, gone forth from a Sakyan family—has arrived at Khāṇumata and is staying at Ambalaṭṭhikā.

## dn5:2.3
- **PLI**: Taṁ kho pana bhavantaṁ gotamaṁ evaṁ kalyāṇo kittisaddo abbhuggato:
- **EN**: He has this good reputation:

## dn5:2.4
- **PLI**: ‘itipi so bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho vijjācaraṇasampanno sugato lokavidū anuttaro purisadammasārathi satthā devamanussānaṁ buddho bhagavā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘That Blessed One is perfected, a fully awakened Buddha, accomplished in knowledge and conduct, holy, knower of the world, supreme guide for those fit for training, teacher of gods and humans, awakened, blessed.’

## dn5:2.5
- **PLI**: So imaṁ lokaṁ sadevakaṁ samārakaṁ sabrahmakaṁ sassamaṇabrāhmaṇiṁ pajaṁ sadevamanussaṁ sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā pavedeti.
- **EN**: He has realized with his own insight this world—with its gods, Māras, and divinities, this population with its ascetics and brahmins, gods and humans—and he makes it known to others.

## dn5:2.6
- **PLI**: So dhammaṁ deseti ādikalyāṇaṁ majjhekalyāṇaṁ pariyosānakalyāṇaṁ sātthaṁ sabyañjanaṁ kevalaparipuṇṇaṁ parisuddhaṁ brahmacariyaṁ pakāseti.
- **EN**: He teaches Dhamma that’s good in the beginning, good in the middle, and good in the end, meaningful and well-phrased. And he reveals a spiritual practice that’s entirely full and pure.

## dn5:2.7
- **PLI**: Sādhu kho pana tathārūpānaṁ arahataṁ dassanaṁ hotī”ti.
- **EN**: It’s good to see such perfected ones.”

## dn5:2.8  `ms6D_495, msdiv325, vri1.113`
- **PLI**: Atha kho khāṇumatakā brāhmaṇagahapatikā khāṇumatā nikkhamitvā saṅghasaṅghī gaṇībhūtā yena ambalaṭṭhikā tenupasaṅkamanti.
- **EN**: Then, exiting Khāṇumata, they formed into companies and headed to Ambalaṭṭhikā.

## dn5:3.2

- **EN**: “My butler, why are the brahmins and householders headed for Ambalaṭṭhikā?”

- **EN**: “The ascetic Gotama has arrived at Khāṇumata and is staying at Ambalaṭṭhikā.

## dn5:3.6
- **PLI**: Taṁ kho pana bhavantaṁ gotamaṁ evaṁ kalyāṇo kittisaddo abbhuggato:
- **EN**: He has this good reputation:

## dn5:3.7
- **PLI**: ‘itipi so bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho vijjācaraṇasampanno sugato lokavidū anuttaro purisadammasārathi satthā devamanussānaṁ buddho bhagavā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘That Blessed One is perfected, a fully awakened Buddha, accomplished in knowledge and conduct, holy, knower of the world, supreme guide for those fit for training, teacher of gods and humans, awakened, blessed.’

## dn5:3.8

- **EN**: They’re going to see that worthy Gotama.”

## dn5:4.1  `km15.5, ms6D_498, msdiv328, pts-cs4, pts-vp-pli1.129, sya9.164`
- **PLI**: Atha kho kūṭadantassa brāhmaṇassa etadahosi:
- **EN**: Then Kūṭadanta thought,

## dn5:4.2
- **PLI**: “sutaṁ kho pana metaṁ:
- **EN**: “I’ve heard that

## dn5:4.3
- **PLI**: ‘samaṇo gotamo tividhaṁ yaññasampadaṁ soḷasaparikkhāraṁ jānātī’ti.
- **EN**: the ascetic Gotama knows how to accomplish the sacrifice with three modes and sixteen accessories.
- **NOTE**: News of the Buddha had spread in Brahmanical circles. In <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn3/en/sujato'>DN 3</a> we saw the Buddha use his rhetorical technique of adapting his teaching to reframe Brahmanical doctrines in order to establish a common ground. Here we see the downside to such techniques, as the details of the reframing have been lost.

## dn5:4.4
- **PLI**: Na kho panāhaṁ jānāmi tividhaṁ yaññasampadaṁ soḷasaparikkhāraṁ.
- **EN**: I don’t know about that,

## dn5:4.5
- **PLI**: Icchāmi cāhaṁ mahāyaññaṁ yajituṁ.
- **EN**: but I wish to perform a great sacrifice.

## dn5:4.6
- **PLI**: Yannūnāhaṁ samaṇaṁ gotamaṁ upasaṅkamitvā tividhaṁ yaññasampadaṁ soḷasaparikkhāraṁ puccheyyan”ti.
- **EN**: Why don’t I ask him how to accomplish the sacrifice with three modes and sixteen accessories?”
- **NOTE**: No such sacrifice has been identified in Brahmanical texts.
