## dn5:29.0
- **PLI**: 7. Sotāpattiphalasacchikiriyā
- **EN**: 7. The Realization of the Fruit of Stream-Entry

## dn5:29.1  `dr9.201, km15.52, ms6D_591, msdiv355, pts-cs29, sya9.190, vri1.133`
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā kūṭadantassa brāhmaṇassa anupubbiṁ kathaṁ kathesi, seyyathidaṁ—
- **EN**: Then the Buddha taught Kūṭadanta step by step, with

## dn5:29.2
- **PLI**: dānakathaṁ sīlakathaṁ saggakathaṁ; kāmānaṁ ādīnavaṁ okāraṁ saṅkilesaṁ nekkhamme ānisaṁsaṁ pakāsesi.
- **EN**: a talk on giving, ethical conduct, and heaven. He revealed the drawbacks of sensual pleasures, so sordid and corrupt, and the benefit of renunciation.

## dn5:29.3
- **PLI**: Yadā bhagavā aññāsi kūṭadantaṁ brāhmaṇaṁ kallacittaṁ muducittaṁ vinīvaraṇacittaṁ udaggacittaṁ pasannacittaṁ, atha yā buddhānaṁ sāmukkaṁsikā dhammadesanā, taṁ pakāsesi—
- **EN**: And when he knew that Kūṭadanta’s mind was ready, pliable, rid of hindrances, elated, and confident he revealed the special teaching of the Buddhas:

## dn5:29.4
- **PLI**: dukkhaṁ samudayaṁ nirodhaṁ maggaṁ.
- **EN**: suffering, its origin, its cessation, and the path.

## dn5:29.5
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi nāma suddhaṁ vatthaṁ apagatakāḷakaṁ sammadeva rajanaṁ paṭiggaṇheyya;
- **EN**: Just as a clean cloth rid of stains would properly absorb dye,

## dn5:29.6
- **PLI**: evameva kūṭadantassa brāhmaṇassa tasmiññeva āsane virajaṁ vītamalaṁ dhammacakkhuṁ udapādi:
- **EN**: in that very seat the stainless, immaculate vision of the Dhamma arose in the brahmin Kūṭadanta:

## dn5:29.7
- **PLI**: “yaṁ kiñci samudayadhammaṁ sabbaṁ taṁ nirodhadhamman”ti.
- **EN**: “Everything that is liable to arise is liable to cease.”

- **EN**: Then Kūṭadanta saw, attained, understood, and fathomed the Dhamma. He went beyond doubt, got rid of indecision, and became self-assured and independent of others regarding the Teacher’s instructions. He said to the Buddha,

- **EN**: The Buddha consented with silence.

## dn5:30.4  `bj7.322, ms6D_593, msdiv357`
