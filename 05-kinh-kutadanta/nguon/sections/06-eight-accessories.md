# Muc 13 — The Eight Accessories  (12 đoạn)

## dn5:13.0
- **PLI**: 4.2. Aṭṭhaparikkhāra
- **EN**: 4.2. The Eight Accessories

## dn5:13.1  `bj7.266, dr9.185, km15.24, km15.25, mc9.138, ms6D_551, msdiv340, pts-cs13, sya9.175, vri1.122`
- **PLI**: Rājā mahāvijito aṭṭhahaṅgehi samannāgato—
- **EN**: King Mahāvijita possessed eight factors.
- **NOTE**: Royal authority is not based just on birth, conquest, ritual, or power, but on quality of character.

## dn5:13.2
- **PLI**: ubhato sujāto mātito ca pitito ca saṁsuddhagahaṇiko yāva sattamā pitāmahayugā akkhitto anupakkuṭṭho jātivādena;
- **EN**: He was well born on both his mother’s and father’s side, stemming from a clean vessel, with irrefutable and impeccable genealogy back to the seventh paternal generation.

## dn5:13.3
- **PLI**: abhirūpo dassanīyo pāsādiko paramāya vaṇṇapokkharatāya samannāgato brahmavaṇṇī brahmavacchasī akhuddāvakāso dassanāya;
- **EN**: He was attractive, good-looking, lovely, of surpassing beauty. He had divine looks and lustre, remarkable to behold.

## dn5:13.4
- **PLI**: aḍḍho mahaddhano mahābhogo pahūtajātarūparajato pahūtavittūpakaraṇo pahūtadhanadhañño paripuṇṇakosakoṭṭhāgāro;
- **EN**: He was rich, affluent, and wealthy, with lots of gold and silver, lots of property and assets, lots of money and grain, and a full treasury and storehouses.

## dn5:13.5
- **PLI**: balavā caturaṅginiyā senāya samannāgato assavāya ovādapaṭikarāya sahati maññe paccatthike yasasā;
- **EN**: He was powerful, having an army of four divisions that was obedient and carried out instructions. He’d probably prevail over his enemies just with his reputation.
- **NOTE**: Read <i lang='pi' translate='no'>sahati</i> (“prevails”) over the several variants.

## dn5:13.6
- **PLI**: saddho dāyako dānapati anāvaṭadvāro samaṇabrāhmaṇakapaṇaddhikavaṇibbakayācakānaṁ opānabhūto puññāni karoti;
- **EN**: He was faithful, generous, a donor, his door always open. He was a well-spring of support, making merit with ascetics and brahmins, for paupers, vagrants, supplicants, and beggars.

## dn5:13.7
- **PLI**: bahussuto tassa tassa sutajātassa, tassa tasseva kho pana bhāsitassa atthaṁ jānāti:
- **EN**: He was very learned in diverse fields of learning. He understood the meaning of diverse statements, saying:
- **NOTE**: Showing the importance of comprehension over blind adherence to tradition.

## dn5:13.8
- **PLI**: ‘ayaṁ imassa bhāsitassa attho ayaṁ imassa bhāsitassa attho’ti;
- **EN**: ‘This is what that statement means; that is what this statement means.’

## dn5:13.9
- **PLI**: paṇḍito, viyatto, medhāvī, paṭibalo, atītānāgatapaccuppanne atthe cintetuṁ.
- **EN**: He was astute, competent, and intelligent, able to think issues through as they bear upon the past, future, and present.
- **NOTE**: Meditators focus on the present, but that does not mean they cannot think about the past or future; it just means they are not trapped in useless thoughts.

## dn5:13.10
- **PLI**: Rājā mahāvijito imehi aṭṭhahaṅgehi samannāgato.
- **EN**: These are the eight factors that King Mahāvijita possessed.

## dn5:13.11
- **PLI**: Iti imānipi aṭṭhaṅgāni tasseva yaññassa parikkhārā bhavanti.
- **EN**: And so these eight factors also became accessories to the sacrifice.
