- **EN**: Next, before the sacrifice, the brahmin high priest dispelled the king’s regret regarding the recipients in ten respects:

## dn5:16.2
- **PLI**: ‘Āgamissanti kho bhoto yaññaṁ pāṇātipātinopi pāṇātipātā paṭiviratāpi.
- **EN**: ‘There will come to the sacrifice those who kill living creatures and those who refrain from killing living creatures.

## dn5:16.3
- **PLI**: Ye tattha pāṇātipātino, tesaññeva tena.
- **EN**: As to those who kill living creatures, the outcome of that is theirs alone.

## dn5:16.4
- **PLI**: Ye tattha pāṇātipātā paṭiviratā, te ārabbha yajataṁ bhavaṁ, sajjataṁ bhavaṁ, modataṁ bhavaṁ, cittameva bhavaṁ antaraṁ pasādetu.
- **EN**: But as to those who refrain from killing living creatures, it is for their sakes that the king should sacrifice, relinquish, rejoice, and gain confidence in his heart.
- **NOTE**: PTS edition acknowledges <i lang='pi' translate='no'>sajjata</i> only as a variant reading, but it is in the commentary, so should be accepted in the text. It is from √<i lang='pi' translate='no'>sajj</i> (relinquish).
