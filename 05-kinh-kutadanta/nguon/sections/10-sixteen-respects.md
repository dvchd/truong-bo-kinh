## dn5:21.10
- **PLI**: ‘evaṁ me sutan’ti vā ‘evaṁ arahati bhavitun’ti vā;
- **EN**: ‘So I have heard’ or ‘It ought to be like this.’
- **NOTE**: “So I have heard” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>evaṁ me sutaṁ</i>) is the standard opening for Buddhist suttas. This tag was used to indicate that the speaker was not present at the events, but “heard” about them. This is in contrast with the phrase “I heard and learned this in the presence” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>sammukhā sutaṁ, sammukhā paṭiggahitaṁ</i>), which is used when reporting a teaching heard directly from the Buddha (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn55.52/en/sujato#5.1'>SN 55.52:5.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn22.90/en/sujato#9.1'>SN 22.90:9.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn47/en/sujato#10.7'>MN 47:10.7</a>, etc.)
