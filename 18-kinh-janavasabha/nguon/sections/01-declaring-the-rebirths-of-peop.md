# Muc 1 — Declaring the Rebirths of People From Ñātika and Elsewhere  (22 đoạn)

## dn18:1.0
- **PLI**: 1. Nātikiyādibyākaraṇa
- **EN**: 1. Declaring the Rebirths of People From Ñātika and Elsewhere

## dn18:1.1  `bj8.306, cck10.244, csp1ed7.162, csp2ed7.162, dr10.229, mc10.172, ms7D_596, msdiv273, ndp7.151, pts-cs1, pts-vp-pli2.200, sya10.229, vri2.148`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—
- **EN**: So I have heard.

## dn18:1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā nātike viharati giñjakāvasathe.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying at Ñātika in the brick house.
- **NOTE**: This picks up from the events of <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#2.5.1'>DN 16:2.5.1</a>, which however discusses only the Ñātikans (who are reckoned among the Vajjis). Like <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn17/en/sujato'>DN 17</a>, it bears many signs of a late sutta. The overall theme is the presence of a cosmic order where the liberating teaching of the Buddha is reconciled with the requirements of worldly power.

## dn18:1.3
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena bhagavā parito parito janapadesu paricārake abbhatīte kālaṅkate upapattīsu byākaroti kāsikosalesu vajjimallesu cetivaṁsesu kurupañcālesu majjhasūrasenesu:
- **EN**: Now at that time the Buddha was explaining the rebirths of devotees all over the nations; the Kāsis and Kosalans, Vajjis and Mallas, Cetīs and Vacchas, Kurus and Pañcālas, Macchas and Sūrasenas:
- **NOTE**: This list of ten nations is unique. The omission of the Aṅgas and Magadhans is noted below. The more famous list of “sixteen nations” adds Avanti and Assaka in the south, and Gandhāra and Kamboja in the north-west (eg. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an3.70/en/sujato#28.3'>AN 3.70:28.3</a>). The Buddha did not visit these lands, which explains why they are omitted.

## dn18:1.4
- **PLI**: “asu amutra upapanno, asu amutra upapanno.
- **EN**: “This one was reborn here, while that one was reborn there.

## dn18:1.5
- **PLI**: Paropaññāsa nātikiyā paricārakā abbhatītā kālaṅkatā pañcannaṁ orambhāgiyānaṁ saṁyojanānaṁ parikkhayā opapātikā tattha parinibbāyino anāvattidhammā tasmā lokā.
- **EN**: Over fifty devotees in Ñātika have passed away having ended the five lower fetters. They’ve been reborn spontaneously, and will be extinguished there, not liable to return from that world.
- **NOTE**: “Devotees” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>paricāraka</i>, which normally means “servant”. It is not in the relevant passages of DN 16. This sense is found in only a couple of other places, both late (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/snp5.18/en/sujato#1.2'>Snp 5.18:1.2</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#22.14.8'>Kd 1:22.14.8</a>).

## dn18:1.6
- **PLI**: Sādhikā navuti nātikiyā paricārakā abbhatītā kālaṅkatā tiṇṇaṁ saṁyojanānaṁ parikkhayā rāgadosamohānaṁ tanuttā sakadāgāmino, sakideva imaṁ lokaṁ āgantvā dukkhassantaṁ karissanti.
- **EN**: More than ninety devotees in Ñātika have passed away having ended three fetters, and weakened greed, hate, and delusion. They’re once-returners, who will come back to this world once only, then make an end of suffering.

## dn18:1.7
- **PLI**: Sātirekāni pañcasatāni nātikiyā paricārakā abbhatītā kālaṅkatā tiṇṇaṁ saṁyojanānaṁ parikkhayā sotāpannā avinipātadhammā niyatā sambodhiparāyaṇā”ti.
- **EN**: More than five hundred devotees in Ñātika have passed away having ended three fetters. They’re stream-enterers, not liable to be reborn in the underworld, assured, destined for awakening.”
