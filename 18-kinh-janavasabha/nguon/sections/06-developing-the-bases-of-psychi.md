# Muc 22.2 — Developing the Bases of Psychic Power  (16 đoạn)

## dn18:22.2.0
- **PLI**: 6. Bhāvitaiddhipāda
- **EN**: 6. Developing the Bases of Psychic Power

## dn18:22.3  `mc10.183, ms7D_631, msdiv287`
- **PLI**: “Taṁ kiṁ maññanti, bhonto devā tāvatiṁsā, yāva supaññattā cime tena bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhena cattāro iddhipādā paññattā iddhipahutāya iddhivisavitāya iddhivikubbanatāya.
- **EN**: “What do the good gods of the thirty-three think? How well described by the Blessed One—who knows and sees, the perfected one, the fully awakened Buddha—are the four bases of psychic power! They are taught for the amplification, burgeoning, and transformation of psychic power.
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Yāva supaññattā</i>, which is also at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn51/en/sujato#4.3'>MN 51:4.3</a>, is a variation of the common exclamation <i lang='pi' translate='no'>yāva subhāsita</i>. The phrase <i lang='pi' translate='no'>iddhipahutāya iddhivisavitāya iddhivikubbanatāya</i> is unique. The Paṭisambhidāmagga draws on this passage to explain <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vikubbana</i> both as a general term for development of psychic powers (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/ps3.2/en/sujato#4.3'>Ps 3.2:4.3</a>) and as specific kind of psychic power, namely the transformation of one’s apparent form as demonstrated by Sanaṅkumāra (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/ps3.2/en/sujato#15.1'>Ps 3.2:15.1</a>).

## dn18:22.8
- **PLI**: Vīmaṁsāsamādhippadhānasaṅkhārasamannāgataṁ iddhipādaṁ bhāveti.
- **EN**: They develop the basis of psychic power that has immersion due to inquiry, and active effort.

## dn18:22.9
- **PLI**: Ime kho, bho, tena bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhena cattāro iddhipādā paññattā iddhipahutāya iddhivisavitāya iddhivikubbanatāya.
- **EN**: These are the four bases of psychic power taught by the Buddha for the amplification, burgeoning, and transformation of psychic power.
