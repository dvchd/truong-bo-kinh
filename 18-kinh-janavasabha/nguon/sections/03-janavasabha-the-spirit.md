# Muc 9.4 — Janavasabha the Spirit  (29 đoạn)

## dn18:9.4.0
- **PLI**: 3. Janavasabhayakkha
- **EN**: 3. Janavasabha the Spirit

## dn18:9.5  `cck10.251, dr10.235, ms7D_604, msdiv280, vri2.152`
- **PLI**: Atha kho, ānanda, antarahito yakkho saddamanussāvesi:
- **EN**: “Then, Ānanda, an invisible spirit called out:

## dn18:9.6
- **PLI**: ‘janavasabho ahaṁ, bhagavā;
- **EN**: ‘I am Janavasabha, Blessed One!

## dn18:9.7
- **PLI**: janavasabho ahaṁ, sugatā’ti.
- **EN**: I am Janavasabha, Holy One!’

## dn18:9.8
- **PLI**: Abhijānāsi no tvaṁ, ānanda, ito pubbe evarūpaṁ nāmadheyyaṁ sutaṁ yadidaṁ janavasabho”ti?
- **EN**: Ānanda, do you recall having previously heard such a name as Janavasabha?”
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Nāmadheyyaṁ</i> means “name (borne by someone or something)”, not “one who bears the name” (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn50/en/sujato#22.1'>MN 50:22.1</a>).

## dn18:9.9  `csp1ed7.167, csp2ed7.167, mc10.177, ms7D_605, pts-vp-pli2.206, sya10.236`
- **PLI**: “Na kho ahaṁ, bhante, abhijānāmi ito pubbe evarūpaṁ nāmadheyyaṁ sutaṁ yadidaṁ janavasabhoti, api ca me, bhante, lomāni haṭṭhāni ‘janavasabho’ti nāmadheyyaṁ sutvā.
- **EN**: “No, worthy sir. But when I heard the word, I got goosebumps!

## dn18:9.10
- **PLI**: Tassa mayhaṁ, bhante, etadahosi:
- **EN**: I thought,

## dn18:9.11
- **PLI**: ‘na hi nūna so orako yakkho bhavissati yadidaṁ evarūpaṁ nāmadheyyaṁ supaññattaṁ yadidaṁ janavasabho’”ti.
- **EN**: ‘This must be no ordinary spirit to bear such an exalted name as Janavasabha.’”
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Janavasabha</i> is “chief of men”, spelled <i lang='pi' translate='no'>janesabha</i> at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn20/en/sujato#10.10'>DN 20:10.10</a> and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn32/en/sujato#10.9'>DN 32:10.9</a>. The synonym <i lang='pi' translate='no'>narāsabha</i> is an occasional poetic epithet of the Buddha (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/snp3.11/en/sujato#6.2'>Snp 3.11:6.2</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/snp5.1/en/sujato#21.3'>Snp 5.1:21.3</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn11.3/en/sujato#14.2'>SN 11.3:14.2</a>). In Sanskrit we find <i lang='pi' translate='no'>puruṣaṛṣabha</i> in the same sense.

## dn18:10.1  `pts-cs10`
- **PLI**: “Anantarā kho, ānanda, saddapātubhāvā uḷāravaṇṇo me yakkho sammukhe pāturahosi.
- **EN**: “After making himself heard while invisible, Ānanda, an exceptionally beautiful spirit appeared in front of me.
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Uḷāravaṇṇa</i> describes beautiful people at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn96/en/sujato#7.8'>MN 96:7.8</a>.

## dn18:10.2
- **PLI**: Dutiyakampi saddamanussāvesi:
- **EN**: And for a second time he called out:

## dn18:10.3
- **PLI**: ‘bimbisāro ahaṁ, bhagavā;
- **EN**: ‘I am Bimbisāra, Blessed One!

## dn18:10.4
- **PLI**: bimbisāro ahaṁ, sugatāti.
- **EN**: I am Bimbisāra, Holy One!

## dn18:10.5
- **PLI**: Idaṁ sattamaṁ kho ahaṁ, bhante, vessavaṇassa mahārājassa sahabyataṁ upapajjāmi, so tato cuto manussarājā bhavituṁ pahomi.
- **EN**: This is the seventh time I am reborn in the company of the Great King Vessavaṇa. When I pass away from here, I can become a king of men.
- **NOTE**: Read <i lang='pi' translate='no'>ito</i>. I think this implies what is stated more explicitly in Anuruddha’s partly parallel verses at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/thag16.9/en/sujato#23.1'>Thag 16.9:23.1</a>: after each of seven rebirths under Vessavaṇa he can become a king of men. This explains the name Janavasabha.

## dn18:10.6  `ms7D_606`
- **PLI**: Ito satta tato satta,
- **EN**: Seven from here, seven from there—

## dn18:10.7
- **PLI**: saṁsārāni catuddasa;
- **EN**: fourteen transmigrations in all.
- **NOTE**: It is rare to see <i lang='pi' translate='no'>saṁsāra</i> used as a countable noun, but see <a href='https://suttacentral.net/thag2.48/en/sujato#2.2'>Thag 2.48:2.2</a>.

## dn18:10.8
- **PLI**: Nivāsamabhijānāmi,
- **EN**: I remember these lives

## dn18:10.9
- **PLI**: yattha me vusitaṁ pure.
- **EN**: where I lived before.

## dn18:10.10  `ms7D_607, msdiv281`
- **PLI**: Dīgharattaṁ kho ahaṁ, bhante, avinipāto avinipātaṁ sañjānāmi, āsā ca pana me santiṭṭhati sakadāgāmitāyā’”ti.
- **EN**: For a long time I’ve known that I won’t be reborn in the underworld, but that I still hope to become a once-returner.’
- **NOTE**: As a stream-enterer he is freed from any lower rebirths, yet he still aspires to a higher realization.

## dn18:10.11  `bj8.316, cck10.252, dr10.236, ms7D_608, ndp7.156, pts-vp-pli2.207, sya10.237`
- **PLI**: “Acchariyamidaṁ āyasmato janavasabhassa yakkhassa, abbhutamidaṁ āyasmato janavasabhassa yakkhassa.
- **EN**: ‘It’s incredible and amazing that you, the venerable spirit Janavasabha, should say:

## dn18:10.12
- **PLI**: ‘Dīgharattaṁ kho ahaṁ, bhante, avinipāto avinipātaṁ sañjānāmī’ti ca vadesi, ‘āsā ca pana me santiṭṭhati sakadāgāmitāyā’ti ca vadesi, kutonidānaṁ panāyasmā janavasabho yakkho evarūpaṁ uḷāraṁ visesādhigamaṁ sañjānātī”ti?
- **EN**: “For a long time I’ve been aware that I won’t be reborn in the underworld” and also “But I still hope to become a once-returner.” But from what source do you know that you’ve achieved such a high distinction?’
- **NOTE**: In this idiom, the verb for “aware” varies between <i lang='pi' translate='no'>sañjānāti</i>, <i lang='pi' translate='no'>jānāti</i>, <i lang='pi' translate='no'>pajānāti</i>, and <i lang='pi' translate='no'>sampajānāti</i>.

## dn18:11.1  `pts-cs11`
- **PLI**: “‘Na aññatra, bhagavā, tava sāsanā, na aññatra, sugata, tava sāsanā;
- **EN**: ‘None other than the Blessed One’s instruction! None other than the Holy One’s instruction!
- **NOTE**: Compare <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn1.50/en/sujato#5.1'>SN 1.50:5.1</a> and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn2.24/en/sujato#6.1'>SN 2.24:6.1</a>.

## dn18:11.2
- **PLI**: yadagge ahaṁ, bhante, bhagavati ekantikato abhippasanno, tadagge ahaṁ, bhante, dīgharattaṁ avinipāto avinipātaṁ sañjānāmi, āsā ca pana me santiṭṭhati sakadāgāmitāya.
- **EN**: From the day I had absolute devotion to the Buddha I have known that I won’t be reborn in the underworld, but that I still hope to become a once-returner.

## dn18:11.3
- **PLI**: Idhāhaṁ, bhante, vessavaṇena mahārājena pesito virūḷhakassa mahārājassa santike kenacideva karaṇīyena addasaṁ bhagavantaṁ antarāmagge giñjakāvasathaṁ pavisitvā māgadhake paricārake ārabbha aṭṭhiṁ katvā manasikatvā sabbaṁ cetasā samannāharitvā nisinnaṁ:
- **EN**: Just now, sir, I had been sent out by the great king Vessavaṇa to the great king Virūḷhaka’s presence on some business, and on the way I saw the Buddha giving his attention to the fate of the Magadhan devotees.

## dn18:11.4
- **PLI**: “gatiṁ nesaṁ jānissāmi abhisamparāyaṁ, yaṅgatikā te bhavanto yaṁabhisamparāyā”ti.
- **EN**: “I shall know their destiny, where they are reborn in the next life.”  (= dn18:7.3)

## dn18:11.5
- **PLI**: Anacchariyaṁ kho panetaṁ, bhante, yaṁ vessavaṇassa mahārājassa tassaṁ parisāyaṁ bhāsato sammukhā sutaṁ sammukhā paṭiggahitaṁ:
- **EN**: But it comes as no surprise that I have heard and learned the fate of the Magadhan devotees in the presence of the great king Vessavaṇa as he was speaking to his assembly.

## dn18:11.6
- **PLI**: “yaṅgatikā te bhavanto yaṁabhisamparāyā”ti.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn18:11.7
- **PLI**: Tassa mayhaṁ, bhante, etadahosi:
- **EN**: It occurred to me,

## dn18:11.8
- **PLI**: “bhagavantañca dakkhāmi, idañca bhagavato ārocessāmī”ti.
- **EN**: “I shall see the Buddha and inform him of this.”

## dn18:11.9
- **PLI**: Ime kho me, bhante, dvepaccayā bhagavantaṁ dassanāya upasaṅkamituṁ.
- **EN**: These are the two reasons I’ve come to see the Buddha.
