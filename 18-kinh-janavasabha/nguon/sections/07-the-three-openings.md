# Muc 23.3 — The Three Openings  (30 đoạn)

## dn18:23.3.0
- **PLI**: 7. Tividhaokāsādhigama
- **EN**: 7. The Three Openings

## dn18:23.4  `cck10.261, dr10.244, mc10.184, ms7D_633, msdiv288, ndp7.161, sya10.245, vri2.158`
- **PLI**: “Taṁ kiṁ maññanti, bhonto devā tāvatiṁsā, yāvañcidaṁ tena bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhena tayo okāsādhigamā anubuddhā sukhassādhigamāya.
- **EN**: “What do the good gods of the thirty-three think? How well understood by the Buddha are the three opportunities for achieving happiness!
- **NOTE**: These “three opportunities” are not found elsewhere.

## dn18:23.5
- **PLI**: Katame tayo?
- **EN**: What three?

## dn18:23.6
- **PLI**: Idha bho ekacco saṁsaṭṭho viharati kāmehi saṁsaṭṭho akusalehi dhammehi.
- **EN**: First, take someone who lives mixed up with sensual pleasures and unskillful qualities.
- **NOTE**: This unique term is the reverse of the phrase that begins the first <i lang='pi' translate='no'>jhāna</i>. The passage, however, speaks only of lifestyle rather than deep meditation.

## dn18:23.7
- **PLI**: So aparena samayena ariyadhammaṁ suṇāti, yoniso manasi karoti, dhammānudhammaṁ paṭipajjati.
- **EN**: After some time they hear the teaching of the noble ones, rationally apply the mind to how it applies to them, and practice in line with the teaching.

## dn18:23.8
- **PLI**: So ariyadhammassavanaṁ āgamma yonisomanasikāraṁ dhammānudhammappaṭipattiṁ asaṁsaṭṭho viharati kāmehi asaṁsaṭṭho akusalehi dhammehi.
- **EN**: They live aloof from sensual pleasures and unskillful qualities.
- **NOTE**: “Aloof” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>asaṁsaṭṭha</i>) is a synonym of “secluded” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>vivicca</i>). The <i lang='pi' translate='no'>jhāna</i> formulas are extremely stable and it is rare to find them played with like this.

## dn18:23.9
- **PLI**: Tassa asaṁsaṭṭhassa kāmehi asaṁsaṭṭhassa akusalehi dhammehi uppajjati sukhaṁ, sukhā bhiyyo somanassaṁ.
- **EN**: That gives rise to pleasure, and more than pleasure, happiness,
- **NOTE**: Since <i lang='pi' translate='no'>jhāna</i> arises from pleasure, it cannot be meant here.

## dn18:23.10
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, bho, pamudā pāmojjaṁ jāyetha;
- **EN**: like the joy that’s born from gladness.

## dn18:23.11
- **PLI**: evameva kho, bho, asaṁsaṭṭhassa kāmehi asaṁsaṭṭhassa akusalehi dhammehi uppajjati sukhaṁ, sukhā bhiyyo somanassaṁ.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn18:23.12
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ kho, bho, tena bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhena paṭhamo okāsādhigamo anubuddho sukhassādhigamāya.
- **EN**: This is the first opportunity for achieving happiness.

## dn18:24.1  `csp1ed7.174, csp2ed7.174, ms7D_634, pts-cs24, pts-vp-pli2.215`
