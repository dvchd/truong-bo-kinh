# 7 — Thấy hai tướng  (37 đoạn)

## dn3:2.11.0
- **PLI**: 7. Dvelakkhaṇādassana
- **EN**: 7. Seeing the Two Marks

## dn3:2.11.1  `csp1ed6.99, csp2ed6.99, km14.253, lv1.287, ms6D_412, msdiv287, ndp6.92, pts-cs2.11, pts-vp-pli1.106`
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā vihārā nikkhamma caṅkamaṁ abbhuṭṭhāsi.
- **EN**: Then the Buddha left his dwelling and proceeded to begin walking mindfully,
- **NOTE**: This transition occurs nowhere else.

## dn3:2.11.2
- **PLI**: Ambaṭṭhopi māṇavo vihārā nikkhamma caṅkamaṁ abbhuṭṭhāsi.
- **EN**: and Ambaṭṭha did likewise.

## dn3:2.11.3
- **PLI**: Atha kho ambaṭṭho māṇavo bhagavantaṁ caṅkamantaṁ anucaṅkamamāno bhagavato kāye dvattiṁsamahāpurisalakkhaṇāni samannesi.
- **EN**: Then while walking beside the Buddha, Ambaṭṭha scrutinized his body for the thirty-two marks of a great man.
- **NOTE**: Finally he remembers what his teacher Pokkharasāti told him in <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn3/en/sujato#1.5.2'>DN 3:1.5.2</a>: he will know the Buddha by his marks.

## dn3:2.11.4
- **PLI**: Addasā kho ambaṭṭho māṇavo bhagavato kāye dvattiṁsamahāpurisalakkhaṇāni yebhuyyena ṭhapetvā dve.
- **EN**: He saw all of them except two,

## dn3:2.11.5
- **PLI**: Dvīsu mahāpurisalakkhaṇesu kaṅkhati vicikicchati nādhimuccati na sampasīdati—
- **EN**: which he had doubts about:

## dn3:2.11.6
- **PLI**: kosohite ca vatthaguyhe pahūtajivhatāya ca.
- **EN**: whether the private parts are sheathed in a foreskin, and the largeness of the tongue.

## dn3:2.12.1  `bj7.214, cck9.135, dr9.140, km14.254, lv1.288, mc9.105, ms6D_413, msdiv288, pts-cs2.12, sya9.136, vri1.93`
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavato etadahosi:
- **EN**: Then it occurred to the Buddha,

## dn3:2.12.2
- **PLI**: “passati kho me ayaṁ ambaṭṭho māṇavo dvattiṁsamahāpurisalakkhaṇāni yebhuyyena ṭhapetvā dve.
- **EN**: “This student Ambaṭṭha sees all the marks except two,

## dn3:2.12.3
- **PLI**: Dvīsu mahāpurisalakkhaṇesu kaṅkhati vicikicchati nādhimuccati na sampasīdati—
- **EN**: which he has doubts about:

## dn3:2.12.4
- **PLI**: kosohite ca vatthaguyhe pahūtajivhatāya cā”ti.
- **EN**: whether the private parts are sheathed in a foreskin, and the largeness of the tongue.”

## dn3:2.12.5
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā tathārūpaṁ iddhābhisaṅkhāraṁ abhisaṅkhāsi. Yathā addasa ambaṭṭho māṇavo bhagavato kosohitaṁ vatthaguyhaṁ.
- **EN**: Then the Buddha used his psychic power to will that Ambaṭṭha would see his private parts sheathed in a foreskin.
- **NOTE**: This exceedingly strange “miracle” is also found at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn91/en/sujato#7.1'>MN 91:7.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn92/en/sujato#14.1'>MN 92:14.1</a>, and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/snp3.7/en/sujato#11.5'>Snp 3.7:11.5</a>.

## dn3:2.12.6
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā jivhaṁ ninnāmetvā ubhopi kaṇṇasotāni anumasi paṭimasi, ubhopi nāsikasotāni anumasi paṭimasi, kevalampi nalāṭamaṇḍalaṁ jivhāya chādesi.
- **EN**: And he stuck out his tongue and stroked back and forth on his ear holes and nostrils, and covered his entire forehead with his tongue.

## dn3:2.12.7
- **PLI**: Atha kho ambaṭṭhassa māṇavassa etadahosi:
- **EN**: Then Ambaṭṭha thought,

## dn3:2.12.8
- **PLI**: “samannāgato kho samaṇo gotamo dvattiṁsamahāpurisalakkhaṇehi paripuṇṇehi, no aparipuṇṇehī”ti.
- **EN**: “The ascetic Gotama possesses the thirty-two marks completely, lacking none.”

## dn3:2.12.9
- **PLI**: Bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: He said to the Buddha,

## dn3:2.12.10
- **PLI**: “handa ca dāni mayaṁ, bho gotama, gacchāma, bahukiccā mayaṁ bahukaraṇīyā”ti.
- **EN**: “Well, now, sir, I must go. I have many duties, and much to do.”

## dn3:2.12.11
- **PLI**: “Yassadāni tvaṁ, ambaṭṭha, kālaṁ maññasī”ti.
- **EN**: “Please, Ambaṭṭha, go at your convenience.”

## dn3:2.12.12
- **PLI**: Atha kho ambaṭṭho māṇavo vaḷavārathamāruyha pakkāmi.
- **EN**: Then Ambaṭṭha mounted his chariot drawn by mares and left.

## dn3:2.13.1  `lv1.289, ms6D_414, msdiv289, pts-cs2.13`
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena brāhmaṇo pokkharasāti ukkaṭṭhāya nikkhamitvā mahatā brāhmaṇagaṇena saddhiṁ sake ārāme nisinno hoti ambaṭṭhaṁyeva māṇavaṁ paṭimānento.
- **EN**: Now at that time the brahmin Pokkharasāti had come out from Ukkaṭṭhā together with a large group of brahmins and was sitting in his own park just waiting for Ambaṭṭha.

## dn3:2.13.2
- **PLI**: Atha kho ambaṭṭho māṇavo yena sako ārāmo tena pāyāsi.
- **EN**: Then Ambaṭṭha entered the park.

## dn3:2.14.1  `bj7.216, cck9.136, csp1ed6.100, csp2ed6.100, dr9.141, km14.255, km14.256, lv1.290, lv1.291, ms6D_415, msdiv290, ndp6.93, pts-cs2.14, pts-vp-pli1.107, sya9.137`
- **PLI**: Yāvatikā yānassa bhūmi, yānena gantvā yānā paccorohitvā pattikova yena brāhmaṇo pokkharasāti tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā brāhmaṇaṁ pokkharasātiṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi. Ekamantaṁ nisinnaṁ kho ambaṭṭhaṁ māṇavaṁ brāhmaṇo pokkharasāti etadavoca:
- **EN**: He went by carriage as far as the terrain allowed, then descended and approached the brahmin Pokkharasāti on foot. He bowed and sat down to one side, and Pokkharasāti said to him:

## dn3:2.14.2
- **PLI**: “kacci, tāta ambaṭṭha, addasa taṁ bhavantaṁ gotaman”ti?
- **EN**: “I hope, dear Ambaṭṭha, you saw the worthy Gotama?”

## dn3:2.14.3
- **PLI**: “Addasāma kho mayaṁ, bho, taṁ bhavantaṁ gotaman”ti.
- **EN**: “I saw him, sir.”

## dn3:2.14.4
- **PLI**: “Kacci, tāta ambaṭṭha, taṁ bhavantaṁ gotamaṁ tathā santaṁyeva saddo abbhuggato no aññathā;
- **EN**: “Well, does he live up to his reputation or not?”

## dn3:2.14.5
- **PLI**: kacci pana so bhavaṁ gotamo tādiso no aññādiso”ti?
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn3:2.14.6
- **PLI**: “Tathā santaṁyeva, bho, taṁ bhavantaṁ gotamaṁ saddo abbhuggato no aññathā, tādisova so bhavaṁ gotamo no aññādiso.
- **EN**: “He does, sir.

## dn3:2.14.7
- **PLI**: Samannāgato ca so bhavaṁ gotamo dvattiṁsamahāpurisalakkhaṇehi paripuṇṇehi no aparipuṇṇehī”ti.
- **EN**: The worthy Gotama possesses the thirty-two marks completely, lacking none.”

## dn3:2.14.8
- **PLI**: “Ahu pana te, tāta ambaṭṭha, samaṇena gotamena saddhiṁ kocideva kathāsallāpo”ti?
- **EN**: “And did you have some discussion with him?”

## dn3:2.14.9
- **PLI**: “Ahu kho me, bho, samaṇena gotamena saddhiṁ kocideva kathāsallāpo”ti.
- **EN**: “I did.”

## dn3:2.14.10
- **PLI**: “Yathā kathaṁ pana te, tāta ambaṭṭha, ahu samaṇena gotamena saddhiṁ kocideva kathāsallāpo”ti?
- **EN**: “And what kind of discussion did you have with him?”

## dn3:2.14.11
- **PLI**: Atha kho ambaṭṭho māṇavo yāvatako ahosi bhagavatā saddhiṁ kathāsallāpo, taṁ sabbaṁ brāhmaṇassa pokkharasātissa ārocesi.
- **EN**: Then Ambaṭṭha informed Pokkharasāti of all they had discussed.

## dn3:2.15.1  `cck9.137, dr9.142, km14.257, lv1.292, mc9.106, ms6D_416, msdiv291, pts-cs2.15, sya9.138`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ vutte, brāhmaṇo pokkharasāti ambaṭṭhaṁ māṇavaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: Then Pokkharasāti said to Ambaṭṭha,

## dn3:2.15.2
- **PLI**: “aho vata re amhākaṁ, paṇḍitaka, aho vata re amhākaṁ, bahussutaka, aho vata re amhākaṁ, tevijjaka, evarūpena kira, bho, puriso atthacarakena kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā apāyaṁ duggatiṁ vinipātaṁ nirayaṁ upapajjeyya.
- **EN**: “Oh, our bloody fake scholar, our fake learned man, who pretends to be proficient in the three Vedas! A man who behaves like this ought, when their body breaks up, after death, to be reborn in a place of loss, a bad place, the underworld, hell.
- **NOTE**: The diminutive ending for <i lang='pi' translate='no'>paṇḍitaka</i> is the same as in <i lang='pi' translate='no'>samaṇaka</i>, which Ambaṭṭha used of the Buddha. | For <i lang='pi' translate='no'>re</i> (“bloody”), compare <i lang='pi' translate='no'>cara pi re</i> at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-bu-vb-pc70/en/sujato#1.35'>Bu Pc 70:1.35</a> and <i lang='pi' translate='no'>he je kāḷī</i> at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn21/en/sujato#9.13'>MN 21:9.13</a>. Hard as the Buddha was on Ambaṭṭha, his own teacher was harder.

## dn3:2.15.3
- **PLI**: Yadeva kho tvaṁ, ambaṭṭha, taṁ bhavantaṁ gotamaṁ evaṁ āsajja āsajja avacāsi, atha kho so bhavaṁ gotamo amhepi evaṁ upaneyya upaneyya avaca.
- **EN**: It’s only because you repeatedly attacked the worthy Gotama like that that he kept bringing up charges against us!”
- **NOTE**: Pokkharasāti shows his astuteness, for in many other dialogues the Buddha engaged with brahmins perfectly politely, as he does in the next sutta (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn4/en/sujato'>DN 4</a>).

## dn3:2.15.4
- **PLI**: Aho vata re amhākaṁ, paṇḍitaka, aho vata re amhākaṁ, bahussutaka, aho vata re amhākaṁ, tevijjaka, evarūpena kira, bho, puriso atthacarakena kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā apāyaṁ duggatiṁ vinipātaṁ nirayaṁ upapajjeyyā”ti, kupito anattamano ambaṭṭhaṁ māṇavaṁ padasāyeva pavattesi.
- **EN**: Angry and upset, he kicked Ambaṭṭha over,
- **NOTE**: Illustrating the lack of restraint of even a senior brahmin teacher.

## dn3:2.15.5
- **PLI**: Icchati ca tāvadeva bhagavantaṁ dassanāya upasaṅkamituṁ.
- **EN**: and wanted to go and see the Buddha right away.
