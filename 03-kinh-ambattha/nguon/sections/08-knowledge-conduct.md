# 4 — Trí tuệ và hạnh tu  (31 đoạn)

## dn3:2.1.0
- **PLI**: 4. Vijjācaraṇakathā
- **EN**: 4. Knowledge and Conduct

## dn3:2.1.1  `bj7.172, cck9.128, dr9.132, km14.241, km14.242, lv1.272, mc9.99, ms6D_385, msdiv278, ndp6.87, pts-cs2.1, pts-vp-pli1.100, sya9.129`
- **PLI**: “Katamaṁ pana taṁ, bho gotama, caraṇaṁ, katamā ca pana sā vijjā”ti?
- **EN**: “But what, worthy Gotama, is that conduct, and what is that knowledge?”
- **NOTE**: To his credit, after that thorough humiliation, Ambaṭṭha is ready to learn.

## dn3:2.1.2
- **PLI**: “Na kho, ambaṭṭha, anuttarāya vijjācaraṇasampadāya jātivādo vā vuccati, gottavādo vā vuccati, mānavādo vā vuccati:
- **EN**: “Ambaṭṭha, in the supreme knowledge and conduct there is no discussion of genealogy or clan or pride—
- **NOTE**: Reading <i lang='pi' translate='no'>anuttarāya vijjācaraṇasampadāya</i> as locative, in agreement with <i lang='pi' translate='no'>yattha</i> below.

## dn3:2.1.3
- **PLI**: ‘arahasi vā maṁ tvaṁ, na vā maṁ tvaṁ arahasī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘You deserve me’ or ‘You don’t deserve me.’

## dn3:2.1.4
- **PLI**: Yattha kho, ambaṭṭha, āvāho vā hoti, vivāho vā hoti, āvāhavivāho vā hoti, etthetaṁ vuccati jātivādo vā itipi gottavādo vā itipi mānavādo vā itipi:
- **EN**: Wherever there is giving and taking in marriage there is such discussion.

## dn3:2.1.5
- **PLI**: ‘arahasi vā maṁ tvaṁ, na vā maṁ tvaṁ arahasī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘You deserve me’ or ‘You don’t deserve me.’  (= dn3:2.1.3)

## dn3:2.1.6
- **PLI**: Ye hi keci, ambaṭṭha, jātivādavinibaddhā vā gottavādavinibaddhā vā mānavādavinibaddhā vā āvāhavivāhavinibaddhā vā, ārakā te anuttarāya vijjācaraṇasampadāya.
- **EN**: Whoever is shackled to questions of genealogy or clan or pride, or to giving and taking in marriage, is far from the supreme knowledge and conduct.

## dn3:2.1.7
- **PLI**: Pahāya kho, ambaṭṭha, jātivādavinibaddhañca gottavādavinibaddhañca mānavādavinibaddhañca āvāhavivāhavinibaddhañca anuttarāya vijjācaraṇasampadāya sacchikiriyā hotī”ti.
- **EN**: The realization of supreme knowledge and conduct occurs when you’ve given up such things.”
- **NOTE**: The Buddha emphasizes that his “knowledge and conduct” rejects the notion of birth that is so essential to Brahmanism.

## dn3:2.2.1  `csp1ed6.94, csp2ed6.94, ms6D_386, msdiv279, pts-cs2.2, vri1.87`

## dn3:2.2.2
- **PLI**: “Idha, ambaṭṭha, tathāgato loke uppajjati arahaṁ sammāsambuddho vijjācaraṇasampanno sugato lokavidū anuttaro purisadammasārathi satthā devamanussānaṁ buddho bhagavā.
- **EN**: “Ambaṭṭha, it’s when a Realized One arises in the world, perfected, a fully awakened Buddha, accomplished in knowledge and conduct, holy, knower of the world, supreme guide for those fit for training, teacher of gods and humans, awakened, blessed.

## dn3:2.2.3
- **PLI**: So imaṁ lokaṁ sadevakaṁ samārakaṁ sabrahmakaṁ sassamaṇabrāhmaṇiṁ pajaṁ sadevamanussaṁ sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā pavedeti.
- **EN**: He has realized with his own insight this world—with its gods, Māras, and divinities, this population with its ascetics and brahmins, gods and humans—and he makes it known to others.

## dn3:2.2.4
- **PLI**: So dhammaṁ deseti ādikalyāṇaṁ majjhekalyāṇaṁ pariyosānakalyāṇaṁ sātthaṁ sabyañjanaṁ kevalaparipuṇṇaṁ parisuddhaṁ brahmacariyaṁ pakāseti.
- **EN**: He teaches Dhamma that’s good in the beginning, good in the middle, and good in the end, meaningful and well-phrased. And he reveals a spiritual practice that’s entirely full and pure.

## dn3:2.2.5
- **PLI**: Taṁ dhammaṁ suṇāti gahapati vā gahapatiputto vā aññatarasmiṁ vā kule paccājāto.
- **EN**: A householder hears that teaching, or a householder’s child, or someone reborn in a good family.

## dn3:2.2.6
- **PLI**: So taṁ dhammaṁ sutvā tathāgate saddhaṁ paṭilabhati.
- **EN**: They gain faith in the Realized One

## dn3:2.2.7
