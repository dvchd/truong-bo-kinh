# 9 — Pokkharasāti xin quy y  (10 đoạn)

## dn3:2.22.0
- **PLI**: 9. Pokkharasātiupāsakattapaṭivedanā
- **EN**: 9. Pokkharasāti Declares Himself a Lay Follower

## dn3:2.22.1  `csp1ed6.103, csp2ed6.103, dr9.146, km14.262, km14.263, lv1.299, lv1.300, mc9.109, ms6D_424, msdiv299, ndp6.96, pts-cs2.22, vri1.96`
- **PLI**: Atha kho brāhmaṇo pokkharasāti diṭṭhadhammo pattadhammo viditadhammo pariyogāḷhadhammo tiṇṇavicikiccho vigatakathaṅkatho vesārajjappatto aparappaccayo satthusāsane bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: Then Pokkharasāti saw, attained, understood, and fathomed the Dhamma. He went beyond doubt, got rid of indecision, and became self-assured and independent of others regarding the Teacher’s instructions. He said to the Buddha,

## dn3:2.22.2
- **PLI**: “abhikkantaṁ, bho gotama, abhikkantaṁ, bho gotama.
- **EN**: “Excellent, worthy Gotama! Excellent!

## dn3:2.22.3
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, bho gotama, nikkujjitaṁ vā ukkujjeyya, paṭicchannaṁ vā vivareyya, mūḷhassa vā maggaṁ ācikkheyya, andhakāre vā telapajjotaṁ dhāreyya: ‘cakkhumanto rūpāni dakkhantī’ti; evamevaṁ bhotā gotamena anekapariyāyena dhammo pakāsito.
- **EN**: As if he were righting the overturned, or revealing the hidden, or pointing out the path to the lost, or lighting a lamp in the dark so people with clear eyes can see what’s there, just so has the worthy Gotama made the Teaching clear in many ways.

## dn3:2.22.4
- **PLI**: Esāhaṁ, bho gotama, saputto sabhariyo sapariso sāmacco bhavantaṁ gotamaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi dhammañca bhikkhusaṅghañca.
- **EN**: Together with my children, wives, retinue, and ministers, I go for refuge to the worthy Gotama, to the teaching, and to the mendicant Saṅgha.

## dn3:2.22.5
- **PLI**: Upāsakaṁ maṁ bhavaṁ gotamo dhāretu ajjatagge pāṇupetaṁ saraṇaṁ gataṁ.
- **EN**: From this day forth, may the worthy Gotama remember me as a lay follower who has gone for refuge for life.

## dn3:2.22.6
- **PLI**: Yathā ca bhavaṁ gotamo ukkaṭṭhāya aññāni upāsakakulāni upasaṅkamati, evameva bhavaṁ gotamo pokkharasātikulaṁ upasaṅkamatu.
- **EN**: Just as the worthy Gotama visits other devoted families in Ukkaṭṭhā, may he visit mine.
- **NOTE**: When wandering for alms, mendicants would often roam randomly through the village (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>sapadānacārī</i>). However if an invitation such as this were issued, the mendicant may visit that place for a meal. It was considered a special ascetic practice to refuse such invitations. The same invitation was issued by Lohicca to Mahākaccāna at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn35.132/en/sujato#14.7'>SN 35.132:14.7</a>.

## dn3:2.22.7
- **PLI**: Tattha ye te māṇavakā vā māṇavikā vā bhavantaṁ gotamaṁ abhivādessanti vā paccuṭṭhissanti vā āsanaṁ vā udakaṁ vā dassanti cittaṁ vā pasādessanti, tesaṁ taṁ bhavissati dīgharattaṁ hitāya sukhāyā”ti.
- **EN**: The brahmin boys and girls there will bow to you, rise in your presence, give you a seat and water, and gain confidence in their hearts. That will be for their lasting welfare and happiness.”
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Māṇavikā</i> is also mentioned at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn56/en/sujato#27.1'>MN 56:27.1</a> and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/ud2.6/en/sujato#1.3'>Ud 2.6:1.3</a> of a young married woman; and at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an5.192/en/sujato#8.5'>AN 5.192:8.5</a> of a baby being born. Thus it does not seem that it meant “female student of the Vedas”.

## dn3:2.22.8
- **PLI**: “Kalyāṇaṁ vuccati, brāhmaṇā”ti.
- **EN**: “That’s nice of you to say, householder.”
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Kalyāṇaṁ vuccati</i> is a politely ambiguous phrase. It is spoken twice elsewhere in the Pali, and both times the mendicant who said it immediately departed and never returned (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn41.3/en/sujato#7.13'>SN 41.3:7.13</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn41.4/en/sujato#6.7'>SN 41.4:6.7</a>).

## dn3:2.22.9
- **PLI**: Ambaṭṭhasuttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ tatiyaṁ.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
