# 3 — Giai cấp sát-đế-lợi là cao quý nhất  (65 đoạn)

## dn3:1.24.0
- **PLI**: 3. Khattiyaseṭṭhabhāva
- **EN**: 3. The Supremacy of the Aristocrats

## dn3:1.24.1  `bj7.168, dr9.128, km14.237, lv1.266, ms6D_376, msdiv275, ndp6.85, pts-cs1.24`
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā ambaṭṭhaṁ māṇavaṁ āmantesi:
- **EN**: Then the Buddha addressed Ambaṭṭha,

## dn3:1.24.2
- **PLI**: “Taṁ kiṁ maññasi, ambaṭṭha,
- **EN**: “What do you think, Ambaṭṭha?

## dn3:1.24.3
- **PLI**: idha khattiyakumāro brāhmaṇakaññāya saddhiṁ saṁvāsaṁ kappeyya, tesaṁ saṁvāsamanvāya putto jāyetha.
- **EN**: Suppose an aristocrat boy was to sleep with a brahmin girl, and they had a son.

## dn3:1.24.4
- **PLI**: Yo so khattiyakumārena brāhmaṇakaññāya putto uppanno, api nu so labhetha brāhmaṇesu āsanaṁ vā udakaṁ vā”ti?
- **EN**: Would he receive a seat and water from the brahmins?”

## dn3:1.24.5
- **PLI**: “Labhetha, bho gotama”.
- **EN**: “He would, worthy Gotama.”

## dn3:1.24.6
- **PLI**: “Api nu naṁ brāhmaṇā bhojeyyuṁ saddhe vā thālipāke vā yaññe vā pāhune vā”ti?
- **EN**: “And would the brahmins feed him at an offering of food for ancestors, an offering of a dish of milk-rice, a sacrifice, or a feast for guests?”

## dn3:1.24.7
- **PLI**: “Bhojeyyuṁ, bho gotama”.
- **EN**: “They would.”

## dn3:1.24.8
- **PLI**: “Api nu naṁ brāhmaṇā mante vāceyyuṁ vā no vā”ti?
- **EN**: “And would the brahmins teach him the hymns or not?”

## dn3:1.24.9
- **PLI**: “Vāceyyuṁ, bho gotama”.
- **EN**: “They would.”

## dn3:1.24.10
- **PLI**: “Api nussa itthīsu āvaṭaṁ vā assa anāvaṭaṁ vā”ti?
- **EN**: “And would he be kept from the women or not?”
- **NOTE**: In <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn56/en/sujato#19.2'>MN 56:19.2</a> <i lang='pi' translate='no'>āvaṭa</i>/<i lang='pi' translate='no'>anāvaṭa</i> is used in reference to Upāli “shutting his gate” against the Jains and opening it for the Buddhists. In <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn17/en/sujato#1.23.2'>DN 17:1.23.2</a> <i lang='pi' translate='no'>anāvaṭa</i> means “open to the public”.

## dn3:1.24.11
- **PLI**: “Anāvaṭaṁ hissa, bho gotama”.
- **EN**: “He would not.”

## dn3:1.24.12
- **PLI**: “Api nu naṁ khattiyā khattiyābhisekena abhisiñceyyun”ti?
- **EN**: “And would the aristocrats anoint him as king?”

## dn3:1.24.13
- **PLI**: “No hidaṁ, bho gotama”.
- **EN**: “No, worthy Gotama.

## dn3:1.24.14
- **PLI**: “Taṁ kissa hetu”?
- **EN**: Why is that?

## dn3:1.24.15
- **PLI**: “Mātito hi, bho gotama, anupapanno”ti.
- **EN**: Because his maternity is unsuitable.”

## dn3:1.28.1  `pts-cs1.28`
- **PLI**: Brahmunā pesā, ambaṭṭha, sanaṅkumārena gāthā bhāsitā:
- **EN**: The divinity Sanaṅkumāra also spoke this verse:
- **NOTE**: Sanaṅkumāra (“Everyoung”) became a Hindu deity closely associated with the worship of Krishna. He first appears in the seventh chapter of the Chāndogya Upaniṣad. There he teaches the learned Nārada what lies beyond the mere surface of words (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>nāma</i>) by giving a progressive meditation that ultimately reveals the highest Self. Thus he is a perfect foil for Ambaṭṭha. The occasion he spoke this verse is recorded at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn6.11/en/sujato'>SN 6.11</a>, and it is repeated several times in the suttas.
