# 2.2–2.3 — Lần thứ hai và thứ ba  (17 đoạn)

## dn3:1.13.0
- **PLI**: 2.2. Dutiyaibbhavāda
- **EN**: 2.2. The Second Use of the Word “Primitives”

## dn3:1.13.1  `bj7.158, cck9.118, dr9.120, km14.225, lv1.249, lv1.250, ms6D_359, msdiv265, pts-cs1.13, vri1.80`
- **PLI**: “Kiṁ pana te, ambaṭṭha, sakyā aparaddhun”ti?
- **EN**: “But Ambaṭṭha, how have the Sakyans wronged you?”

## dn3:1.13.2
- **PLI**: “Ekamidāhaṁ, bho gotama, samayaṁ ācariyassa brāhmaṇassa pokkharasātissa kenacideva karaṇīyena kapilavatthuṁ agamāsiṁ.
- **EN**: “This one time, worthy Gotama, I went to Kapilavatthu on some business for my tutor, the brahmin Pokkharasāti.

## dn3:1.13.3
- **PLI**: Yena sakyānaṁ sandhāgāraṁ tenupasaṅkamiṁ.
- **EN**: I approached the Sakyans in their town hall.

## dn3:1.13.4
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena sambahulā sakyā ceva sakyakumārā ca sandhāgāre uccesu āsanesu nisinnā honti aññamaññaṁ aṅgulipatodakehi sañjagghantā saṅkīḷantā, aññadatthu mamaññeva maññe anujagghantā, na maṁ koci āsanenapi nimantesi.
- **EN**: Now at that time several Sakyans and Sakyan princes were sitting on high seats, poking each other with their fingers, giggling and playing together. In fact, they even presumed to giggle at me, and didn’t invite me to a seat.

## dn3:1.13.5
- **PLI**: Tayidaṁ, bho gotama, nacchannaṁ, tayidaṁ nappatirūpaṁ, yadime sakyā ibbhā santā ibbhā samānā na brāhmaṇe sakkaronti, na brāhmaṇe garuṁ karonti, na brāhmaṇe mānenti, na brāhmaṇe pūjenti, na brāhmaṇe apacāyantī”ti.
- **EN**: It is neither proper nor appropriate that the Sakyans—primitives that they are—don’t honor, respect, revere, worship, or venerate brahmins.”

## dn3:1.13.6
- **PLI**: Itiha ambaṭṭho māṇavo idaṁ dutiyaṁ sakyesu ibbhavādaṁ nipātesi.
- **EN**: And that’s how Ambaṭṭha denigrated the Sakyans with the word “primitives” for the second time.

## dn3:1.14.0
- **PLI**: 2.3. Tatiyaibbhavāda
- **EN**: 2.3. The Third Use of the Word “Primitives”

## dn3:1.14.1  `km14.226, lv1.251, mc9.92, ms6D_360, msdiv266, ndp6.80, pts-cs1.14, pts-vp-pli1.92, sya9.120`
- **PLI**: “Laṭukikāpi kho, ambaṭṭha, sakuṇikā sake kulāvake kāmalāpinī hoti.
- **EN**: “Even a little quail, Ambaṭṭha, speaks as she likes in her own nest.

## dn3:1.14.2
- **PLI**: Sakaṁ kho panetaṁ, ambaṭṭha, sakyānaṁ yadidaṁ kapilavatthuṁ, nārahatāyasmā ambaṭṭho imāya appamattāya abhisajjitun”ti.
- **EN**: Kapilavatthu is the Sakyans’ own place, Ambaṭṭha. It’s not worthy of the Venerable Ambaṭṭha to lose his temper over such a small thing.”
- **NOTE**: The Buddha’s use of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>āyasmā</i> is noteworthy here: he is taking a conciliatory tone.

## dn3:1.14.3
- **PLI**: “Cattārome, bho gotama, vaṇṇā—
- **EN**: “Worthy Gotama, there are these four classes:

## dn3:1.14.4
- **PLI**: khattiyā brāhmaṇā vessā suddā.
- **EN**: aristocrats, brahmins, peasants, and menials.

## dn3:1.14.5
- **PLI**: Imesañhi, bho gotama, catunnaṁ vaṇṇānaṁ tayo vaṇṇā—
- **EN**: Three of these classes—

## dn3:1.14.6
- **PLI**: khattiyā ca vessā ca suddā ca—
- **EN**: aristocrats, peasants, and menials—

## dn3:1.14.7
- **PLI**: aññadatthu brāhmaṇasseva paricārakā sampajjanti.
- **EN**: in fact succeed only when serving the brahmins.

## dn3:1.14.8
- **PLI**: Tayidaṁ, bho gotama, nacchannaṁ, tayidaṁ nappatirūpaṁ, yadime sakyā ibbhā santā ibbhā samānā na brāhmaṇe sakkaronti, na brāhmaṇe garuṁ karonti, na brāhmaṇe mānenti, na brāhmaṇe pūjenti, na brāhmaṇe apacāyantī”ti.
- **EN**: It is neither proper nor appropriate that the Sakyans—primitives that they are—don’t honor, respect, revere, worship, or venerate brahmins.”

## dn3:1.14.9
- **PLI**: Itiha ambaṭṭho māṇavo idaṁ tatiyaṁ sakyesu ibbhavādaṁ nipātesi.
- **EN**: And that’s how Ambaṭṭha denigrated the Sakyans with the word “primitives” for the third time.
