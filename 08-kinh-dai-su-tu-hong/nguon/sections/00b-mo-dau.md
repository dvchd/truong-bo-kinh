## dn8:1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā uruññāyaṁ viharati kaṇṇakatthale migadāye.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying near Ujuññā in the deer park at Kaṇṇakatthala.
- **NOTE**: Ujuññā was a Kosalan town at which King Pasenadi visited the Buddha in <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn90/en/sujato'>MN 90</a>. | “Deer parks” were nature reservations where the animals were safe from hunters.

## dn8:1.3
- **PLI**: Atha kho acelo kassapo yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavatā saddhiṁ sammodi.
- **EN**: Then the naked ascetic Kassapa went up to the Buddha and exchanged greetings with him.
- **NOTE**: Naked ascetics are still found in India today. Some Jains went naked, but if he were a Jain he would have been introduced as such. Kassapa is an ancient clan name of the brahmins, and we meet four naked ascetics named Kassapa in the canon (here, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn12.17/en/sujato'>SN 12.17</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn41.9/en/sujato'>SN 41.9</a>, and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn124/en/sujato'>MN 124</a>). They cannot be the same person, for at the end of each account it is said they went forth and attained arahantship.

## dn8:1.4
- **PLI**: Sammodanīyaṁ kathaṁ sāraṇīyaṁ vītisāretvā ekamantaṁ aṭṭhāsi. Ekamantaṁ ṭhito kho acelo kassapo bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: When the greetings and polite conversation were over, he stood to one side and said to the Buddha:

## dn8:2.1  `pts-cs2`
- **PLI**: “sutaṁ metaṁ, bho gotama:
- **EN**: “Worthy Gotama, I have heard the following:

## dn8:2.2
- **PLI**: ‘samaṇo gotamo sabbaṁ tapaṁ garahati, sabbaṁ tapassiṁ lūkhājīviṁ ekaṁsena upakkosati upavadatī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘The ascetic Gotama criticizes all fervent mortification. He categorically condemns and denounces all fervent mortifiers who live rough.’
- **NOTE**: In his first sermon, the Buddha rejected extremes of self-mortification. There the term was <i lang='pi' translate='no'>attakilamatha</i> (“self-mortification”), whereas here it is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>tapas</i> (“heat, burning, fervor”). These refer to the same practices, but <i lang='pi' translate='no'>tapas</i> points to the fervent ardor of the practitioner, generating an inner heat that “burns off” the corrupting traces of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>kamma</i> and defilements. This topic is also discussed in <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an10.94/en/sujato'>AN 10.94</a>.

## dn8:2.3
- **PLI**: Ye te, bho gotama, evamāhaṁsu: ‘samaṇo gotamo sabbaṁ tapaṁ garahati, sabbaṁ tapassiṁ lūkhājīviṁ ekaṁsena upakkosati upavadatī’ti, kacci te bhoto gotamassa vuttavādino, na ca bhavantaṁ gotamaṁ abhūtena abbhācikkhanti, dhammassa cānudhammaṁ byākaronti, na ca koci sahadhammiko vādānuvādo gārayhaṁ ṭhānaṁ āgacchati?
- **EN**: I trust those who say this repeat what the worthy Gotama has said, and do not misrepresent him with an untruth? Is their explanation in line with the teaching? Are there any legitimate grounds for rebuttal and criticism?

## dn8:2.4
- **PLI**: Anabbhakkhātukāmā hi mayaṁ bhavantaṁ gotaman”ti.
- **EN**: For we don’t want to misrepresent the worthy Gotama.”

## dn8:3.1  `cck9.206, dr9.219, km15.80, ms6D_631, msdiv382, pts-cs3, pts-vp-pli1.162, sya9.206`
- **PLI**: “Ye te, kassapa, evamāhaṁsu: ‘samaṇo gotamo sabbaṁ tapaṁ garahati, sabbaṁ tapassiṁ lūkhājīviṁ ekaṁsena upakkosati upavadatī’ti, na me te vuttavādino, abbhācikkhanti ca pana maṁ te asatā abhūtena.
- **EN**: “Kassapa, those who say this do not repeat what I have said. They misrepresent me with what is false, baseless, and untrue.

## dn8:3.2
- **PLI**: Idhāhaṁ, kassapa, ekaccaṁ tapassiṁ lūkhājīviṁ passāmi dibbena cakkhunā visuddhena atikkantamānusakena kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā apāyaṁ duggatiṁ vinipātaṁ nirayaṁ upapannaṁ.
- **EN**: With clairvoyance that is purified and superhuman, I see some fervent mortifier who lives rough reborn in a place of loss, a bad place, the underworld, hell.

## dn8:3.3
- **PLI**: Idha panāhaṁ, kassapa, ekaccaṁ tapassiṁ lūkhājīviṁ passāmi dibbena cakkhunā visuddhena atikkantamānusakena kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā sugatiṁ saggaṁ lokaṁ upapannaṁ.
- **EN**: But I see another fervent mortifier who lives rough reborn in a good place, a heavenly realm.
- **NOTE**: While the self-mortification itself may be useless, the person who practices it may have other good qualities. The Buddha is cautioning against rash judgment.

## dn8:3.6
- **PLI**: Yohaṁ, kassapa, imesaṁ tapassīnaṁ evaṁ āgatiñca gatiñca cutiñca upapattiñca yathābhūtaṁ pajānāmi, sohaṁ kiṁ sabbaṁ tapaṁ garahissāmi, sabbaṁ vā tapassiṁ lūkhājīviṁ ekaṁsena upakkosissāmi upavadissāmi?
- **EN**: Since I truly understand the coming and going, passing away and rebirth of these fervent mortifiers in this way, how could I criticize all forms of mortification, or categorically condemn and denounce those fervent mortifiers who live rough?
