# Muc 23 — The Probation For One Previously Ordained  (19 đoạn)

## dn8:23.0
- **PLI**: 7. Titthiyaparivāsakathā
- **EN**: 7. The Probation For One Previously Ordained

## dn8:23.1  `bj7.386, dr9.236, km15.106, ms6D_681, msdiv404, ndp6.149, pts-cs23, pts-vp-pli1.176, sya9.221`
- **PLI**: Ekamidāhaṁ, kassapa, samayaṁ rājagahe viharāmi gijjhakūṭe pabbate.
- **EN**: Kassapa, this one time I was staying near Rājagaha, on the Vulture’s Peak Mountain.

## dn8:23.2
- **PLI**: Tatra maṁ aññataro tapabrahmacārī nigrodho nāma adhijegucche pañhaṁ apucchi.
- **EN**: There a certain fervent celibate named Nigrodha asked me about the higher disgust of sin.
- **NOTE**: The Buddha is referring to the events of the Udumbarikasutta (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn25/en/sujato'>DN 25</a>). There Nigrodha is referred to as a “wanderer” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>paribbājaka</i>) who according to the commentary was clothed. The term <i lang='pi' translate='no'>tapabrahmacārī</i> here is unique and is not explained in the commentary. I think it means he was a celibate student of a Brahmanical teacher. <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Tapas</i> and <i lang='pi' translate='no'>brahmacariya</i> are closely linked in Atharvaveda 11.5.

## dn8:23.3
- **PLI**: Tassāhaṁ adhijegucche pañhaṁ puṭṭho byākāsiṁ.
- **EN**: I answered his question.

## dn8:23.4
- **PLI**: Byākate ca pana me attamano ahosi paraṁ viya mattāyā”ti.
- **EN**: He was extremely happy with my answer.”

## dn8:23.5
- **PLI**: “Ko hi, bhante, bhagavato dhammaṁ sutvā na attamano assa paraṁ viya mattāya?
- **EN**: “Sir, who wouldn’t be extremely happy after hearing the Buddha’s teaching?

## dn8:23.6
- **PLI**: Ahampi hi, bhante, bhagavato dhammaṁ sutvā attamano paraṁ viya mattāya.
- **EN**: For I too am extremely happy after hearing the Buddha’s teaching!

## dn8:23.7
- **PLI**: Abhikkantaṁ, bhante, abhikkantaṁ, bhante.
- **EN**: Excellent, sir! Excellent!

## dn8:23.8
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, bhante, nikkujjitaṁ vā ukkujjeyya, paṭicchannaṁ vā vivareyya, mūḷhassa vā maggaṁ ācikkheyya, andhakāre vā telapajjotaṁ dhāreyya: ‘cakkhumanto rūpāni dakkhantī’ti; evamevaṁ bhagavatā anekapariyāyena dhammo pakāsito.
- **EN**: As if he were righting the overturned, or revealing the hidden, or pointing out the path to the lost, or lighting a lamp in the dark so people with clear eyes can see what’s there, so too the Buddha has made the teaching clear in many ways.

## dn8:23.9
- **PLI**: Esāhaṁ, bhante, bhagavantaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi, dhammañca bhikkhusaṅghañca.
- **EN**: I go for refuge to the Buddha, to the teaching, and to the mendicant Saṅgha.

## dn8:23.10
- **PLI**: Labheyyāhaṁ, bhante, bhagavato santike pabbajjaṁ, labheyyaṁ upasampadan”ti.
- **EN**: Sir, may I receive the going forth, the ordination in the Buddha’s presence?”
- **NOTE**: In the early period, there was no distinction between “going forth” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>pabbajjā</i>) and “ordination” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>upasampadā</i>). They refer to two sides of the same coin: leaving the home life and entering the ascetic life. Ordination was originally granted by the Buddha with the simple call, “Come mendicant!” (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#6.32.1'>Kd 1:6.32.1</a>). As the community grew, the Buddha authorized the Saṅgha to perform the “going forth and ordination” (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#12.1.1'>Kd 1:12.1.1</a>). Later still, the novice (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>sāmaṇera</i>) platform was introduced for teenagers and became known as “going forth” (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#54.3.2'>Kd 1:54.3.2</a>). Finally, the novice ordination was standardized for all ordinands, even if it was a mere preliminary for the “full ordination”.

## dn8:24.1  `cck9.222, dr9.237, km15.107, ms6D_682, msdiv405, pts-cs24, vri1.159`
- **PLI**: “Yo kho, kassapa, aññatitthiyapubbo imasmiṁ dhammavinaye ākaṅkhati pabbajjaṁ, ākaṅkhati upasampadaṁ, so cattāro māse parivasati, catunnaṁ māsānaṁ accayena āraddhacittā bhikkhū pabbājenti, upasampādenti bhikkhubhāvāya.
- **EN**: “Kassapa, if someone formerly ordained in another sect wishes to take the going forth, the ordination in this teaching and training, they must spend four months on probation. When four months have passed, if the mendicants are satisfied, they’ll give the going forth, the ordination into monkhood.
- **NOTE**: This probation is laid down in the Vinaya at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#38.1.5'>Kd 1:38.1.5</a>. The candidate shaves, dons the robes, takes refuge, and asks for probation. They must show good conduct and restraint, diligence in duties, and enthusiasm for the Buddha’s teachings and practice.

## dn8:24.2
- **PLI**: Api ca mettha puggalavemattatā viditā”ti.
- **EN**: However, I have recognized individual differences in this matter.”
- **NOTE**: In addition to individual exceptions, there are general exceptions for dreadlocked fire-worshipping ascetics, since they believe in kamma, and for the Buddha’s relatives (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#38.11.3'>Kd 1:38.11.3</a>).

## dn8:24.3
- **PLI**: “Sace, bhante, aññatitthiyapubbā imasmiṁ dhammavinaye ākaṅkhanti pabbajjaṁ, ākaṅkhanti upasampadaṁ, cattāro māse parivasanti, catunnaṁ māsānaṁ accayena āraddhacittā bhikkhū pabbājenti, upasampādenti bhikkhubhāvāya. Ahaṁ cattāri vassāni parivasissāmi, catunnaṁ vassānaṁ accayena āraddhacittā bhikkhū pabbājentu, upasampādentu bhikkhubhāvāyā”ti.
- **EN**: “Sir, if four months probation are required in such a case, I’ll spend four years on probation. When four years have passed, if the mendicants are satisfied, let them give me the going forth, the ordination into monkhood.”

## dn8:24.4  `csp1ed6.166, csp2ed6.166, km15.108, mc9.175, ms6D_683, pts-vp-pli1.177, sya9.222`
- **PLI**: Alattha kho acelo kassapo bhagavato santike pabbajjaṁ, alattha upasampadaṁ.
- **EN**: And the naked ascetic Kassapa received the going forth, the ordination in the Buddha’s presence.

## dn8:24.5
- **PLI**: Acirūpasampanno kho panāyasmā kassapo eko vūpakaṭṭho appamatto ātāpī pahitatto viharanto na cirasseva—yassatthāya kulaputtā sammadeva agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajanti, tadanuttaraṁ—brahmacariyapariyosānaṁ diṭṭheva dhamme sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja vihāsi.
- **EN**: Not long after his ordination, Venerable Kassapa, living alone, withdrawn, diligent, keen, and resolute, soon realized the supreme culmination of the spiritual path in this very life. He lived having achieved with his own insight the goal for which gentlemen rightly go forth from the lay life to homelessness.

## dn8:24.6
- **PLI**: “Khīṇā jāti, vusitaṁ brahmacariyaṁ, kataṁ karaṇīyaṁ, nāparaṁ itthattāyā”ti abbhaññāsi.
- **EN**: He understood: “Rebirth is ended; the spiritual journey has been completed; what had to be done has been done; there is nothing further for this place.”

## dn8:24.7

- **EN**: And Venerable Kassapa became one of the perfected.
