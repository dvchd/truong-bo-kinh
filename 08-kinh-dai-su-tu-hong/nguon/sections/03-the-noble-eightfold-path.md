# Muc 13 — The Noble Eightfold Path  (8 đoạn)

## dn8:13.0
- **PLI**: 2. Ariyaaṭṭhaṅgikamagga
- **EN**: 2. The Noble Eightfold Path

## dn8:13.1  `bj7.364, km15.87, ms6D_643, msdiv393, pts-cs13, sya9.210`
- **PLI**: Atthi, kassapa, maggo atthi paṭipadā, yathāpaṭipanno sāmaññeva ñassati sāmaṁ dakkhati:
- **EN**: There is, Kassapa, a path, there is a practice, practicing in accordance with which you will know and see for yourself:

## dn8:13.2
- **PLI**: ‘samaṇova gotamo kālavādī bhūtavādī atthavādī dhammavādī vinayavādī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Only the ascetic Gotama’s words are timely, true, and meaningful, in line with the teaching and training.’
- **NOTE**: Here <i lang='pi' translate='no'>-va</i> has an exclusive sense (= <i lang='pi' translate='no'>eva</i>). Compare <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dhp274/en/sujato'>Dhp 274</a>: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>eseva maggo natthañño</i> (“This is the path, there is no other”).

## dn8:13.3
- **PLI**: Katamo ca, kassapa, maggo, katamā ca paṭipadā, yathāpaṭipanno sāmaññeva ñassati sāmaṁ dakkhati:
- **EN**: And what is that path?

## dn8:13.4
- **PLI**: ‘samaṇova gotamo kālavādī bhūtavādī atthavādī dhammavādī vinayavādī’ti?
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn8:13.5
- **PLI**: Ayameva ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo.
- **EN**: It is simply this noble eightfold path, that is:

## dn8:13.6
- **PLI**: Seyyathidaṁ—sammādiṭṭhi sammāsaṅkappo sammāvācā sammākammanto sammāājīvo sammāvāyāmo sammāsati sammāsamādhi.
- **EN**: right view, right purpose, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, and right immersion.

## dn8:13.7
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ kho, kassapa, maggo, ayaṁ paṭipadā, yathāpaṭipanno sāmaññeva ñassati sāmaṁ dakkhati ‘samaṇova gotamo kālavādī bhūtavādī atthavādī dhammavādī vinayavādī’”ti.
- **EN**: This is the path, this is the practice, practicing in accordance with which you will know and see for yourself: ‘Only the ascetic Gotama’s words are timely, true, and meaningful, in line with the teaching and training.’”
- **NOTE**: Compare <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#5.27.1'>DN 16:5.27.1</a>.
