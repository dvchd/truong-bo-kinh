- **EN**: but if they haven’t developed and realized any accomplishment in ethics, mind, and wisdom, they are far from being a true ascetic or brahmin.
- **NOTE**: The term “accomplishment in mind” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>cittasampadā</i>) is equivalent to “accomplishment in immersion” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>samādhisampadā</i>). More generally, when <i lang='pi' translate='no'>citta</i> is used in the context of meditation, it is normally a synonym of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>samādhi</i>.

## dn8:15.3
- **PLI**: Yato kho, kassapa, bhikkhu averaṁ abyāpajjaṁ mettacittaṁ bhāveti, āsavānañca khayā anāsavaṁ cetovimuttiṁ paññāvimuttiṁ diṭṭheva dhamme sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja viharati.
- **EN**: But take a mendicant who develops a heart of love, free of enmity and ill will. And they realize the undefiled freedom of heart and freedom by wisdom, and live having realized it with their own insight in this very life due to the ending of defilements.
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Mettā</i> is universal love and good will free from attachment. As well as being a foundation for good character and healthy emotional development, it serves to lead the mind into deep meditation of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>jhāna</i>.

## dn8:15.4
