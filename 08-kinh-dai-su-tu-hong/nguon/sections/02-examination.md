# Muc 5 — Examination  (31 đoạn)

## dn8:5.0
- **PLI**: 1. Samanuyuñjāpanakathā
- **EN**: 1. Examination

## dn8:5.1  `ms6D_635, msdiv385, pts-cs5`
- **PLI**: Tyāhaṁ upasaṅkamitvā evaṁ vadāmi:
- **EN**: I go up to them and say:

## dn8:5.2
- **PLI**: ‘yesu no, āvuso, ṭhānesu na sameti, tiṭṭhantu tāni ṭhānāni.
- **EN**: ‘Let us leave aside those matters on which we disagree.
- **NOTE**: Again we see the Buddha’s preferred method of establishing common ground first, then building an argument from there.

## dn8:5.3
- **PLI**: Yesu ṭhānesu sameti, tattha viññū samanuyuñjantaṁ samanugāhantaṁ samanubhāsantaṁ satthārā vā satthāraṁ saṅghena vā saṅghaṁ:
- **EN**: But there are some matters on which we agree. Regarding these, sensible people, pursuing, pressing, and grilling, would compare teacher with teacher or community with community:

## dn8:5.4
- **PLI**: “ye imesaṁ bhavataṁ dhammā akusalā akusalasaṅkhātā, sāvajjā sāvajjasaṅkhātā, asevitabbā asevitabbasaṅkhātā, na alamariyā na alamariyasaṅkhātā, kaṇhā kaṇhasaṅkhātā.
- **EN**: “There are things that are unskillful, blameworthy, not to be cultivated, unworthy of the noble ones, and dark—and are reckoned as such.

## dn8:5.5
- **PLI**: Ko ime dhamme anavasesaṁ pahāya vattati, samaṇo vā gotamo, pare vā pana bhonto gaṇācariyā”ti?
- **EN**: Who proceeds having totally given these things up: the ascetic Gotama, or the tutors of other communities?”’
- **NOTE**: Rather than logical hair-splitting, the Buddha recommends looking at a person’s conduct.

## dn8:6.1  `bj7.360, csp1ed6.155, csp2ed6.155, dr9.221, km15.83, mc9.164, ms6D_636, msdiv386, pts-cs6, vri1.148`

- **EN**: It’s possible that they might say:

## dn8:6.2

## dn8:6.3
- **PLI**: Samaṇo gotamo ime dhamme anavasesaṁ pahāya vattati, yaṁ vā pana bhonto pare gaṇācariyā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘The ascetic Gotama proceeds having totally given those unskillful things up, compared with the tutors of other communities.’

## dn8:6.4
- **PLI**: Itiha, kassapa, viññū samanuyuñjantā samanugāhantā samanubhāsantā amheva tattha yebhuyyena pasaṁseyyuṁ.
- **EN**: And that’s how, when sensible people pursue the matter, they will mostly praise us.
