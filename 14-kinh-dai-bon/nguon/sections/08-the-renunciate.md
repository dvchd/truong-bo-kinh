# Muc 2.12 — The Renunciate  (29 đoạn)

## dn14:2.12.0

- **EN**: 8. The Renunciate

## dn14:2.12.1  `cck10.35, ms7D_105, msdiv52, pts-cs2.12, pts-vp-pli2.28, sya10.32`
- **PLI**: Atha kho, bhikkhave, bandhumassa rañño etadahosi:
- **EN**: Then King Bandhumā thought,

## dn14:2.12.2
- **PLI**: ‘mā heva kho vipassī kumāro na rajjaṁ kāresi, mā heva vipassī kumāro agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbaji, mā heva nemittānaṁ brāhmaṇānaṁ saccaṁ assa vacanan’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Prince Vipassī must not renounce the throne. He must not go forth from the lay life to homelessness. And the words of the brahmin soothsayers must not come true.’

## dn14:2.12.3
- **PLI**: Atha kho, bhikkhave, bandhumā rājā vipassissa kumārassa bhiyyoso mattāya pañca kāmaguṇāni upaṭṭhāpesi:

## dn14:2.13.7
- **PLI**: Atha kho, bhikkhave, vipassī kumāro bhaddaṁ bhaddaṁ yānaṁ abhiruhitvā bhaddehi bhaddehi yānehi uyyānabhūmiṁ niyyāsi.
- **EN**: Then Prince Vipassī mounted a fine carriage and, along with other fine carriages, set out for the park.  (= dn14:2.1.6)

## dn14:2.14.1  `bj8.44, cck10.36, dr10.34, mc10.25, ms7D_107, msdiv53, ndp7.24, pts-cs2.14, pts-vp-pli2.29, sya10.33, vri2.22`
- **PLI**: Addasā kho, bhikkhave, vipassī kumāro uyyānabhūmiṁ niyyanto purisaṁ bhaṇḍuṁ pabbajitaṁ kāsāyavasanaṁ.
- **EN**: Along the way he saw a man, a renunciate with shaven head, wearing an ocher robe.
- **NOTE**: “Renunciate” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>pabbajita</i> (“one who has gone forth”), one of the many words for religious ascetics. It is a general term and does not specify his affiliation.

## dn14:2.14.2
- **PLI**: Disvā sārathiṁ āmantesi:
- **EN**: He addressed his charioteer,

## dn14:2.14.3
- **PLI**: ‘ayaṁ pana, samma sārathi, puriso kiṅkato?
- **EN**: ‘My dear charioteer, what has that man done?

## dn14:2.14.4
- **PLI**: Sīsampissa na yathā aññesaṁ, vatthānipissa na yathā aññesan’ti?
- **EN**: For his head and his clothes are unlike those of other men.’

## dn14:2.14.5
- **PLI**: ‘Eso kho, deva, pabbajito nāmā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘That, Your Majesty, is called a renunciate.’

## dn14:2.14.6
- **PLI**: ‘Kiṁ paneso, samma sārathi, pabbajito nāmā’ti?
- **EN**: ‘But why is he called a renunciate?’

## dn14:2.14.7
- **PLI**: ‘Eso kho, deva, pabbajito nāma sādhu dhammacariyā sādhu samacariyā sādhu kusalakiriyā sādhu puññakiriyā sādhu avihiṁsā sādhu bhūtānukampā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘He is called a renunciate because he celebrates principled and fair conduct, skillful actions, good deeds, harmlessness, and sympathy for living creatures.’
- **NOTE**: “Celebrate” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>sādhu</i>, the famous Buddhist words of approval and rejoicing still heard every day in Theravāda Buddhist cultures. It later acquired the meaning “renunciate” but does not have that sense in early Pali. The virtues that he celebrates are common values of Indian religions.

## dn14:2.14.8
- **PLI**: ‘Sādhu kho so, samma sārathi, pabbajito nāma, sādhu dhammacariyā sādhu samacariyā sādhu kusalakiriyā sādhu puññakiriyā sādhu avihiṁsā sādhu bhūtānukampā.
- **EN**: ‘Then I celebrate the one called a renunciate, who celebrates principled and fair conduct, skillful actions, good deeds, harmlessness, and sympathy for living creatures!

## dn14:2.14.9
- **PLI**: Tena hi, samma sārathi, yena so pabbajito tena rathaṁ pesehī’ti.
- **EN**: Well then, drive the chariot up to that renunciate.’

## dn14:2.14.10
- **PLI**: ‘Evaṁ, devā’ti kho, bhikkhave, sārathi vipassissa kumārassa paṭissutvā yena so pabbajito tena rathaṁ pesesi.
- **EN**: ‘Yes, Your Majesty,’ replied the charioteer, and did so.

## dn14:2.14.11
- **PLI**: Atha kho, bhikkhave, vipassī kumāro taṁ pabbajitaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: Then Prince Vipassī said to that renunciate,

## dn14:2.14.12
- **PLI**: ‘tvaṁ pana, samma, kiṅkato,
- **EN**: ‘My dear, what have you done?

## dn14:2.14.13
- **PLI**: sīsampi te na yathā aññesaṁ, vatthānipi te na yathā aññesan’ti?
- **EN**: For your head and your clothes are unlike those of other men.’

## dn14:2.14.14
- **PLI**: ‘Ahaṁ kho, deva, pabbajito nāmā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Your Majesty, I am what is called a renunciate.’

## dn14:2.14.15
- **PLI**: ‘Kiṁ pana tvaṁ, samma, pabbajito nāmā’ti?
- **EN**: ‘But why are you called a renunciate?’

## dn14:2.14.16
- **PLI**: ‘Ahaṁ kho, deva, pabbajito nāma, sādhu dhammacariyā sādhu samacariyā sādhu kusalakiriyā sādhu puññakiriyā sādhu avihiṁsā sādhu bhūtānukampā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘I am called a renunciate because I celebrate principled and fair conduct, skillful actions, good deeds, harmlessness, and sympathy for living creatures.’

## dn14:2.14.17
- **PLI**: ‘Sādhu kho tvaṁ, samma, pabbajito nāma sādhu dhammacariyā sādhu samacariyā sādhu kusalakiriyā sādhu puññakiriyā sādhu avihiṁsā sādhu bhūtānukampā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Then I celebrate the one called a renunciate, who celebrates principled and fair conduct, skillful actions, good deeds, harmlessness, and sympathy for living creatures!’
