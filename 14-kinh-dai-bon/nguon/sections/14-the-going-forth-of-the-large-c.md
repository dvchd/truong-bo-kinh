# Muc 3.14 — The Going Forth of the Large Crowd  (24 đoạn)

## dn14:3.14.0
- **PLI**: 14. Mahājanakāyapabbajjā
- **EN**: 14. The Going Forth of the Large Crowd

## dn14:3.14.1  `bj8.64, cck10.53, dr10.49, mc10.38, ms7D_163, msdiv78, ndp7.35, pts-cs3.14, pts-vp-pli2.43, sya10.49`
- **PLI**: Assosi kho, bhikkhave, bandhumatiyā rājadhāniyā mahājanakāyo caturāsītipāṇasahassāni:
- **EN**: A large crowd of 84,000 people in the capital of Bandhumatī heard

## dn14:3.14.2
- **PLI**: ‘vipassī kira bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho bandhumatiṁ rājadhāniṁ anuppatto kheme migadāye viharati.
- **EN**: that the Blessed One Vipassī, the perfected one, the fully awakened Buddha, had arrived at Bandhumatī and was staying in the deer park named Sanctuary.

## dn14:3.14.3
- **PLI**: Khaṇḍo ca kira rājaputto tisso ca purohitaputto vipassissa bhagavato arahato sammāsambuddhassa santike kesamassuṁ ohāretvā kāsāyāni vatthāni acchādetvā agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajitā’ti.
- **EN**: And they heard that the king’s son Khaṇḍa and the high priest’s son Tissa had shaved off their hair and beard, dressed in ocher robes, and gone forth from the lay life to homelessness in the Buddha’s presence.

## dn14:3.14.4
- **PLI**: Sutvāna nesaṁ etadahosi:
- **EN**: It occurred to them,

## dn14:3.14.5
- **PLI**: ‘na hi nūna so orako dhammavinayo, na sā orakā pabbajjā, yattha khaṇḍo ca rājaputto tisso ca purohitaputto kesamassuṁ ohāretvā kāsāyāni vatthāni acchādetvā agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajitā.
- **EN**: ‘This must be no ordinary teaching and training, no ordinary going forth in which the king’s son Khaṇḍa and the high priest’s son Tissa have gone forth.

## dn14:3.14.6
- **PLI**: Khaṇḍo ca rājaputto tisso ca purohitaputto kesamassuṁ ohāretvā kāsāyāni vatthāni acchādetvā agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajissanti, kimaṅgaṁ pana mayan’ti.
- **EN**: If even they go forth, why don’t we do the same?’

## dn14:3.14.7
- **PLI**: Atha kho so, bhikkhave, mahājanakāyo caturāsītipāṇasahassāni bandhumatiyā rājadhāniyā nikkhamitvā yena khemo migadāyo yena vipassī bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho tenupasaṅkamiṁsu; upasaṅkamitvā vipassiṁ bhagavantaṁ arahantaṁ sammāsambuddhaṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ nisīdiṁsu.
- **EN**: Then those 84,000 people left Bandhumatī for the deer park named Sanctuary, where they approached the Buddha Vipassī, bowed and sat down to one side.

## dn14:3.15.1  `cck10.54, csp1ed7.37, csp2ed7.37, dr10.50, ms7D_164, msdiv79, pts-cs3.15, vri2.34`
- **PLI**: Tesaṁ vipassī bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho anupubbiṁ kathaṁ kathesi.
- **EN**: The Buddha Vipassī taught them step by step, with

## dn14:3.15.2
- **PLI**: Seyyathidaṁ—dānakathaṁ sīlakathaṁ saggakathaṁ kāmānaṁ ādīnavaṁ okāraṁ saṅkilesaṁ nekkhamme ānisaṁsaṁ pakāsesi.
- **EN**: a talk on giving, ethical conduct, and heaven. He revealed the drawbacks of sensual pleasures, so sordid and corrupt, and the benefit of renunciation.

## dn14:3.15.3
- **PLI**: Yadā te bhagavā aññāsi kallacitte muducitte vinīvaraṇacitte udaggacitte pasannacitte, atha yā buddhānaṁ sāmukkaṁsikā dhammadesanā, taṁ pakāsesi—
- **EN**: And when he knew that their minds were ready, pliable, rid of hindrances, elated, and confident he revealed the special teaching of the Buddhas:

## dn14:3.15.4
- **PLI**: dukkhaṁ samudayaṁ nirodhaṁ maggaṁ.
- **EN**: suffering, its origin, its cessation, and the path.

## dn14:3.15.5
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi nāma suddhaṁ vatthaṁ apagatakāḷakaṁ sammadeva rajanaṁ paṭiggaṇheyya;
- **EN**: Just as a clean cloth rid of stains would properly absorb dye,

## dn14:3.15.6
- **PLI**: evameva tesaṁ caturāsītipāṇasahassānaṁ tasmiṁyeva āsane virajaṁ vītamalaṁ dhammacakkhuṁ udapādi:
- **EN**: in that very seat the stainless, immaculate vision of the Dhamma arose in those 84,000 people:

## dn14:3.15.7
- **PLI**: ‘yaṁ kiñci samudayadhammaṁ sabbaṁ taṁ nirodhadhamman’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Everything that is liable to arise is liable to cease.’

- **EN**: They saw, attained, understood, and fathomed the Dhamma. They went beyond doubt, got rid of indecision, and became self-assured and independent of others regarding the Teacher’s instructions. They said to the Buddha Vipassī,
