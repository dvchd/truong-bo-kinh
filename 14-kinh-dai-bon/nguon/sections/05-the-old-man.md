# Muc 2.1 — The Old Man  (36 đoạn)

## dn14:2.1.0
- **PLI**: 5. Jiṇṇapurisa
- **EN**: 5. The Old Man

## dn14:2.1.1  `bj8.32, dr10.24, mc10.19, ms7D_92, msdiv43, ndp7.18, pts-cs2.1, vri2.17`
- **PLI**: Atha kho, bhikkhave, vipassī kumāro bahūnaṁ vassānaṁ bahūnaṁ vassasatānaṁ bahūnaṁ vassasahassānaṁ accayena sārathiṁ āmantesi:
- **EN**: Then, after many years, many hundred years, many thousand years had passed, Prince Vipassī addressed his charioteer,
- **NOTE**: Here begins the story of the four signs that led to Vipassī’s going forth—an old man, a sick man, a dead man, and a renunciate. Later texts apply the same story to Siddhattha, indeed to all bodhisattvas. | These four “signs” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>nimitta</i>) are also called “messengers of the gods”, reckoned as five (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn130/en/sujato'>MN 130</a>) or three (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/an3.36/en/sujato'>AN 3.36</a>).

## dn14:2.1.2
- **PLI**: ‘yojehi, samma sārathi, bhaddāni bhaddāni yānāni uyyānabhūmiṁ gacchāma subhūmidassanāyā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘My dear charioteer, harness the finest chariots. We will go to a park and see the scenery.’

## dn14:2.1.3
- **PLI**: ‘Evaṁ, devā’ti kho, bhikkhave, sārathi vipassissa kumārassa paṭissutvā bhaddāni bhaddāni yānāni yojetvā vipassissa kumārassa paṭivedesi:
- **EN**: ‘Yes, sir,’ replied the charioteer. He harnessed the chariots and informed the prince,

## dn14:2.1.4
- **PLI**: ‘yuttāni kho te, deva, bhaddāni bhaddāni yānāni,
- **EN**: ‘Your Majesty, the finest chariots are harnessed.

## dn14:2.1.5
- **PLI**: yassadāni kālaṁ maññasī’ti.
- **EN**: Please go at your convenience.’

## dn14:2.1.6
- **PLI**: Atha kho, bhikkhave, vipassī kumāro bhaddaṁ bhaddaṁ yānaṁ abhiruhitvā bhaddehi bhaddehi yānehi uyyānabhūmiṁ niyyāsi.
- **EN**: Then Prince Vipassī mounted a fine carriage and, along with other fine carriages, set out for the park.

## dn14:2.2.1  `cck10.27, csp1ed7.19, csp2ed7.19, dr10.25, ms7D_93, msdiv44, pts-cs2.2, pts-vp-pli2.22, sya10.25`
- **PLI**: Addasā kho, bhikkhave, vipassī kumāro uyyānabhūmiṁ niyyanto purisaṁ jiṇṇaṁ gopānasivaṅkaṁ bhoggaṁ daṇḍaparāyanaṁ pavedhamānaṁ gacchantaṁ āturaṁ gatayobbanaṁ.
- **EN**: Along the way he saw an elderly man, bent double, crooked, leaning on a staff, trembling as he walked, ailing, past his prime.

## dn14:2.2.2
- **PLI**: Disvā sārathiṁ āmantesi:
- **EN**: He addressed his charioteer,

## dn14:2.2.3
- **PLI**: ‘ayaṁ pana, samma sārathi, puriso kiṅkato?
- **EN**: ‘My dear charioteer, what has that man done?

## dn14:2.2.4
- **PLI**: Kesāpissa na yathā aññesaṁ, kāyopissa na yathā aññesan’ti.
- **EN**: For his hair and his body are unlike those of other men.’

## dn14:2.2.5
- **PLI**: ‘Eso kho, deva, jiṇṇo nāmā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘That, Your Majesty, is called an old man.’

## dn14:2.2.6
- **PLI**: ‘Kiṁ paneso, samma sārathi, jiṇṇo nāmā’ti?
- **EN**: ‘But why is he called an old man?’

## dn14:2.2.7
- **PLI**: ‘Eso kho, deva, jiṇṇo nāma. Na dāni tena ciraṁ jīvitabbaṁ bhavissatī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘He’s called an old man because now he has not long to live.’

## dn14:2.2.8
- **PLI**: ‘Kiṁ pana, samma sārathi, ahampi jarādhammo, jaraṁ anatīto’ti?
- **EN**: ‘But my dear charioteer, am I liable to grow old? Am I not exempt from old age?’

## dn14:2.2.9
- **PLI**: ‘Tvañca, deva, mayañcamha sabbe jarādhammā, jaraṁ anatītā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Everyone is liable to grow old, Your Majesty, including you. No one is exempt from old age.’

## dn14:2.2.10
- **PLI**: ‘Tena hi, samma sārathi, alaṁ dānajja uyyānabhūmiyā. Itova antepuraṁ paccaniyyāhī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Well then, my dear charioteer, that’s enough of the park for today. Let’s return to the royal compound.’
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Antepuraṁ</i> (“royal compound”) was the inner sanctum of the royal residence. Maximally it referred to the area enclosed by walls within which the ruling families and staff lived. It later became used in the more restricted sense of “harem”.

## dn14:2.2.11
- **PLI**: ‘Evaṁ, devā’ti kho, bhikkhave, sārathi vipassissa kumārassa paṭissutvā tatova antepuraṁ paccaniyyāsi.
- **EN**: ‘Yes, Your Majesty,’ replied the charioteer and did so.

## dn14:2.2.12
- **PLI**: Tatra sudaṁ, bhikkhave, vipassī kumāro antepuraṁ gato dukkhī dummano pajjhāyati:
- **EN**: Back at the royal compound, the prince brooded, miserable and sad:
- **NOTE**: “Brood” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>pajjhāyati</i>. He is having an existential crisis.

## dn14:2.2.13
- **PLI**: ‘dhiratthu kira, bho, jāti nāma, yatra hi nāma jātassa jarā paññāyissatī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Curse this thing called rebirth, since old age will come to anyone who’s born.’
