# Muc 2.16 — A Great Crowd Goes Forth  (11 đoạn)

## dn14:2.16.0
- **PLI**: 10. Mahājanakāyaanupabbajjā
- **EN**: 10. A Great Crowd Goes Forth

## dn14:2.16.1  `dr10.35, mc10.26, ms7D_109, msdiv55, ndp7.25, pts-cs2.16, pts-vp-pli2.30, vri2.23`
- **PLI**: Assosi kho, bhikkhave, bandhumatiyā rājadhāniyā mahājanakāyo caturāsītipāṇasahassāni:
- **EN**: A large crowd of 84,000 people in the capital of Bandhumatī heard

## dn14:2.16.2
- **PLI**: ‘vipassī kira kumāro kesamassuṁ ohāretvā kāsāyāni vatthāni acchādetvā agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajito’ti.
- **EN**: that Vipassī had gone forth.

## dn14:2.16.3
- **PLI**: Sutvāna tesaṁ etadahosi:
- **EN**: It occurred to them,

## dn14:2.16.4
- **PLI**: ‘na hi nūna so orako dhammavinayo, na sā orakā pabbajjā, yattha vipassī kumāro kesamassuṁ ohāretvā kāsāyāni vatthāni acchādetvā agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajito.
- **EN**: ‘This must be no ordinary teaching and training, no ordinary going forth in which Prince Vipassī has gone forth.

## dn14:2.16.5
- **PLI**: Vipassīpi nāma kumāro kesamassuṁ ohāretvā kāsāyāni vatthāni acchādetvā agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajissati, kimaṅgaṁ pana mayan’ti.
- **EN**: If even the prince goes forth, why don’t we do the same?’

## dn14:2.16.6  `ms7D_110`
- **PLI**: Atha kho, so bhikkhave, mahājanakāyo caturāsītipāṇasahassāni kesamassuṁ ohāretvā kāsāyāni vatthāni acchādetvā vipassiṁ bodhisattaṁ agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajitaṁ anupabbajiṁsu.
- **EN**: Then that great crowd of 84,000 people shaved off their hair and beard, dressed in ocher robes, and followed the one intent on awakening, Vipassī, by going forth from the lay life to homelessness.
- **NOTE**: The idea that a whole populace would follow the bodhisatta on his renunciate path occurs several times in the Jātakas.

## dn14:2.16.7
- **PLI**: Tāya sudaṁ, bhikkhave, parisāya parivuto vipassī bodhisatto gāmanigamajanapadarājadhānīsu cārikaṁ carati.
- **EN**: Escorted by that assembly, Vipassī wandered on tour among the villages, towns, and capital cities.
- **NOTE**: Following the PTS edition in omitting <i lang='pi' translate='no'>janapada</i>, which is absent from the commentary and the parallel passage at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn19/en/sujato#58.3'>DN 19:58.3</a>.

## dn14:2.17.1  `cck10.38, csp1ed7.26, csp2ed7.26, ms7D_111, msdiv56, pts-cs2.17, sya10.35`
- **PLI**: Atha kho, bhikkhave, vipassissa bodhisattassa rahogatassa paṭisallīnassa evaṁ cetaso parivitakko udapādi:
- **EN**: Then as he was in private retreat this thought came to his mind,

## dn14:2.17.2
- **PLI**: ‘na kho metaṁ patirūpaṁ yohaṁ ākiṇṇo viharāmi, yannūnāhaṁ eko gaṇamhā vūpakaṭṭho vihareyyan’ti.
- **EN**: ‘It’s not appropriate for me to live in a crowd. Why don’t I live alone, withdrawn from the group?’

## dn14:2.17.3
- **PLI**: Atha kho, bhikkhave, vipassī bodhisatto aparena samayena eko gaṇamhā vūpakaṭṭho vihāsi, aññeneva tāni caturāsītipabbajitasahassāni agamaṁsu, aññena maggena vipassī bodhisatto.
- **EN**: After some time he withdrew from the group to live alone. The 84,000 went one way, but Vipassī went another.
