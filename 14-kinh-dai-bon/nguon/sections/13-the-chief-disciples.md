# Muc 3.8 — The Chief Disciples  (31 đoạn)

## dn14:3.8.0
- **PLI**: 13. Aggasāvakayuga
- **EN**: 13. The Chief Disciples

## dn14:3.8.1  `bj8.60, cck10.50, ms7D_157, msdiv72, ndp7.33, pts-cs3.8, sya10.46`
- **PLI**: Atha kho, bhikkhave, vipassissa bhagavato arahato sammāsambuddhassa etadahosi:
- **EN**: Then it occurred to the Blessed One Vipassī, the perfected one, the fully awakened Buddha,

## dn14:3.8.2
- **PLI**: ‘kassa nu kho ahaṁ paṭhamaṁ dhammaṁ deseyyaṁ,
- **EN**: ‘Who should I teach first of all?

## dn14:3.8.3
- **PLI**: ko imaṁ dhammaṁ khippameva ājānissatī’ti?
- **EN**: Who will quickly understand this teaching?’

## dn14:3.8.4
- **PLI**: Atha kho, bhikkhave, vipassissa bhagavato arahato sammāsambuddhassa etadahosi:
- **EN**: Then he thought,

## dn14:3.8.5
- **PLI**: ‘ayaṁ kho khaṇḍo ca rājaputto tisso ca purohitaputto bandhumatiyā rājadhāniyā paṭivasanti paṇḍitā viyattā medhāvino dīgharattaṁ apparajakkhajātikā.
- **EN**: ‘That Khaṇḍa, the king’s son, and Tissa, the high priest’s son, are astute, competent, intelligent, and have long had little dust in their eyes.
- **NOTE**: In the account of Gotama, he first thinks to teach his former colleagues under whom he practiced the formless attainments. Here we see the start of a tendency in the legends of past Buddhas to erase the education among other spiritual teachers before awakening. Note that these two, who will become Vipassī’s chief disciples, are leading <i lang='pi' translate='no'>khattiya</i> and brahmin sons of the royal household. Khaṇḍa was Vipassī’s brother, and the priest’s son was virtually family.

## dn14:3.8.6
- **PLI**: Yannūnāhaṁ khaṇḍassa ca rājaputtassa, tissassa ca purohitaputtassa paṭhamaṁ dhammaṁ deseyyaṁ, te imaṁ dhammaṁ khippameva ājānissantī’ti.
- **EN**: Why don’t I teach them first of all? They will quickly understand this teaching.’

- **EN**: ‘My dear park keeper, please enter the city and say this to the king’s son Khaṇḍa and the high priest’s son Tissa:

## dn14:3.9.4

- **EN**: “Sirs, the Blessed One Vipassī, the perfected one, the fully awakened Buddha, has arrived at Bandhumatī and is staying in the deer park named Sanctuary. He wishes to see you.”’

## dn14:3.9.5

## dn14:3.9.6

## dn14:3.13.1  `ms7D_162, msdiv77, pts-cs3.13, sya10.48`
- **PLI**: Alatthuṁ kho, bhikkhave, khaṇḍo ca rājaputto, tisso ca purohitaputto vipassissa bhagavato arahato sammāsambuddhassa santike pabbajjaṁ alatthuṁ upasampadaṁ.
- **EN**: And they received the going forth, the ordination in the Buddha Vipassī’s presence.
- **NOTE**: In the early period, there was no distinction between “going forth” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>pabbajjā</i>) and “ordination” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>upasampadā</i>). They refer to two sides of the same coin: leaving the home life and entering the ascetic life. Ordination was originally granted with the simple call, “Come mendicant!” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>ehi bhikkhu</i>).

## dn14:3.13.2
- **PLI**: Te vipassī bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho dhammiyā kathāya sandassesi samādapesi samuttejesi sampahaṁsesi;
- **EN**: Then the Buddha Vipassī educated, encouraged, fired up, and inspired them with a Dhamma talk.

## dn14:3.13.3
- **PLI**: saṅkhārānaṁ ādīnavaṁ okāraṁ saṅkilesaṁ nibbāne ānisaṁsaṁ pakāsesi.
- **EN**: He revealed the drawbacks of conditioned phenomena, so sordid and corrupt, and the benefit of extinguishment.

## dn14:3.13.4
- **PLI**: Tesaṁ vipassinā bhagavatā arahatā sammāsambuddhena dhammiyā kathāya sandassiyamānānaṁ samādapiyamānānaṁ samuttejiyamānānaṁ sampahaṁsiyamānānaṁ nacirasseva anupādāya āsavehi cittāni vimucciṁsu.
- **EN**: Being taught like this their minds were soon freed from defilements by not grasping.
- **NOTE**: Thus they became arahants, realizing the same truth that Vipassī had.
