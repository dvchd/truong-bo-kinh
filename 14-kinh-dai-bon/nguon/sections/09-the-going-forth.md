# Muc 2.15 — The Going Forth  (6 đoạn)

## dn14:2.15.0
- **PLI**: 9. Bodhisattapabbajjā
- **EN**: 9. The Going Forth

## dn14:2.15.1  `cck10.37, csp1ed7.25, csp2ed7.25, ms7D_108, msdiv54, pts-cs2.15, sya10.34`
- **PLI**: Atha kho, bhikkhave, vipassī kumāro sārathiṁ āmantesi:
- **EN**: Then the prince addressed the charioteer,

## dn14:2.15.2
- **PLI**: ‘tena hi, samma sārathi, rathaṁ ādāya itova antepuraṁ paccaniyyāhi.
- **EN**: ‘Well then, my dear charioteer, take the chariot and return to the royal compound.

## dn14:2.15.3
- **PLI**: Ahaṁ pana idheva kesamassuṁ ohāretvā kāsāyāni vatthāni acchādetvā agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajissāmī’ti.
- **EN**: I shall shave off my hair and beard right here, dress in ocher robes, and go forth from the lay life to homelessness.’
- **NOTE**: His apparently sudden decision to go forth arises only after an extensive period of crisis and contemplation.

## dn14:2.15.4
- **PLI**: ‘Evaṁ, devā’ti kho, bhikkhave, sārathi vipassissa kumārassa paṭissutvā rathaṁ ādāya tatova antepuraṁ paccaniyyāsi.
- **EN**: ‘Yes, Your Majesty,’ replied the charioteer and did so.

## dn14:2.15.5
- **PLI**: Vipassī pana kumāro tattheva kesamassuṁ ohāretvā kāsāyāni vatthāni acchādetvā agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbaji.
- **EN**: Then Prince Vipassī shaved off his hair and beard, dressed in ocher robes, and went forth from the lay life to homelessness.
