# Muc 2.8 — The Dead Man  (49 đoạn)

## dn14:2.8.0
- **PLI**: 7. Kālaṅkatapurisa
- **EN**: 7. The Dead Man

## dn14:2.8.1  `bj8.38, dr10.29, ms7D_101, msdiv49, pts-cs2.8, sya10.29`
- **PLI**: Atha kho, bhikkhave, bandhumassa rañño etadahosi:
- **EN**: Then King Bandhumā thought,

## dn14:2.8.2
- **PLI**: ‘mā heva kho vipassī kumāro na rajjaṁ kāresi, mā heva vipassī kumāro agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbaji, mā heva nemittānaṁ brāhmaṇānaṁ saccaṁ assa vacanan’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Prince Vipassī must not renounce the throne. He must not go forth from the lay life to homelessness. And the words of the brahmin soothsayers must not come true.’

## dn14:2.8.3
- **PLI**: Atha kho, bhikkhave, bandhumā rājā vipassissa kumārassa bhiyyoso mattāya pañca kāmaguṇāni upaṭṭhāpesi:
- **EN**: To this end he provided the prince with even more of the five kinds of sensual stimulation,

## dn14:2.8.4
- **PLI**: ‘yathā vipassī kumāro rajjaṁ kareyya, yathā vipassī kumāro na agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajeyya, yathā nemittānaṁ brāhmaṇānaṁ micchā assa vacanan’ti.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn14:2.9.1  `ms7D_102, pts-cs2.9`
- **PLI**: Tatra sudaṁ, bhikkhave, vipassī kumāro pañcahi kāmaguṇehi samappito samaṅgībhūto paricāreti.
- **EN**: with which the prince amused himself.

## dn14:2.9.2
- **PLI**: Atha kho, bhikkhave, vipassī kumāro bahūnaṁ vassānaṁ …pe…
- **EN**: Then, after many thousand years had passed, Prince Vipassī had his charioteer drive him to the park once more.

## dn14:2.10.1  `bj8.40, cck10.32, cck10.33, csp1ed7.22, csp2ed7.22, dr10.30, mc10.23, ms7D_103, msdiv50, ndp7.22, pts-cs2.10, pts-vp-pli2.26, sya10.30, vri2.20`
- **PLI**: Addasā kho, bhikkhave, vipassī kumāro uyyānabhūmiṁ niyyanto mahājanakāyaṁ sannipatitaṁ nānārattānañca dussānaṁ vilātaṁ kayiramānaṁ.
- **EN**: Along the way he saw a large crowd gathered making a bier out of garments of different colors.
- **NOTE**: Neither reading <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vilāta</i> or <i lang='pi' translate='no'>milāta</i> appears to occur elsewhere in this sense. The commentary says it is a bier (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>sivika</i>).

## dn14:2.10.2
- **PLI**: Disvā sārathiṁ āmantesi:
- **EN**: He addressed his charioteer,

## dn14:2.10.3
- **PLI**: ‘kiṁ nu kho so, samma sārathi, mahājanakāyo sannipatito nānārattānañca dussānaṁ vilātaṁ kayiratī’ti?
- **EN**: ‘My dear charioteer, why is that crowd making a bier?’

## dn14:2.10.4
- **PLI**: ‘Eso kho, deva, kālaṅkato nāmā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘That, Your Majesty, is for someone who’s departed.’

## dn14:2.10.5
- **PLI**: ‘Tena hi, samma sārathi, yena so kālaṅkato tena rathaṁ pesehī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Well then, drive the chariot up to the departed.’

## dn14:2.10.6
- **PLI**: ‘Evaṁ, devā’ti kho, bhikkhave, sārathi vipassissa kumārassa paṭissutvā yena so kālaṅkato tena rathaṁ pesesi.
- **EN**: ‘Yes, Your Majesty,’ replied the charioteer, and did so.

## dn14:2.10.7
- **PLI**: Addasā kho, bhikkhave, vipassī kumāro petaṁ kālaṅkataṁ, disvā sārathiṁ āmantesi:
- **EN**: When the prince saw the corpse of the departed, he addressed the charioteer,

## dn14:2.10.8
- **PLI**: ‘kiṁ panāyaṁ, samma sārathi, kālaṅkato nāmā’ti?
- **EN**: ‘But why is he called departed?’

## dn14:2.10.9
- **PLI**: ‘Eso kho, deva, kālaṅkato nāma. Na dāni taṁ dakkhanti mātā vā pitā vā aññe vā ñātisālohitā, sopi na dakkhissati mātaraṁ vā pitaraṁ vā aññe vā ñātisālohite’ti.
- **EN**: ‘He’s called departed because now his mother and father, his relatives and kin shall see him no more, and he shall never again see them.’

## dn14:2.10.10
- **PLI**: ‘Kiṁ pana, samma sārathi, ahampi maraṇadhammo maraṇaṁ anatīto;
- **EN**: ‘But my dear charioteer, am I liable to die? Am I not exempt from death?

## dn14:2.10.11
- **PLI**: mampi na dakkhanti devo vā devī vā aññe vā ñātisālohitā;
- **EN**: Will the king and queen and my other relatives and kin see me no more?

## dn14:2.10.12
- **PLI**: ahampi na dakkhissāmi devaṁ vā deviṁ vā aññe vā ñātisālohite’ti?
- **EN**: And shall I never again see them?’

## dn14:2.10.13
- **PLI**: ‘Tvañca, deva, mayañcamha sabbe maraṇadhammā maraṇaṁ anatītā;
- **EN**: ‘Everyone is liable to die, Your Majesty, including you. No one is exempt from death.

## dn14:2.10.14
- **PLI**: tampi na dakkhanti devo vā devī vā aññe vā ñātisālohitā;
- **EN**: The king and queen and your other relatives and kin shall see you no more,

## dn14:2.10.15
- **PLI**: tvampi na dakkhissasi devaṁ vā deviṁ vā aññe vā ñātisālohite’ti.
- **EN**: and you shall never again see them.’

## dn14:2.10.16
- **PLI**: ‘Tena hi, samma sārathi, alaṁ dānajja uyyānabhūmiyā, itova antepuraṁ paccaniyyāhī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Well then, my dear charioteer, that’s enough of the park for today. Let’s return to the royal compound.’

## dn14:2.10.17
- **PLI**: ‘Evaṁ, devā’ti kho, bhikkhave, sārathi vipassissa kumārassa paṭissutvā tatova antepuraṁ paccaniyyāsi.
- **EN**: ‘Yes, Your Majesty,’ replied the charioteer and did so.

## dn14:2.10.18
- **PLI**: Tatra sudaṁ, bhikkhave, vipassī kumāro antepuraṁ gato dukkhī dummano pajjhāyati:
- **EN**: Back at the royal compound, the prince brooded, miserable and sad:

## dn14:2.10.19
- **PLI**: ‘dhiratthu kira, bho, jāti nāma, yatra hi nāma jātassa jarā paññāyissati, byādhi paññāyissati, maraṇaṁ paññāyissatī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Curse this thing called rebirth, since old age, sickness, and death will come to anyone who’s born.’
