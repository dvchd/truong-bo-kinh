# Muc 3.18 — The 84,000 Who Had Gone Forth Previously  (20 đoạn)

## dn14:3.18.0
- **PLI**: 15. Purimapabbajitānaṁ dhammābhisamaya
- **EN**: 15. The 84,000 Who Had Gone Forth Previously

## dn14:3.18.1  `bj8.66, cck10.55, csp1ed7.38, csp2ed7.38, dr10.51, ms7D_167, msdiv82, ndp7.36, pts-cs3.18`
- **PLI**: Assosuṁ kho, bhikkhave, tāni purimāni caturāsītipabbajitasahassāni:
- **EN**: The 84,000 people who had gone forth previously also heard:

## dn14:3.18.2
- **PLI**: ‘vipassī kira bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho bandhumatiṁ rājadhāniṁ anuppatto kheme migadāye viharati, dhammañca kira desetī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘It seems the Blessed One Vipassī, the perfected one, the fully awakened Buddha, has arrived at Bandhumatī and is staying in the deer park named Sanctuary. And he is teaching the Dhamma!’

## dn14:3.18.3
- **PLI**: Atha kho, bhikkhave, tāni caturāsītipabbajitasahassāni yena bandhumatī rājadhānī yena khemo migadāyo yena vipassī bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho tenupasaṅkamiṁsu; upasaṅkamitvā vipassiṁ bhagavantaṁ arahantaṁ sammāsambuddhaṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ nisīdiṁsu.
- **EN**: Then they too went to the Buddha Vipassī, realized the Dhamma, went forth, and became freed from defilements.

## dn14:3.19.1  `ms7D_168, msdiv83, pts-cs3.19, sya10.51, vri2.35`
- **PLI**: Tesaṁ vipassī bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho anupubbiṁ kathaṁ kathesi.
- **EN**: The Buddha Vipassī taught them step by step, with  (= dn14:3.15.1)

## dn14:3.19.2
- **PLI**: Seyyathidaṁ—dānakathaṁ sīlakathaṁ saggakathaṁ kāmānaṁ ādīnavaṁ okāraṁ saṅkilesaṁ nekkhamme ānisaṁsaṁ pakāsesi.
- **EN**: a talk on giving, ethical conduct, and heaven. He revealed the drawbacks of sensual pleasures, so sordid and corrupt, and the benefit of renunciation.  (= dn14:3.15.2)

## dn14:3.19.3
- **PLI**: Yadā te bhagavā aññāsi kallacitte muducitte vinīvaraṇacitte udaggacitte pasannacitte, atha yā buddhānaṁ sāmukkaṁsikā dhammadesanā, taṁ pakāsesi—
- **EN**: And when he knew that their minds were ready, pliable, rid of hindrances, elated, and confident he revealed the special teaching of the Buddhas:  (= dn14:3.11.3)

## dn14:3.19.4
- **PLI**: dukkhaṁ samudayaṁ nirodhaṁ maggaṁ.
- **EN**: suffering, its origin, its cessation, and the path.  (= dn14:3.11.4)
