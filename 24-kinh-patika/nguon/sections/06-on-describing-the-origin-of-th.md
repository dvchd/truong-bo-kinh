# Muc 2.14 — On Describing the Origin of the World  (129 đoạn)

## dn24:2.14.0
- **PLI**: 6. Aggaññapaññattikathā
- **EN**: 6. On Describing the Origin of the World

## dn24:2.14.1  `ms8D_78, msdiv36, pts-cs2.14, sya11.30`
- **PLI**: Aggaññañcāhaṁ, bhaggava, pajānāmi.
- **EN**: Bhaggava, I understand the origin of the world.

## dn24:2.14.2
- **PLI**: Tañca pajānāmi, tato ca uttaritaraṁ pajānāmi, tañca pajānaṁ na parāmasāmi, aparāmasato ca me paccattaññeva nibbuti viditā, yadabhijānaṁ tathāgato no anayaṁ āpajjati.
- **EN**: I understand this, and what goes beyond it. Yet since I do not misapprehend that understanding, I have realized quenching within myself. Directly knowing this, the Realized One does not come to ruin.
- **NOTE**: Compare <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn1/en/sujato#1.36.3'>DN 1:1.36.3</a>. The following passage is largely adapted from the Brahmajālasutta.

## dn24:2.14.3  `dr11.29, ms8D_79, msdiv37`
- **PLI**: Santi, bhaggava, eke samaṇabrāhmaṇā issarakuttaṁ brahmakuttaṁ ācariyakaṁ aggaññaṁ paññapenti.
- **EN**: There are some ascetics and brahmins who describe the origin of the world in their denomination as created by the God Almighty, by the Divinity.

## dn24:2.14.4
- **PLI**: Tyāhaṁ upasaṅkamitvā evaṁ vadāmi:
- **EN**: I go up to them and say,

## dn24:2.14.5
- **PLI**: ‘saccaṁ kira tumhe āyasmanto issarakuttaṁ brahmakuttaṁ ācariyakaṁ aggaññaṁ paññapethā’ti?
- **EN**: ‘Is it really true that this is the venerables’ view?’

## dn24:2.14.6
- **PLI**: Te ca me evaṁ puṭṭhā, ‘āmo’ti paṭijānanti.
- **EN**: And they answer, ‘Yes’.

## dn24:2.14.7
- **PLI**: Tyāhaṁ evaṁ vadāmi:
- **EN**: I say to them,

## dn24:2.14.8
- **PLI**: ‘kathaṁvihitakaṁ pana tumhe āyasmanto issarakuttaṁ brahmakuttaṁ ācariyakaṁ aggaññaṁ paññapethā’ti?
- **EN**: ‘But how do you describe in your denomination that the origin of the world came about as created by the God Almighty, by the Divinity?’

## dn24:2.14.9
- **PLI**: Te mayā puṭṭhā na sampāyanti, asampāyantā mamaññeva paṭipucchanti.
- **EN**: But they are stumped by my question, and they even question me in return.

## dn24:2.14.10
- **PLI**: Tesāhaṁ puṭṭho byākaromi:

## dn24:2.14.11  `bj9.46, ms8D_80, msdiv38`

- **EN**: ‘There comes a time when, reverends, after a very long period has passed, this cosmos contracts.

## dn24:2.14.12
- **PLI**: Saṁvaṭṭamāne loke yebhuyyena sattā ābhassarasaṁvattanikā honti.
- **EN**: As the cosmos contracts, sentient beings are mostly headed for the realm of sublime luminosity.

## dn24:2.14.13
- **PLI**: Te tattha honti manomayā pītibhakkhā sayaṁpabhā antalikkhacarā subhaṭṭhāyino ciraṁ dīghamaddhānaṁ tiṭṭhanti.
- **EN**: There they are mind-made, feeding on rapture, self-radiant, wandering in midair, steadily glorious, and they remain like that for a very long time.

## dn24:2.15.1  `cck11.31, csp1ed8.24, csp2ed8.24, ms8D_81, pts-cs2.15, pts-vp-pli3.29, vri3.21`

## dn24:2.15.2

- **EN**: As it expands an empty mansion of divinity appears.

## dn24:2.15.3
- **PLI**: Atha kho aññataro satto āyukkhayā vā puññakkhayā vā ābhassarakāyā cavitvā suññaṁ brahmavimānaṁ upapajjati.
- **EN**: Then a certain sentient being—due to the running out of their life-span or merit—passes away from that host of luminous deities and is reborn in that empty mansion of divinity.

## dn24:2.15.4
- **PLI**: So tattha hoti manomayo pītibhakkho sayampabho antalikkhacaro subhaṭṭhāyī, ciraṁ dīghamaddhānaṁ tiṭṭhati.
- **EN**: There they are mind-made, feeding on rapture, self-radiant, wandering in midair, steadily glorious, and they remain like that for a very long time.

## dn24:2.16.1  `dr11.30, ms8D_82, ndp8.24, pts-cs2.16, sya11.31`

## dn24:2.16.2

- **EN**: Then other sentient beings—due to the running out of their life-span or merit—pass away from that host of luminous deities and are reborn in that mansion of divinity in company with that being.

- **EN**: There they too are mind-made, feeding on rapture, self-radiant, wandering in midair, steadily glorious, and they remain like that for a very long time.
