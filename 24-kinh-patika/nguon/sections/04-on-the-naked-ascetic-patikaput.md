# Muc 1.15 — On the Naked Ascetic Pāṭikaputta  (74 đoạn)

## dn24:1.15.0
- **PLI**: 4. Acelapāthikaputtavatthu
- **EN**: 4. On the Naked Ascetic Pāṭikaputta

## dn24:1.15.1  `bj9.20, cck11.13, dr11.12, ms8D_27, msdiv15, pts-cs1.15, pts-vp-pli3.13, sya11.13`
- **PLI**: Ekamidāhaṁ, bhaggava, samayaṁ tattheva vesāliyaṁ viharāmi mahāvane kūṭāgārasālāyaṁ.
- **EN**: This one time, Bhaggava, I was staying right there near Vesālī, at the Great Wood, in the hall with the peaked roof.

## dn24:1.15.2
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena acelo pāthikaputto vesāliyaṁ paṭivasati lābhaggappatto ceva yasaggappatto ca vajjigāme.
- **EN**: Now at that time the naked ascetic Pāṭikaputta was residing in Vesālī. And in the Vajjian capital he had reached the peak of material things and fame.
- **NOTE**: The Burmese reading <i lang='pi' translate='no'>pāthikaputta</i> means “son of a traveler”. However the commentary says Pāṭika (or Pāthika) was the name of his father.

## dn24:1.15.3
- **PLI**: So vesāliyaṁ parisati evaṁ vācaṁ bhāsati:
- **EN**: He was telling a crowd in Vesālī:

## dn24:1.15.4
- **PLI**: ‘samaṇopi gotamo ñāṇavādo, ahampi ñāṇavādo.
- **EN**: ‘Both the ascetic Gotama and I speak from knowledge.
- **NOTE**: For <i lang='pi' translate='no'>ñāṇavādo</i> (“one who speaks from knowledge”) see <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an9.38/en/sujato#3.3'>AN 9.38:3.3</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an10.24/en/sujato#2.1'>AN 10.24:2.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn26/en/sujato#15.7'>MN 26:15.7</a>.

## dn24:1.15.5
- **PLI**: Ñāṇavādo kho pana ñāṇavādena arahati uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriyaṁ dassetuṁ.
- **EN**: One who speaks from knowledge ought to display a superhuman demonstration of psychic power to another who speaks from knowledge.

## dn24:1.15.6
- **PLI**: Samaṇo gotamo upaḍḍhapathaṁ āgaccheyya, ahampi upaḍḍhapathaṁ gaccheyyaṁ.
- **EN**: If the ascetic Gotama meets me half-way,

## dn24:1.15.7
- **PLI**: Te tattha ubhopi uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriyaṁ kareyyāma.
- **EN**: there we should both perform a superhuman demonstration of psychic power.

## dn24:1.15.8
- **PLI**: Ekañce samaṇo gotamo uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriyaṁ karissati, dvāhaṁ karissāmi.
- **EN**: If he performs one demonstration of psychic power, I’ll perform two.

## dn24:1.15.9
- **PLI**: Dve ce samaṇo gotamo uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriyāni karissati, cattārāhaṁ karissāmi.
- **EN**: If he performs two, I’ll perform four.

## dn24:1.15.10
- **PLI**: Cattāri ce samaṇo gotamo uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriyāni karissati, aṭṭhāhaṁ karissāmi.
- **EN**: If he performs four, I’ll perform eight.

## dn24:1.15.11
- **PLI**: Iti yāvatakaṁ yāvatakaṁ samaṇo gotamo uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriyaṁ karissati, taddiguṇaṁ taddiguṇāhaṁ karissāmī’ti.
- **EN**: However many demonstrations of psychic power the ascetic Gotama performs, I’ll perform double.’
