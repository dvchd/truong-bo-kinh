# Muc 1.11 — On the Naked Ascetic Kaḷāramaṭṭaka  (44 đoạn)

## dn24:1.11.0
- **PLI**: 3. Acelakaḷāramaṭṭakavatthu
- **EN**: 3. On the Naked Ascetic Kaḷāramaṭṭaka

## dn24:1.11.1  `bj9.16, cck11.10, ms8D_21, msdiv11, pts-cs1.11, pts-vp-pli3.10, sya11.10, vri3.7`
- **PLI**: Ekamidāhaṁ, bhaggava, samayaṁ vesāliyaṁ viharāmi mahāvane kūṭāgārasālāyaṁ.
- **EN**: This one time, Bhaggava, I was staying near Vesālī, at the Great Wood, in the hall with the peaked roof.

## dn24:1.11.2
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena acelo kaḷāramaṭṭako vesāliyaṁ paṭivasati lābhaggappatto ceva yasaggappatto ca vajjigāme.
- **EN**: Now at that time the naked ascetic Kaḷāramaṭṭaka was residing in Vesālī. And in the Vajjian capital he had reached the peak of material things and fame.
- **NOTE**: Spellings for Kaḷāramaṭṭaka vary greatly.

## dn24:1.11.3
- **PLI**: Tassa sattavatapadāni samattāni samādinnāni honti:
- **EN**: He had undertaken these seven vows.
- **NOTE**: In these practices we see some examples of the vows or observances (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>vata</i>) that Buddha rejected as “grasping at precepts and observances”

## dn24:1.11.4
- **PLI**: ‘yāvajīvaṁ acelako assaṁ, na vatthaṁ paridaheyyaṁ, yāvajīvaṁ brahmacārī assaṁ, na methunaṁ dhammaṁ paṭiseveyyaṁ, yāvajīvaṁ surāmaṁseneva yāpeyyaṁ, na odanakummāsaṁ bhuñjeyyaṁ.
- **EN**: ‘As long as I live, I will be a naked ascetic, not wearing clothes. As long as I live, I will be chaste, not having sex. As long as I live, I will consume only meat and beer, not eating rice and porridge.
- **NOTE**: A diet of pure meat is still undertaken today by certain would-be gurus.

## dn24:1.11.5
- **PLI**: Puratthimena vesāliṁ udenaṁ nāma cetiyaṁ, taṁ nātikkameyyaṁ, dakkhiṇena vesāliṁ gotamakaṁ nāma cetiyaṁ, taṁ nātikkameyyaṁ, pacchimena vesāliṁ sattambaṁ nāma cetiyaṁ, taṁ nātikkameyyaṁ, uttarena vesāliṁ bahuputtaṁ nāma cetiyaṁ taṁ nātikkameyyan’ti.
- **EN**: And I will not go past the following shrines near Vesālī: the Udena Shrine to the east, the Gotamaka to the south, the Seven Maidens to the west, and the Many Sons to the north.’
- **NOTE**: Compare the “vow of restraint in the directions” in the Jain Tattvārthasūtra 7.1.

## dn24:1.11.6
- **PLI**: So imesaṁ sattannaṁ vatapadānaṁ samādānahetu lābhaggappatto ceva yasaggappatto ca vajjigāme.
- **EN**: And it was due to undertaking these seven vows that he had reached the peak of material things and fame.

## dn24:1.12.1  `dr11.10, ms8D_22, msdiv12, pts-cs1.12`
- **PLI**: Atha kho, bhaggava, sunakkhatto licchaviputto yena acelo kaḷāramaṭṭako tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā acelaṁ kaḷāramaṭṭakaṁ pañhaṁ apucchi.
- **EN**: So, Bhaggava, Sunakkhatta went to see Kaḷāramaṭṭaka and asked him a question.

## dn24:1.12.2
- **PLI**: Tassa acelo kaḷāramaṭṭako pañhaṁ puṭṭho na sampāyāsi.
- **EN**: But when it stumped him,

## dn24:1.12.3
- **PLI**: Asampāyanto kopañca dosañca appaccayañca pātvākāsi.
- **EN**: he displayed annoyance, hate, and bitterness.

## dn24:1.12.4
- **PLI**: Atha kho, bhaggava, sunakkhattassa licchaviputtassa etadahosi:
- **EN**: So Sunakkhatta thought,

## dn24:1.12.5
- **PLI**: ‘sādhurūpaṁ vata bho arahantaṁ samaṇaṁ āsādimhase.
- **EN**: ‘I’ve offended the holy man, the perfected one, the ascetic.
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Āsādimhase</i> is middle aorist plural. Below Sunakkhatta again refers to himself in plural, but as usual I render with singular.

## dn24:1.12.6
- **PLI**: Mā vata no ahosi dīgharattaṁ ahitāya dukkhāyā’ti.
- **EN**: I mustn’t create lasting harm and suffering for myself!’

## dn24:1.13.16

- **EN**: will shortly be clothed, living with a partner, eating rice and porridge, having gone past all the shrines near Vesālī. And he will die after losing all his fame.’
