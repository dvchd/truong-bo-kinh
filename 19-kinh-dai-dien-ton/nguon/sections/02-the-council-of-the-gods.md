# Muc 2 — The Council of the Gods  (31 đoạn)

## dn19:2.0
- **PLI**: 1. Devasabhā
- **EN**: 1. The Council of the Gods

## dn19:2.1  `cck10.269, dr10.251, ms7D_645, msdiv294, pts-cs2, pts-vp-pli2.221, sya10.253`
- **PLI**: “Purimāni, bhante, divasāni purimatarāni tadahuposathe pannarase pavāraṇāya puṇṇāya puṇṇamāya rattiyā kevalakappā ca devā tāvatiṁsā sudhammāyaṁ sabhāyaṁ sannisinnā honti sannipatitā;
- **EN**: “Sir, it was more than a few days ago—on the fifteenth day sabbath on the full moon day at the invitation to admonish held at the end of the rainy season—when all the gods of the thirty-three were sitting together in the Hall of Clear Right.
- **NOTE**: The council of gods described by Janavasabha at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn18/en/sujato#12.1'>DN 18:12.1</a>, while otherwise similar, took place at the entry to a rainy season, whereas this one is at the end of a rainy season. This cannot have been the Buddha’s last rains, for he was already in Vajji by then.

## dn19:2.2
- **PLI**: mahatī ca dibbaparisā samantato nisinnā honti, cattāro ca mahārājāno catuddisā nisinnā honti;
- **EN**: A large assembly of gods was sitting all around, and the four great kings were seated at the four quarters.

## dn19:2.3
- **PLI**: puratthimāya disāya dhataraṭṭho mahārājā pacchimābhimukho nisinno hoti deve purakkhatvā;
- **EN**: The Great King Dhataraṭṭha was seated to the east, facing west, in front of his gods.

## dn19:2.4
- **PLI**: dakkhiṇāya disāya virūḷhako mahārājā uttarābhimukho nisinno hoti deve purakkhatvā;
- **EN**: The Great King Virūḷhaka was seated to the south, facing north, in front of his gods.

## dn19:2.5
- **PLI**: pacchimāya disāya virūpakkho mahārājā puratthābhimukho nisinno hoti deve purakkhatvā;
- **EN**: The Great King Virūpakkha was seated to the west, facing east, in front of his gods.

## dn19:2.6
- **PLI**: uttarāya disāya vessavaṇo mahārājā dakkhiṇābhimukho nisinno hoti deve purakkhatvā.
- **EN**: The Great King Vessavaṇa was seated to the north, facing south, in front of his gods.

## dn19:2.7
- **PLI**: Yadā, bhante, kevalakappā ca devā tāvatiṁsā sudhammāyaṁ sabhāyaṁ sannisinnā honti sannipatitā, mahatī ca dibbaparisā samantato nisinnā honti, cattāro ca mahārājāno catuddisā nisinnā honti, idaṁ nesaṁ hoti āsanasmiṁ;
- **EN**: When the gods of the thirty-three have a gathering like this, that is how they are seated.

## dn19:2.8
- **PLI**: atha pacchā amhākaṁ āsanaṁ hoti.
- **EN**: After that come our seats.

## dn19:3.1  `csp1ed7.179, csp2ed7.179, mc10.190, ms7D_646, pts-cs3, vri2.163`
- **PLI**: Ye te, bhante, devā bhagavati brahmacariyaṁ caritvā adhunūpapannā tāvatiṁsakāyaṁ, te aññe deve atirocanti vaṇṇena ceva yasasā ca.
- **EN**: Sir, those gods who had been recently reborn in the company of the thirty-three after leading the spiritual life under the Buddha outshine the other gods in beauty and glory.

## dn19:3.2

- **EN**: ‘The heavenly hosts swell, while the titan hosts dwindle!’
