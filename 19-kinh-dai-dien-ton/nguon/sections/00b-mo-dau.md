# Mo dau  (5 đoạn)

## dn19:1.1  `bj8.334, cck10.268, csp1ed7.178, csp2ed7.178, dr10.250, mc10.189, ms7D_644, msdiv293, ndp7.165, pts-cs1, pts-vp-pli2.220, sya10.252, vri2.162`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—
- **EN**: So I have heard.

## dn19:1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā rājagahe viharati gijjhakūṭe pabbate.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying near Rājagaha, on the Vulture’s Peak Mountain.

## dn19:1.3
- **PLI**: Atha kho pañcasikho gandhabbaputto abhikkantāya rattiyā abhikkantavaṇṇo kevalakappaṁ gijjhakūṭaṁ pabbataṁ obhāsetvā yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ aṭṭhāsi. Ekamantaṁ ṭhito kho pañcasikho gandhabbaputto bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: Then, late at night, the centaur Pañcasikha, lighting up the entire Vulture’s Peak, went up to the Buddha, bowed, stood to one side, and said to him,
- **NOTE**: We were introduced to Pañcasikha in <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn18/en/sujato'>DN 18</a>, which is similar to the current sutta in many other details as well. Thus this sutta can be considered as a distant cousin to the Mahāparinibbāna cycle.

## dn19:1.4
- **PLI**: “yaṁ kho me, bhante, devānaṁ tāvatiṁsānaṁ sammukhā sutaṁ sammukhā paṭiggahitaṁ, ārocemi taṁ bhagavato”ti.
- **EN**: “Sir, I would tell you of what I heard and learned directly from the gods of the thirty-three.”

## dn19:1.5
- **PLI**: “Ārocehi me tvaṁ, pañcasikhā”ti bhagavā avoca.
- **EN**: “Tell me, Pañcasikha,” said the Buddha.
