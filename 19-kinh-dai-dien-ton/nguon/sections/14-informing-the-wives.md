# Muc 57 — Informing the Wives  (8 đoạn)

## dn19:57.0
- **PLI**: 6.7. Bhariyānaṁāmantanā
- **EN**: 6.7. Informing the Wives

## dn19:57.1  `cck10.300, csp1ed7.200, csp2ed7.200, ms7D_737, msdiv325, pts-cs57, sya10.283`
- **PLI**: Atha kho, bho, mahāgovindo brāhmaṇo yena cattārīsā bhariyā sādisiyo tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā cattārīsā bhariyā sādisiyo etadavoca:
- **EN**: Then the Great Steward went to his forty equal wives and said,

## dn19:57.2
- **PLI**: “yā bhotīnaṁ icchati, sakāni vā ñātikulāni gacchatu aññaṁ vā bhattāraṁ pariyesatu.
- **EN**: “Ladies, please do whatever you wish, whether returning to your own families, or finding another husband.

## dn19:57.3
- **PLI**: Icchāmahaṁ, bhotī, agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajituṁ.
- **EN**: I wish to go forth from the lay life to homelessness.

## dn19:57.4
- **PLI**: Yathā kho pana me sutaṁ brahmuno āmagandhe bhāsamānassa, te na sunimmadayā agāraṁ ajjhāvasatā.
- **EN**: As I understand what the Divinity says about putrefaction, it’s not easy to quell while living at home.

## dn19:57.5
- **PLI**: Pabbajissāmahaṁ, bhotī, agārasmā anagāriyan”ti.
- **EN**: I shall go forth from the lay life to homelessness.”

## dn19:57.6
- **PLI**: “Tvaññeva no ñāti ñātikāmānaṁ, tvaṁ pana bhattā bhattukāmānaṁ.
- **EN**: “You are the only family we want! You are the only husband we want!

## dn19:57.7
- **PLI**: Sace bhavaṁ govindo agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajissati, mayampi agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajissāma, atha yā te gati, sā no gati bhavissatī”ti.
- **EN**: If you are going forth from the lay life to homelessness, we shall do so too. Your destiny shall be ours.”
- **NOTE**: It is regarded as normal that women should renounce.
