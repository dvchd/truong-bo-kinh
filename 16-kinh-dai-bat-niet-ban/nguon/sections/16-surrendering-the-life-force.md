# Muc 3.10 — Surrendering the Life Force  (8 đoạn)

## dn16:3.10.0
- **PLI**: 15. Āyusaṅkhāraossajjana
- **EN**: 15. Surrendering the Life Force

## dn16:3.10.1  `csp1ed7.90, csp2ed7.90, dr10.126, mc10.96, ms7D_362, msdiv169, pts-cs3.10, pts-vp-pli2.107`
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā cāpāle cetiye sato sampajāno āyusaṅkhāraṁ ossaji.
- **EN**: So at the Cāpāla Shrine the Buddha, mindful and aware, surrendered the life force.
- **NOTE**: The Cāpāla shrine is unknown outside of this context. The Udāna commentary says that it was named after the <i lang='pi' translate='no'>yakkha</i> who formerly lived there. Now, <i lang='pi' translate='no'>cāpalla</i> means “fickleness, propensity to tremble” and is from the same root as earth-“quake” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>bhumicāla</i>; <i lang='pi' translate='no'>capala</i> = <i lang='pi' translate='no'>pacala</i> via metathesis). It is no great leap to <i lang='pi' translate='no'>cāpāla</i>. If this is correct, it suggests that the shrine was dedicated to an earth spirit who commanded earthquakes; a fitting setting for the events to follow.

## dn16:3.10.2
- **PLI**: Ossaṭṭhe ca bhagavatā āyusaṅkhāre mahābhūmicālo ahosi bhiṁsanako salomahaṁso, devadundubhiyo ca phaliṁsu.
- **EN**: When he did so there was a great earthquake, awe-inspiring and hair-raising, and thunder cracked the sky.
- **NOTE**: Northern India lies in the shadow of the seismically active Alpide Belt, so the occurrence of earthquakes is realistic even if the causes are not scientific.

## dn16:3.10.3
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā etamatthaṁ viditvā tāyaṁ velāyaṁ imaṁ udānaṁ udānesi:
- **EN**: Then, understanding this matter, on that occasion the Buddha expressed this heartfelt sentiment:

## dn16:3.10.4  `ms7D_363`
- **PLI**: “Tulamatulañca sambhavaṁ,
- **EN**: “Comparing the incomparable <j>with the creation of prolonged life,
- **NOTE**: For this difficult verse, I generally follow Bhikkhu Bodhi’s long discussion in <em>Connected Discourses of the Buddha</em>, note 255 on the Mahāvagga. | <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Sambhava</i> means “production, creation”, and here I think it refers to the prolonging of life which the Buddha had just rejected.

## dn16:3.10.5
- **PLI**: Bhavasaṅkhāramavassaji muni;
- **EN**: the sage surrendered the life force.
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Bhavasaṅkhāra</i> (“life force”) here is equivalent to <i lang='pi' translate='no'>āyusaṅkhāra</i> and <i lang='pi' translate='no'>jīvitasaṅkhāra</i>.

## dn16:3.10.6
- **PLI**: Ajjhattarato samāhito,
- **EN**: Happy inside, serene,

## dn16:3.10.7
- **PLI**: Abhindi kavacamivattasambhavan”ti.
- **EN**: he shattered self-creation like a suit of armor.”
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Attasambhava</i> (“self-creation”) harks back to <i lang='pi' translate='no'>sambhava</i> in the first line.
