## dn16:3.25.1  `pts-cs3.25`
- **PLI**: Ajjhattaṁ rūpasaññī eko bahiddhā rūpāni passati parittāni suvaṇṇadubbaṇṇāni.
- **EN**: Perceiving form internally, someone sees forms externally, limited, both pretty and ugly.
- **NOTE**: “Perceiving form (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>rūpa</i>) internally” refers to someone developing meditation based on an aspect of their own body, such as the breath or the parts of the body. The “forms” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>rūpā</i>) seen externally are the lights or other meditation phenomena that today are usually called <i lang='pi' translate='no'>nimitta</i>. An “ugly” form is the mental image that arises in such contemplations as the parts of the body. A “beautiful” image arises from practices such as mindfulness of breathing.

## dn16:3.25.2
- **PLI**: ‘Tāni abhibhuyya jānāmi passāmī’ti evaṁsaññī hoti.
- **EN**: Mastering them, they perceive: ‘I know and see.’

## dn16:3.25.3
- **PLI**: Idaṁ paṭhamaṁ abhibhāyatanaṁ.
- **EN**: This is the first dimension of mastery.

## dn16:3.29.1  `ms7D_379, pts-cs3.29`
- **PLI**: Ajjhattaṁ arūpasaññī eko bahiddhā rūpāni passati nīlāni nīlavaṇṇāni nīlanidassanāni nīlanibhāsāni.
- **EN**: Not perceiving form internally, someone sees forms externally that are blue, with blue color and blue appearance.
- **NOTE**: This is the meditation where one contemplates an external color, either a prepared disk or a natural phenomena such as the sky or a flower, which eventually gives rise to a “counterpart” image. Today such meditations are called <i lang='pi' translate='no'>kasiṇa</i> following the Visuddhimagga, but in the early texts <i lang='pi' translate='no'>kasiṇa</i> means “totality” and refers rather to the state of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>samādhi</i> that results.

## dn16:3.29.2
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi nāma umāpupphaṁ nīlaṁ nīlavaṇṇaṁ nīlanidassanaṁ nīlanibhāsaṁ. Seyyathā vā pana taṁ vatthaṁ bārāṇaseyyakaṁ ubhatobhāgavimaṭṭhaṁ nīlaṁ nīlavaṇṇaṁ nīlanidassanaṁ nīlanibhāsaṁ.
- **EN**: They’re like a flax flower that’s blue, with blue color and blue appearance. Or a cloth from Varanasi that’s smoothed on both sides, blue, with blue color and blue appearance.
