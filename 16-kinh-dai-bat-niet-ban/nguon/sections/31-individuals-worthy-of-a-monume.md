# Muc 5.12 — Individuals Worthy of a Monument  (21 đoạn)

## dn16:5.12.0
- **PLI**: 30. Thūpārahapuggala
- **EN**: 30. Individuals Worthy of a Monument

## dn16:5.12.1  `ms7D_460, msdiv206, pts-cs5.12`
- **PLI**: Cattārome, ānanda, thūpārahā.
- **EN**: Ānanda, these four are worthy of a monument.

## dn16:5.12.2
- **PLI**: Katame cattāro?
- **EN**: What four?

## dn16:5.12.3
- **PLI**: Tathāgato arahaṁ sammāsambuddho thūpāraho, paccekasambuddho thūpāraho, tathāgatassa sāvako thūpāraho, rājā cakkavattī thūpārahoti.
- **EN**: A Realized One, a perfected one, a fully awakened Buddha; an independent Buddha; a disciple of a Realized One; and a wheel-turning monarch.

## dn16:5.12.4  `cck10.178, csp1ed7.118, csp2ed7.118, ms7D_461`
- **PLI**: Kiñcānanda, atthavasaṁ paṭicca tathāgato arahaṁ sammāsambuddho thūpāraho?
- **EN**: And for what reason is a Realized One worthy of a monument?

## dn16:5.12.5
- **PLI**: ‘Ayaṁ tassa bhagavato arahato sammāsambuddhassa thūpo’ti, ānanda, bahujanā cittaṁ pasādenti.
- **EN**: So that many people will inspire confidence in their hearts, thinking: ‘This is the monument for that Blessed One, perfected and fully awakened!’

## dn16:5.12.6
- **PLI**: Te tattha cittaṁ pasādetvā kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā sugatiṁ saggaṁ lokaṁ upapajjanti.
- **EN**: And having done so, when their body breaks up, after death, they are reborn in a good place, a heavenly realm.

## dn16:5.12.7
- **PLI**: Idaṁ kho, ānanda, atthavasaṁ paṭicca tathāgato arahaṁ sammāsambuddho thūpāraho.
- **EN**: It is for this reason that a Realized One is worthy of a monument.

## dn16:5.12.8  `ms7D_462, pts-vp-pli2.143, vri2.108`
- **PLI**: Kiñcānanda, atthavasaṁ paṭicca paccekasambuddho thūpāraho?
- **EN**: And for what reason is an independent Buddha worthy of a monument?
- **NOTE**: “Independent Buddhas” are sages who discover the Dhamma and are awakened independently, but who do not themselves go on to found a dispensation or establish a monastic order. Text has <i lang='pi' translate='no'>paccekasambuddho</i> rather than the usual <i lang='pi' translate='no'>paccekabuddho</i>.
