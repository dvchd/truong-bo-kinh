# Muc 4.1 — The Elephant Look  (32 đoạn)

## dn16:4.1.0
- **PLI**: 21. Nāgāpalokita
- **EN**: 21. The Elephant Look

## dn16:4.1.1  `bj8.190, cck10.153, dr10.143, ms7D_409, msdiv186, pts-cs4.1, pts-vp-pli2.122`
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā pubbaṇhasamayaṁ nivāsetvā pattacīvaramādāya vesāliṁ piṇḍāya pāvisi.
- **EN**: Then the Buddha robed up in the morning and, taking his bowl and robe, entered Vesālī for alms.

## dn16:4.1.2
- **PLI**: Vesāliyaṁ piṇḍāya caritvā pacchābhattaṁ piṇḍapātapaṭikkanto nāgāpalokitaṁ vesāliṁ apaloketvā āyasmantaṁ ānandaṁ āmantesi:
- **EN**: Then, after the meal, on his return from almsround, he turned to gaze back at Vesālī, the way that elephants do. He said to Venerable Ānanda:
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Nāgāpalokitaṁ</i> is the “elephant look”. There is a similar Sanskrit term <i lang='pi' translate='no'>siṁhāvalokana</i>, the “lion look”, said to be the slow glance back that a lion makes as he leaves his kill. There is a nuance in meaning here, because while <i lang='pi' translate='no'>apalokana</i> literally means to “look back”, it is used in the sense to “take leave” before setting out on a journey.

## dn16:4.1.3
- **PLI**: “idaṁ pacchimakaṁ, ānanda, tathāgatassa vesāliyā dassanaṁ bhavissati.
- **EN**: “Ānanda, this will be the last time the Realized One sees Vesālī.

## dn16:4.1.4
- **PLI**: Āyāmānanda, yena bhaṇḍagāmo tenupasaṅkamissāmā”ti.
- **EN**: Come, Ānanda, let’s go to Wares Village.”
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Bhaṇḍagāma</i>, where <i lang='pi' translate='no'>bhaṇḍa</i> means “wares”; it must have been a trading post. It seems the villages in this passage were named after their chief economic activity, so I translate them to highlight this pragmatic system.

## dn16:4.1.5
- **PLI**: “Evaṁ, bhante”ti kho āyasmā ānando bhagavato paccassosi.
- **EN**: “Yes, sir,” Ānanda replied.

## dn16:4.2.1  `cck10.154, csp1ed7.102, csp2ed7.102, mc10.109, ms7D_410, pts-cs4.2, pts-vp-pli2.123, sya10.143`
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā mahatā bhikkhusaṅghena saddhiṁ yena bhaṇḍagāmo tadavasari.
- **EN**: Then the Buddha together with a large Saṅgha of mendicants arrived at Wares Village,

## dn16:4.2.2
- **PLI**: Tatra sudaṁ bhagavā bhaṇḍagāme viharati.
- **EN**: and stayed there.

## dn16:4.2.3
- **PLI**: Tatra kho bhagavā bhikkhū āmantesi:
- **EN**: There the Buddha addressed the mendicants:

## dn16:4.2.4
- **PLI**: “catunnaṁ, bhikkhave, dhammānaṁ ananubodhā appaṭivedhā evamidaṁ dīghamaddhānaṁ sandhāvitaṁ saṁsaritaṁ mamañceva tumhākañca.
- **EN**: “Mendicants, due to not understanding and not penetrating four things, both you and I have wandered and transmigrated for such a very long time.

## dn16:4.2.5
- **PLI**: Katamesaṁ catunnaṁ?
- **EN**: What four?

## dn16:4.2.6
- **PLI**: Ariyassa, bhikkhave, sīlassa ananubodhā appaṭivedhā evamidaṁ dīghamaddhānaṁ sandhāvitaṁ saṁsaritaṁ mamañceva tumhākañca.
- **EN**: Noble ethics,
- **NOTE**: These four also found at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an4.1/en/sujato#2.3'>AN 4.1:2.3</a> and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an7.66/en/sujato#14.4'>AN 7.66:14.4</a>.

## dn16:4.2.7
- **PLI**: Ariyassa, bhikkhave, samādhissa ananubodhā appaṭivedhā evamidaṁ dīghamaddhānaṁ sandhāvitaṁ saṁsaritaṁ mamañceva tumhākañca.
- **EN**: immersion,

## dn16:4.2.8
- **PLI**: Ariyāya, bhikkhave, paññāya ananubodhā appaṭivedhā evamidaṁ dīghamaddhānaṁ sandhāvitaṁ saṁsaritaṁ mamañceva tumhākañca.
- **EN**: wisdom,

## dn16:4.2.9
- **PLI**: Ariyāya, bhikkhave, vimuttiyā ananubodhā appaṭivedhā evamidaṁ dīghamaddhānaṁ sandhāvitaṁ saṁsaritaṁ mamañceva tumhākañca.
- **EN**: and freedom.

## dn16:4.2.10
- **PLI**: Tayidaṁ, bhikkhave, ariyaṁ sīlaṁ anubuddhaṁ paṭividdhaṁ, ariyo samādhi anubuddho paṭividdho, ariyā paññā anubuddhā paṭividdhā, ariyā vimutti anubuddhā paṭividdhā, ucchinnā bhavataṇhā, khīṇā bhavanetti, natthi dāni punabbhavo”ti.
- **EN**: These noble ethics, immersion, wisdom, and freedom have been understood and comprehended. Craving for continued existence has been cut off; the leash to existence is ended; now there’ll be no more future lives.”
