## dn16:5.13.2

- **EN**: “Enough, Ānanda! Do not grieve, do not lament. Did I not prepare for this when I explained that
- **NOTE**: When admonishing Ānanda, the Buddha first gently but firmly restrains him, then gives words of support and encouragement.

## dn16:5.14.3
- **PLI**: ‘sabbeheva piyehi manāpehi nānābhāvo vinābhāvo aññathābhāvo’;
- **EN**: we must be parted and separated from all we hold dear and beloved?

## dn16:5.14.4
- **PLI**: taṁ kutettha, ānanda, labbhā. Yaṁ taṁ jātaṁ bhūtaṁ saṅkhataṁ palokadhammaṁ, ‘taṁ vata tathāgatassāpi sarīraṁ mā palujjī’ti netaṁ ṭhānaṁ vijjati.
- **EN**: How could it possibly be so that what is born, created, conditioned, and liable to wear out should not wear out, even the Realized One’s body?

## dn16:5.14.5
- **PLI**: Dīgharattaṁ kho te, ānanda, tathāgato paccupaṭṭhito mettena kāyakammena hitena sukhena advayena appamāṇena, mettena vacīkammena hitena sukhena advayena appamāṇena, mettena manokammena hitena sukhena advayena appamāṇena.
- **EN**: For a long time, Ānanda, you’ve treated the Realized One with deeds of body, speech, and mind that are loving, beneficial, pleasant, undivided, and limitless.
- **NOTE**: The phrase “undivided and limitless” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>advayena appamāṇena</i>) normally describes <i lang='pi' translate='no'>kasiṇa</i> meditation (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/an10.25/en/sujato#1.3'>AN 10.25:1.3</a>); here it is also found in the Sanskrit: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>ānanda maitreṇa kāyakarmaṇā hitena sukhenādvayenāpramāṇena</i>.

## dn16:5.14.6
- **PLI**: Katapuññosi tvaṁ, ānanda, padhānamanuyuñja, khippaṁ hohisi anāsavo”ti.
- **EN**: You have done good deeds, Ānanda. Devote yourself to meditation, and you will soon be free of defilements.”
- **NOTE**: On the eve of the First Council—in about six months time—Ānanda devoted himself to meditation and achieved arahantship.

## dn16:5.16.1  `cck10.181, csp1ed7.120, csp2ed7.120, mc10.128, ms7D_468, msdiv209, pts-cs5.16`
- **PLI**: Cattārome, bhikkhave, acchariyā abbhutā dhammā ānande.
- **EN**: There are these four incredible and amazing things about Ānanda.
- **NOTE**: As at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an4.130/en/sujato#6.4'>AN 4.130:6.4</a>. Ānanda was in awe of the “incredible and amazing” qualities of the Buddha, and spoke of them often. Here, when Ānanda is at his most vulnerable, the Buddha turns the teaching around, pointing out that Ānanda is incredible and amazing too.
