# Muc 3.33 — The Eight Liberations  (44 đoạn)

## dn16:3.33.0
- **PLI**: 19. Aṭṭhavimokkha
- **EN**: 19. The Eight Liberations

## dn16:3.33.1  `bj8.176, cck10.141, dr10.132, ms7D_383, msdiv174, pts-cs3.33, pts-vp-pli2.112`
- **PLI**: Aṭṭha kho ime, ānanda, vimokkhā.
- **EN**: Ānanda, there are these eight liberations.
- **NOTE**: Already encountered at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn15/en/sujato#35.1'>DN 15:35.1</a>.

## dn16:3.33.2
- **PLI**: Katame aṭṭha?
- **EN**: What eight?

## dn16:3.33.3
- **PLI**: Rūpī rūpāni passati,
- **EN**: Having physical form, they see forms.

## dn16:3.33.4
- **PLI**: ayaṁ paṭhamo vimokkho.
- **EN**: This is the first liberation.

## dn16:3.33.5
- **PLI**: Ajjhattaṁ arūpasaññī bahiddhā rūpāni passati,
- **EN**: Not perceiving form internally, they see forms externally.

## dn16:3.33.6
- **PLI**: ayaṁ dutiyo vimokkho.
- **EN**: This is the second liberation.

## dn16:3.33.7
- **PLI**: Subhanteva adhimutto hoti,
- **EN**: They’re focused only on beauty.

## dn16:3.33.8
- **PLI**: ayaṁ tatiyo vimokkho.
- **EN**: This is the third liberation.

## dn16:3.33.9
- **PLI**: Sabbaso rūpasaññānaṁ samatikkamā paṭighasaññānaṁ atthaṅgamā nānattasaññānaṁ amanasikārā ‘ananto ākāso’ti ākāsānañcāyatanaṁ upasampajja viharati,
- **EN**: Going totally beyond perceptions of form, with the disappearance of perceptions of impingement, not focusing on perceptions of diversity, aware that ‘space is infinite’, they enter and remain in the dimension of infinite space.

## dn16:3.33.10
- **PLI**: ayaṁ catuttho vimokkho.
- **EN**: This is the fourth liberation.

## dn16:3.33.11
- **PLI**: Sabbaso ākāsānañcāyatanaṁ samatikkamma ‘anantaṁ viññāṇan’ti viññāṇañcāyatanaṁ upasampajja viharati,
- **EN**: Going totally beyond the dimension of infinite space, aware that ‘consciousness is infinite’, they enter and remain in the dimension of infinite consciousness.

## dn16:3.33.12
- **PLI**: ayaṁ pañcamo vimokkho.
- **EN**: This is the fifth liberation.

## dn16:3.33.13
- **PLI**: Sabbaso viññāṇañcāyatanaṁ samatikkamma ‘natthi kiñcī’ti ākiñcaññāyatanaṁ upasampajja viharati,
- **EN**: Going totally beyond the dimension of infinite consciousness, aware that ‘there is nothing at all’, they enter and remain in the dimension of nothingness.

## dn16:3.33.14
- **PLI**: ayaṁ chaṭṭho vimokkho.
- **EN**: This is the sixth liberation.

## dn16:3.33.15
- **PLI**: Sabbaso ākiñcaññāyatanaṁ samatikkamma nevasaññānāsaññāyatanaṁ upasampajja viharati,
- **EN**: Going totally beyond the dimension of nothingness, they enter and remain in the dimension of neither perception nor non-perception.

## dn16:3.33.16
- **PLI**: ayaṁ sattamo vimokkho.
- **EN**: This is the seventh liberation.

## dn16:3.33.17
- **PLI**: Sabbaso nevasaññānāsaññāyatanaṁ samatikkamma saññāvedayitanirodhaṁ upasampajja viharati,
- **EN**: Going totally beyond the dimension of neither perception nor non-perception, they enter and remain in the cessation of perception and feeling.

## dn16:3.33.18
- **PLI**: ayaṁ aṭṭhamo vimokkho.
- **EN**: This is the eighth liberation.

## dn16:3.33.19
- **PLI**: Ime kho, ānanda, aṭṭha vimokkhā.
- **EN**: These are the eight liberations.

## dn16:3.34.1  `cck10.142, mc10.101, ms7D_385, msdiv175, pts-cs3.34, sya10.132, vri2.87`
- **PLI**: Ekamidāhaṁ, ānanda, samayaṁ uruvelāyaṁ viharāmi najjā nerañjarāya tīre ajapālanigrodhe paṭhamābhisambuddho.
- **EN**: Ānanda, this one time, when I was first awakened, I was staying in Uruvelā at the goatherd’s banyan tree on the bank of the Nerañjarā River.
- **NOTE**: This period is depicted in the Pali text of the first Khandhaka of the Vinaya (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato'>Kd 1</a>), but this event is not recorded there. However, the Sanskrit Sarvāstivāda Catuṣparisatsūtra, which depicts the same period as a Sutta, includes it immediately after the encounter with the merchants Tapussa and Bhallika.

## dn16:3.34.2
- **PLI**: Atha kho, ānanda, māro pāpimā yenāhaṁ tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā ekamantaṁ aṭṭhāsi. Ekamantaṁ ṭhito kho, ānanda, māro pāpimā maṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: Then Māra the wicked approached me, stood to one side, and said:

## dn16:3.34.3
- **PLI**: ‘parinibbātu dāni, bhante, bhagavā; parinibbātu sugato, parinibbānakālo dāni, bhante, bhagavato’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Sir, may the Blessed One now be fully extinguished! May the Holy One now be fully extinguished! Now is the time for the full extinguishment of the Buddha.’
