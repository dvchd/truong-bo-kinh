# Mo dau  (14 đoạn)

## dn16:1.1.1  `bj8.110, cck10.91, csp1ed7.61, csp2ed7.61, dr10.86, mc10.65, ms7D_242, msdiv131, ndp7.58, pts-cs1.1, pts-vp-pli2.72, sya10.85, vri2.56`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—
- **EN**: So I have heard.
- **NOTE**: The longest of all early discourses, this dramatic and moving narrative tells the story of the Buddha’s slow journey toward his final passing.

## dn16:1.1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā rājagahe viharati gijjhakūṭe pabbate.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying near Rājagaha, on the Vulture’s Peak Mountain.

## dn16:1.1.3
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena rājā māgadho ajātasattu vedehiputto vajjī abhiyātukāmo hoti.
- **EN**: Now at that time King Ajātasattu of Magadha, son of the princess of Videha, wanted to invade the Vajjis.
- **NOTE**: After the events of <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn2/en/sujato'>DN 2</a>, Ajātasattu retained and consolidated his power. Magadha ultimately conquered the Vajji Federation and continued to expand until almost all of India was under its sway.

## dn16:1.1.4
- **PLI**: So evamāha:
- **EN**: He said:

## dn16:1.1.5
- **PLI**: “ahaṁ hime vajjī evaṁmahiddhike evaṁmahānubhāve ucchecchāmi vajjī, vināsessāmi vajjī, anayabyasanaṁ āpādessāmī”ti.
- **EN**: “I shall wipe out these Vajjis, so mighty and powerful! I shall destroy them, and lay ruin and devastation upon them!”
- **NOTE**: According to the commentary, Ajātasattu’s anger was rooted in a dispute on trade routes. Control of shipping on the Ganges was essential for establishing international trade. There was a port on the Ganges extending over a league, split half and half between Magadha and Vajji. Valuable products were sourced from a mountain and brought down for trade, but the Vajjis kept absconding with the whole lot. The place is not identified, but Munger, a strategic port east of Patna, fits the description. The nearby hills have been mined since paleolithic times. It is also likely, as maintained in Jain tradition, that the Vajjis disputed Ajātasattu’s accession after committing regicide. His threatening posture toward the Vajjis is also mentioned in <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn20.8/en/sujato#2.2'>SN 20.8:2.2</a>.

## dn16:1.2.1  `ms7D_243, msdiv132, pts-cs1.2, pts-vp-pli2.73`
- **PLI**: Atha kho rājā māgadho ajātasattu vedehiputto vassakāraṁ brāhmaṇaṁ magadhamahāmattaṁ āmantesi:
- **EN**: And then King Ajātasattu addressed Vassakāra the brahmin, a chief minister of Magadha,
- **NOTE**: Vassakāra appears in the suttas as a devoted follower of the Buddha.

## dn16:1.2.2
- **PLI**: “ehi tvaṁ, brāhmaṇa, yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkama; upasaṅkamitvā mama vacanena bhagavato pāde sirasā vandāhi, appābādhaṁ appātaṅkaṁ lahuṭṭhānaṁ balaṁ phāsuvihāraṁ puccha:
- **EN**: “Please, brahmin, go to the Buddha, and in my name bow with your head to his feet. Ask him if he is healthy and well, nimble, strong, and living comfortably.

## dn16:1.2.3
- **PLI**: ‘rājā, bhante, māgadho ajātasattu vedehiputto bhagavato pāde sirasā vandati, appābādhaṁ appātaṅkaṁ lahuṭṭhānaṁ balaṁ phāsuvihāraṁ pucchatī’ti.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn16:1.2.4
- **PLI**: Evañca vadehi:
- **EN**: And then say:

## dn16:1.2.5
- **PLI**: ‘rājā, bhante, māgadho ajātasattu vedehiputto vajjī abhiyātukāmo.
- **EN**: ‘Sir, King Ajātasattu of Magadha, son of the princess of Videha, wants to invade the Vajjis.

## dn16:1.2.6
- **PLI**: So evamāha:
- **EN**: He says,

## dn16:1.2.7
- **PLI**: “ahaṁ hime vajjī evaṁmahiddhike evaṁmahānubhāve ucchecchāmi vajjī, vināsessāmi vajjī, anayabyasanaṁ āpādessāmī”’ti.
- **EN**: “I shall wipe out these Vajjis, so mighty and powerful! I shall destroy them, and lay ruin and devastation upon them!”’
- **NOTE**: The Vajji Federation harks back to an early settlement founded by the legendary Nābhānediṣṭha in the Vedic period. It built its wealth on its extensive fertile plains and the trading possibilities opened up by the Gandak and Ganges rivers.

## dn16:1.2.8
- **PLI**: Yathā te bhagavā byākaroti, taṁ sādhukaṁ uggahetvā mama āroceyyāsi.
- **EN**: Remember well how the Buddha answers and tell it to me.

## dn16:1.2.9
- **PLI**: Na hi tathāgatā vitathaṁ bhaṇantī”ti.
- **EN**: For Realized Ones say nothing that is not so.”
- **NOTE**: While it may seem strange to consult the Buddha on such a violent plan, Ajātasattu knows from his experience in DN 2 that the Buddha will not hesitate to tell him the truth, even if it is bad news. It seems he is trying to avoid the downfall of tyrants who are surrounded only with yes men.
