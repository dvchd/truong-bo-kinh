# Muc 2.1 — Talk on the Noble Truths  (30 đoạn)

## dn16:2.1.0
- **PLI**: 8. Ariyasaccakathā
- **EN**: 8. Talk on the Noble Truths

## dn16:2.1.1  `bj8.142, dr10.108, mc10.82, ms7D_325, msdiv155, pts-cs2.1, pts-vp-pli2.90, sya10.107, vri2.71`
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā āyasmantaṁ ānandaṁ āmantesi:
- **EN**: Then the Buddha said to Venerable Ānanda,

## dn16:2.1.2
- **PLI**: “āyāmānanda, yena koṭigāmo tenupasaṅkamissāmā”ti.
- **EN**: “Come, Ānanda, let’s go to the village of Koṭi.”
- **NOTE**: Koṭigāma lay a short distance from the Ganges. The Buddha’s stay here must therefore have followed the events of <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn47.14/en/sujato'>SN 47.14</a> at Ukkacelā on the Vajjian bank of the Ganges, where he laments the passing of Sāriputta and Moggallāna. He would have heard of Sāriputta’s passing before then, while on the road from Nāḷandā to Pāṭaligāma, even though that is implausibly said to have happened at Sāvatthī (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn47.13/en/sujato'>SN 47.13</a>).

## dn16:2.1.3
- **PLI**: “Evaṁ, bhante”ti kho āyasmā ānando bhagavato paccassosi.
- **EN**: “Yes, sir,” Ānanda replied.

## dn16:2.1.4
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā mahatā bhikkhusaṅghena saddhiṁ yena koṭigāmo tadavasari.
- **EN**: Then the Buddha together with a large Saṅgha of mendicants arrived at the village of Koṭi,

## dn16:2.1.5
- **PLI**: Tatra sudaṁ bhagavā koṭigāme viharati.
- **EN**: and stayed there.

## dn16:2.2.1  `pts-cs2.2`
- **PLI**: Tatra kho bhagavā bhikkhū āmantesi:
- **EN**: There he addressed the mendicants:
- **NOTE**: Also at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn56.21/en/sujato'>SN 56.21</a>.

## dn16:2.2.2  `cck10.115, csp1ed7.77, csp2ed7.77, dr10.109, ms7D_326, ndp7.73`
- **PLI**: “Catunnaṁ, bhikkhave, ariyasaccānaṁ ananubodhā appaṭivedhā evamidaṁ dīghamaddhānaṁ sandhāvitaṁ saṁsaritaṁ mamañceva tumhākañca.
- **EN**: “Mendicants, due to not understanding and not penetrating four noble truths, both you and I have wandered and transmigrated for such a very long time.

## dn16:2.2.3
- **PLI**: Katamesaṁ catunnaṁ?
- **EN**: What four?

## dn16:2.2.4
- **PLI**: Dukkhassa, bhikkhave, ariyasaccassa ananubodhā appaṭivedhā evamidaṁ dīghamaddhānaṁ sandhāvitaṁ saṁsaritaṁ mamañceva tumhākañca.
- **EN**: The noble truths of suffering,

## dn16:2.2.5
- **PLI**: Dukkhasamudayassa, bhikkhave, ariyasaccassa ananubodhā appaṭivedhā evamidaṁ dīghamaddhānaṁ sandhāvitaṁ saṁsaritaṁ mamañceva tumhākañca.
- **EN**: the origin of suffering,

## dn16:2.2.6
- **PLI**: Dukkhanirodhassa, bhikkhave, ariyasaccassa ananubodhā appaṭivedhā evamidaṁ dīghamaddhānaṁ sandhāvitaṁ saṁsaritaṁ mamañceva tumhākañca.
- **EN**: the cessation of suffering,

## dn16:2.2.7
- **PLI**: Dukkhanirodhagāminiyā paṭipadāya, bhikkhave, ariyasaccassa ananubodhā appaṭivedhā evamidaṁ dīghamaddhānaṁ sandhāvitaṁ saṁsaritaṁ mamañceva tumhākañca.
- **EN**: and the practice that leads to the cessation of suffering.

## dn16:2.2.8
- **PLI**: Tayidaṁ, bhikkhave, dukkhaṁ ariyasaccaṁ anubuddhaṁ paṭividdhaṁ, dukkhasamudayaṁ ariyasaccaṁ anubuddhaṁ paṭividdhaṁ, dukkhanirodhaṁ ariyasaccaṁ anubuddhaṁ paṭividdhaṁ, dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā ariyasaccaṁ anubuddhaṁ paṭividdhaṁ, ucchinnā bhavataṇhā, khīṇā bhavanetti, natthi dāni punabbhavo”ti.
- **EN**: These noble truths of suffering, origin, cessation, and the path have been understood and comprehended. Craving for continued existence has been cut off; the leash to existence is ended; now there’ll be no more future lives.”

## dn16:2.3.2

- **EN**: “Because of not truly seeing

- **EN**: the leash to existence is eradicated.

## dn16:2.3.9
- **PLI**: Ucchinnaṁ mūlaṁ dukkhassa,
- **EN**: The root of suffering is cut off,
