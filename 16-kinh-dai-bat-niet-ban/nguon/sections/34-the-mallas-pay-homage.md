# Muc 5.20 — The Mallas Pay Homage  (17 đoạn)

## dn16:5.20.0
- **PLI**: 33. Mallānaṁvandanā
- **EN**: 33. The Mallas Pay Homage

## dn16:5.20.1  `dr10.173, mc10.130, ms7D_473, msdiv211, pts-cs5.20, pts-vp-pli2.148`
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena kosinārakā mallā sandhāgāre sannipatitā honti kenacideva karaṇīyena.
- **EN**: Now at that time the Mallas of Kusinārā were sitting together at the town hall on some business.
- **NOTE**: Evidently the Mallas, like the Vajjis, met frequently.

## dn16:5.20.2
- **PLI**: Atha kho āyasmā ānando yena kosinārakānaṁ mallānaṁ sandhāgāraṁ tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā kosinārakānaṁ mallānaṁ ārocesi:
- **EN**: Ānanda went up to them, and announced:

## dn16:5.20.3
- **PLI**: “ajja kho, vāseṭṭhā, rattiyā pacchime yāme tathāgatassa parinibbānaṁ bhavissati.
- **EN**: “This very day, Vāseṭṭhas, in the last watch of the night, the Realized One will be fully extinguished.

## dn16:5.20.4
- **PLI**: Abhikkamatha, vāseṭṭhā, abhikkamatha, vāseṭṭhā.
- **EN**: Come forth, Vāseṭṭhas! Come forth, Vāseṭṭhas!

## dn16:5.20.5
- **PLI**: Mā pacchā vippaṭisārino ahuvattha:
- **EN**: Don’t regret it later, thinking:

## dn16:5.20.6
- **PLI**: ‘amhākañca no gāmakkhette tathāgatassa parinibbānaṁ ahosi, na mayaṁ labhimhā pacchime kāle tathāgataṁ dassanāyā’”ti.
- **EN**: ‘The Realized One became fully extinguished in our own village district, but we didn’t get a chance to see him in his final hour.’”

## dn16:5.21.1  `bj8.232, cck10.185, csp1ed7.123, csp2ed7.123, ms7D_474, ndp7.115, pts-cs5.21, sya10.172, vri2.112`
- **PLI**: Idamāyasmato ānandassa vacanaṁ sutvā mallā ca mallaputtā ca mallasuṇisā ca mallapajāpatiyo ca aghāvino dummanā cetodukkhasamappitā appekacce kese pakiriya kandanti, bāhā paggayha kandanti, chinnapātaṁ papatanti, āvaṭṭanti vivaṭṭanti:
- **EN**: When they heard what Ānanda had to say, the Mallas, their sons, daughters-in-law, and wives became distraught, saddened, and grief-stricken. And some, with hair disheveled and arms raised, falling down like their feet were chopped off, rolling back and forth, lamented,

## dn16:5.21.2
- **PLI**: “atikhippaṁ bhagavā parinibbāyissati, atikhippaṁ sugato parinibbāyissati, atikhippaṁ cakkhuṁ loke antaradhāyissatī”ti.
- **EN**: “Too soon the Blessed One will be fully extinguished! Too soon the Holy One will be fully extinguished! Too soon the Eye of the World will vanish!”

## dn16:5.21.3
- **PLI**: Atha kho mallā ca mallaputtā ca mallasuṇisā ca mallapajāpatiyo ca aghāvino dummanā cetodukkhasamappitā yena upavattanaṁ mallānaṁ sālavanaṁ yenāyasmā ānando tenupasaṅkamiṁsu.
- **EN**: Then the Mallas, their sons, daughters-in-law, and wives, distraught, saddened, and grief-stricken went to the Mallian sal grove at Upavattana and approached Ānanda.

## dn16:5.22.1  `pts-cs5.22`
- **PLI**: Atha kho āyasmato ānandassa etadahosi:
- **EN**: Then Ānanda thought,

## dn16:5.22.2
- **PLI**: “sace kho ahaṁ kosinārake malle ekamekaṁ bhagavantaṁ vandāpessāmi, avandito bhagavā kosinārakehi mallehi bhavissati, athāyaṁ ratti vibhāyissati.
- **EN**: “If I have the Mallas pay homage to the Buddha one by one, they won’t be finished before first light.

## dn16:5.22.3
- **PLI**: Yannūnāhaṁ kosinārake malle kulaparivattaso kulaparivattaso ṭhapetvā bhagavantaṁ vandāpeyyaṁ:
- **EN**: I’d better separate them family by family and then have them pay homage, saying:

## dn16:5.22.4
- **PLI**: ‘itthannāmo, bhante, mallo saputto sabhariyo sapariso sāmacco bhagavato pāde sirasā vandatī’”ti.
- **EN**: ‘Sir, the Malla named so-and-so with children, wives, retinue, and ministers bows with his head to your feet.’”

## dn16:5.22.5
- **PLI**: Atha kho āyasmā ānando kosinārake malle kulaparivattaso kulaparivattaso ṭhapetvā bhagavantaṁ vandāpesi:
- **EN**: And so that’s what he did.

## dn16:5.22.6
- **PLI**: “itthannāmo, bhante, mallo saputto sabhariyo sapariso sāmacco bhagavato pāde sirasā vandatī”ti.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn16:5.22.7
- **PLI**: Atha kho āyasmā ānando etena upāyena paṭhameneva yāmena kosinārake malle bhagavantaṁ vandāpesi.
- **EN**: So by this means Ānanda got the Mallas to finish paying homage to the Buddha in the first watch of the night.
