# Muc 1.3 — The Brahmin Vassakāra  (8 đoạn)

## dn16:1.3.0
- **PLI**: 1. Vassakārabrāhmaṇa
- **EN**: 1. The Brahmin Vassakāra

## dn16:1.3.1  `bj8.112, cck10.92, csp1ed7.62, csp2ed7.62, dr10.87, mc10.66, ms7D_244, msdiv133, ndp7.59, pts-cs1.3, sya10.86, vri2.57`
- **PLI**: “Evaṁ, bho”ti kho vassakāro brāhmaṇo magadhamahāmatto rañño māgadhassa ajātasattussa vedehiputtassa paṭissutvā bhaddāni bhaddāni yānāni yojetvā bhaddaṁ bhaddaṁ yānaṁ abhiruhitvā bhaddehi bhaddehi yānehi rājagahamhā niyyāsi, yena gijjhakūṭo pabbato tena pāyāsi.
- **EN**: “Yes, worthy sir,” Vassakāra replied. He had the finest carriages harnessed. Then he mounted a fine carriage and, along with other fine carriages, set out from Rājagaha for the Vulture’s Peak Mountain.
- **NOTE**: Reading <i lang='pi' translate='no'>yojapetvā</i> (“had them harnessed”), which is found in the Mahāsaṅgīti in similar passages, and in this passage in the PTS edition.

## dn16:1.3.2
- **PLI**: Yāvatikā yānassa bhūmi, yānena gantvā, yānā paccorohitvā pattikova yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavatā saddhiṁ sammodi.
- **EN**: He went by carriage as far as the terrain allowed, then descended and approached the Buddha on foot, and exchanged greetings with him.

## dn16:1.3.3
- **PLI**: Sammodanīyaṁ kathaṁ sāraṇīyaṁ vītisāretvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi. Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho vassakāro brāhmaṇo magadhamahāmatto bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: When the greetings and polite conversation were over, he sat down to one side and said to the Buddha,

## dn16:1.3.4
- **PLI**: “rājā, bho gotama, māgadho ajātasattu vedehiputto bhoto gotamassa pāde sirasā vandati, appābādhaṁ appātaṅkaṁ lahuṭṭhānaṁ balaṁ phāsuvihāraṁ pucchati.
- **EN**: “Worthy Gotama, King Ajātasattu of Magadha, son of the princess of Videha, bows with his head to your feet. He asks if you are healthy and well, nimble, strong, and living comfortably.

## dn16:1.3.5
- **PLI**: Rājā, bho gotama, māgadho ajātasattu vedehiputto vajjī abhiyātukāmo.
- **EN**: Worthy Gotama, King Ajātasattu wants to invade the Vajjis.

## dn16:1.3.6
- **PLI**: So evamāha:
- **EN**: He said:

## dn16:1.3.7
- **PLI**: ‘ahaṁ hime vajjī evaṁmahiddhike evaṁmahānubhāve ucchecchāmi vajjī, vināsessāmi vajjī, anayabyasanaṁ āpādessāmī’”ti.
- **EN**: ‘I shall wipe out these Vajjis, so mighty and powerful! I shall destroy them, and lay ruin and devastation upon them!’”
