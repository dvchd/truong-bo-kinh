# Muc 5.1 — The Pair of Sal Trees  (25 đoạn)

## dn16:5.1.0
- **PLI**: 26. Yamakasālā
- **EN**: 26. The Pair of Sal Trees

## dn16:5.1.1  `bj8.214, dr10.160, mc10.121, ms7D_446, msdiv198, pts-cs5.1, pts-vp-pli2.137, sya10.160, vri2.104`
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā āyasmantaṁ ānandaṁ āmantesi:
- **EN**: Then the Buddha said to Ānanda,

## dn16:5.1.2
- **PLI**: “āyāmānanda, yena hiraññavatiyā nadiyā pārimaṁ tīraṁ, yena kusinārā upavattanaṁ mallānaṁ sālavanaṁ tenupasaṅkamissāmā”ti.
- **EN**: “Come, Ānanda, let’s go to the far shore of the Golden River, and on to the sal forest of the Mallas at Upavattana near Kusinārā.”
- **NOTE**: Known today as Kushinagar, it is a popular site for pilgrims, with many ancient stupas, Buddha images, and a peaceful park for meditation.

## dn16:5.1.3
- **PLI**: “Evaṁ, bhante”ti kho āyasmā ānando bhagavato paccassosi.
- **EN**: “Yes, sir,” Ānanda replied.

## dn16:5.1.4
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā mahatā bhikkhusaṅghena saddhiṁ yena hiraññavatiyā nadiyā pārimaṁ tīraṁ, yena kusinārā upavattanaṁ mallānaṁ sālavanaṁ tenupasaṅkami. upasaṅkamitvā āyasmantaṁ ānandaṁ āmantesi:
- **EN**: And that’s where they went. Then the Buddha addressed Ānanda,

## dn16:5.1.5
- **PLI**: “iṅgha me tvaṁ, ānanda, antarena yamakasālānaṁ uttarasīsakaṁ mañcakaṁ paññapehi, kilantosmi, ānanda, nipajjissāmī”ti.
- **EN**: “Go on then, Ānanda, set up a cot for me between the twin sal trees, with my head to the north. I am tired and will lie down.”

## dn16:5.1.6
- **PLI**: “Evaṁ, bhante”ti kho āyasmā ānando bhagavato paṭissutvā antarena yamakasālānaṁ uttarasīsakaṁ mañcakaṁ paññapesi.
- **EN**: “Yes, sir,” replied Ānanda, and did as he was asked.

## dn16:5.1.7
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā dakkhiṇena passena sīhaseyyaṁ kappesi pāde pādaṁ accādhāya sato sampajāno.
- **EN**: And then the Buddha laid down in the lion’s posture—on the right side, placing one foot on top of the other—mindful and aware.
- **NOTE**: Normally when the Buddha lies down, his mind is focused on getting up. But now he knows that he will not rise again.

## dn16:5.2.1  `cck10.172, csp1ed7.114, csp2ed7.114, ms7D_447, ndp7.107, pts-cs5.2, pts-vp-pli2.138`
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena yamakasālā sabbaphāliphullā honti akālapupphehi.
- **EN**: Now at that time the twin sal trees were in full blossom with flowers out of season.
- **NOTE**: Sal trees blossom in April/May. This detail agrees with the Buddha’s final extinguishment in December/January, rather than in May (Vesak) as is currently celebrated (see note to <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#3.9.3'>DN 16:3.9.3</a>).

## dn16:5.2.2
- **PLI**: Te tathāgatassa sarīraṁ okiranti ajjhokiranti abhippakiranti tathāgatassa pūjāya.
- **EN**: They sprinkled and bestrewed the Realized One’s body in honor of the Realized One.

## dn16:5.2.3
- **PLI**: Dibbānipi mandāravapupphāni antalikkhā papatanti, tāni tathāgatassa sarīraṁ okiranti ajjhokiranti abhippakiranti tathāgatassa pūjāya.
- **EN**: And the flowers of the heavenly Flame Tree fell from the sky, and they too sprinkled and bestrewed the Realized One’s body in honor of the Realized One.

## dn16:5.2.4
- **PLI**: Dibbānipi candanacuṇṇāni antalikkhā papatanti, tāni tathāgatassa sarīraṁ okiranti ajjhokiranti abhippakiranti tathāgatassa pūjāya.
- **EN**: And heavenly sandalwood powder fell from the sky, and it too sprinkled and bestrewed the Realized One’s body in honor of the Realized One.

## dn16:5.2.5
- **PLI**: Dibbānipi tūriyāni antalikkhe vajjanti tathāgatassa pūjāya.
- **EN**: And heavenly music played in midair in honor of the Realized One.

## dn16:5.2.6
- **PLI**: Dibbānipi saṅgītāni antalikkhe vattanti tathāgatassa pūjāya.
- **EN**: And heavenly choirs sang in midair in honor of the Realized One.

## dn16:5.3.1  `cck10.173, dr10.161, mc10.122, ms7D_448, msdiv199, pts-cs5.3, sya10.161`
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā āyasmantaṁ ānandaṁ āmantesi:
- **EN**: Then the Buddha pointed out to Ānanda what was happening, adding:

## dn16:5.3.2
- **PLI**: “sabbaphāliphullā kho, ānanda, yamakasālā akālapupphehi.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn16:5.3.3
- **PLI**: Te tathāgatassa sarīraṁ okiranti ajjhokiranti abhippakiranti tathāgatassa pūjāya.
- **EN**: They sprinkled and bestrewed the Realized One’s body in honor of the Realized One.  (= dn16:5.2.2)

## dn16:5.3.4
- **PLI**: Dibbānipi mandāravapupphāni antalikkhā papatanti, tāni tathāgatassa sarīraṁ okiranti ajjhokiranti abhippakiranti tathāgatassa pūjāya.
- **EN**: And the flowers of the heavenly Flame Tree fell from the sky, and they too sprinkled and bestrewed the Realized One’s body in honor of the Realized One.  (= dn16:5.2.3)

## dn16:5.3.5
- **PLI**: Dibbānipi candanacuṇṇāni antalikkhā papatanti, tāni tathāgatassa sarīraṁ okiranti ajjhokiranti abhippakiranti tathāgatassa pūjāya.
- **EN**: And heavenly sandalwood powder fell from the sky, and it too sprinkled and bestrewed the Realized One’s body in honor of the Realized One.  (= dn16:5.2.4)

## dn16:5.3.6
- **PLI**: Dibbānipi tūriyāni antalikkhe vajjanti tathāgatassa pūjāya.
- **EN**: And heavenly music played in midair in honor of the Realized One.  (= dn16:5.2.5)

## dn16:5.3.7
- **PLI**: Dibbānipi saṅgītāni antalikkhe vattanti tathāgatassa pūjāya.
- **EN**: And heavenly choirs sang in midair in honor of the Realized One.  (= dn16:5.2.6)

## dn16:5.3.8
- **PLI**: Na kho, ānanda, ettāvatā tathāgato sakkato vā hoti garukato vā mānito vā pūjito vā apacito vā.
- **EN**: “That’s not the full extent of how the Realized One is honored, respected, revered, venerated, and esteemed.

## dn16:5.3.9
- **PLI**: Yo kho, ānanda, bhikkhu vā bhikkhunī vā upāsako vā upāsikā vā dhammānudhammappaṭipanno viharati sāmīcippaṭipanno anudhammacārī, so tathāgataṁ sakkaroti garuṁ karoti māneti pūjeti apaciyati, paramāya pūjāya.
- **EN**: Any monk or nun or male or female lay follower who practices in line with the teaching, practicing properly, living in line with the teaching—they honor, respect, revere, venerate, and esteem the Realized One with the highest honor.
- **NOTE**: This calls back to the description of the fourfold assembly in the Māra section above (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#3.7.4'>DN 16:3.7.4</a>).

## dn16:5.3.10
- **PLI**: Tasmātihānanda, dhammānudhammappaṭipannā viharissāma sāmīcippaṭipannā anudhammacārinoti.
- **EN**: So Ānanda, you should train like this: ‘We shall practice in line with the teaching, practicing properly, living in line with the teaching.’”
- **NOTE**: This kind of narrative elevation is characteristic of the Buddha’s teaching. He did not try to deny or eliminate any belief in the miraculous, or in the power of devotion, but rather to show that such things were of limited worth compared with practice.

## dn16:5.3.11
- **PLI**: Evañhi vo, ānanda, sikkhitabban”ti.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
