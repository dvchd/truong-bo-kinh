# Muc 6.19 — Mahākassapa’s Arrival  (46 đoạn)

## dn16:6.19.0
- **PLI**: 38. Mahākassapattheravatthu
- **EN**: 38. Mahākassapa’s Arrival

## dn16:6.19.1  `bj8.256, cck10.203, dr10.189, mc10.142, ms7D_510, msdiv231, ndp7.125, pts-cs6.19, sya10.188, vri2.122`
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena āyasmā mahākassapo pāvāya kusināraṁ addhānamaggappaṭipanno hoti mahatā bhikkhusaṅghena saddhiṁ pañcamattehi bhikkhusatehi.
- **EN**: Now at that time Venerable Mahākassapa was traveling along the road from Pāvā to Kusinārā together with a large Saṅgha of five hundred mendicants.
- **NOTE**: With the passing of Sāriputta and Moggallāna, Mahākassapa was the most senior of the remaining leading mendicants. As a solitary recluse, it was unusual for him to be travelling with such a large group, or with anyone at all really. He was to cite the events depicted here at the start of the First Council (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#1.1.1'>Kd 1:1.1.1</a>).

## dn16:6.19.2
- **PLI**: Atha kho āyasmā mahākassapo maggā okkamma aññatarasmiṁ rukkhamūle nisīdi.
- **EN**: Then he left the road and sat at the root of a tree.

## dn16:6.19.3
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena aññataro ājīvako kusinārāya mandāravapupphaṁ gahetvā pāvaṁ addhānamaggappaṭipanno hoti.
- **EN**: Now at that time a certain Ājīvaka ascetic had picked up a Flame Tree flower in Kusinārā and was traveling along the road to Pāvā.
- **NOTE**: A follower of the Bamboo-staffed Ascetic Gosāla (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn2/en/sujato#19.1'>DN 2:19.1</a>). | This may be an inspiration for the so-called “Flower Sermon”, a medieval Zen story that depicts the Buddha holding up a flower and Mahakassapa smiling.

## dn16:6.19.4

- **EN**: Mahākassapa saw him coming off in the distance and said to him,

## dn16:6.19.5

- **EN**: “Yes, reverend. Seven days ago the ascetic Gotama was fully quenched.

## dn16:6.19.7

- **EN**: From there I picked up this Flame Tree flower.”

## dn16:6.19.9

- **EN**: “Too soon the Blessed One has become fully quenched! Too soon the Holy One has become fully quenched! Too soon the Eye of the World has vanished!”

## dn16:6.19.10
- **PLI**: Ye pana te bhikkhū vītarāgā, te satā sampajānā adhivāsenti:
- **EN**: But the mendicants who were free of desire endured, mindful and aware, thinking,

## dn16:6.19.11

- **EN**: “Conditions are impermanent. How could it possibly be otherwise?”
