# Muc 4.21 — Bringing a Drink  (29 đoạn)

## dn16:4.21.0
- **PLI**: 24. Pānīyāharaṇa
- **EN**: 24. Bringing a Drink

## dn16:4.21.1  `cck10.161, csp1ed7.107, csp2ed7.107, dr10.150, mc10.114, ms7D_426, msdiv191, ndp7.100, pts-cs4.21, pts-vp-pli2.129, sya10.150, vri2.98`
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā maggā okkamma yena aññataraṁ rukkhamūlaṁ tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā āyasmantaṁ ānandaṁ āmantesi:
- **EN**: Then the Buddha left the road and went to the root of a certain tree, where he addressed Ānanda,

## dn16:4.21.2
- **PLI**: “iṅgha me tvaṁ, ānanda, catugguṇaṁ saṅghāṭiṁ paññapehi, kilantosmi, ānanda, nisīdissāmī”ti.
- **EN**: “Go on then, Ānanda, fold my outer robe in four and spread it out for me. I am tired and will sit down.”
- **NOTE**: The <i lang='pi' translate='no'>saṅghāṭi</i> (“outer robe” or “cloak”) is double-layered, and served both for warmth and as bedding. The Buddha has not recovered fully from his illness.

## dn16:4.21.3
- **PLI**: “Evaṁ, bhante”ti kho āyasmā ānando bhagavato paṭissutvā catugguṇaṁ saṅghāṭiṁ paññapesi.
- **EN**: “Yes, sir,” replied Ānanda, and did as he was asked.

## dn16:4.21.4
- **PLI**: Nisīdi bhagavā paññatte āsane.
- **EN**: The Buddha sat down on the seat spread out.

## dn16:4.22.1  `pts-cs4.22`
- **PLI**: Nisajja kho bhagavā āyasmantaṁ ānandaṁ āmantesi:
- **EN**: When he was seated he said to Venerable Ānanda,

## dn16:4.22.2
- **PLI**: “iṅgha me tvaṁ, ānanda, pānīyaṁ āhara, pipāsitosmi, ānanda, pivissāmī”ti.
- **EN**: “Go on then, Ānanda, fetch me some water. I am thirsty and will drink.”
- **NOTE**: The Buddha was an exemplary patient. He did not complain, but made his needs known clearly to his carer.

## dn16:4.22.3
- **PLI**: Evaṁ vutte, āyasmā ānando bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: When he said this, Venerable Ānanda said to the Buddha,

## dn16:4.22.4
- **PLI**: “idāni, bhante, pañcamattāni sakaṭasatāni atikkantāni, taṁ cakkacchinnaṁ udakaṁ parittaṁ luḷitaṁ āvilaṁ sandati.
- **EN**: “Sir, just now around five hundred carts have passed by. The shallow water has been churned up by their wheels, and it flows cloudy and murky.

## dn16:4.22.5
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ, bhante, kakudhā nadī avidūre acchodakā sātodakā sītodakā setodakā suppatitthā ramaṇīyā.
- **EN**: The Kakudhā River is not far away, with clear, sweet, cool water, clean, with smooth banks, delightful.

## dn16:4.22.6
- **PLI**: Ettha bhagavā pānīyañca pivissati, gattāni ca sītī karissatī”ti.
- **EN**: There the Buddha can drink and cool his limbs.”
- **NOTE**: It would seem that, since the Buddha was enduring his illness without complaint and with dignity, Ānanda did not fully realize how weak he has become.

## dn16:4.24.4
- **PLI**: Atha kho sā nadikā cakkacchinnā parittā luḷitā āvilā sandamānā, āyasmante ānande upasaṅkamante acchā vippasannā anāvilā sandittha.
- **EN**: Now, though the shallow water in that creek had been churned up by wheels, and flowed cloudy and murky, when Ānanda approached it flowed transparent, clear, and unclouded.
