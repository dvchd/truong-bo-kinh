## dn16:1.19.2
- **PLI**: “āyāmānanda, yena pāṭaligāmo tenupasaṅkamissāmā”ti.
- **EN**: “Come, Ānanda, let’s go to Pāṭali Village.”
- **NOTE**: This is modern Patna. In this account we see how it was developed from the simple riverside village of Pāṭaligāma to the citadel of Pāṭaliputta. Ajātasattu ultimately moved the capital of Magadha from the defensively-postured Rājagaha surrounded by hills to this trading center on open waters. Under Ashoka it became one of the greatest cities of the ancient world. It was named after the <i lang='pi' translate='no'>pāṭalī</i> tree (<em>Stereospermum chelonoides</em>), known as <em>patala</em> in Hindi.

- **EN**: “Householders, there are these five drawbacks for an unethical person because of their failure in ethics.
- **NOTE**: Also at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an5.213/en/sujato'>AN 5.213</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/ud8.6/en/sujato#4.1'>Ud 8.6:4.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn33/en/sujato#2.1.36'>DN 33:2.1.36</a>, and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#28.4.1'>Kd 1:28.4.1</a>.

## dn16:1.23.3

- **EN**: What five?

## dn16:1.23.4
- **PLI**: Idha, gahapatayo, dussīlo sīlavipanno pamādādhikaraṇaṁ mahatiṁ bhogajāniṁ nigacchati.
- **EN**: Firstly, an unethical person loses great wealth on account of negligence.
- **NOTE**: The Buddha begins with the things most obvious and pertinent to the audience.

## dn16:1.23.5
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ paṭhamo ādīnavo dussīlassa sīlavipattiyā.
- **EN**: This is the first drawback for an unethical person because of their failure in ethics.

## dn16:1.23.6  `ms7D_301`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, gahapatayo, dussīlassa sīlavipannassa pāpako kittisaddo abbhuggacchati.

## dn16:1.23.7
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ dutiyo ādīnavo dussīlassa sīlavipattiyā.
- **EN**: This is the second drawback.

## dn16:1.23.8  `ms7D_302`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, gahapatayo, dussīlo sīlavipanno yaññadeva parisaṁ upasaṅkamati—yadi khattiyaparisaṁ yadi brāhmaṇaparisaṁ yadi gahapatiparisaṁ yadi samaṇaparisaṁ—avisārado upasaṅkamati maṅkubhūto.
- **EN**: Furthermore, an unethical person enters any kind of assembly unconfident and embarrassed, whether it’s an assembly of aristocrats, brahmins, householders, or ascetics.

## dn16:1.23.9

- **EN**: This is the third drawback.
