# Muc 1.24 — The Benefits of Ethical Conduct  (18 đoạn)

## dn16:1.24.0
- **PLI**: 6. Sīlavantaānisaṁsa
- **EN**: 6. The Benefits of Ethical Conduct

## dn16:1.24.1  `dr10.103, ms7D_305, msdiv150, pts-cs1.24, pts-vp-pli2.86, sya10.102`
- **PLI**: Pañcime, gahapatayo, ānisaṁsā sīlavato sīlasampadāya.
- **EN**: There are these five benefits for an ethical person because of their accomplishment in ethics.

## dn16:1.24.2
- **PLI**: Katame pañca?
- **EN**: What five?

## dn16:1.24.3
- **PLI**: Idha, gahapatayo, sīlavā sīlasampanno appamādādhikaraṇaṁ mahantaṁ bhogakkhandhaṁ adhigacchati.
- **EN**: Firstly, an ethical person gains great wealth on account of diligence.

## dn16:1.24.4
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ paṭhamo ānisaṁso sīlavato sīlasampadāya.
- **EN**: This is the first benefit.

## dn16:1.24.5  `ms7D_306, vri2.68`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, gahapatayo, sīlavato sīlasampannassa kalyāṇo kittisaddo abbhuggacchati.
- **EN**: Furthermore, an ethical person gets a good reputation.

## dn16:1.24.6
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ dutiyo ānisaṁso sīlavato sīlasampadāya.
- **EN**: This is the second benefit.

## dn16:1.24.7  `ms7D_307`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, gahapatayo, sīlavā sīlasampanno yaññadeva parisaṁ upasaṅkamati—yadi khattiyaparisaṁ yadi brāhmaṇaparisaṁ yadi gahapatiparisaṁ yadi samaṇaparisaṁ visārado upasaṅkamati amaṅkubhūto.
- **EN**: Furthermore, an ethical person enters any kind of assembly bold and self-assured, whether it’s an assembly of aristocrats, brahmins, householders, or ascetics.

## dn16:1.24.8
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ tatiyo ānisaṁso sīlavato sīlasampadāya.
- **EN**: This is the third benefit.

## dn16:1.24.9  `ms7D_308`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, gahapatayo, sīlavā sīlasampanno asammūḷho kālaṁ karoti.
- **EN**: Furthermore, an ethical person dies not feeling lost.

## dn16:1.24.10
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ catuttho ānisaṁso sīlavato sīlasampadāya.
- **EN**: This is the fourth benefit.

## dn16:1.24.11  `bj8.136, ms7D_309`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, gahapatayo, sīlavā sīlasampanno kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā sugatiṁ saggaṁ lokaṁ upapajjati.
- **EN**: Furthermore, when an ethical person’s body breaks up, after death, they’re reborn in a good place, a heavenly realm.

## dn16:1.24.12
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ pañcamo ānisaṁso sīlavato sīlasampadāya.
- **EN**: This is the fifth benefit.

## dn16:1.24.13
- **PLI**: Ime kho, gahapatayo, pañca ānisaṁsā sīlavato sīlasampadāyā”ti.
- **EN**: These are the five benefits for an ethical person because of their accomplishment in ethics.”

## dn16:1.25.1  `cck10.110, dr10.104, mc10.79, ms7D_310, msdiv151, ndp7.70, pts-cs1.25, sya10.103`
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā pāṭaligāmike upāsake bahudeva rattiṁ dhammiyā kathāya sandassetvā samādapetvā samuttejetvā sampahaṁsetvā uyyojesi:
- **EN**: The Buddha spent much of the night educating, encouraging, firing up, and inspiring the lay followers of Pāṭali Village with a Dhamma talk. Then he sent them off,

## dn16:1.25.2
- **PLI**: “abhikkantā kho, gahapatayo, ratti, yassadāni tumhe kālaṁ maññathā”ti.
- **EN**: “The night is getting late, householders. Please go at your convenience.”

## dn16:1.25.3
- **PLI**: “Evaṁ, bhante”ti kho pāṭaligāmikā upāsakā bhagavato paṭissutvā uṭṭhāyāsanā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā padakkhiṇaṁ katvā pakkamiṁsu.
- **EN**: “Yes, sir,” replied the lay followers of Pāṭali Village. They got up from their seat, bowed and respectfully circled the Buddha, keeping him on their right, before leaving.

## dn16:1.25.4
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā acirapakkantesu pāṭaligāmikesu upāsakesu suññāgāraṁ pāvisi.
- **EN**: Soon after they left the Buddha entered a private cubicle.
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Suññāgāra</i> normally means an “empty dwelling”, but here the commentary describes it as a curtained-off cubicle.
