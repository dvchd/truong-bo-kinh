## dn16:1.16.1  `cck10.104, ms7D_291, msdiv145, pts-cs1.16, pts-vp-pli2.82`
- **PLI**: Atha kho āyasmā sāriputto yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi. Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho āyasmā sāriputto bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: Then Sāriputta went up to the Buddha, bowed, sat down to one side, and said to him,
- **NOTE**: This was apparently the last time that the Buddha met Sāriputta. This encounter is recorded in an independent sutta at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn47.12/en/sujato'>SN 47.12</a> and expanded into a long discourse at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn28/en/sujato'>DN 28</a>. The Saṁyutta follows this meeting with the record of Sāriputta’s passing at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn47.13/en/sujato'>SN 47.13</a>, and then the lament for the absence of both Sāriputta and Moggallāna at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn47.14/en/sujato'>SN 47.14</a>. For some reason these events were omitted from the present discourse, even though they fit well thematically.
