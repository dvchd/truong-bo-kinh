- **EN**: where he addressed Venerable Cundaka,
- **NOTE**: The monk Cundaka makes an abrupt appearance here; this is the only mention of this name. The commentary to this passage calls him <i lang='pi' translate='no'>cunda</i>, while the gloss to the parallel passage at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/ud8.5/en/sujato#14.3'>Ud 8.5:14.3</a> has <i lang='pi' translate='no'>cundaka</i> throughout. The diminutive ending <i lang='pi' translate='no'>-ka</i> is perhaps meant to differentiate him from the smith Cunda. There are various Cundas whose connection is unclear. Mahācunda was one of the great disciples (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn8/en/sujato'>MN 8</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn118/en/sujato'>MN 118</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an6.17/en/sujato'>AN 6.17</a>), who later brought the Dhamma to the land of the Cetīs (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/an6.46/en/sujato'>AN 6.46</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an10.24/en/sujato'>AN 10.24</a>). He once stayed with Channa and Sāriputta (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn144/en/sujato#4.1'>MN 144:4.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn35.87/en/sujato#1.2'>SN 35.87:1.2</a>); the commentaries say he was in fact Sāriputta’s younger brother. They also identify him with the “novice Cunda” who reported the deaths of Sāriputta (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn47.13/en/sujato#1.3'>SN 47.13:1.3</a>) and Mahāvīra (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn29/en/sujato#2.1'>DN 29:2.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn104/en/sujato#3.1'>MN 104:3.1</a>), explaining that the title “novice” was a nickname that persisted from the time he ordained as a young novice. It is unclear whether the commentaries take the Cundaka of this passage to be the same person, but he is here performing a similar role as carer adjacent to the Buddha’s death. Later there appears a Cūḷacunda (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/tha-ap52/en/sujato#13.3'>Tha Ap 52:13.3</a>). Thus there may have been one person known by different names, or several people whose stories have become conflated.
