# Muc 17 — The Truths  (5 đoạn)

## dn22:17.0
- **PLI**: 4.5. Dhammānupassanāsaccapabba
- **EN**: 4.5. The Truths

## dn22:17.1  `cck10.357, dr10.337, mc10.260, ms7D_1034, msdiv386, pts-cs17`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, bhikkhu dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati catūsu ariyasaccesu.
- **EN**: Furthermore, a mendicant meditates by observing an aspect of principles with respect to the four noble truths.
- **NOTE**: Due to their development of the two wings of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>samatha</i> and <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vipassanā</i> meditation as described in this sutta, practiced in the context of the teaching and training as a whole, the meditator realizes the four noble truths at the moment of stream-entry.

## dn22:17.2
- **PLI**: Kathañca pana, bhikkhave, bhikkhu dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati catūsu ariyasaccesu?
- **EN**: And how does a mendicant meditate observing an aspect of principles with respect to the four noble truths?

## dn22:17.3
- **PLI**: Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu ‘idaṁ dukkhan’ti yathābhūtaṁ pajānāti, ‘ayaṁ dukkhasamudayo’ti yathābhūtaṁ pajānāti, ‘ayaṁ dukkhanirodho’ti yathābhūtaṁ pajānāti, ‘ayaṁ dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā’ti yathābhūtaṁ pajānāti.
- **EN**: It’s when a mendicant truly understands: ‘This is suffering’ … ‘This is the origin of suffering’ … ‘This is the cessation of suffering’ … ‘This is the practice that leads to the cessation of suffering.’

## dn22:17.4
- **PLI**: Paṭhamabhāṇavāro niṭṭhito.
- **EN**: The first recitation section is complete.
