# Muc 11 — Observing the Feelings  (16 đoạn)

## dn22:11.0
- **PLI**: 2. Vedanānupassanā
- **EN**: 2. Observing the Feelings

## dn22:11.1  `dr10.329, ms7D_977, msdiv380, pts-cs11, sya10.333`
- **PLI**: Kathañca pana, bhikkhave, bhikkhu vedanāsu vedanānupassī viharati?
- **EN**: And how does a mendicant meditate observing an aspect of feelings?
- **NOTE**: Literally “a feeling among the feelings”; the practice shows that the meditator contemplates specific feelings as they occur.

## dn22:11.2
- **PLI**: Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu sukhaṁ vā vedanaṁ vedayamāno ‘sukhaṁ vedanaṁ vedayāmī’ti pajānāti.
- **EN**: It’s when a mendicant who feels a pleasant feeling knows: ‘I feel a pleasant feeling.’
- **NOTE**: Pali employs direct quotes to indicate reflexive awareness: you feel the feeling and you know that you feel the feeling. It does not mean that you have to literally say “I feel a pleasant feeling”, although some adopt that as a meditation method.

## dn22:11.3  `cck10.349, ms7D_978`
- **PLI**: Dukkhaṁ vā vedanaṁ vedayamāno ‘dukkhaṁ vedanaṁ vedayāmī’ti pajānāti.
- **EN**: When they feel a painful feeling, they know: ‘I feel a painful feeling.’

## dn22:11.4  `ms7D_979`
- **PLI**: Adukkhamasukhaṁ vā vedanaṁ vedayamāno ‘adukkhamasukhaṁ vedanaṁ vedayāmī’ti pajānāti.
- **EN**: When they feel a neutral feeling, they know: ‘I feel a neutral feeling.’

## dn22:11.5  `bj8.462, ms7D_980`
- **PLI**: Sāmisaṁ vā sukhaṁ vedanaṁ vedayamāno ‘sāmisaṁ sukhaṁ vedanaṁ vedayāmī’ti pajānāti.
- **EN**: When they feel a pleasant feeling of the flesh, they know: ‘I feel a pleasant feeling of the flesh.’
- **NOTE**: Feelings “of the flesh” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>sāmisa</i>) are associated with the body and sensual desires (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn36.31/en/sujato#4.1'>SN 36.31:4.1</a>).

## dn22:11.6  `ms7D_981`
- **PLI**: Nirāmisaṁ vā sukhaṁ vedanaṁ vedayamāno ‘nirāmisaṁ sukhaṁ vedanaṁ vedayāmī’ti pajānāti.
- **EN**: When they feel a pleasant feeling not of the flesh, they know: ‘I feel a pleasant feeling not of the flesh.’
- **NOTE**: Feelings “not of the flesh” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>nirāmisa</i>) are associated with renunciation and especially with the <i lang='pi' translate='no'>jhānas</i> and liberation (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn36.31/en/sujato#5.1'>SN 36.31:5.1</a>).

## dn22:11.7  `ms7D_982`
- **PLI**: Sāmisaṁ vā dukkhaṁ vedanaṁ vedayamāno ‘sāmisaṁ dukkhaṁ vedanaṁ vedayāmī’ti pajānāti.
- **EN**: When they feel a painful feeling of the flesh, they know: ‘I feel a painful feeling of the flesh.’

## dn22:11.8  `ms7D_983`
- **PLI**: Nirāmisaṁ vā dukkhaṁ vedanaṁ vedayamāno ‘nirāmisaṁ dukkhaṁ vedanaṁ vedayāmī’ti pajānāti.
- **EN**: When they feel a painful feeling not of the flesh, they know: ‘I feel a painful feeling not of the flesh.’
- **NOTE**: This would include the feelings of loss, doubt, and dejection that can occur during the spiritual path (see <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn44/en/sujato#28.6'>MN 44:28.6</a>).

## dn22:11.9  `ms7D_984`
- **PLI**: Sāmisaṁ vā adukkhamasukhaṁ vedanaṁ vedayamāno ‘sāmisaṁ adukkhamasukhaṁ vedanaṁ vedayāmī’ti pajānāti.
- **EN**: When they feel a neutral feeling of the flesh, they know: ‘I feel a neutral feeling of the flesh.’

## dn22:11.10  `ms7D_985`
- **PLI**: Nirāmisaṁ vā adukkhamasukhaṁ vedanaṁ vedayamāno ‘nirāmisaṁ adukkhamasukhaṁ vedanaṁ vedayāmī’ti pajānāti.
- **EN**: When they feel a neutral feeling not of the flesh, they know: ‘I feel a neutral feeling not of the flesh.’
- **NOTE**: The feeling of the fourth <i lang='pi' translate='no'>jhāna</i> and higher liberations (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn36.31/en/sujato#8.2'>SN 36.31:8.2</a>).

## dn22:11.11  `csp1ed7.237, csp2ed7.237, ms7D_986, ndp7.222, pts-vp-pli2.299, vri2.221`
- **PLI**: Iti ajjhattaṁ vā vedanāsu vedanānupassī viharati, bahiddhā vā vedanāsu vedanānupassī viharati, ajjhattabahiddhā vā vedanāsu vedanānupassī viharati.
- **EN**: And so they meditate observing an aspect of feelings internally, externally, and both internally and externally.

## dn22:11.12
- **PLI**: Samudayadhammānupassī vā vedanāsu viharati, vayadhammānupassī vā vedanāsu viharati, samudayavayadhammānupassī vā vedanāsu viharati.
- **EN**: With respect to feelings, they meditate observing the liability to originate, to vanish, and to originate and vanish.

## dn22:11.13
- **PLI**: ‘Atthi vedanā’ti vā panassa sati paccupaṭṭhitā hoti yāvadeva ñāṇamattāya paṭissatimattāya anissito ca viharati, na ca kiñci loke upādiyati.
- **EN**: Or mindfulness is established that feelings exist, to the extent necessary for knowledge and mindfulness. They meditate independent, not grasping at anything in the world.

## dn22:11.14
- **PLI**: Evampi kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhu vedanāsu vedanānupassī viharati.
- **EN**: That’s how a mendicant meditates by observing an aspect of feelings.

## dn22:11.15
- **PLI**: Vedanānupassanā niṭṭhitā.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
