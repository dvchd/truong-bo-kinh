# Muc 16 — The Awakening Factors  (15 đoạn)

## dn22:16.0
- **PLI**: 4.4. Dhammānupassanābojjhaṅgapabba
- **EN**: 4.4. The Awakening Factors

## dn22:16.1  `mc10.259, ms7D_1025, msdiv385, ndp7.226, pts-cs16, vri2.226`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, bhikkhu dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati sattasu bojjhaṅgesu.
- **EN**: Furthermore, a mendicant meditates by observing an aspect of principles with respect to the seven awakening factors.
- **NOTE**: These seven factors that lead to awakening (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>bojjhaṅgā</i>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn46.5/en/sujato'>SN 46.5</a>) are commonly presented in opposition to the five hindrances (eg. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn46.2/en/sujato'>SN 46.2</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn46.23/en/sujato'>SN 46.23</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn46.55/en/sujato'>SN 46.55</a>).

## dn22:16.2
- **PLI**: Kathañca pana, bhikkhave, bhikkhu dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati sattasu bojjhaṅgesu?
- **EN**: And how does a mendicant meditate observing an aspect of principles with respect to the seven awakening factors?

## dn22:16.3
- **PLI**: Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu santaṁ vā ajjhattaṁ satisambojjhaṅgaṁ ‘atthi me ajjhattaṁ satisambojjhaṅgo’ti pajānāti, asantaṁ vā ajjhattaṁ satisambojjhaṅgaṁ ‘natthi me ajjhattaṁ satisambojjhaṅgo’ti pajānāti, yathā ca anuppannassa satisambojjhaṅgassa uppādo hoti tañca pajānāti, yathā ca uppannassa satisambojjhaṅgassa bhāvanāya pāripūrī hoti tañca pajānāti.
- **EN**: It’s when a mendicant who has the awakening factor of mindfulness in them understands: ‘I have the awakening factor of mindfulness in me.’ When they don’t have the awakening factor of mindfulness in them, they understand: ‘I don’t have the awakening factor of mindfulness in me.’ They understand how the awakening factor of mindfulness that has not arisen comes to arise; and how the awakening factor of mindfulness that has arisen becomes fully developed.
- **NOTE**: “Mindfulness” includes the recollection of the teachings (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn46.3/en/sujato#1.8'>SN 46.3:1.8</a>) as well as mindfulness meditation.

## dn22:16.11

- **EN**: They meditate observing, with respect to the principles, the liability to originate, to vanish, and to originate and vanish.

## dn22:16.12

- **EN**: Or mindfulness is established that principles exist, to the extent necessary for knowledge and mindfulness. They meditate independent, not grasping at anything in the world.
