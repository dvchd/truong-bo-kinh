## dn22:15.2
- **PLI**: Kathañca pana, bhikkhave, bhikkhu dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati chasu ajjhattikabāhiresu āyatanesu?
- **EN**: And how does a mendicant meditate observing an aspect of principles with respect to the six interior and exterior sense fields?

## dn22:15.3  `bj8.472, dr10.334, ms7D_1017`
- **PLI**: Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu cakkhuñca pajānāti, rūpe ca pajānāti, yañca tadubhayaṁ paṭicca uppajjati saṁyojanaṁ tañca pajānāti, yathā ca anuppannassa saṁyojanassa uppādo hoti tañca pajānāti, yathā ca uppannassa saṁyojanassa pahānaṁ hoti tañca pajānāti, yathā ca pahīnassa saṁyojanassa āyatiṁ anuppādo hoti tañca pajānāti.
- **EN**: It’s when a mendicant understands the eye, sights, and the fetter that arises dependent on both of these. They understand how the fetter that has not arisen comes to arise; how the arisen fetter comes to be abandoned; and how the abandoned fetter comes to not rise again in the future.
- **NOTE**: At <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn35.232/en/sujato#3.2'>SN 35.232:3.2</a> the “fetter that arises dependent on both” is identified as “desire and lust” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>chandarāga</i>).

## dn22:15.10
- **PLI**: Samudayadhammānupassī vā dhammesu viharati, vayadhammānupassī vā dhammesu viharati, samudayavayadhammānupassī vā dhammesu viharati.
- **EN**: They meditate observing the principles as liable to originate, as liable to fall, and as liable to both originate and vanish.  (= dn22:13.10)

## dn22:15.11
- **PLI**: ‘Atthi dhammā’ti vā panassa sati paccupaṭṭhitā hoti yāvadeva ñāṇamattāya paṭissatimattāya, anissito ca viharati, na ca kiñci loke upādiyati.
- **EN**: Or mindfulness is established that principles exist, to the extent necessary for knowledge and mindfulness. They meditate independent, not grasping at anything in the world.  (= dn22:13.11)
