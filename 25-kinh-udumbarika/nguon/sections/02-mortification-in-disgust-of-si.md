## dn25:7.16
- **PLI**: Iṅgha tvaṁ maṁ, nigrodha, sake ācariyake adhijegucche pañhaṁ puccha:
- **EN**: Please ask me a question about the higher disgust of sin in your own denomination:
- **NOTE**: “Higher disgust of sin” renders <i lang='pi' translate='no'>adhijegucche</i>. The idea is that people are tainted with corruption, somewhat comparable to the notion of “original sin”, although due to their own actions in the past rather than ancestral legacy. In “disgust” at that sin, they practice forms of fervent mortification (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>tapas</i>) to burn out the corruption.

## dn25:7.17

- **EN**: ‘How are the conditions for the fervent mortification in disgust of sin completed, and how are they incomplete?’”
- **NOTE**: The phrase <i lang='pi' translate='no'>kathaṁ santā</i> is unique; it echoes <i lang='pi' translate='no'>evaṁ sante</i> below.
