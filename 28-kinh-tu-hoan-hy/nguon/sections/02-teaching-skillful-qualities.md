# Muc 3 — Teaching Skillful Qualities  (7 đoạn)

## dn28:3.0
- **PLI**: 1.1. Kusaladhammadesanā
- **EN**: 1.1. Teaching Skillful Qualities

## dn28:3.1  `dr11.113, ms8D_296, msdiv145, pts-cs3, sya11.111`
- **PLI**: Aparaṁ pana, bhante, etadānuttariyaṁ, yathā bhagavā dhammaṁ deseti kusalesu dhammesu.
- **EN**: And moreover, sir, how the Buddha teaches skillful qualities is unsurpassable.

## dn28:3.2
- **PLI**: Tatrime kusalā dhammā seyyathidaṁ—
- **EN**: This consists of such skillful qualities as

## dn28:3.3
- **PLI**: cattāro satipaṭṭhānā, cattāro sammappadhānā, cattāro iddhipādā, pañcindriyāni, pañca balāni, satta bojjhaṅgā, ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo.
- **EN**: the four kinds of mindfulness meditation, the four right efforts, the four bases of psychic power, the five faculties, the five powers, the seven awakening factors, and the noble eightfold path.
- **NOTE**: Also at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#3.50.5'>DN 16:3.50.5</a>, etc.

## dn28:3.4
- **PLI**: Idha, bhante, bhikkhu āsavānaṁ khayā anāsavaṁ cetovimuttiṁ paññāvimuttiṁ diṭṭheva dhamme sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja viharati.
- **EN**: By these a mendicant realizes the undefiled freedom of heart and freedom by wisdom. And they live having realized it with their own insight in this very life due to the ending of defilements.

## dn28:3.5
- **PLI**: Etadānuttariyaṁ, bhante, kusalesu dhammesu.
- **EN**: This is unsurpassable when it comes to skillful qualities.

## dn28:3.6
- **PLI**: Taṁ bhagavā asesamabhijānāti, taṁ bhagavato asesamabhijānato uttari abhiññeyyaṁ natthi, yadabhijānaṁ añño samaṇo vā brāhmaṇo vā bhagavatā bhiyyobhiññataro assa, yadidaṁ kusalesu dhammesu.
- **EN**: The Buddha understands this without exception. There is nothing to be understood beyond this whereby another ascetic or brahmin might be superior in direct knowledge to the Buddha when it comes to skillful qualities.
