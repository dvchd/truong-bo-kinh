# Muc 6 — Ways of Revealing  (20 đoạn)

## dn28:6.0
- **PLI**: 1.4. Ādesanavidhādesanā
- **EN**: 1.4. Ways of Revealing

## dn28:6.1  `dr11.115, ms8D_302, msdiv148, pts-cs6`
- **PLI**: Aparaṁ pana, bhante, etadānuttariyaṁ, yathā bhagavā dhammaṁ deseti ādesanavidhāsu.
- **EN**: And moreover, sir, how the Buddha teaches the different ways of revealing is unsurpassable.

## dn28:6.2
- **PLI**: Catasso imā, bhante, ādesanavidhā.
- **EN**: There are these four ways of revealing.
- **NOTE**: Called “demonstrations of revealing” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>ādesanāpāṭihāriya</i>) at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an3.60/en/sujato#9.1'>AN 3.60:9.1</a>.

## dn28:6.3
- **PLI**: Idha, bhante, ekacco nimittena ādisati:
- **EN**: Firstly, someone reveals by means of a sign,

## dn28:6.4
- **PLI**: ‘evampi te mano, itthampi te mano, itipi te cittan’ti.
- **EN**: ‘This is what you’re thinking, such is your thought, and thus is your state of mind.’

## dn28:6.5
- **PLI**: So bahuñcepi ādisati, tatheva taṁ hoti, no aññathā.
- **EN**: And even if they reveal this many times, it turns out exactly so, not otherwise.

## dn28:6.6
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ paṭhamā ādesanavidhā.
- **EN**: This is the first way of revealing.
