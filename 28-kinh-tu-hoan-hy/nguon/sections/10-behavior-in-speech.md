# Muc 11 — Behavior in Speech  (8 đoạn)

## dn28:11.0
- **PLI**: 1.9. Bhassasamācārādidesanā
- **EN**: 1.9. Behavior in Speech

## dn28:11.1  `dr11.119, ms8D_313, msdiv153, ndp8.83, pts-cs11`
- **PLI**: Aparaṁ pana, bhante, etadānuttariyaṁ, yathā bhagavā dhammaṁ deseti bhassasamācāre.
- **EN**: And moreover, sir, how the Buddha teaches behavior in speech is unsurpassable.

## dn28:11.2
- **PLI**: Idha, bhante, ekacco na ceva musāvādupasañhitaṁ vācaṁ bhāsati na ca vebhūtiyaṁ na ca pesuṇiyaṁ na ca sārambhajaṁ jayāpekkho;
- **EN**: It’s when someone doesn’t use speech that’s connected with lying, or divisive, or backbiting, or aggressively trying to win.
- **NOTE**: These descriptions are unique. | <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Vebhūtiya</i> is also at (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn30/en/sujato#2.21.2'>DN 30:2.21.2</a>, where the context shows it is a synonym for <i lang='pi' translate='no'>pesuṇiya</i>. See too <a href='https://suttacentral.net/snp1.9/en/sujato#6.4'>Snp 1.9:6.4</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/snp3.10/en/sujato#16.1'>Snp 3.10:16.1</a>.

## dn28:11.3
- **PLI**: mantā mantā ca vācaṁ bhāsati nidhānavatiṁ kālena.
- **EN**: They speak only wise counsel, valuable and timely.

## dn28:11.4
- **PLI**: Etadānuttariyaṁ, bhante, bhassasamācāre.
- **EN**: This is unsurpassable when it comes to behavior in speech.

## dn28:12.1  `cck11.119, ms8D_314, pts-cs12, pts-vp-pli3.107`
- **PLI**: Aparaṁ pana, bhante, etadānuttariyaṁ, yathā bhagavā dhammaṁ deseti purisasīlasamācāre.
- **EN**: And moreover, sir, how the Buddha teaches a person’s ethical behavior is unsurpassable.
- **NOTE**: The Mahāsaṅgīti omits a heading for this section.

## dn28:12.2
- **PLI**: Idha, bhante, ekacco sacco cassa saddho ca, na ca kuhako, na ca lapako, na ca nemittiko, na ca nippesiko, na ca lābhena lābhaṁ nijigīsanako, indriyesu guttadvāro, bhojane mattaññū, samakārī, jāgariyānuyogamanuyutto, atandito, āraddhavīriyo, jhāyī, satimā, kalyāṇapaṭibhāno, gatimā, dhitimā, matimā, na ca kāmesu giddho, sato ca nipako ca.
- **EN**: It’s when someone is honest and faithful. They don’t use deceit, flattery, hinting, or belittling, and they don’t use material things to chase after other material things. They guard the sense doors and eat in moderation. They’re fair, dedicated to wakefulness, tireless, energetic, and meditative. They have good memory, eloquence, rememberance, retention, and thoughtfulness. They’re not greedy for sensual pleasures. They are mindful and alert.
- **NOTE**: The sequence from “memory” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>satimā</i>) to “retention” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>dhitimā</i>) is related to the good qualities of Ānanda (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/an1.220/en/sujato#1.1'>AN 1.220:1.1</a>), and pertain to textual fluency. <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Satimā</i> here is “memory”, not “mindfulness”, which is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>sato</i> at the end.

## dn28:12.3
- **PLI**: Etadānuttariyaṁ, bhante, purisasīlasamācāre.
- **EN**: This is unsurpassable when it comes to a person’s ethical behavior.
