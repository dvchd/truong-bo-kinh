# Muc 9 — Kinds of Striving  (4 đoạn)

## dn28:9.0
- **PLI**: 1.7. Padhānadesanā
- **EN**: 1.7. Kinds of Striving

## dn28:9.1  `csp1ed8.88, csp2ed8.88, dr11.118, ms8D_311, msdiv151, pts-cs9, pts-vp-pli3.106`
- **PLI**: Aparaṁ pana, bhante, etadānuttariyaṁ, yathā bhagavā dhammaṁ deseti padhānesu.
- **EN**: And moreover, sir, how the Buddha teaches the kinds of striving is unsurpassable.

## dn28:9.2
- **PLI**: Sattime, bhante, sambojjhaṅgā satisambojjhaṅgo dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo vīriyasambojjhaṅgo pītisambojjhaṅgo passaddhisambojjhaṅgo samādhisambojjhaṅgo upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo.
- **EN**: There are these seven awakening factors: the awakening factors of mindfulness, investigation of principles, energy, rapture, tranquility, immersion, and equanimity.
- **NOTE**: Normally we would expect the four kinds of right striving here. This is the only place the seven awakening factors are described in this way.

## dn28:9.3
- **PLI**: Etadānuttariyaṁ, bhante, padhānesu.
- **EN**: This is unsurpassable when it comes to the kinds of striving.
