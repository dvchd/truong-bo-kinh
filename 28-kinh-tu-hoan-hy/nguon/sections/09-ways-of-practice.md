# Muc 10 — Ways of Practice  (11 đoạn)

## dn28:10.0
- **PLI**: 1.8. Paṭipadādesanā
- **EN**: 1.8. Ways of Practice

## dn28:10.1  `bj9.178, cck11.118, ms8D_312, msdiv152, pts-cs10, sya11.116, vri3.79`
- **PLI**: Aparaṁ pana, bhante, etadānuttariyaṁ, yathā bhagavā dhammaṁ deseti paṭipadāsu.
- **EN**: And moreover, sir, how the Buddha teaches the ways of practice is unsurpassable.
- **NOTE**: As at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an4.166/en/sujato'>AN 4.166</a>.

## dn28:10.2
- **PLI**: Catasso imā, bhante, paṭipadā dukkhā paṭipadā dandhābhiññā,
- **EN**: Painful practice with slow insight,

## dn28:10.3
- **PLI**: dukkhā paṭipadā khippābhiññā,
- **EN**: painful practice with swift insight,

## dn28:10.4
- **PLI**: sukhā paṭipadā dandhābhiññā,
- **EN**: pleasant practice with slow insight, and

## dn28:10.5
- **PLI**: sukhā paṭipadā khippābhiññāti.
- **EN**: pleasant practice with swift insight.

## dn28:10.6
- **PLI**: Tatra, bhante, yāyaṁ paṭipadā dukkhā dandhābhiññā, ayaṁ, bhante, paṭipadā ubhayeneva hīnā akkhāyati dukkhattā ca dandhattā ca.
- **EN**: Of these, the painful practice with slow insight is said to be inferior both ways: because it’s painful and because it’s slow.

## dn28:10.7
- **PLI**: Tatra, bhante, yāyaṁ paṭipadā dukkhā khippābhiññā, ayaṁ pana, bhante, paṭipadā dukkhattā hīnā akkhāyati.
- **EN**: The painful practice with swift insight is said to be inferior because it’s painful.

## dn28:10.8
- **PLI**: Tatra, bhante, yāyaṁ paṭipadā sukhā dandhābhiññā, ayaṁ pana, bhante, paṭipadā dandhattā hīnā akkhāyati.
- **EN**: The pleasant practice with slow insight is said to be inferior because it’s slow.

## dn28:10.9
- **PLI**: Tatra, bhante, yāyaṁ paṭipadā sukhā khippābhiññā, ayaṁ pana, bhante, paṭipadā ubhayeneva paṇītā akkhāyati sukhattā ca khippattā ca.
- **EN**: But the pleasant practice with swift insight is said to be superior both ways: because it’s pleasant and because it’s swift.

## dn28:10.10
- **PLI**: Etadānuttariyaṁ, bhante, paṭipadāsu.
- **EN**: This is unsurpassable when it comes to the ways of practice.
