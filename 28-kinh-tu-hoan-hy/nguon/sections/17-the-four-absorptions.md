# Muc 19 — The Four Absorptions  (4 đoạn)

## dn28:19.0
- **PLI**: 1.16. Aññathāsatthuguṇadassana
- **EN**: 1.16. The Four Absorptions

## dn28:19.1  `bj9.190, ms8D_324, msdiv160, pts-cs19, sya11.124`
- **PLI**: Yaṁ taṁ, bhante, saddhena kulaputtena pattabbaṁ āraddhavīriyena thāmavatā purisathāmena purisavīriyena purisaparakkamena purisadhorayhena, anuppattaṁ taṁ bhagavatā.
- **EN**: The Buddha has achieved what should be achieved by a faithful gentleman by being energetic and strong, by human strength, energy, vigor, and exertion.

## dn28:19.2
- **PLI**: Na ca, bhante, bhagavā kāmesu kāmasukhallikānuyogamanuyutto hīnaṁ gammaṁ pothujjanikaṁ anariyaṁ anatthasaṁhitaṁ, na ca attakilamathānuyogamanuyutto dukkhaṁ anariyaṁ anatthasaṁhitaṁ.
- **EN**: The Buddha doesn’t indulge in sensual pleasures, which are low, crude, ordinary, ignoble, and pointless. And he doesn’t indulge in self-mortification, which is painful, ignoble, and pointless.
- **NOTE**: From the first sermon at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn56.11/en/sujato#2.3'>SN 56.11:2.3</a>.

## dn28:19.3
- **PLI**: Catunnañca bhagavā jhānānaṁ ābhicetasikānaṁ diṭṭhadhammasukhavihārānaṁ nikāmalābhī akicchalābhī akasiralābhī.
- **EN**: He gets the four absorptions—blissful meditations in this life that belong to the higher mind—when he wants, without trouble or difficulty.
- **NOTE**: Here the jhānas stand in the place of the path as a whole. In <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn29/en/sujato#24.5'>DN 29:24.5</a> the Buddha uses a similar framing.
