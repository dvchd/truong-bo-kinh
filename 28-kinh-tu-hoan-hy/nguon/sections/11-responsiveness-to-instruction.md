# Muc 13 — Responsiveness to Instruction  (12 đoạn)

## dn28:13.0
- **PLI**: 1.10. Anusāsanavidhādesanā
- **EN**: 1.10. Responsiveness to Instruction

## dn28:13.1  `bj9.180, csp1ed8.89, csp2ed8.89, dr11.120, ms8D_315, msdiv154, ndp8.84, pts-cs13, sya11.117, vri3.80`
- **PLI**: Aparaṁ pana, bhante, etadānuttariyaṁ, yathā bhagavā dhammaṁ deseti anusāsanavidhāsu.
- **EN**: And moreover, sir, how the Buddha teaches the different degrees of responsiveness to instruction is unsurpassable.

## dn28:13.2
- **PLI**: Catasso imā, bhante, anusāsanavidhā—
- **EN**: There are these four degrees of responsiveness to instruction.
- **NOTE**: We don’t find this exact passage elsewhere, but it is similar to <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an10.46/en/sujato'>AN 10.46</a>. When the Buddha speaks of future consequences it is conditional. He is not a fortune-teller, but he understands the results of the practice.

## dn28:13.3
- **PLI**: jānāti, bhante, bhagavā aparaṁ puggalaṁ paccattaṁ yonisomanasikārā
- **EN**: The Buddha knows by investigating inside another individual:
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Paccattaṁ yonisomanasikārā</i> occurs at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn46.8/en/sujato#2.1'>SN 46.8:2.1</a> and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn50/en/sujato#3.1'>MN 50:3.1</a>, where it means “investigate inside oneself”. Here it is applied to another.

## dn28:13.4
- **PLI**: ‘ayaṁ puggalo yathānusiṭṭhaṁ tathā paṭipajjamāno tiṇṇaṁ saṁyojanānaṁ parikkhayā sotāpanno bhavissati avinipātadhammo niyato sambodhiparāyaṇo’ti.
- **EN**: ‘By practicing as instructed this individual will, with the ending of three fetters, become a stream-enterer, not liable to be reborn in the underworld, assured, destined for awakening.’

## dn28:13.5
- **PLI**: Jānāti, bhante, bhagavā paraṁ puggalaṁ paccattaṁ yonisomanasikārā:
- **EN**: The Buddha knows by investigating inside another individual:

## dn28:13.6
- **PLI**: ‘ayaṁ puggalo yathānusiṭṭhaṁ tathā paṭipajjamāno tiṇṇaṁ saṁyojanānaṁ parikkhayā rāgadosamohānaṁ tanuttā sakadāgāmī bhavissati, sakideva imaṁ lokaṁ āgantvā dukkhassantaṁ karissatī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘By practicing as instructed this individual will, with the ending of three fetters, and the weakening of greed, hate, and delusion, become a once-returner. They will come back to this world once only, then make an end of suffering.’

## dn28:13.7
- **PLI**: Jānāti, bhante, bhagavā paraṁ puggalaṁ paccattaṁ yonisomanasikārā:
- **EN**: The Buddha knows by investigating inside to another individual:

## dn28:13.8
- **PLI**: ‘ayaṁ puggalo yathānusiṭṭhaṁ tathā paṭipajjamāno pañcannaṁ orambhāgiyānaṁ saṁyojanānaṁ parikkhayā opapātiko bhavissati tattha parinibbāyī anāvattidhammo tasmā lokā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘By practicing as instructed this individual will, with the ending of the five lower fetters, be reborn spontaneously. They will be extinguished there, and are not liable to return from that world.’

## dn28:13.9
- **PLI**: Jānāti, bhante, bhagavā paraṁ puggalaṁ paccattaṁ yonisomanasikārā:
- **EN**: The Buddha knows by investigating inside another individual:

## dn28:13.10
- **PLI**: ‘ayaṁ puggalo yathānusiṭṭhaṁ tathā paṭipajjamāno āsavānaṁ khayā anāsavaṁ cetovimuttiṁ paññāvimuttiṁ diṭṭheva dhamme sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja viharissatī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘By practicing as instructed this individual will realize the undefiled freedom of heart and freedom by wisdom, and live having realized it with their own insight in this very life due to the ending of defilements.’

## dn28:13.11
- **PLI**: Etadānuttariyaṁ, bhante, anusāsanavidhāsu.
- **EN**: This is unsurpassable when it comes to the different degrees of responsiveness to instruction.
