# Muc 15 — Eternalism  (27 đoạn)

## dn28:15.0
- **PLI**: 1.12. Sassatavādadesanā
- **EN**: 1.12. Eternalism

## dn28:15.1  `cck11.121, dr11.122, ms8D_317, msdiv156, ndp8.85, pts-cs15, pts-vp-pli3.109, sya11.119, vri3.81`
- **PLI**: Aparaṁ pana, bhante, etadānuttariyaṁ, yathā bhagavā dhammaṁ deseti sassatavādesu.
- **EN**: And moreover, sir, how the Buddha teaches eternalist doctrines is unsurpassable.

## dn28:15.2
- **PLI**: Tayome, bhante, sassatavādā.
- **EN**: There are these three eternalist doctrines.
- **NOTE**: Compare the four kinds of eternalism at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn1/en/sujato#3.1.1'>DN 1:3.1.1</a>. The theories based on rationality are omitted.

## dn28:15.3
- **PLI**: Idha, bhante, ekacco samaṇo vā brāhmaṇo vā ātappamanvāya …pe… tathārūpaṁ cetosamādhiṁ phusati, yathāsamāhite citte anekavihitaṁ pubbenivāsaṁ anussarati.
- **EN**: Firstly, some ascetic or brahmin—by dint of keen, resolute, committed, and diligent effort, and right application of mind—experiences an immersion of the heart of such a kind that they recollect many hundreds of thousands of past lives,

## dn28:15.4
- **PLI**: Seyyathidaṁ—ekampi jātiṁ dvepi jātiyo tissopi jātiyo catassopi jātiyo pañcapi jātiyo dasapi jātiyo vīsampi jātiyo tiṁsampi jātiyo cattālīsampi jātiyo paññāsampi jātiyo jātisatampi jātisahassampi jātisatasahassampi anekānipi jātisatāni anekānipi jātisahassāni anekānipi jātisatasahassāni, ‘amutrāsiṁ evaṁnāmo evaṅgotto evaṁvaṇṇo evamāhāro evaṁsukhadukkhappaṭisaṁvedī evamāyupariyanto, so tato cuto amutra udapādiṁ; tatrāpāsiṁ evaṁnāmo evaṅgotto evaṁvaṇṇo evamāhāro evaṁsukhadukkhappaṭisaṁvedī evamāyupariyanto, so tato cuto idhūpapanno’ti. Iti sākāraṁ sauddesaṁ anekavihitaṁ pubbenivāsaṁ anussarati.
- **EN**: with features and details.

## dn28:15.5
- **PLI**: So evamāha:
- **EN**: They say,

## dn28:15.6
- **PLI**: ‘atītampāhaṁ addhānaṁ jānāmi—saṁvaṭṭi vā loko vivaṭṭi vāti.
- **EN**: ‘I know that in the past the cosmos expanded or contracted.

## dn28:15.7
- **PLI**: Anāgataṁpāhaṁ addhānaṁ jānāmi—saṁvaṭṭissati vā loko vivaṭṭissati vāti.
- **EN**: I don’t know whether in the future the cosmos will expand or contract.
- **NOTE**: According to the PTS edition, the Sinhala-script manuscripts read <i lang='pi' translate='no'>na jānāmi</i> (“don’t know”) against the Mahāsaṅgīti <i lang='pi' translate='no'>jānāmi</i>. | The means of knowledge given here is solely based on the recollection of past lives, which agrees with the Brahmajālasutta that these are views based on the past. It seems the sectarian here is admitting the limits of their experiential knowledge, but nonetheless inferring eternalism in both past and future.

## dn28:15.8
- **PLI**: Sassato attā ca loko ca vañjho kūṭaṭṭho esikaṭṭhāyiṭṭhito. Te ca sattā sandhāvanti saṁsaranti cavanti upapajjanti, atthi tveva sassatisaman’ti.
- **EN**: The self and the cosmos are eternal, barren, steady as a mountain peak, standing firm like a pillar. They remain the same for all eternity, while these sentient beings wander and transmigrate and pass away and rearise.’

## dn28:15.9
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ paṭhamo sassatavādo.
- **EN**: This is the first eternalist doctrine.

## dn28:15.10  `bj9.184, cck11.122, csp1ed8.91, csp2ed8.91, dr11.123, ms8D_318, pts-vp-pli3.110, sya11.120`
