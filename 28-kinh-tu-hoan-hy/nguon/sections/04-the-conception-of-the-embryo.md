# Muc 5 — The Conception of the Embryo  (8 đoạn)

## dn28:5.0
- **PLI**: 1.3. Gabbhāvakkantidesanā
- **EN**: 1.3. The Conception of the Embryo

## dn28:5.1  `csp1ed8.85, csp2ed8.85, dr11.114, ms8D_298, msdiv147, pts-cs5, sya11.112`
- **PLI**: Aparaṁ pana, bhante, etadānuttariyaṁ, yathā bhagavā dhammaṁ deseti gabbhāvakkantīsu.
- **EN**: And moreover, sir, how the Buddha teaches the conception of the embryo is unsurpassable.

## dn28:5.2
- **PLI**: Catasso imā, bhante, gabbhāvakkantiyo.
- **EN**: There are these four kinds of conception.

## dn28:5.3
- **PLI**: Idha, bhante, ekacco asampajāno mātukucchiṁ okkamati; asampajāno mātukucchismiṁ ṭhāti; asampajāno mātukucchimhā nikkhamati. Ayaṁ paṭhamā gabbhāvakkanti.
- **EN**: Firstly, someone is unaware when conceived in their mother’s womb, unaware as they remain there, and unaware as they emerge. This is the first kind of conception.
- **NOTE**: The Bodhisatta is said elsewhere to be conceived in awareness (eg. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#3.15.1'>DN 16:3.15.1</a>), but this systematic analysis is found only here and at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn33/en/sujato#1.11.176'>DN 33:1.11.176</a>. It seems like an abrupt shift from central Dhamma topics.

## dn28:5.4  `ms8D_299, ndp8.80, vri3.76`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, bhante, idhekacco sampajāno mātukucchiṁ okkamati; asampajāno mātukucchismiṁ ṭhāti; asampajāno mātukucchimhā nikkhamati. Ayaṁ dutiyā gabbhāvakkanti.
- **EN**: Furthermore, someone is aware when conceived in their mother’s womb, but unaware as they remain there, and unaware as they emerge. This is the second kind of conception.

## dn28:5.5  `ms8D_300`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, bhante, idhekacco sampajāno mātukucchiṁ okkamati; sampajāno mātukucchismiṁ ṭhāti; asampajāno mātukucchimhā nikkhamati. Ayaṁ tatiyā gabbhāvakkanti.
- **EN**: Furthermore, someone is aware when conceived in their mother’s womb, aware as they remain there, but unaware as they emerge. This is the third kind of conception.

## dn28:5.6  `ms8D_301`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, bhante, idhekacco sampajāno mātukucchiṁ okkamati; sampajāno mātukucchismiṁ ṭhāti; sampajāno mātukucchimhā nikkhamati. Ayaṁ catutthā gabbhāvakkanti.
- **EN**: Furthermore, someone is aware when conceived in their mother’s womb, aware as they remain there, and aware as they emerge. This is the fourth kind of conception.

## dn28:5.7
- **PLI**: Etadānuttariyaṁ, bhante, gabbhāvakkantīsu.
- **EN**: This is unsurpassable when it comes to the conception of the embryo.
