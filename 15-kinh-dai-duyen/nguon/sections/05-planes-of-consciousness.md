# Muc 33 — Planes of Consciousness  (28 đoạn)

## dn15:33.0
- **PLI**: 5. Sattaviññāṇaṭṭhiti
- **EN**: 5. Planes of Consciousness

## dn15:33.1  `bj8.104, cck10.88, csp1ed7.59, csp2ed7.59, dr10.82, mc10.62, ms7D_236, msdiv127, pts-cs33, pts-vp-pli2.69, sya10.82, vri2.54`
- **PLI**: Satta kho, ānanda, viññāṇaṭṭhitiyo, dve āyatanāni.
- **EN**: Ānanda, there are seven planes of consciousness and two dimensions.
- **NOTE**: The Buddha returns once more to the question of rebirth, describing various states of rebirth in terms of consciousness. The seven planes are also mentioned at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn33/en/sujato#2.3.28'>DN 33:2.3.28</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn34/en/sujato#1.8.11'>DN 34:1.8.11</a>, and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an7.44/en/sujato#1.1'>AN 7.44:1.1</a>.

## dn15:33.2
- **PLI**: Katamā satta?
- **EN**: What seven?

## dn15:33.3
- **PLI**: Santānanda, sattā nānattakāyā nānattasaññino, seyyathāpi manussā, ekacce ca devā, ekacce ca vinipātikā.
- **EN**: There are sentient beings that are diverse in body and diverse in perception, such as human beings, some gods, and some beings in the underworld.

## dn15:33.4
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ paṭhamā viññāṇaṭṭhiti.
- **EN**: This is the first plane of consciousness.
- **NOTE**: “Plane of consciousness” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>viññāṇaṭṭhiti</i>, which could also be rendered “station”.

## dn15:33.5
- **PLI**: Santānanda, sattā nānattakāyā ekattasaññino, seyyathāpi devā brahmakāyikā paṭhamābhinibbattā.
- **EN**: There are sentient beings that are diverse in body and unified in perception, such as the gods reborn in the Divinity’s host through the first absorption.

## dn15:33.6
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ dutiyā viññāṇaṭṭhiti.
- **EN**: This is the second plane of consciousness.

## dn15:33.7
- **PLI**: Santānanda, sattā ekattakāyā nānattasaññino, seyyathāpi devā ābhassarā.
- **EN**: There are sentient beings that are unified in body and diverse in perception, such as the gods of sublime luminosity.

## dn15:33.8
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ tatiyā viññāṇaṭṭhiti.
- **EN**: This is the third plane of consciousness.

## dn15:33.9
- **PLI**: Santānanda, sattā ekattakāyā ekattasaññino, seyyathāpi devā subhakiṇhā.
- **EN**: There are sentient beings that are unified in body and unified in perception, such as the gods of universal beauty.

## dn15:33.10
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ catutthī viññāṇaṭṭhiti.
- **EN**: This is the fourth plane of consciousness.

## dn15:33.11
- **PLI**: Santānanda, sattā sabbaso rūpasaññānaṁ samatikkamā paṭighasaññānaṁ atthaṅgamā nānattasaññānaṁ amanasikārā ‘ananto ākāso’ti ākāsānañcāyatanūpagā.
- **EN**: There are sentient beings that have gone totally beyond perceptions of form. With the disappearance of perceptions of impingement, not focusing on perceptions of diversity, aware that ‘space is infinite’, they have been reborn in the dimension of infinite space.

## dn15:33.12
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ pañcamī viññāṇaṭṭhiti.
- **EN**: This is the fifth plane of consciousness.

## dn15:33.13
- **PLI**: Santānanda, sattā sabbaso ākāsānañcāyatanaṁ samatikkamma ‘anantaṁ viññāṇan’ti viññāṇañcāyatanūpagā.
- **EN**: There are sentient beings that have gone totally beyond the dimension of infinite space. Aware that ‘consciousness is infinite’, they have been reborn in the dimension of infinite consciousness.

## dn15:33.14
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ chaṭṭhī viññāṇaṭṭhiti.
- **EN**: This is the sixth plane of consciousness.

## dn15:33.15
- **PLI**: Santānanda, sattā sabbaso viññāṇañcāyatanaṁ samatikkamma ‘natthi kiñcī’ti ākiñcaññāyatanūpagā.
- **EN**: There are sentient beings that have gone totally beyond the dimension of infinite consciousness. Aware that ‘there is nothing at all’, they have been reborn in the dimension of nothingness.

## dn15:33.16
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ sattamī viññāṇaṭṭhiti.
- **EN**: This is the seventh plane of consciousness.

## dn15:33.17
- **PLI**: Asaññasattāyatanaṁ nevasaññānāsaññāyatanameva dutiyaṁ.
- **EN**: Then there is the dimension of non-percipient beings, and secondly, the dimension of neither perception nor non-perception.
- **NOTE**: In the first of these dimensions there is no consciousness at all, and in the second there is no consciousness in the normal sense, which is why they cannot be called “planes of consciousness”.
