# Mo dau  (24 đoạn)

## dn11:1.1  `bj7.484, cck9.275, csp1ed6.205, csp2ed6.205, dr9.293, km15.201, mc9.215, ms6D_827, msdiv481, ndp6.183, pts-cs1, pts-vp-pli1.211, sya9.273, vri1.195`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—
- **EN**: So I have heard.

## dn11:1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā nāḷandāyaṁ viharati pāvārikambavane.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying near Nāḷandā in Pāvārika’s Mango Grove.
- **NOTE**: This was the scene for some controversial discussions with Jains (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn56/en/sujato'>MN 56</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn42.8/en/sujato'>SN 42.8</a>), and Sāriputta’s touching declaration of faith shortly before his passing (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn47.12/en/sujato'>SN 47.12</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#1.16.1'>DN 16:1.16.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn28/en/sujato'>DN 28</a>). It is probably the Pāvā (modern Pawapuri) at which Mahāvīra died according to the Jain tradition.

## dn11:1.3
- **PLI**: Atha kho kevaṭṭo gahapatiputto yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi. Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho kevaṭṭo gahapatiputto bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: Then the householder Kevaḍḍha went up to the Buddha, bowed, sat down to one side, and said to him,
- **NOTE**: Kevaḍḍha is mentioned only here. Manuscripts spell his name variously as Kevaddha or Kevaṭṭa (“fisherman”). The Chinese form <span lang='lzh'>堅固</span> means “sturdy” (from <i lang='pi' translate='no'>dṛḍha</i>) and thus supports Kevaḍḍha.

## dn11:1.4
- **PLI**: “ayaṁ, bhante, nāḷandā iddhā ceva phītā ca bahujanā ākiṇṇamanussā bhagavati abhippasannā.
- **EN**: “Sir, this Nāḷandā is successful and prosperous, populous and full of people.

## dn11:1.5
- **PLI**: Sādhu, bhante, bhagavā ekaṁ bhikkhuṁ samādisatu, yo uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriyaṁ karissati;
- **EN**: Please direct a mendicant to perform a superhuman demonstration of psychic power.
- **NOTE**: I have been asked to do the same thing for the same reason.

## dn11:1.6
- **PLI**: evāyaṁ nāḷandā bhiyyoso mattāya bhagavati abhippasīdissatī”ti.
- **EN**: Then Nāḷandā will become even more devoted to the Buddha!”

## dn11:1.7
- **PLI**: Evaṁ vutte, bhagavā kevaṭṭaṁ gahapatiputtaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: When he said this, the Buddha said,

## dn11:1.8
- **PLI**: “na kho ahaṁ, kevaṭṭa, bhikkhūnaṁ evaṁ dhammaṁ desemi:
- **EN**: “Kevaḍḍha, I do not teach Dhamma to the mendicants like this:

## dn11:1.9
- **PLI**: ‘etha tumhe, bhikkhave, gihīnaṁ odātavasanānaṁ uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriyaṁ karothā’”ti.
- **EN**: ‘Come now, mendicants, perform a superhuman demonstration of psychic power for the white-clothed laypeople.’”
- **NOTE**: In fact it is forbidden in <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd15/en/sujato#8.2.23'>Kd 15:8.2.23</a>.

## dn11:2.1  `km15.202, ms6D_828, msdiv482, pts-cs2, pts-vp-pli1.212`
- **PLI**: Dutiyampi kho kevaṭṭo gahapatiputto bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: For a second time, Kevaḍḍha made the same request,

## dn11:2.2
- **PLI**: “nāhaṁ, bhante, bhagavantaṁ dhaṁsemi;
- **EN**: “Sir, I am not teaching you the Dhamma,
- **NOTE**: The reading <i lang='pi' translate='no'>dhaṁsemi</i> is dubious. An old Burmese manuscript has the reading <i lang='pi' translate='no'>dhammaṁ desemi</i>, which echoes the Buddha just above. Note too that Kevaḍḍha urges the Buddha to “direct” the monks (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>samādisatu</i>, from the same root as <i lang='pi' translate='no'>desemi</i>). I think the tension is deliberate: Kevaḍḍha says he isn’t telling the Buddha how to teach, but he absolutely is. Other readings convey the sense “attack, insult”, but this seems out of place.

## dn11:2.3
- **PLI**: api ca evaṁ vadāmi:
- **EN**: but nonetheless I say:

## dn11:2.4
- **PLI**: ‘ayaṁ, bhante, nāḷandā iddhā ceva phītā ca bahujanā ākiṇṇamanussā bhagavati abhippasannā.
- **EN**: ‘Sir, this Nāḷandā is successful and prosperous, populous and full of people.

## dn11:2.5
- **PLI**: Sādhu, bhante, bhagavā ekaṁ bhikkhuṁ samādisatu, yo uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriyaṁ karissati;
- **EN**: Please direct a mendicant to perform a superhuman demonstration of psychic power.

## dn11:2.6
- **PLI**: evāyaṁ nāḷandā bhiyyoso mattāya bhagavati abhippasīdissatī’”ti.
- **EN**: Then Nāḷandā will become even more devoted to the Buddha!’”

## dn11:2.7
- **PLI**: Dutiyampi kho bhagavā kevaṭṭaṁ gahapatiputtaṁ etadavoca:

## dn11:2.8

- **EN**: “Kevaḍḍha, I do not teach Dhamma to the mendicants like this:  (= dn11:1.8)

- **EN**: ‘Come now, mendicants, perform a superhuman demonstration of psychic power for the white-clothed laypeople.’”  (= dn11:1.9)
