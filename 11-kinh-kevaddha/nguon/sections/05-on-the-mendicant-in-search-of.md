## dn11:67.4
- **PLI**: ‘kattha nu kho ime cattāro mahābhūtā aparisesā nirujjhanti, seyyathidaṁ—pathavīdhātu āpodhātu tejodhātu vāyodhātū’ti?
- **EN**: ‘Where do these four principal states cease without remainder, namely, the elements of earth, water, fire, and air?’
- **NOTE**: The question is about meditation, not the annihilation of the material world. The first four <i lang='pi' translate='no'>jhānas</i> are based on the “subtle form” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>sukhumarūpa</i>) that manifests as light in deep meditation. He is asking how to go beyond this to the formless attainments.
