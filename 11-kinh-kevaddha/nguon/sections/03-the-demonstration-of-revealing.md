# Muc 6 — The Demonstration of Revealing  (18 đoạn)

## dn11:6.0
- **PLI**: 2. Ādesanāpāṭihāriya
- **EN**: 2. The Demonstration of Revealing

## dn11:6.1  `bj7.488, dr9.296, ms6D_836, msdiv485, ndp6.185, pts-cs6`
- **PLI**: Katamañca, kevaṭṭa, ādesanāpāṭihāriyaṁ?
- **EN**: And what is the demonstration of revealing?
- **NOTE**: “Revealing” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>ādesana</i>, from root <i lang='pi' translate='no'>dis</i> “to indicate, show, or point”.

## dn11:6.2
- **PLI**: Idha, kevaṭṭa, bhikkhu parasattānaṁ parapuggalānaṁ cittampi ādisati, cetasikampi ādisati, vitakkitampi ādisati, vicāritampi ādisati:
- **EN**: It’s when a mendicant reveals the mind, mentality, thoughts, and reflections of other beings and individuals:
- **NOTE**: The Pali terms here are <i lang='pi' translate='no'>citta</i>, <i lang='pi' translate='no'>cetasika</i>, <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vitakka</i>, and <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vicāra</i>.

## dn11:6.3
- **PLI**: ‘evampi te mano, itthampi te mano, itipi te cittan’ti.
- **EN**: ‘This is what you’re thinking, such is your thought, and thus is your state of mind.’
- **NOTE**: Here we have <i lang='pi' translate='no'>mano</i> (twice) and <i lang='pi' translate='no'>citta</i>.

## dn11:6.4  `cck9.278, km15.206, ms6D_837, pts-vp-pli1.214`
- **PLI**: Tamenaṁ aññataro saddho pasanno passati taṁ bhikkhuṁ parasattānaṁ parapuggalānaṁ cittampi ādisantaṁ, cetasikampi ādisantaṁ, vitakkitampi ādisantaṁ, vicāritampi ādisantaṁ:
- **EN**: Someone with faith and confidence sees that mendicant revealing another individual’s thoughts.

## dn11:6.5
- **PLI**: ‘evampi te mano, itthampi te mano, itipi te cittan’ti.
- **EN**: ‘This is what you’re thinking, such is your thought, and thus is your state of mind.’  (= dn11:6.3)

## dn11:6.6
- **PLI**: Tamenaṁ so saddho pasanno aññatarassa assaddhassa appasannassa āroceti:
- **EN**: They tell someone else who lacks faith and confidence:

## dn11:6.7
- **PLI**: ‘acchariyaṁ vata bho, abbhutaṁ vata bho, samaṇassa mahiddhikatā mahānubhāvatā.
- **EN**: ‘Oh lord, how incredible, how amazing! The ascetic has such psychic power and might!

## dn11:6.8
- **PLI**: Amāhaṁ bhikkhuṁ addasaṁ parasattānaṁ parapuggalānaṁ cittampi ādisantaṁ, cetasikampi ādisantaṁ, vitakkitampi ādisantaṁ, vicāritampi ādisantaṁ:
- **EN**: I saw him myself, revealing the thoughts of another individual!’

## dn11:6.9
- **PLI**: “evampi te mano, itthampi te mano, itipi te cittan”’ti.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn11:7.1  `ms6D_838, pts-cs7, sya9.276`
- **PLI**: Tamenaṁ so assaddho appasanno taṁ saddhaṁ pasannaṁ evaṁ vadeyya:
- **EN**: But the one lacking faith and confidence would say to them:

## dn11:7.2
- **PLI**: ‘atthi kho, bho, maṇikā nāma vijjā;
- **EN**: ‘There’s a spell named Māṇikā.
- **NOTE**: From <i lang='pi' translate='no'>maṇi</i>, “gem”. Magical gems are a common feature of Indian storytelling. Buddhist stories often feature the “wish-granting gem” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>cintāmaṇi</i>), which according to the commentary is meant here.

## dn11:7.3
- **PLI**: tāya so bhikkhu parasattānaṁ parapuggalānaṁ cittampi ādisati, cetasikampi ādisati, vitakkitampi ādisati, vicāritampi ādisati:
- **EN**: Using that a mendicant can reveal another individual’s thoughts.’

## dn11:7.4
- **PLI**: “evampi te mano, itthampi te mano, itipi te cittan”’ti.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## dn11:7.5  `km15.207, ms6D_839, vri1.198`
- **PLI**: Taṁ kiṁ maññasi, kevaṭṭa,
- **EN**: What do you think, Kevaḍḍha?

## dn11:7.6
- **PLI**: api nu so assaddho appasanno taṁ saddhaṁ pasannaṁ evaṁ vadeyyā”ti?
- **EN**: Wouldn’t someone lacking faith speak like that?”

## dn11:7.7
- **PLI**: “Vadeyya, bhante”ti.
- **EN**: “They would, sir.”

## dn11:7.8
- **PLI**: “Imaṁ kho ahaṁ, kevaṭṭa, ādesanāpāṭihāriye ādīnavaṁ sampassamāno ādesanāpāṭihāriyena aṭṭīyāmi harāyāmi jigucchāmi.
- **EN**: “Seeing this drawback in revealing, I’m horrified, repelled, and disgusted by demonstrations of revealing.
