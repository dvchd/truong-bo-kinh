## dn33:1.10.120  `cck11.224, ms8D_868`

## dn33:1.10.121

- **EN**: Immersion with placing the mind and keeping it connected. Immersion without placing the mind, merely keeping it connected. Immersion without placing the mind or keeping it connected.
- **NOTE**: This threefold presentation of the process of absorption focuses on <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vitakka</i> (“placing the mind”) and <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vicāra</i> (“keeping it connected”), looking closely at how they cease (see also <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn34/en/sujato#1.4.7'>DN 34:1.4.7</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn128/en/sujato#31.4'>MN 128:31.4</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn43.3/en/sujato#1.2'>SN 43.3:1.2</a>, and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an8.63/en/sujato#3.1'>AN 8.63:3.1</a>). The standard <i lang='pi' translate='no'>jhāna</i> formula focuses more on the refinement of feelings, from which perspective the first two stages of immersion here fall under the “rapture and bliss born of seclusion”, while only the third qualifies as “rapture and bliss born of immersion”. Thus Analayo describes the two descriptions as “complementary perspectives on the same process of deepening concentration” (<em>Comparative Study</em>, vol. ii, pg. 739, note 263).
