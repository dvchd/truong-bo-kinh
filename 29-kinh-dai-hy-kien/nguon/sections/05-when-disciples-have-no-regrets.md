# Muc 9 — When Disciples Have No Regrets  (11 đoạn)

## dn29:9.0
- **PLI**: 4. Sāvakānanutappasatthu
- **EN**: 4. When Disciples Have No Regrets

## dn29:9.1  `bj9.204, cck11.136, dr11.137, ms8D_344, msdiv171, ndp8.95, pts-cs9, pts-vp-pli3.123, sya11.134, vri3.91`
- **PLI**: Idha pana, cunda, satthā ca loke udapādi arahaṁ sammāsambuddho. Dhammo ca svākkhāto suppavedito niyyāniko upasamasaṁvattaniko sammāsambuddhappavedito.
- **EN**: Take the case where a teacher arises in the world who is perfected, a fully awakened Buddha. The teaching is well explained and well propounded, emancipating, leading to peace, proclaimed by someone who is fully awakened.

## dn29:9.2
- **PLI**: Viññāpitatthā cassa honti sāvakā saddhamme, kevalañca tesaṁ paripūraṁ brahmacariyaṁ āvikataṁ hoti uttānīkataṁ sabbasaṅgāhapadakataṁ sappāṭihīrakataṁ yāva devamanussehi suppakāsitaṁ.
- **EN**: The disciples have been educated in the meaning of that good teaching. And the spiritual practice that’s entirely full and pure has been disclosed and divulged to them; its sayings have all been collected; and it has been fully revealed with its demonstrable basis among gods and humans.

## dn29:9.3
- **PLI**: Atha nesaṁ satthuno antaradhānaṁ hoti.
- **EN**: And then their teacher passes away.

## dn29:9.4
- **PLI**: Evarūpo kho, cunda, satthā sāvakānaṁ kālaṅkato ananutappo hoti.
- **EN**: When such a teacher has passed away the disciples are free of regrets.

## dn29:9.5
- **PLI**: Taṁ kissa hetu?
- **EN**: Why is that?

## dn29:9.6
- **PLI**: ‘Satthā ca no loke udapādi arahaṁ sammāsambuddho.
- **EN**: They think: ‘Our teacher was perfected, a fully awakened Buddha.

## dn29:9.7
- **PLI**: Dhammo ca svākkhāto suppavedito niyyāniko upasamasaṁvattaniko sammāsambuddhappavedito.
- **EN**: His teaching was well explained,

## dn29:9.8
- **PLI**: Viññāpitatthā camha saddhamme, kevalañca no paripūraṁ brahmacariyaṁ āvikataṁ hoti uttānīkataṁ sabbasaṅgāhapadakataṁ sappāṭihīrakataṁ yāva devamanussehi suppakāsitaṁ.
- **EN**: we were educated in its meaning, and the spiritual practice was fully revealed to us.

## dn29:9.9
- **PLI**: Atha no satthuno antaradhānaṁ hotī’ti.
- **EN**: And then our teacher passed away.’

## dn29:9.10
- **PLI**: Evarūpo kho, cunda, satthā sāvakānaṁ kālaṅkato ananutappo hoti.
