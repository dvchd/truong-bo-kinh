# Muc 6 — The Teaching of the Awakened  (24 đoạn)

## dn29:6.0
- **PLI**: 2. Sammāsambuddhappaveditadhammavinaya
- **EN**: 2. The Teaching of the Awakened

## dn29:6.1  `bj9.200, cck11.134, csp1ed8.100, csp2ed8.100, dr11.135, ms8D_341, msdiv168, pts-cs6, sya11.132`
- **PLI**: Idha pana, cunda, satthā ca hoti sammāsambuddho, dhammo ca svākkhāto suppavedito niyyāniko upasamasaṁvattaniko sammāsambuddhappavedito,
- **EN**: Take the case where a teacher is awakened, and the teaching is well explained and well propounded, emancipating, leading to peace, proclaimed by someone who is a fully awakened Buddha.

## dn29:6.2
- **PLI**: sāvako ca tasmiṁ dhamme na dhammānudhammappaṭipanno viharati, na sāmīcippaṭipanno, na anudhammacārī, vokkamma ca tamhā dhammā vattati.
- **EN**: A disciple in that teaching does not practice in line with the teaching, does not practice following that procedure, does not live in line with the teaching. They proceed having turned away from that teaching.

## dn29:6.3
- **PLI**: So evamassa vacanīyo:
- **EN**: You should say this to them,

## dn29:6.4
- **PLI**: ‘tassa te, āvuso, alābhā, tassa te dulladdhaṁ, satthā ca te sammāsambuddho, dhammo ca svākkhāto suppavedito niyyāniko upasamasaṁvattaniko sammāsambuddhappavedito.
- **EN**: ‘It’s your loss, reverend, it’s your misfortune! For your teacher is awakened, and their teaching is well explained and well propounded, emancipating, leading to peace, proclaimed by someone who is a fully awakened Buddha.

## dn29:6.5
- **PLI**: Tvañca tasmiṁ dhamme na dhammānudhammappaṭipanno viharasi, na sāmīcippaṭipanno, na anudhammacārī, vokkamma ca tamhā dhammā vattasī’ti.
- **EN**: But you don’t practice in line with that teaching, you don’t practice following that procedure, you don’t live in line with the teaching. You proceed having turned away from that teaching.’

## dn29:6.6
- **PLI**: Iti kho, cunda, satthāpi tattha pāsaṁso, dhammopi tattha pāsaṁso, sāvako ca tattha evaṁ gārayho.
- **EN**: In such a case the teacher and the teaching deserve praise, but the disciple is to blame.

## dn29:6.7
- **PLI**: Yo kho, cunda, evarūpaṁ sāvakaṁ evaṁ vadeyya:
- **EN**: Suppose someone was to say to such a disciple,

## dn29:6.8
- **PLI**: ‘etāyasmā tathā paṭipajjatu yathā te satthārā dhammo desito paññatto’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Come on, venerable, practice as taught and pointed out by your teacher.’

## dn29:6.9
- **PLI**: Yo ca samādapeti, yañca samādapeti, yo ca samādapito tathattāya paṭipajjati. Sabbe te bahuṁ puññaṁ pasavanti.
- **EN**: The one who encourages, the one who they encourage, and the one who practices accordingly all brim with much merit.

## dn29:6.10
- **PLI**: Taṁ kissa hetu?
- **EN**: Why is that?

## dn29:6.11
- **PLI**: Evañhetaṁ, cunda, hoti svākkhāte dhammavinaye suppavedite niyyānike upasamasaṁvattanike sammāsambuddhappavedite.
- **EN**: It’s because that teaching and training is well explained and well propounded, emancipating, leading to peace, proclaimed by someone who is a fully awakened Buddha.
