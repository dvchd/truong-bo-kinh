# Muc 26 — Things Impossible for the Perfected  (10 đoạn)

## dn29:26.0
- **PLI**: 11. Khīṇāsavaabhabbaṭhāna
- **EN**: 11. Things Impossible for the Perfected

## dn29:26.1  `bj9.222, cck11.149, dr11.150, ms8D_377, msdiv186, pts-cs26, pts-vp-pli3.133, sya11.147`
- **PLI**: Ṭhānaṁ kho panetaṁ, cunda, vijjati yaṁ aññatitthiyā paribbājakā evaṁ vadeyyuṁ:
- **EN**: It’s possible that wanderers of other religions might say,

## dn29:26.2
- **PLI**: ‘aṭṭhitadhammā samaṇā sakyaputtiyā viharantī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘The ascetics who follow the Sakyan are fickle.’
- **NOTE**: At <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an8.19/en/sujato#3.1'>AN 8.19:3.1</a> the ocean is said to be <i lang='pi' translate='no'>ṭhitadhammo</i>, “naturally stable”. Both places are glossed as <i lang='pi' translate='no'>ṭhitasabhāvā</i>. It seems this is in reference to the fact that the Buddhist Vinaya is gentle and flexible. The Buddha was ready to alter and adjust details when circumstances required, yet the fundamental principles hold firm.

## dn29:26.3
- **PLI**: Evaṁvādino, cunda, aññatitthiyā paribbājakā evamassu vacanīyā:
- **EN**: You should say to them,

## dn29:26.4
- **PLI**: ‘atthi kho, āvuso, tena bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhena sāvakānaṁ dhammā desitā paññattā yāvajīvaṁ anatikkamanīyā.
- **EN**: ‘Reverends, these things have been taught and pointed out for his disciples by the Blessed One, who knows and sees, the perfected one, the fully awakened Buddha, not to be transgressed so long as life lasts.

## dn29:26.5
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, āvuso, indakhīlo vā ayokhīlo vā gambhīranemo sunikhāto acalo asampavedhī;
- **EN**: Suppose there was an Indra’s pillar or an iron pillar with deep foundations, firmly embedded, imperturbable and unshakable.

## dn29:26.6
- **PLI**: evameva kho, āvuso, tena bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhena sāvakānaṁ dhammā desitā paññattā yāvajīvaṁ anatikkamanīyā.
- **EN**: In the same way, these things have been taught and pointed out for his disciples by the Blessed One, who knows and sees, the perfected one, the fully awakened Buddha, not to be transgressed so long as life lasts.

## dn29:26.7
- **PLI**: Yo so, āvuso, bhikkhu arahaṁ khīṇāsavo vusitavā katakaraṇīyo ohitabhāro anuppattasadattho parikkhīṇabhavasaṁyojano sammadaññāvimutto, abhabbo so nava ṭhānāni ajjhācarituṁ.
- **EN**: A mendicant who is perfected—with defilements ended, who has completed the spiritual journey, done what had to be done, laid down the burden, achieved their heart’s goal, utterly ended the fetter of continued existence, and is rightly freed through enlightenment—can’t transgress in nine respects.
- **NOTE**: As at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an9.7/en/sujato#3.3'>AN 9.7:3.3</a>, etc.

## dn29:26.8
- **PLI**: Abhabbo, āvuso, khīṇāsavo bhikkhu sañcicca pāṇaṁ jīvitā voropetuṁ; abhabbo khīṇāsavo bhikkhu adinnaṁ theyyasaṅkhātaṁ ādiyituṁ; abhabbo khīṇāsavo bhikkhu methunaṁ dhammaṁ paṭisevituṁ; abhabbo khīṇāsavo bhikkhu sampajānamusā bhāsituṁ; abhabbo khīṇāsavo bhikkhu sannidhikārakaṁ kāme paribhuñjituṁ, seyyathāpi pubbe āgārikabhūto; abhabbo khīṇāsavo bhikkhu chandāgatiṁ gantuṁ; abhabbo khīṇāsavo bhikkhu dosāgatiṁ gantuṁ; abhabbo khīṇāsavo bhikkhu mohāgatiṁ gantuṁ; abhabbo khīṇāsavo bhikkhu bhayāgatiṁ gantuṁ.
- **EN**: A mendicant with defilements ended can’t deliberately take the life of a living creature, take something with the intention to steal, have sex, tell a deliberate lie, or store up goods for their own enjoyment like they used to as a layperson. And they can’t make decisions prejudiced by favoritism, hostility, stupidity, or cowardice.

## dn29:26.9
- **PLI**: Yo so, āvuso, bhikkhu arahaṁ khīṇāsavo vusitavā katakaraṇīyo ohitabhāro anuppattasadattho parikkhīṇabhavasaṁyojano sammadaññāvimutto, abhabbo so imāni nava ṭhānāni ajjhācaritun’ti.
- **EN**: A mendicant who is perfected can’t transgress in these nine respects.’
