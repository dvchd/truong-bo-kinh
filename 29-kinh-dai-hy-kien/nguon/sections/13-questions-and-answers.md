- **PLI**: Ṭhānaṁ kho panetaṁ, cunda, vijjati, yaṁ aññatitthiyā paribbājakā evaṁ vadeyyuṁ:
- **EN**: It’s possible that wanderers of other religions might say,

## dn29:27.2
- **PLI**: ‘atītaṁ kho addhānaṁ ārabbha samaṇo gotamo atīrakaṁ ñāṇadassanaṁ paññapeti, no ca kho anāgataṁ addhānaṁ ārabbha atīrakaṁ ñāṇadassanaṁ paññapeti, tayidaṁ kiṁsu tayidaṁ kathaṁsū’ti?
- **EN**: ‘The ascetic Gotama demonstrates boundless knowledge and vision of the past, but not of the future. What’s up with that?’

## dn29:27.3
- **PLI**: Te ca aññatitthiyā paribbājakā aññavihitakena ñāṇadassanena aññavihitakaṁ ñāṇadassanaṁ paññapetabbaṁ maññanti yathariva bālā abyattā.
- **EN**: Those wanderers, like incompetent fools, seem to imagine that one kind of knowledge and vision can be demonstrated by means of another kind of knowledge and vision.
- **NOTE**: It is possible to remember the past, but knowledge of the future is restricted to inferences in specific contexts and cannot be predicted in general.

## dn29:27.4
- **PLI**: Atītaṁ kho, cunda, addhānaṁ ārabbha tathāgatassa satānusāriñāṇaṁ hoti;
- **EN**: Regarding the past, the Realized One has knowledge stemming from memory.

## dn29:27.5
- **PLI**: so yāvatakaṁ ākaṅkhati tāvatakaṁ anussarati.
- **EN**: He recollects as far as he wants.

## dn29:27.6
- **PLI**: Anāgatañca kho addhānaṁ ārabbha tathāgatassa bodhijaṁ ñāṇaṁ uppajjati:
- **EN**: Regarding the future, the Realized One has the knowledge born of awakening:
- **NOTE**: The Buddha claims no gift of prophecy, unlike some other sages, but he knows that which matters.

## dn29:27.7
- **PLI**: ‘ayamantimā jāti, natthi dāni punabbhavo’ti.
- **EN**: ‘This is my last rebirth; now there’ll be no more future lives.’

## dn29:29.1  `pts-cs29`
- **PLI**: Yañca kho, cunda, sadevakassa lokassa samārakassa sabrahmakassa sassamaṇabrāhmaṇiyā pajāya sadevamanussāya diṭṭhaṁ sutaṁ mutaṁ viññātaṁ pattaṁ pariyesitaṁ anuvicaritaṁ manasā, sabbaṁ tathāgatena abhisambuddhaṁ,
- **EN**: In this world—with its gods, Māras, and divinities, this population with its ascetics and brahmins, its gods and humans—whatever is seen, heard, thought, known, attained, sought, and explored by the mind, all that has been understood by the Realized One.

- **EN**: That’s why he’s called the ‘Realized One’.

## dn29:29.3
- **PLI**: Yañca, cunda, rattiṁ tathāgato anuttaraṁ sammāsambodhiṁ abhisambujjhati, yañca rattiṁ anupādisesāya nibbānadhātuyā parinibbāyati, yaṁ etasmiṁ antare bhāsati lapati niddisati. Sabbaṁ taṁ tatheva hoti no aññathā,
- **EN**: From the night when the Realized One awakens to the supreme perfect awakening until the night he becomes fully extinguished—in the element of extinguishment with no residue—everything he speaks, says, and expresses is real, not otherwise.
