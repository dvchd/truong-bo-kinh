# Muc 4 — The Teaching of the Unawakened  (25 đoạn)

## dn29:4.0
- **PLI**: 1. Asammāsambuddhappaveditadhammavinaya
- **EN**: 1. The Teaching of the Unawakened

## dn29:4.1  `bj9.198, cck11.132, dr11.133, ms8D_339, msdiv166, pts-cs4, pts-vp-pli3.119, sya11.130`
- **PLI**: “Evaṁ hetaṁ, cunda, hoti durakkhāte dhammavinaye duppavedite aniyyānike anupasamasaṁvattanike asammāsambuddhappavedite.
- **EN**: “That’s what happens, Cunda, when a teaching and training is poorly explained and poorly propounded, not emancipating, not leading to peace, proclaimed by someone who is not a fully awakened Buddha.
- **NOTE**: This harsh critique is repeated at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn33/en/sujato#1.7.4'>DN 33:1.7.4</a>, but not at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn104/en/sujato#5.1'>MN 104:5.1</a>, where the Buddha’s response focuses on his own community.

## dn29:4.2
- **PLI**: Idha, cunda, satthā ca hoti asammāsambuddho, dhammo ca durakkhāto duppavedito aniyyāniko anupasamasaṁvattaniko asammāsambuddhappavedito,
- **EN**: Take the case where a teacher is not awakened, and the teaching is poorly explained and poorly propounded, not emancipating, not leading to peace, proclaimed by someone who is not a fully awakened Buddha.

## dn29:4.3
- **PLI**: sāvako ca tasmiṁ dhamme na dhammānudhammappaṭipanno viharati na sāmīcippaṭipanno na anudhammacārī, vokkamma ca tamhā dhammā vattati.
- **EN**: A disciple in that teaching does not practice in line with the teaching, does not practice following that procedure, does not live in line with the teaching. They proceed having turned away from that teaching.
- **NOTE**: Normally we find <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vokkamma</i> (“having turned away”) in the sense of straying from the Dhamma. But this passage shows the phrase is, in itself, neutral, as it is good to turn from a bad teaching.

## dn29:4.4
- **PLI**: So evamassa vacanīyo:
- **EN**: You should say this to them,

## dn29:4.5
- **PLI**: ‘tassa te, āvuso, lābhā, tassa te suladdhaṁ,
- **EN**: ‘You’re fortunate, reverend, you’re so very fortunate!

## dn29:4.6
- **PLI**: satthā ca te asammāsambuddho, dhammo ca durakkhāto duppavedito aniyyāniko anupasamasaṁvattaniko asammāsambuddhappavedito.
- **EN**: For your teacher is not awakened, and their teaching is poorly explained and poorly propounded, not emancipating, not leading to peace, proclaimed by someone who is not a fully awakened Buddha.

## dn29:4.7
- **PLI**: Tvañca tasmiṁ dhamme na dhammānudhammappaṭipanno viharasi, na sāmīcippaṭipanno, na anudhammacārī, vokkamma ca tamhā dhammā vattasī’ti.
- **EN**: But you don’t practice in line with that teaching, you don’t practice following that procedure, you don’t live in line with the teaching. You proceed having turned away from that teaching.’

## dn29:4.8
- **PLI**: Iti kho, cunda, satthāpi tattha gārayho, dhammopi tattha gārayho, sāvako ca tattha evaṁ pāsaṁso.
- **EN**: In such a case the teacher and the teaching are to blame, but the disciple deserves praise.
- **NOTE**: The Dhamma is primary, not the teacher. This is applied to Buddhism as well; for example, a student is expected to help a mentor who is falling away from Dhamma (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#25.20.1'>Kd 1:25.20.1</a>).

## dn29:4.9
- **PLI**: Yo kho, cunda, evarūpaṁ sāvakaṁ evaṁ vadeyya:
- **EN**: Suppose someone was to say to such a disciple,

## dn29:4.10
- **PLI**: ‘etāyasmā tathā paṭipajjatu, yathā te satthārā dhammo desito paññatto’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Come on, venerable, practice as taught and pointed out by your teacher.’

## dn29:4.11
- **PLI**: Yo ca samādapeti, yañca samādapeti, yo ca samādapito tathattāya paṭipajjati. Sabbe te bahuṁ apuññaṁ pasavanti.
- **EN**: The one who encourages, the one who they encourage, and the one who practices accordingly all brim with much wickedness.
- **NOTE**: Compare <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an1.320/en/sujato'>AN 1.320</a>.

## dn29:4.12
- **PLI**: Taṁ kissa hetu?
- **EN**: Why is that?

## dn29:4.13
- **PLI**: Evaṁ hetaṁ, cunda, hoti durakkhāte dhammavinaye duppavedite aniyyānike anupasamasaṁvattanike asammāsambuddhappavedite.
- **EN**: It’s because that teaching and training is poorly explained and poorly propounded, not emancipating, not leading to peace, proclaimed by someone who is not a fully awakened Buddha.

## dn29:5.1  `cck11.133, csp1ed8.99, csp2ed8.99, dr11.134, ms8D_340, msdiv167, ndp8.93, pts-cs5, pts-vp-pli3.120, sya11.131, vri3.89`

- **EN**: ‘Clearly the venerable is practicing systematically and will succeed in that system.’

## dn29:5.9
- **PLI**: Yo ca pasaṁsati, yañca pasaṁsati, yo ca pasaṁsito bhiyyoso mattāya vīriyaṁ ārabhati. Sabbe te bahuṁ apuññaṁ pasavanti.
- **EN**: The one who praises, the one who they praise, and the one who, being praised, rouses up even more energy all brim with much wickedness.
