## dn29:34.13
- **PLI**: sayaṅkato attā ca loko ca …
- **EN**: The self and the cosmos are made by oneself,
- **NOTE**: The idea “made by oneself” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>sayaṅkata</i>) assumes a metaphysical “self” which creates “the self and the cosmos”. This implies the Vedic creation doctrine, whereby Prajāpati created the cosmos and all things in it from himself by an act of self-sacrifice. Thus the sacrificer offers to Prajāpati as the sun with the words, “You are self-made” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>svāṁkṛta</i>, Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa 4.1.1.22, 4.1.2.21). For more details on this teaching, see <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn12.17/en/sujato'>SN 12.17</a> and notes there.
