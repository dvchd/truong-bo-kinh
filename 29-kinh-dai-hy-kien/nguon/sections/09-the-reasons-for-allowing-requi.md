# Muc 22 — The Reasons for Allowing Requisites  (13 đoạn)

## dn29:22.0
- **PLI**: 8. Paccayānuññātakāraṇa
- **EN**: 8. The Reasons for Allowing Requisites

## dn29:22.1  `bj9.216, cck11.145, ms8D_360, msdiv182, ndp8.101, pts-cs22, pts-vp-pli3.130, sya11.143`
- **PLI**: Na vo ahaṁ, cunda, diṭṭhadhammikānaṁyeva āsavānaṁ saṁvarāya dhammaṁ desemi.
- **EN**: Cunda, I do not teach you solely for restraining defilements that affect this life.

## dn29:22.2
- **PLI**: Na panāhaṁ, cunda, samparāyikānaṁyeva āsavānaṁ paṭighātāya dhammaṁ desemi.
- **EN**: Nor do I teach solely for protecting against defilements that affect lives to come.

## dn29:22.3
- **PLI**: Diṭṭhadhammikānaṁ cevāhaṁ, cunda, āsavānaṁ saṁvarāya dhammaṁ desemi;
- **EN**: I teach both for restraining defilements that affect this life and

## dn29:22.4
- **PLI**: samparāyikānañca āsavānaṁ paṭighātāya.
- **EN**: protecting against defilements that affect lives to come.

## dn29:22.5
- **PLI**: Tasmātiha, cunda, yaṁ vo mayā cīvaraṁ anuññātaṁ, alaṁ vo taṁ
- **EN**: And that’s why I have allowed robes for you that suffice
- **NOTE**: These are the four basic requisites for monastics, said to overcome defilements by usage (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/an6.58/en/sujato#4.1'>AN 6.58:4.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn2/en/sujato#13.1'>MN 2:13.1</a>).

## dn29:22.6
- **PLI**: yāvadeva sītassa paṭighātāya, uṇhassa paṭighātāya, ḍaṁsamakasavātātapasarīsapasamphassānaṁ paṭighātāya, yāvadeva hirikopīnapaṭicchādanatthaṁ.
- **EN**: only for the sake of warding off cold and heat; for warding off the touch of flies, mosquitoes, wind, sun, and reptiles; and for covering up the private parts.

## dn29:22.7
- **PLI**: Yo vo mayā piṇḍapāto anuññāto, alaṁ vo so
- **EN**: I have allowed almsfood for you that suffices

## dn29:22.8
- **PLI**: yāvadeva imassa kāyassa ṭhitiyā yāpanāya vihiṁsūparatiyā brahmacariyānuggahāya, iti purāṇañca vedanaṁ paṭihaṅkhāmi, navañca vedanaṁ na uppādessāmi, yātrā ca me bhavissati anavajjatā ca phāsuvihāro ca.
- **EN**: only to sustain this body, avoid harm, and support spiritual practice; so that you will put an end to old discomfort and not give rise to new discomfort, and will keep on living blamelessly and at ease.

## dn29:22.9
- **PLI**: Yaṁ vo mayā senāsanaṁ anuññātaṁ, alaṁ vo taṁ
- **EN**: I have allowed lodgings for you that suffice

## dn29:22.10
- **PLI**: yāvadeva sītassa paṭighātāya, uṇhassa paṭighātāya, ḍaṁsamakasavātātapasarīsapasamphassānaṁ paṭighātāya, yāvadeva utuparissayavinodanapaṭisallānārāmatthaṁ.
- **EN**: only for the sake of warding off cold and heat; for warding off the touch of flies, mosquitoes, wind, sun, and reptiles; to shelter from harsh weather and to enjoy retreat.

## dn29:22.11
- **PLI**: Yo vo mayā gilānapaccayabhesajjaparikkhāro anuññāto, alaṁ vo so
- **EN**: I have allowed medicines and supplies for the sick for you that suffice

## dn29:22.12
- **PLI**: yāvadeva uppannānaṁ veyyābādhikānaṁ vedanānaṁ paṭighātāya abyāpajjaparamatāya.
- **EN**: only for the sake of warding off the pains of illness and to promote good health.
