# Muc 8 — When Disciples Have Regrets  (9 đoạn)

## dn29:8.0
- **PLI**: 3. Sāvakānutappasatthu
- **EN**: 3. When Disciples Have Regrets

## dn29:8.1  `csp1ed8.101, csp2ed8.101, dr11.136, ms8D_343, msdiv170, pts-cs8, pts-vp-pli3.122`
- **PLI**: Idha pana, cunda, satthā ca loke udapādi arahaṁ sammāsambuddho, dhammo ca svākkhāto suppavedito niyyāniko upasamasaṁvattaniko sammāsambuddhappavedito,
- **EN**: Take the case where a teacher arises in the world who is perfected, a fully awakened Buddha. The teaching is well explained and well propounded, emancipating, leading to peace, proclaimed by someone who is fully awakened.

## dn29:8.2
- **PLI**: aviññāpitatthā cassa honti sāvakā saddhamme, na ca tesaṁ kevalaṁ paripūraṁ brahmacariyaṁ āvikataṁ hoti uttānīkataṁ sabbasaṅgāhapadakataṁ sappāṭihīrakataṁ yāva devamanussehi suppakāsitaṁ.
- **EN**: But the disciples have not been educated in the meaning of that good teaching. And the spiritual practice that’s entirely full and pure has not been disclosed and divulged to them; its sayings have not all been collected; and it has not been fully revealed with its demonstrable basis among gods and humans.
- **NOTE**: “Collected sayings” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>saṅgāhapada</i>, a unique reference to a formal collection of scripture. Here, the sense seems to be that the process of gathering and organizing teachings is incomplete. Therefore I take the negative <i lang='pi' translate='no'>na</i> to be distributed separately among the clauses.

## dn29:8.3
- **PLI**: Atha nesaṁ satthuno antaradhānaṁ hoti.
- **EN**: And then their teacher passes away.

## dn29:8.4
- **PLI**: Evarūpo kho, cunda, satthā sāvakānaṁ kālaṅkato anutappo hoti.
- **EN**: When such a teacher has passed away the disciples are tormented by regrets.

## dn29:8.5
- **PLI**: Taṁ kissa hetu?
- **EN**: Why is that?

## dn29:8.6
- **PLI**: ‘Satthā ca no loke udapādi arahaṁ sammāsambuddho, dhammo ca svākkhāto suppavedito niyyāniko upasamasaṁvattaniko sammāsambuddhappavedito, aviññāpitatthā camha saddhamme, na ca no kevalaṁ paripūraṁ brahmacariyaṁ āvikataṁ hoti uttānīkataṁ sabbasaṅgāhapadakataṁ sappāṭihīrakataṁ yāva devamanussehi suppakāsitaṁ.
- **EN**: They think: ‘Our teacher was perfected, a fully awakened Buddha. His teaching was well explained, but we were not educated in its meaning. And the spiritual practice was not fully disclosed and divulged to us; its sayings have not all been collected; and it has not been fully revealed with its demonstrable basis among gods and humans.

## dn29:8.7
- **PLI**: Atha no satthuno antaradhānaṁ hotī’ti.
- **EN**: And then our teacher passed away.’

## dn29:8.8
