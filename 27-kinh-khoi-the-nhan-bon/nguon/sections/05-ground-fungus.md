# Muc 14 — Ground-Fungus  (12 đoạn)

## dn27:14.0
- **PLI**: 4. Bhūmipappaṭakapātubhāva
- **EN**: 4. Ground-Fungus

## dn27:14.1  `bj9.148, dr11.95, ms8D_256, msdiv123, pts-cs14, pts-vp-pli3.87, sya11.95`
- **PLI**: Atha kho tesaṁ, vāseṭṭha, sattānaṁ rasāya pathaviyā antarahitāya bhūmipappaṭako pāturahosi.
- **EN**: When the earth’s nectar had vanished, ground-fungus appeared to those beings.
- **NOTE**: “Fungus” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>pappaṭaka</i> (Sanskrit <i lang='pi' translate='no'>parpaṭaka</i>). At <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-bu-vb-pj1/en/sujato#2.2.5'>Bu Pj 1:2.2.5</a>, Moggallāna suggests that, to alleviate famine, the monks might eat the <i lang='pi' translate='no'>pappaṭakojaṁ</i> (“fungus-nutrition”) under the earth. Given that it is compared to a mushroom, I think it is the fungal mycelia of a mycorrhizal network. Such fungi spread like tendrils underground, sometimes appearing above the surface, sharing nutrients and information between organisms.

## dn27:14.2
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi nāma ahicchattako; evameva pāturahosi.
- **EN**: It appeared just like a mushroom.

## dn27:14.3
- **PLI**: So ahosi vaṇṇasampanno gandhasampanno rasasampanno, seyyathāpi nāma sampannaṁ vā sappi sampannaṁ vā navanītaṁ evaṁvaṇṇo ahosi.
- **EN**: It was beautiful, fragrant, and delicious, like ghee or butter.

## dn27:14.4
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi nāma khuddamadhuṁ aneḷakaṁ; evamassādo ahosi.
- **EN**: And it was as sweet as pure dwarf-bee honey.

## dn27:14.5  `csp1ed8.72, csp2ed8.72, ms8D_257`
- **PLI**: Atha kho te, vāseṭṭha, sattā bhūmipappaṭakaṁ upakkamiṁsu paribhuñjituṁ.
- **EN**: Then those beings started to eat the ground-fungus.

## dn27:14.6
- **PLI**: Te taṁ paribhuñjantā tambhakkhā tadāhārā ciraṁ dīghamaddhānaṁ aṭṭhaṁsu.
- **EN**: With that as their food and nourishment, they remained for a very long time.

## dn27:14.7
- **PLI**: Yathā yathā kho te, vāseṭṭha, sattā bhūmipappaṭakaṁ paribhuñjantā tambhakkhā tadāhārā ciraṁ dīghamaddhānaṁ aṭṭhaṁsu tathā tathā tesaṁ sattānaṁ bhiyyoso mattāya kharattañceva kāyasmiṁ okkami, vaṇṇavevaṇṇatā ca paññāyittha.
- **EN**: But so long as they ate that ground-fungus, their bodies became more solid and they diverged in appearance;

## dn27:14.8
- **PLI**: Ekidaṁ sattā vaṇṇavanto honti, ekidaṁ sattā dubbaṇṇā.
- **EN**: some beautiful, some ugly.

## dn27:14.9
- **PLI**: Tattha ye te sattā vaṇṇavanto, te dubbaṇṇe satte atimaññanti:
- **EN**: And the beautiful beings looked down on the ugly ones:

## dn27:14.10
- **PLI**: ‘mayametehi vaṇṇavantatarā, amhehete dubbaṇṇatarā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘We’re more beautiful, they’re the ugly ones!’

## dn27:14.11
- **PLI**: Tesaṁ vaṇṇātimānapaccayā mānātimānajātikānaṁ bhūmipappaṭako antaradhāyi.
- **EN**: And the vanity of the beautiful ones made the ground-fungus vanish.
