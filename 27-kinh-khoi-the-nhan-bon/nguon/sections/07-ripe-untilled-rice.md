# Muc 16 — Ripe Untilled Rice  (6 đoạn)

## dn27:16.0
- **PLI**: 6. Akaṭṭhapākasālipātubhāva
- **EN**: 6. Ripe Untilled Rice

## dn27:16.1  `bj9.150, cck11.99, dr11.97, ms8D_261, msdiv125, pts-cs16`
- **PLI**: Atha kho tesaṁ, vāseṭṭha, sattānaṁ padālatāya antarahitāya akaṭṭhapāko sāli pāturahosi akaṇo athuso suddho sugandho taṇḍulapphalo.
- **EN**: When the bursting pods had vanished, ripe untilled rice appeared to those beings. It had no powder or husk, pure and fragrant, with only the rice-grain.

## dn27:16.2
- **PLI**: Yaṁ taṁ sāyaṁ sāyamāsāya āharanti, pāto taṁ hoti pakkaṁ paṭivirūḷhaṁ.
- **EN**: What they took for supper at dusk, by daybreak had grown back and ripened.

## dn27:16.3
- **PLI**: Yaṁ taṁ pāto pātarāsāya āharanti, sāyaṁ taṁ hoti pakkaṁ paṭivirūḷhaṁ;
- **EN**: And what they took for breakfast at daybreak had grown back and ripened by the evening,

## dn27:16.4
- **PLI**: nāpadānaṁ paññāyati.
- **EN**: leaving no trace showing.

## dn27:16.5
- **PLI**: Atha kho te, vāseṭṭha, sattā akaṭṭhapākaṁ sāliṁ paribhuñjantā tambhakkhā tadāhārā ciraṁ dīghamaddhānaṁ aṭṭhaṁsu.
- **EN**: Then those beings eating the ripe untilled rice, with that as their food and nourishment, remained for a very long time.
