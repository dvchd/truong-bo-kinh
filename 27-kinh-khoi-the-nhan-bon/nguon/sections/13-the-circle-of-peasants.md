# Muc 24 — The Circle of Peasants  (5 đoạn)

## dn27:24.0
- **PLI**: 12. Vessamaṇḍala
- **EN**: 12. The Circle of Peasants

## dn27:24.1  `csp1ed8.79, csp2ed8.79, ms8D_275, msdiv133, pts-cs24, sya11.104`
- **PLI**: Tesaṁyeva kho, vāseṭṭha, sattānaṁ ekacce sattā methunaṁ dhammaṁ samādāya visukammante payojesuṁ.
- **EN**: Some of those same beings, taking up an active sex life, applied themselves to various jobs.

## dn27:24.2
- **PLI**: Methunaṁ dhammaṁ samādāya visukammante payojentīti kho, vāseṭṭha, ‘vessā, vessā’ tveva akkharaṁ upanibbattaṁ.
- **EN**: ‘Having taken up an active sex life, they apply themselves to various jobs’ is the meaning of ‘peasant’, the term applied to them.
- **NOTE**: “Various” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>visu</i>, which loosely echoes <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vessa</i> (“peasant”). The Sanskrit form is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vaiśya</i>, from <i lang='pi' translate='no'>viś</i>, to “stay” or “settle” on the land. They were probably originally peasants who over time transitioned into more diverse livelihoods. In <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn96/en/sujato#10.10'>MN 96:10.10</a> they are said to engage in cattle-rearing and agriculture, and over time trade was added to that.

## dn27:24.3
- **PLI**: Iti kho, vāseṭṭha, evametassa vessamaṇḍalassa porāṇena aggaññena akkharena abhinibbatti ahosi tesaññeva sattānaṁ anaññesaṁ sadisānaṁyeva, no asadisānaṁ, dhammeneva no adhammena.
- **EN**: And that, Vāseṭṭha, is how the ancient primordial term for the circle of peasants was created; for those very beings, not others; for those like them, not unlike; by virtue of principle, not against principle.

## dn27:24.4
- **PLI**: Dhammo hi, vāseṭṭha, seṭṭho janetasmiṁ diṭṭhe ceva dhamme abhisamparāyañca.
- **EN**: For principle, Vāseṭṭha, is the best thing for people in both this life and the next.
