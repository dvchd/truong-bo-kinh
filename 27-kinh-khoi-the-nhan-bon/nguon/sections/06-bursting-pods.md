# Muc 14.12 — Bursting Pods  (16 đoạn)

## dn27:14.12.0
- **PLI**: 5. Padālatāpātubhāva
- **EN**: 5. Bursting Pods

## dn27:14.13  `cck11.98, dr11.96, ms8D_258, msdiv124`
- **PLI**: Bhūmipappaṭake antarahite padālatā pāturahosi, seyyathāpi nāma kalambukā; evameva pāturahosi.
- **EN**: When the ground-fungus had vanished, bursting pods appeared, like the fruit of the kadam tree.
- **NOTE**: Readings and meaning of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>padālatā</i> are uncertain. It has usually been understood that the second element is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>latā</i> (“creeper”). But this leaves <i lang='pi' translate='no'>padā</i> unexplained, and the commentary’s gloss of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>bhaddālatā</i> is unconvincing. It also leaves unexplained the connection with <i lang='pi' translate='no'>kalambukā</i>. This is probably the fruit of the kadam tree—fleshy, yellow-orange capsules with about 8000 seeds that split apart when ripe. I think <i lang='pi' translate='no'>padālatā</i> is related to <i lang='pi' translate='no'>padālana</i> in the sense of “bursting”: a seed pod that splits open like a fig or pomegranate.
