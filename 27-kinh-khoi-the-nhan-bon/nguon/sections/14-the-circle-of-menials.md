# Muc 25 — The Circle of Menials  (13 đoạn)

## dn27:25.0
- **PLI**: 13. Suddamaṇḍala
- **EN**: 13. The Circle of Menials

## dn27:25.1  `dr11.106, ms8D_276, msdiv134, pts-cs25`
- **PLI**: Tesaññeva kho, vāseṭṭha, sattānaṁ ye te sattā avasesā te luddācārā khuddācārā ahesuṁ.
- **EN**: The remaining beings lived by venal hunting and minor tasks.

## dn27:25.2
- **PLI**: Luddācārā khuddācārāti kho, vāseṭṭha, ‘suddā, suddā’ tveva akkharaṁ upanibbattaṁ.
- **EN**: ‘They live by venal hunting and minor tasks’ is the meaning of ‘menial’, the term applied to them.
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Sudda</i> (“menial”) rhymes with <i lang='pi' translate='no'>ludda</i> (“hunter”) and <i lang='pi' translate='no'>khudda</i> (“minor”). In <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn96/en/sujato#10.14'>MN 96:10.14</a> their wealth is said to come from the “scythe and flail”, i.e. seasonal manual labor such as threshing grain (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn35.248/en/sujato#1.2'>SN 35.248:1.2</a>). At <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn96/en/sujato#16.4'>MN 96:16.4</a>, bamboo-workers, chariot-makers, and waste-collectors are cited as other low-class jobs. They may have originated as a distinct branch of Aryan peoples who were assimilated into Vedic culture (Ram Sharan Sharma, <em>Śudras in Ancient India</em>, 37).

## dn27:25.3
- **PLI**: Iti kho, vāseṭṭha, evametassa suddamaṇḍalassa porāṇena aggaññena akkharena abhinibbatti ahosi tesaṁyeva sattānaṁ anaññesaṁ, sadisānaṁyeva no asadisānaṁ, dhammeneva, no adhammena.
- **EN**: And that, Vāseṭṭha, is how the ancient primordial term for the circle of menials was created; for those very beings, not others; for those like them, not unlike; by virtue of principle, not against principle.

## dn27:25.4
- **PLI**: Dhammo hi, vāseṭṭha, seṭṭho janetasmiṁ diṭṭhe ceva dhamme abhisamparāyañca.
- **EN**: For principle, Vāseṭṭha, is the best thing for people in both this life and the next.
