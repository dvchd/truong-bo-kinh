- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying near Kosambī in Ghosita’s monastery.

## dn7:1.3
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena dve pabbajitā—
- **EN**: Now at that time two renunciates—

## dn7:1.4
- **PLI**: muṇḍiyo ca paribbājako jāliyo ca dārupattikantevāsī yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkamiṁsu; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavatā saddhiṁ sammodiṁsu.
- **EN**: the wanderer Muṇḍiya and Jāliya, the pupil of the wood-bowl ascetic—came to the Buddha and exchanged greetings with him.

## dn7:1.5
- **PLI**: Sammodanīyaṁ kathaṁ sāraṇīyaṁ vītisāretvā ekamantaṁ aṭṭhaṁsu. Ekamantaṁ ṭhitā kho te dve pabbajitā bhagavantaṁ etadavocuṁ:
- **EN**: When the greetings and polite conversation were over, they stood to one side and said to the Buddha,

## dn7:1.6
- **PLI**: “kiṁ nu kho, āvuso gotama, taṁ jīvaṁ taṁ sarīraṁ, udāhu aññaṁ jīvaṁ aññaṁ sarīran”ti?
- **EN**: “Reverend Gotama, are the soul and the body one and the same, or is the soul one thing, the body another?”
