# Mo dau  (10 đoạn)

## dn34:1.1.1  `bj9.468, cck11.278, csp1ed8.227, csp2ed8.227, dr11.306, ms8D_1132, msdiv350, ndp8.210, pts-cs1.1, pts-vp-pli3.272, sya11.288, vri3.217`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—
- **EN**: So I have heard.

## dn34:1.1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā campāyaṁ viharati gaggarāya pokkharaṇiyā tīre mahatā bhikkhusaṅghena saddhiṁ pañcamattehi bhikkhusatehi.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying near Campā on the banks of the Gaggarā Lotus Pond together with a large Saṅgha of five hundred mendicants.
- **NOTE**: As in <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn4/en/sujato'>DN 4</a>.

## dn34:1.1.3
- **PLI**: Tatra kho āyasmā sāriputto bhikkhū āmantesi:
- **EN**: There Sāriputta addressed the mendicants:
- **NOTE**: This discourse is similar to <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn33/en/sujato'>DN 33</a>; seventy items are shared between the two. Here Sāriputta teaches without prompting. The scheme is more rigorous; items listed according to a specific pattern, then the same pattern is amplified one by one. The constraints of this scheme mean that, while almost all the items listed here are found elsewhere, they are mostly not known by the name used here. This sutta seems a little later than <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn33/en/sujato'>DN 33</a>, but such conclusions should be drawn cautiously; in at least one detail, the absence of the <i lang='pi' translate='no'>asura</i> realm from the “lost opportunities”, the passage here is earlier.

## dn34:1.1.4
- **PLI**: “āvuso bhikkhave”ti.
- **EN**: “Reverends, mendicants!”

## dn34:1.1.5
- **PLI**: “Āvuso”ti kho te bhikkhū āyasmato sāriputtassa paccassosuṁ.
- **EN**: “Reverend,” they replied.

## dn34:1.1.6

- **EN**: Sāriputta said this:
