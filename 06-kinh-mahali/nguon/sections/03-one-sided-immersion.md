# Muc 5.6 — One-Sided Immersion  (46 đoạn)

## dn6:5.6.0
- **PLI**: 2.1. Ekaṁsabhāvitasamādhi
- **EN**: 2.1. One-Sided Immersion

## dn6:5.7  `bj7.328, ms6D_604, msdiv365`
- **PLI**: “Santāneva kho, mahāli, sunakkhatto licchaviputto dibbāni saddāni nāssosi piyarūpāni kāmūpasaṁhitāni rajanīyāni, no asantānī”ti.
- **EN**: “Such sounds really do exist, but Sunakkhatta cannot hear them.”
- **NOTE**: This must have wounded his pride.

## dn6:6.1  `pts-cs6`
- **PLI**: “Ko nu kho, bhante, hetu, ko paccayo, yena santāneva sunakkhatto licchaviputto dibbāni saddāni nāssosi piyarūpāni kāmūpasaṁhitāni rajanīyāni, no asantānī”ti?
- **EN**: “What is the cause, sir, what is the reason why Sunakkhatta cannot hear them, even though they really do exist?”

## dn6:6.2  `cck9.196, csp1ed6.146, csp2ed6.146, dr9.207, km15.61, ms6D_605, msdiv366, pts-vp-pli1.153`
- **PLI**: “Idha, mahāli, bhikkhuno puratthimāya disāya ekaṁsabhāvito samādhi hoti dibbānaṁ rūpānaṁ dassanāya piyarūpānaṁ kāmūpasaṁhitānaṁ rajanīyānaṁ, no ca kho dibbānaṁ saddānaṁ savanāya piyarūpānaṁ kāmūpasaṁhitānaṁ rajanīyānaṁ.
- **EN**: “Mahāli, take a mendicant who has developed immersion to the eastern quarter in one aspect: so as to see heavenly sights but not to hear heavenly sounds.
- **NOTE**: This description of meditation is unique in the Pali canon. The Buddha answers Mahāli’s question directly, even though the premise betrays Sunakkhatta’s limited understanding. When a questioner is sincere, answering directly shows respect and builds trust.

## dn6:6.3
- **PLI**: So puratthimāya disāya ekaṁsabhāvite samādhimhi dibbānaṁ rūpānaṁ dassanāya piyarūpānaṁ kāmūpasaṁhitānaṁ rajanīyānaṁ, no ca kho dibbānaṁ saddānaṁ savanāya piyarūpānaṁ kāmūpasaṁhitānaṁ rajanīyānaṁ.
- **EN**: When they have developed immersion for that purpose,

## dn6:6.4
- **PLI**: Puratthimāya disāya dibbāni rūpāni passati piyarūpāni kāmūpasaṁhitāni rajanīyāni, no ca kho dibbāni saddāni suṇāti piyarūpāni kāmūpasaṁhitāni rajanīyāni.
- **EN**: they see heavenly sights but don’t hear heavenly sounds.

## dn6:6.5
- **PLI**: Taṁ kissa hetu?
- **EN**: Why is that?

## dn6:6.6
- **PLI**: Evañhetaṁ, mahāli, hoti bhikkhuno puratthimāya disāya ekaṁsabhāvite samādhimhi dibbānaṁ rūpānaṁ dassanāya piyarūpānaṁ kāmūpasaṁhitānaṁ rajanīyānaṁ, no ca kho dibbānaṁ saddānaṁ savanāya piyarūpānaṁ kāmūpasaṁhitānaṁ rajanīyānaṁ.
- **EN**: Because that is how it is for a mendicant who develops immersion in that way.
