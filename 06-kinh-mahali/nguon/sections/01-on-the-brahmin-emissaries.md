# Muc 1 — On the Brahmin Emissaries  (19 đoạn)

## dn6:1.0
- **PLI**: 1. Brāhmaṇadūtavatthu
- **EN**: 1. On the Brahmin Emissaries

## dn6:1.1  `bj7.324, cck9.192, csp1ed6.143, csp2ed6.143, dr9.203, km15.55, mc9.151, ms6D_597, msdiv359, ndp6.128, pts-cs1, pts-vp-pli1.150, sya9.192, vri1.134`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—
- **EN**: So I have heard.

## dn6:1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā vesāliyaṁ viharati mahāvane kūṭāgārasālāyaṁ.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying near Vesālī, at the Great Wood, in the hall with the peaked roof.
- **NOTE**: Lying some 60 kilometers north of Pāṭaliputra (Patna), Vesālī was the largest city in the Vajji Federation, a republican league in the region north of the Ganges.

## dn6:1.3
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena sambahulā kosalakā ca brāhmaṇadūtā māgadhakā ca brāhmaṇadūtā vesāliyaṁ paṭivasanti kenacideva karaṇīyena.
- **EN**: Now at that time several brahmin emissaries from Kosala and Magadha were residing in Vesālī on some business.
- **NOTE**: In the earlier suttas of this chapter, we have seen how news of the Buddha spread, evidently following Pokkharasāti’s conversion. Here we see an example of the kind of meeting at which such news would be discussed. | The phrase “brahmin emissaries” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>brāhmaṇadūtā</i>) does not seem to occur elsewhere and is not explained in the commentary. Perhaps they were emissaries of the kings, meeting in a neutral location. Or perhaps they were emissaries of their respective communities of brahmins.

## dn6:1.4
- **PLI**: Assosuṁ kho te kosalakā ca brāhmaṇadūtā māgadhakā ca brāhmaṇadūtā:
- **EN**: They heard:

## dn6:1.5
- **PLI**: “samaṇo khalu, bho, gotamo sakyaputto sakyakulā pabbajito vesāliyaṁ viharati mahāvane kūṭāgārasālāyaṁ.
- **EN**: “It seems the ascetic Gotama—a Sakyan, gone forth from a Sakyan family—is staying near Vesālī, at the Great Wood, in the hall with the peaked roof.
- **NOTE**: This monastery features prominently as the Buddha’s usual place of residence near Vesālī.

## dn6:1.6
- **PLI**: Taṁ kho pana bhavantaṁ gotamaṁ evaṁ kalyāṇo kittisaddo abbhuggato:
- **EN**: He has this good reputation:

## dn6:1.7
- **PLI**: ‘itipi so bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho vijjācaraṇasampanno sugato lokavidū anuttaro purisadammasārathi satthā devamanussānaṁ buddho bhagavā’.
- **EN**: ‘That Blessed One is perfected, a fully awakened Buddha, accomplished in knowledge and conduct, holy, knower of the world, supreme guide for those fit for training, teacher of gods and humans, awakened, blessed.’

## dn6:1.8
- **PLI**: So imaṁ lokaṁ sadevakaṁ samārakaṁ sabrahmakaṁ sassamaṇabrāhmaṇiṁ pajaṁ sadevamanussaṁ sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā pavedeti.
- **EN**: He has realized with his own insight this world—with its gods, Māras, and divinities, this population with its ascetics and brahmins, gods and humans—and he makes it known to others.

## dn6:1.9
- **PLI**: So dhammaṁ deseti ādikalyāṇaṁ majjhekalyāṇaṁ pariyosānakalyāṇaṁ sātthaṁ sabyañjanaṁ kevalaparipuṇṇaṁ parisuddhaṁ brahmacariyaṁ pakāseti.
- **EN**: He teaches Dhamma that’s good in the beginning, good in the middle, and good in the end, meaningful and well-phrased. And he reveals a spiritual practice that’s entirely full and pure.

## dn6:1.10
- **PLI**: Sādhu kho pana tathārūpānaṁ arahataṁ dassanaṁ hotī”ti.
- **EN**: It’s good to see such perfected ones.”

## dn6:2.1  `cck9.193, dr9.204, km15.56, ms6D_598, msdiv360, pts-cs2, pts-vp-pli1.151, sya9.193`
- **PLI**: Atha kho te kosalakā ca brāhmaṇadūtā māgadhakā ca brāhmaṇadūtā yena mahāvanaṁ kūṭāgārasālā tenupasaṅkamiṁsu.
- **EN**: Then they went to the hall with the peaked roof in the Great Wood to see the Buddha.

## dn6:2.2
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena āyasmā nāgito bhagavato upaṭṭhāko hoti.
- **EN**: Now, at that time Venerable Nāgita was the Buddha’s attendant.

## dn6:2.3
- **PLI**: Atha kho te kosalakā ca brāhmaṇadūtā māgadhakā ca brāhmaṇadūtā yenāyasmā nāgito tenupasaṅkamiṁsu. upasaṅkamitvā āyasmantaṁ nāgitaṁ etadavocuṁ:
- **EN**: The brahmin emissaries went up to him and said,

## dn6:2.4
- **PLI**: “kahaṁ nu kho, bho nāgita, etarahi so bhavaṁ gotamo viharati?
- **EN**: “Worthy Nāgita, where is the worthy Gotama at present?

## dn6:2.5
- **PLI**: Dassanakāmā hi mayaṁ taṁ bhavantaṁ gotaman”ti.
- **EN**: For we want to see him.”

## dn6:2.6
- **PLI**: “Akālo kho, āvuso, bhagavantaṁ dassanāya, paṭisallīno bhagavā”ti.
- **EN**: “It’s the wrong time to see the Buddha; he is on retreat.”
- **NOTE**: At some times the Buddha would go on retreat and ask that no one visit him except to bring food; this sometimes happened at the Great Wood (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn54.9/en/sujato#2.1'>SN 54.9:2.1</a>). He also had the habit of withdrawing into the wood itself for meditation (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/an5.58/en/sujato#1.3'>AN 5.58:1.3</a>). At this time, however, he was simply staying in a nearby hut, so it seems Nāgita is being over-zealous.

## dn6:2.7
- **PLI**: Atha kho te kosalakā ca brāhmaṇadūtā māgadhakā ca brāhmaṇadūtā tattheva ekamantaṁ nisīdiṁsu:
- **EN**: So right there the brahmin emissaries sat down to one side, thinking,

## dn6:2.8
- **PLI**: “disvāva mayaṁ taṁ bhavantaṁ gotamaṁ gamissāmā”ti.
- **EN**: “We’ll go only after we’ve seen the worthy Gotama.”
