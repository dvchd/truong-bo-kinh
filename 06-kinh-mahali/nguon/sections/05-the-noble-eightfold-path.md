# Muc 14 — The Noble Eightfold Path  (7 đoạn)

## dn6:14.0
- **PLI**: 2.3. Ariyaaṭṭhaṅgikamagga
- **EN**: 2.3. The Noble Eightfold Path

## dn6:14.1  `dr9.212, ms6D_616, msdiv374, pts-cs14`
- **PLI**: “Atthi pana, bhante, maggo atthi paṭipadā etesaṁ dhammānaṁ sacchikiriyāyā”ti?
- **EN**: “But, sir, is there a path and a practice for realizing these things?”

## dn6:14.2
- **PLI**: “Atthi kho, mahāli, maggo atthi paṭipadā etesaṁ dhammānaṁ sacchikiriyāyā”ti.
- **EN**: “There is, Mahāli.”

## dn6:14.3  `bj7.334, km15.70, ms6D_617, msdiv375, pts-vp-pli1.157, vri1.140`
- **PLI**: “Katamo pana, bhante, maggo katamā paṭipadā etesaṁ dhammānaṁ sacchikiriyāyā”ti?
- **EN**: “Well, what is it?”

## dn6:14.4
- **PLI**: “Ayameva ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo.
- **EN**: “It is simply this noble eightfold path, that is:
- **NOTE**: This is the most fundamental of the Buddha’s teachings on the path, declared in his first sermon (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn56.11/en/sujato'>SN 56.11</a>). It reappears in <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn8/en/sujato#13.5'>DN 8:13.5</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn19/en/sujato#61.7'>DN 19:61.7</a>, and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn22/en/sujato#21.2'>DN 22:21.2</a>.

## dn6:14.5
- **PLI**: Seyyathidaṁ—sammādiṭṭhi sammāsaṅkappo sammāvācā sammākammanto sammāājīvo sammāvāyāmo sammāsati sammāsamādhi.
- **EN**: right view, right purpose, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, and right immersion.
- **NOTE**: The eight factors map roughly on to the Gradual Training thus: hearing the Dhamma gives rise to right view; the choice to renounce is right thought; ethics includes right speech, action, and livelihood; undertaking seclusion and sense restraint is right effort; developing meditation is right mindfulness; and gaining the four <i lang='pi' translate='no'>jhānas</i> is right immersion. Realization of the Dhamma completes the circle by deepening conceptual right view to liberating insight. Sometimes this is expressed by adding two further factors, right knowledge and right liberation.

## dn6:14.6
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ kho, mahāli, maggo ayaṁ paṭipadā etesaṁ dhammānaṁ sacchikiriyāya.
- **EN**: This is the path and the practice for realizing these things.
