- **PLI**: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—
- **EN**: So I have heard.

## dn17:1.1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā kusinārāyaṁ viharati upavattane mallānaṁ sālavane antarena yamakasālānaṁ parinibbānasamaye.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying between a pair of sal trees in the sal forest of the Mallas at Upavattana near Kusinārā at the time of his full extinguishment.
- **NOTE**: Picking up the story of the Buddha’s past life as King Mahāsudassana from <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#5.17.1'>DN 16:5.17.1</a>.

## dn17:1.2.1  `pts-cs1.2`
- **PLI**: Atha kho āyasmā ānando yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi. Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho āyasmā ānando bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: Then Venerable Ānanda went up to the Buddha, bowed, sat down to one side, and said to him,

## dn17:1.2.2
- **PLI**: “mā, bhante, bhagavā imasmiṁ khuddakanagarake ujjaṅgalanagarake sākhānagarake parinibbāyi.
- **EN**: “Sir, please don’t be fully extinguished in this little hamlet, this jungle hamlet, this branch hamlet.

## dn17:1.2.3
- **PLI**: Santi, bhante, aññāni mahānagarāni.
- **EN**: There are other great cities such as

## dn17:1.2.4
- **PLI**: Seyyathidaṁ—campā, rājagahaṁ, sāvatthi, sāketaṁ, kosambī, bārāṇasī;
- **EN**: Campā, Rājagaha, Sāvatthī, Sāketa, Kosambī, and Varanasi.

## dn17:1.2.5
- **PLI**: ettha bhagavā parinibbāyatu.
- **EN**: Let the Buddha be fully extinguished there.

## dn17:1.2.6
- **PLI**: Ettha bahū khattiyamahāsālā brāhmaṇamahāsālā gahapatimahāsālā tathāgate abhippasannā, te tathāgatassa sarīrapūjaṁ karissantī”ti.
- **EN**: There are many well-to-do aristocrats, brahmins, and householders there who are devoted to the Buddha. They will perform the rites of venerating the Realized One’s corpse.”
