# Muc 1.17 — The Commander-Treasure  (7 đoạn)

## dn17:1.17.0
- **PLI**: 2.7. Pariṇāyakaratana
- **EN**: 2.7. The Commander-Treasure

## dn17:1.18.1  `bj8.276, csp1ed7.145, csp2ed7.145, ms7D_548, msdiv251, pts-cs1.18`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, ānanda, rañño mahāsudassanassa pariṇāyakaratanaṁ pāturahosi
- **EN**: Next, the commander-treasure appeared to King Mahāsudassana.

## dn17:1.18.2
- **PLI**: paṇḍito viyatto medhāvī paṭibalo rājānaṁ mahāsudassanaṁ upayāpetabbaṁ upayāpetuṁ, apayāpetabbaṁ apayāpetuṁ, ṭhapetabbaṁ ṭhapetuṁ.
- **EN**: He is astute, competent, intelligent, and capable regarding who should be ushered in to the king’s presence, who should be ushered out, and who should be kept by his side.
- **NOTE**: The “commander” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>pariṇāyaka</i>) is mostly found in Buddhist texts of this context, and is not a regular term of governance. This passage shows that he was responsible for management of the realm, while below he appears as commander of the army (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn17/en/sujato#2.8.2'>DN 17:2.8.2</a>). Elsewhere he is said to excel in strategy (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/an5.134/en/sujato#2.5'>AN 5.134:2.5</a>).

## dn17:1.18.3
- **PLI**: So rājānaṁ mahāsudassanaṁ upasaṅkamitvā evamāha:
- **EN**: He approached the king and said,

## dn17:1.18.4
- **PLI**: ‘appossukko tvaṁ, deva, hohi, ahamanusāsissāmī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Relax, Your Majesty. I shall issue instructions.’

## dn17:1.18.5
- **PLI**: Rañño, ānanda, mahāsudassanassa evarūpaṁ pariṇāyakaratanaṁ pāturahosi.
- **EN**: Such is the commander-treasure that appeared to King Mahāsudassana.

## dn17:1.18.6  `ms7D_549`
- **PLI**: Rājā, ānanda, mahāsudassano imehi sattahi ratanehi samannāgato ahosi.
- **EN**: These are the seven treasures possessed by King Mahāsudassana.
