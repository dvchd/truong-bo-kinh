# Muc 1.12 — The Elephant-Treasure  (8 đoạn)

## dn17:1.12.0
- **PLI**: 2.2. Hatthiratana
- **EN**: 2.2. The Elephant-Treasure

## dn17:1.12.1  `dr10.202, mc10.152, ms7D_543, msdiv246, pts-cs1.12`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, ānanda, rañño mahāsudassanassa hatthiratanaṁ pāturahosi
- **EN**: Next, the elephant-treasure appeared to King Mahāsudassana.

## dn17:1.12.2
- **PLI**: sabbaseto sattappatiṭṭho iddhimā vehāsaṅgamo uposatho nāma nāgarājā.
- **EN**: It was an all-white sky-walker with psychic power, touching the ground in seven places, a king of elephants named Sabbath.
- **NOTE**: The white elephant is a symbol of royalty to this day. The description anticipates the elephant Indra acquired in later legends, Airāvata the “cloudy”, white and huge and moving through the sky. | For <i lang='pi' translate='no'>sattappatiṭṭho</i>, the commentary has <i lang='pi' translate='no'>susaṇṭhitaaṅgapaccaṅga</i> (“well-grounded on each and every limb”), a sense confirmed by the Mūlasarvāstivāda Bhaiṣajyavastu which has <i lang='pi' translate='no'>saptāṅgaḥ supratiṣṭhito</i> (“well-established on seven limbs”). The subcommentary lists the four feet, trunk, tail, and penis (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>varaṅga</i>).

## dn17:1.12.3
- **PLI**: Taṁ disvā rañño mahāsudassanassa cittaṁ pasīdi:
- **EN**: Seeing him, the king was impressed,

## dn17:1.12.4
- **PLI**: ‘bhaddakaṁ vata bho hatthiyānaṁ, sace damathaṁ upeyyā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘This would truly be a fine elephant vehicle, if he would submit to taming.’

## dn17:1.12.5
- **PLI**: Atha kho taṁ, ānanda, hatthiratanaṁ—seyyathāpi nāma gandhahatthājāniyo dīgharattaṁ suparidanto; evameva damathaṁ upagacchi.
- **EN**: Then the elephant-treasure submitted to taming, as if he was a fine thoroughbred elephant that had been tamed for a long time.

## dn17:1.12.6
- **PLI**: Bhūtapubbaṁ, ānanda, rājā mahāsudassano tameva hatthiratanaṁ vīmaṁsamāno pubbaṇhasamayaṁ abhiruhitvā samuddapariyantaṁ pathaviṁ anuyāyitvā kusāvatiṁ rājadhāniṁ paccāgantvā pātarāsamakāsi.
- **EN**: Once it so happened that King Mahāsudassana, testing that same elephant-treasure, mounted him in the morning and traversed the land surrounded by ocean before returning to the royal capital in time for breakfast.

## dn17:1.12.7
- **PLI**: Rañño, ānanda, mahāsudassanassa evarūpaṁ hatthiratanaṁ pāturahosi.
- **EN**: Such is the elephant-treasure that appeared to King Mahāsudassana.
