# Muc 1.13 — The Horse-Treasure  (8 đoạn)

## dn17:1.13.0
- **PLI**: 2.3. Assaratana
- **EN**: 2.3. The Horse-Treasure

## dn17:1.13.1  `cck10.216, csp1ed7.143, csp2ed7.143, dr10.203, ms7D_544, msdiv247, ndp7.134, pts-cs1.13, pts-vp-pli2.175, sya10.202`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, ānanda, rañño mahāsudassanassa assaratanaṁ pāturahosi
- **EN**: Next, the horse-treasure appeared to King Mahāsudassana.

## dn17:1.13.2
- **PLI**: sabbaseto kāḷasīso muñjakeso iddhimā vehāsaṅgamo valāhako nāma assarājā.
- **EN**: It was an all-white sky-walker with psychic power, with head of black and mane like woven reeds, a royal steed named Thundercloud.
- **NOTE**: The sacrificial horse is likewise white with black head or forequarters. It is identified with the sun, thus being a “sky-walker”. “Thundercloud” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>valāhaka</i>; Sanskrit <i lang='pi' translate='no'>balāhaka</i>) is the name of one of the four horses of Kṛṣṇa’s chariot in the Mahābharata. The description here also recalls the Vedic sacred horse Uccaiḥśravas.

## dn17:1.13.3
- **PLI**: Taṁ disvā rañño mahāsudassanassa cittaṁ pasīdi:
- **EN**: Seeing him, the king was impressed,

## dn17:1.13.4
- **PLI**: ‘bhaddakaṁ vata bho assayānaṁ sace damathaṁ upeyyā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘This would truly be a fine horse vehicle, if he would submit to taming.’

## dn17:1.13.5
- **PLI**: Atha kho taṁ, ānanda, assaratanaṁ seyyathāpi nāma bhaddo assājāniyo dīgharattaṁ suparidanto; evameva damathaṁ upagacchi.
- **EN**: Then the horse-treasure submitted to taming, as if he was a fine thoroughbred horse that had been tamed for a long time.

## dn17:1.13.6
- **PLI**: Bhūtapubbaṁ, ānanda, rājā mahāsudassano tameva assaratanaṁ vīmaṁsamāno pubbaṇhasamayaṁ abhiruhitvā samuddapariyantaṁ pathaviṁ anuyāyitvā kusāvatiṁ rājadhāniṁ paccāgantvā pātarāsamakāsi.
- **EN**: Once it so happened that King Mahāsudassana, testing that same horse-treasure, mounted him in the morning and traversed the land surrounded by ocean before returning to the royal capital in time for breakfast.

## dn17:1.13.7
- **PLI**: Rañño, ānanda, mahāsudassanassa evarūpaṁ assaratanaṁ pāturahosi.
- **EN**: Such is the horse-treasure that appeared to King Mahāsudassana.
