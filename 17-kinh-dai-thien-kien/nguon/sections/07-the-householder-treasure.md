# Muc 1.16 — The Householder-Treasure  (14 đoạn)

## dn17:1.16.0
- **PLI**: 2.6. Gahapatiratana
- **EN**: 2.6. The Householder-Treasure

## dn17:1.16.1  `cck10.218, dr10.205, mc10.154, ms7D_547, msdiv250, ndp7.135, pts-cs1.16, pts-vp-pli2.177, sya10.204, vri2.132`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, ānanda, rañño mahāsudassanassa gahapatiratanaṁ pāturahosi.
- **EN**: Next, the householder-treasure appeared to King Mahāsudassana.

## dn17:1.16.2
- **PLI**: Tassa kammavipākajaṁ dibbacakkhu pāturahosi yena nidhiṁ passati sassāmikampi assāmikampi.
- **EN**: The power of clairvoyance manifested in him as a result of past deeds, by which he sees treasure troves, both owned and ownerless.

## dn17:1.16.3
- **PLI**: So rājānaṁ mahāsudassanaṁ upasaṅkamitvā evamāha:
- **EN**: He approached the king and said,

## dn17:1.16.4
- **PLI**: ‘appossukko tvaṁ, deva, hohi, ahaṁ te dhanena dhanakaraṇīyaṁ karissāmī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Relax, Your Majesty. I will take care of the treasury.’

## dn17:1.16.5
- **PLI**: Bhūtapubbaṁ, ānanda, rājā mahāsudassano tameva gahapatiratanaṁ vīmaṁsamāno nāvaṁ abhiruhitvā majjhe gaṅgāya nadiyā sotaṁ ogāhitvā gahapatiratanaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: Once it so happened that the wheel-turning monarch, testing that same householder-treasure, boarded a boat and sailed to the middle of the Ganges River. Then he said to the householder-treasure,

## dn17:1.16.6
- **PLI**: ‘attho me, gahapati, hiraññasuvaṇṇenā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Householder, I need gold, both coined and uncoined.’

## dn17:1.16.7
- **PLI**: ‘Tena hi, mahārāja, ekaṁ tīraṁ nāvā upetū’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Well then, Great King, draw the boat up to one shore.’

## dn17:1.16.8
- **PLI**: ‘Idheva me, gahapati, attho hiraññasuvaṇṇenā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘It’s right here, householder, that I need gold, both coined and uncoined.’

## dn17:1.16.9
- **PLI**: Atha kho taṁ, ānanda, gahapatiratanaṁ ubhohi hatthehi udakaṁ omasitvā pūraṁ hiraññasuvaṇṇassa kumbhiṁ uddharitvā rājānaṁ mahāsudassanaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: Then that householder-treasure, immersing both hands in the water, pulled up a pot full of gold, both coined and uncoined, and said to the king,

## dn17:1.16.10
- **PLI**: ‘alamettāvatā, mahārāja, katamettāvatā, mahārāja, pūjitamettāvatā, mahārājā’ti?
- **EN**: ‘Is this sufficient, Great King? Has enough been done, Great King, enough offered?’

## dn17:1.16.11
- **PLI**: Rājā mahāsudassano evamāha:
- **EN**: The king said,

## dn17:1.16.12
- **PLI**: ‘alamettāvatā, gahapati, katamettāvatā, gahapati, pūjitamettāvatā, gahapatī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘That is sufficient, householder. Enough has been done, enough offered.’

## dn17:1.16.13
- **PLI**: Rañño, ānanda, mahāsudassanassa evarūpaṁ gahapatiratanaṁ pāturahosi.
- **EN**: Such is the householder-treasure that appeared to King Mahāsudassana.
