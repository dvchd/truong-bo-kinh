# Muc 2.13 — Rebirth in the Realm of Divinity  (37 đoạn)

## dn17:2.13.0
- **PLI**: 8. Brahmalokūpagama
- **EN**: 8. Rebirth in the Realm of Divinity

## dn17:2.13.1  `bj8.300, dr10.225, mc10.169, ms7D_588, msdiv269, ndp7.149, pts-cs2.13, pts-vp-pli2.196, vri2.145`
- **PLI**: Atha kho, ānanda, rājā mahāsudassano na cirasseva kālamakāsi.
- **EN**: Not long after that, King Mahāsudassana passed away.

## dn17:2.13.2
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, ānanda, gahapatissa vā gahapatiputtassa vā manuññaṁ bhojanaṁ bhuttāvissa bhattasammado hoti; evameva kho, ānanda, rañño mahāsudassanassa māraṇantikā vedanā ahosi.
- **EN**: And the feeling he had close to death was like a householder or their child falling asleep after eating a satisfying meal.
- **NOTE**: This contrasts with the Buddha’s last meal at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#4.20.1'>DN 16:4.20.1</a>, which caused sickness and distress. The Buddha was rejecting existence entirely, whereas Mahāsudassana was continuing in a pleasant form of conditioned existence.

## dn17:2.13.3
- **PLI**: Kālaṅkato ca, ānanda, rājā mahāsudassano sugatiṁ brahmalokaṁ upapajji.
- **EN**: When he passed away King Mahāsudassana was reborn in a good place, a realm of divinity.

## dn17:2.13.4
- **PLI**: Rājā, ānanda, mahāsudassano caturāsīti vassasahassāni kumārakīḷaṁ kīḷi.
- **EN**: Ānanda, King Mahāsudassana played children’s games for 84,000 years.

## dn17:2.13.5
- **PLI**: Caturāsīti vassasahassāni oparajjaṁ kāresi.
- **EN**: He ruled as viceroy for 84,000 years.

## dn17:2.13.6
- **PLI**: Caturāsīti vassasahassāni rajjaṁ kāresi.
- **EN**: He ruled as king for 84,000 years.

## dn17:2.13.7
- **PLI**: Caturāsīti vassasahassāni gihibhūto dhamme pāsāde brahmacariyaṁ cari.
- **EN**: He led the spiritual life as a layman in the Palace of Principle for 84,000 years.

## dn17:2.13.8
- **PLI**: So cattāro brahmavihāre bhāvetvā kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā brahmalokūpago ahosi.
- **EN**: And having developed the four divine meditations, when his body broke up, after death, he was reborn in a good place, a realm of divinity.
