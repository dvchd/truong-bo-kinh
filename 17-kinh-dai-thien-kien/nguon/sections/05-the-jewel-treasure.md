# Muc 1.14 — The Jewel-Treasure  (7 đoạn)

## dn17:1.14.0
- **PLI**: 2.4. Maṇiratana
- **EN**: 2.4. The Jewel-Treasure

## dn17:1.14.1  `ms7D_545, msdiv248, pts-cs1.14, vri2.131`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, ānanda, rañño mahāsudassanassa maṇiratanaṁ pāturahosi.
- **EN**: Next, the jewel-treasure appeared to King Mahāsudassana.

## dn17:1.14.2
- **PLI**: So ahosi maṇi veḷuriyo subho jātimā aṭṭhaṁso suparikammakato accho vippasanno anāvilo sabbākārasampanno.
- **EN**: It was a beryl gem that was naturally lustrous, eight-faceted, well-worked, transparent, clear, and unclouded, endowed with all good qualities.

## dn17:1.14.3
- **PLI**: Tassa kho panānanda, maṇiratanassa ābhā samantā yojanaṁ phuṭā ahosi.
- **EN**: And the radiance of that jewel spread all-round for a league.

## dn17:1.14.4
- **PLI**: Bhūtapubbaṁ, ānanda, rājā mahāsudassano tameva maṇiratanaṁ vīmaṁsamāno caturaṅginiṁ senaṁ sannayhitvā maṇiṁ dhajaggaṁ āropetvā rattandhakāratimisāya pāyāsi.
- **EN**: Once it so happened that King Mahāsudassana, testing that same jewel-treasure, mobilized his army of four divisions and, with the jewel hoisted on his banner, set out in the dark of the night.

## dn17:1.14.5
- **PLI**: Ye kho panānanda, samantā gāmā ahesuṁ, te tenobhāsena kammante payojesuṁ divāti maññamānā.
- **EN**: Then the villagers around them set off to work, thinking that it was day.

## dn17:1.14.6
- **PLI**: Rañño, ānanda, mahāsudassanassa evarūpaṁ maṇiratanaṁ pāturahosi.
- **EN**: Such is the jewel-treasure that appeared to King Mahāsudassana.
