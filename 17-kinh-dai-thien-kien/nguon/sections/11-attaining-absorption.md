## dn17:2.2.1  `ms7D_576, pts-cs2.2, vri2.139`
- **PLI**: Atha kho, ānanda, rājā mahāsudassano yena mahāviyūhaṁ kūṭāgāraṁ tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā mahāviyūhassa kūṭāgārassa dvāre ṭhito udānaṁ udānesi:
- **EN**: Then he went to the Chamber of the Grand Tower, stood at the door, and expressed this heartfelt sentiment:

## dn17:2.2.2
- **PLI**: ‘tiṭṭha, kāmavitakka, tiṭṭha, byāpādavitakka, tiṭṭha, vihiṁsāvitakka.
- **EN**: ‘Stop here, sensual, malicious, and cruel thoughts—
- **NOTE**: These are the three factors of right thought. The king skillfully uses a transition in physical space to set up his intention to meditate.

## dn17:2.2.3
- **PLI**: Ettāvatā, kāmavitakka, ettāvatā, byāpādavitakka, ettāvatā, vihiṁsāvitakkā’ti.
- **EN**: no further!’

## dn17:2.3.1  `cck10.230, dr10.216, mc10.162, ms7D_577, msdiv261, pts-cs2.3, sya10.215`
- **PLI**: Atha kho, ānanda, rājā mahāsudassano mahāviyūhaṁ kūṭāgāraṁ pavisitvā sovaṇṇamaye pallaṅke nisinno vivicceva kāmehi vivicca akusalehi dhammehi savitakkaṁ savicāraṁ vivekajaṁ pītisukhaṁ paṭhamaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja vihāsi.
- **EN**: Then he entered the Chamber of the Grand Tower and sat on the golden couch. Quite secluded from sensual pleasures, secluded from unskillful qualities, he entered and remained in the first absorption, which has the rapture and bliss born of seclusion, while placing the mind and keeping it connected.
- **NOTE**: Here as in <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn1/en/sujato'>DN 1</a>, <i lang='pi' translate='no'>jhāna</i> is not a uniquely Buddhist practice.

## dn17:2.3.2
- **PLI**: Vitakkavicārānaṁ vūpasamā ajjhattaṁ sampasādanaṁ cetaso ekodibhāvaṁ avitakkaṁ avicāraṁ samādhijaṁ pītisukhaṁ dutiyaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja vihāsi.
- **EN**: As the placing of the mind and keeping it connected were stilled, he entered and remained in the second absorption, which has the rapture and bliss born of immersion, with internal clarity and mind at one, without placing the mind and keeping it connected.

## dn17:2.3.3
- **PLI**: Pītiyā ca virāgā upekkhako ca vihāsi, sato ca sampajāno sukhañca kāyena paṭisaṁvedesi, yaṁ taṁ ariyā ācikkhanti: ‘upekkhako satimā sukhavihārī’ti tatiyaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja vihāsi.
- **EN**: And with the fading away of rapture, he entered and remained in the third absorption, where he meditated with equanimity, mindful and aware, experiencing for themselves the bliss of which the noble ones declare, ‘Equanimous and mindful, one meditates in bliss.’

## dn17:2.3.4
- **PLI**: Sukhassa ca pahānā dukkhassa ca pahānā pubbeva somanassadomanassānaṁ atthaṅgamā adukkhamasukhaṁ upekkhāsatipārisuddhiṁ catutthaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja vihāsi.
- **EN**: With the giving up of pleasure and pain, and the disappearance of former happiness and sadness, he entered and remained in the fourth absorption, without pleasure or pain, with pure equanimity and mindfulness.

## dn17:2.4.1  `csp1ed7.153, csp2ed7.153, ms7D_578, msdiv262, pts-cs2.4, pts-vp-pli2.187, sya10.216`

- **EN**: Then King Mahāsudassana left the Chamber of the Grand Tower and entered the golden chamber, where he sat on the golden couch.

## dn17:2.4.2

- **EN**: He meditated spreading a heart full of love to one direction, and to the second, and to the third, and to the fourth.
