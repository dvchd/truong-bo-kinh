# Muc 1.15 — The Woman-Treasure  (9 đoạn)

## dn17:1.15.0
- **PLI**: 2.5. Itthiratana
- **EN**: 2.5. The Woman-Treasure

## dn17:1.15.1  `bj8.274, cck10.217, csp1ed7.144, csp2ed7.144, dr10.204, mc10.153, ms7D_546, msdiv249, pts-cs1.15, pts-vp-pli2.176, sya10.203`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, ānanda, rañño mahāsudassanassa itthiratanaṁ pāturahosi
- **EN**: Next, the woman-treasure appeared to King Mahāsudassana.

## dn17:1.15.2
- **PLI**: abhirūpā dassanīyā pāsādikā paramāya vaṇṇapokkharatāya samannāgatā nātidīghā nātirassā nātikisā nātithūlā nātikāḷikā nāccodātā atikkantā mānusivaṇṇaṁ appattā dibbavaṇṇaṁ.
- **EN**: She was attractive, good-looking, lovely, of surpassing beauty. She was neither too tall nor too short; neither too thin nor too fat; neither too dark nor too light. She outdid human beauty without reaching heavenly beauty.

## dn17:1.15.3
- **PLI**: Tassa kho panānanda, itthiratanassa evarūpo kāyasamphasso hoti, seyyathāpi nāma tūlapicuno vā kappāsapicuno vā.
- **EN**: And her touch was like a tuft of cotton-wool or kapok.

## dn17:1.15.4
- **PLI**: Tassa kho panānanda, itthiratanassa sīte uṇhāni gattāni honti, uṇhe sītāni.
- **EN**: When it was cool her limbs were warm, and when it was warm her limbs were cool.

## dn17:1.15.5
- **PLI**: Tassa kho panānanda, itthiratanassa kāyato candanagandho vāyati, mukhato uppalagandho.
- **EN**: The fragrance of sandal floated from her body, and lotus from her mouth.

## dn17:1.15.6
- **PLI**: Taṁ kho panānanda, itthiratanaṁ rañño mahāsudassanassa pubbuṭṭhāyinī ahosi pacchānipātinī kiṅkārapaṭissāvinī manāpacārinī piyavādinī.
- **EN**: She got up before the king and went to bed after him, and was obliging, behaving nicely and speaking politely.

## dn17:1.15.7
- **PLI**: Taṁ kho panānanda, itthiratanaṁ rājānaṁ mahāsudassanaṁ manasāpi no aticari, kuto pana kāyena.
- **EN**: The woman-treasure did not betray the wheel-turning monarch even in thought, still less in deed.

## dn17:1.15.8
- **PLI**: Rañño, ānanda, mahāsudassanassa evarūpaṁ itthiratanaṁ pāturahosi.
- **EN**: Such is the woman-treasure that appeared to King Mahāsudassana.
