# Muc 1.18.7 — The Four Blessings  (22 đoạn)

## dn17:1.18.7.0
- **PLI**: 3. Catuiddhisamannāgata
- **EN**: 3. The Four Blessings

## dn17:1.18.8  `cck10.219, dr10.206, ms7D_550, msdiv252`
- **PLI**: Rājā, ānanda, mahāsudassano catūhi iddhīhi samannāgato ahosi.
- **EN**: King Mahāsudassana possessed four blessings.

## dn17:1.18.9
- **PLI**: Katamāhi catūhi iddhīhi?
- **EN**: And what are the four blessings?

## dn17:1.18.10
- **PLI**: Idhānanda, rājā mahāsudassano abhirūpo ahosi dassanīyo pāsādiko paramāya vaṇṇapokkharatāya samannāgato ativiya aññehi manussehi.
- **EN**: He was attractive, good-looking, lovely, of surpassing beauty, more so than other humans.

## dn17:1.18.11
- **PLI**: Rājā, ānanda, mahāsudassano imāya paṭhamāya iddhiyā samannāgato ahosi.
- **EN**: This is the first blessing.

## dn17:1.19.1  `ms7D_551, ndp7.136, pts-cs1.19, sya10.205`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, ānanda, rājā mahāsudassano dīghāyuko ahosi ciraṭṭhitiko ativiya aññehi manussehi.
- **EN**: Furthermore, he was long-lived, more so than other humans.

## dn17:1.19.2
- **PLI**: Rājā, ānanda, mahāsudassano imāya dutiyāya iddhiyā samannāgato ahosi.
- **EN**: This is the second blessing.

## dn17:1.20.1  `ms7D_552, pts-cs1.20, vri2.133`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, ānanda, rājā mahāsudassano appābādho ahosi appātaṅko samavepākiniyā gahaṇiyā samannāgato nātisītāya nāccuṇhāya ativiya aññehi manussehi.
- **EN**: Furthermore, he was rarely ill or unwell, and his stomach digested well, being neither too hot nor too cold, more so than other humans.

## dn17:1.20.2
- **PLI**: Rājā, ānanda, mahāsudassano imāya tatiyāya iddhiyā samannāgato ahosi.
- **EN**: This is the third blessing.

## dn17:1.21.8
- **PLI**: Atha kho, ānanda, brāhmaṇagahapatikā rājānaṁ mahāsudassanaṁ upasaṅkamitvā evamāhaṁsu:

## dn17:1.21.9
- **PLI**: ‘ataramāno, deva, yāhi, yathā taṁ mayaṁ cirataraṁ passeyyāmā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Slow down, Your Majesty, so we may see you longer!’

## dn17:1.21.10
- **PLI**: Rājāpi, ānanda, mahāsudassano sārathiṁ āmantesi:

## dn17:1.21.11

- **EN**: ‘Drive slowly, charioteer, so I can see the brahmins and householders longer!’

## dn17:1.21.12
